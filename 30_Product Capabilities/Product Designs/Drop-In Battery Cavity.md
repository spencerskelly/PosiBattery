# Drop-In Battery Cavity

## Definition

A truck compartment that takes a separate battery module as a unit, so the battery can be lifted, rolled or slid out and replaced (lead-acid tray, or a lithium pack built to the same cavity).

## Notes

- Design characteristic found in product descriptions, not a decision by us. One of two battery-installation terms (the other is [[Vehicle-Integrated Battery]]); terminology proposed 2026-10-14, replacing the informal "drop-in cavity".
- Includes the faster-change forms in [[Quick-Change Battery Compartment]]. A truck can offer both a drop-in lead-acid cavity and a lithium pack in the same cavity (for example the Hyster J-series).
- No Requirement is linked (intentional gap).

## Aliases

- Drop-in battery cavity
- Removable battery compartment
