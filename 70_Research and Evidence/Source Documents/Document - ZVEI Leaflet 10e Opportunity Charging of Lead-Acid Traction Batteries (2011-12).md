# Document - ZVEI Leaflet 10e Opportunity Charging of Lead-Acid Traction Batteries (2011-12)

## Definition

2011 edition of ZVEI leaflet 10e on opportunity charging of lead-acid traction batteries.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Opportunity charges are partial charges between full charges; full state of charge is not reached. Effectiveness drops if charging starts before about 30 percent depth of discharge; acceptance falls once the gassing voltage is reached (2.4 V per cell at 30 C for PzS).
- Life: the extra energy turnover above 80 percent of nominal capacity affects life; for regular opportunity charges above 1 times nominal capacity a battery change is recommended; repeated charges over 80 percent of Cn per day can cause acid stratification in flooded PzS and PzB.
- Temperature: maximum 55 C flooded, 45 C valve-regulated; throughput of up to 1.2 times nominal capacity between full charges raises the average temperature; temperature-compensated charging recommended where it is high.
- Current: PzS maximum 1.5 times I5 (example 100 Ah: I5 = 20 A, limit 30 A), which is 0.3 C5. Above gassing voltage with high current, water use, gassing, heat and sludging rise (C339).
- Ventilation formula and example cross-sections given. No equalizing rule, no cycle counts, no water figures.
