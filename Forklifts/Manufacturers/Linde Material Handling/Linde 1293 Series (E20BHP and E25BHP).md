# Linde 1293 Series (E20BHP and E25BHP)

## Definition

Linde 4,000 to 5,000 lb electric counterbalance forklifts powered by Linde lithium-ion batteries for indoor and outdoor use.

## Notes

- KION North America says the E20BHP and E25BHP are electric counterbalance forklifts of 4,000 to 5,000 lb powered by Linde lithium-ion batteries, for indoor and outdoor applications.
