# BlitzNXT

## Definition

Seller of 24 V to 80 V LiFePO4 forklift batteries known here only through its LinkedIn page; its own website was not located.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** battery maker (lithium); whether it manufactures or relabels is not established.
- Its LinkedIn posts say packs use EVE Grade A LiFePO4 cells, IP67 enclosures and a 10-year warranty, with US support from a warehouse the posts place in Humble, TX in some posts and Houston, TX in another. Source: five BlitzNXT LinkedIn posts (T4), read 2026-10-13.
- A reseller listing (FridayParts) shows packs with dimensions identical to the BlitzNXT models but does not name BlitzNXT, and gives a 1-year warranty; the relationship is unverified (C185). Source: [[Document - FridayParts 24V-80V LiFePO4 Forklift Battery Listing (retrieved 2026-10-13)]] (T3).
- Not found: company registration, address, manufacturer of the packs, UL or other certificates (the posts name UL 2580 and IEC 62619 as references, not as BlitzNXT certifications).
