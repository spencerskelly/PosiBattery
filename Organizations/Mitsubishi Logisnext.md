# Mitsubishi Logisnext

## Definition

Japanese truck group, fifth in the 2024 ranking, selling Mitsubishi, Cat, UniCarriers and Rocla brands and distributing Jungheinrich in North America.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Truck OEM, Brand Owner, Dealer or Distributor.
- The ranking lists Mitsubishi Logisnext fifth with brands UniCarriers, Mitsubishi, CAT, Jungheinrich (North America only) and Rocla. Source: Modern Materials Handling via Logistics Management (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/top_20_lift_truck_suppliers_2024>
- Mitsubishi Logisnext Americas, headquartered in Houston, says its portfolio spans five brands: Mitsubishi forklift trucks, Cat lift trucks, Rocla AGV Solutions, UniCarriers Forklifts and Jungheinrich warehouse and automation products, and it is the exclusive manufacturer and provider of UniCarriers across the Americas. Source: Logisnext Americas release (2022) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisnextamericas.com/en/logisnext/news/articles/unicarriers/2022/MLA-Launches-Two-New-UniCarriers-80V-Electric-Pneumatic-Forklifts>
- Cat lift trucks sold by Mitsubishi Logisnext Americas carry Caterpillar trademarks used under license (page footer). Source: Logisnext Americas Cat page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisnextamericas.com/en-bz/cat/long-term-support/forklift-safety-training>
