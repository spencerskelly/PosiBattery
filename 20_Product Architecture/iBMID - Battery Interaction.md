# BMID Battery Interaction

## Definition

Reusable logical interaction boundary between a BMID-family device and the industrial traction battery whose supported state, identity, or condition information the BMID observes or retains.

## Notes

- This is a logical architecture boundary, not a connector, pinout, harness, or protocol definition.
- Specific sensing mechanisms remain product/variant dependent.
- Use this Port definition for contextual Local Model endpoints where battery-to-BMID interaction is needed without asserting unsupported physical topology.
