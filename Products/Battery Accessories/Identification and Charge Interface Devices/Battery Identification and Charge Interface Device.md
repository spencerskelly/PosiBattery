# Battery Identification and Charge Interface Device

## Definition

Battery-installed device whose primary reusable purpose is to identify a battery and provide battery-specific information or measurements to a compatible charger or charging system.

## Notes

This family covers products where charger interaction is central to the product definition. A monitoring product with optional charger communication may remain under Battery Monitoring Device.

## Aliases

- Battery Identifier
- Battery Charge Interface Device
