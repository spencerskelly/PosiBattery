# Document - Canada Clean Fuel Regulations Section 101 Charging Site Host Credits (Justice Laws)

## Definition

Section 101 of Canada's Clean Fuel Regulations, giving the credit formula for charging-site hosts based on metered kWh and an energy efficiency ratio.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. Section 101 of SOR/2022-140 on credits for charging-site hosts (section 102, for other station types, was not shown).

- **What is allowed:** a charging-site host may create provisional compliance credits by displacing liquid fuel with electricity used by an electric vehicle in a class listed in the Specifications for Fuel LCA Model CI Calculations, supplied through a station other than those in subsection 102(1).
- **Quantity:** kWh supplied to electric vehicles 'as measured by charging stations'; accuracy and load tolerance follow a U.S. NIST document.
- **Formula:** credits = CIdiff x (Q x D) x 10^-6, with CIdiff = (Ree x CIref) - CIe and D = 3.6 MJ per kWh. Ree is the Specifications value for the vehicle class or, by election, 2.5; where a station serves several classes and the energy cannot be split, the lower ratio applies.
- **Forklifts and off-road equipment:** not addressed in the text read; eligibility turns on the vehicle classes in the Specifications document, which was not read (IB-266).
- **Compare:** California's lowest-credit rule for unsplittable lift classes (REQ-00061) has the same shape as the CFR's lower-ratio rule.
