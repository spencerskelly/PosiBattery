# Centralized Battery Room and Charging Area

## Definition

Fixed charging context where multiple batteries, chargers, operators and maintenance activities are concentrated in a dedicated battery-room or centralized charging area.

## Notes

- Use this context for centralized charging, battery handling, watering, equalization, maintenance and charging-cable workflows.
- Typical external constraints include facility electrical service, ventilation, space, traffic flow and charger/battery compatibility.
- Derived from the Battery-room and centralized charging segment in [[PosiCharge Market Segments and Jobs-to-Be-Done]].
