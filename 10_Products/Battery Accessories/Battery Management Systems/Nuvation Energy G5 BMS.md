# Nuvation Energy G5 BMS

## Definition

Configurable third-party BMS for battery stacks; recognized under UL 1973 and stated to be CE conformant.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Configurable third-party BMS for battery stacks; recognized under UL 1973 and stated to be CE conformant.

**Marketed features:**
- Described as the first configurable 3rd party BMS to attain UL 1973 Recognition
- UL Recognized units can reduce UL 1973 certification effort for battery stacks
- G5 BMS is CE conformant

**Summary and features sources:**
Maker or publisher marketing claims as stated, not independently verified.
