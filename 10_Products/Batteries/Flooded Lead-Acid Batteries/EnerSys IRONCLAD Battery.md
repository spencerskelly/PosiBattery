# EnerSys IRONCLAD Battery

## Definition

EnerSys flooded lead-acid forklift battery line.

## Notes

- EnerSys's forklift page lists IRONCLAD batteries, and a dealer lists IRONCLAD WorkHog and Loadhog variants. Source: EnerSys forklift page and Western Materials (T1/T3), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.enersys.com/en/industries/logistics-warehousing/forklifts-pallet-trucks/>
- **Also:** dealer listing <https://www.westmat.com/parts-category/lead-industrial-batteries/>.
- Listed in the Logisnext Promatch parts program (2025). Source: Logisnext Americas release (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisnextamericas.com/en/logisnext/news/mla-enersys-expand-power-solutions-for-material-handling-operations>

- **Official sources found 2026-10-13 (read through a summarizing tool, IB-182):** [[Document - EnerSys IRONCLAD Americas Page (retrieved 2026-10-13)]] <https://enersys.com/en/products/batteries/ironclad/ironclad-americas>; [[Document - EnerSys IRONCLAD Loadhog Technical Data Sheet (AM-ICLH-TD AA, 2020-09)]] <https://www.enersys.com/4a02bc/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/ironclad/amer/enersys_ironclad_loadhog_technical_data_sheet.pdf>; [[Document - EnerSys IRONCLAD Technical Data (EMEA-EN-TD-IC-0524)]] <https://enersys.com/492e6d/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/ironclad/apac/emea-en-td-ic-0524.pdf>; [[Document - EnerSys IRONCLAD Product Guide (APAC-EN-PG-IC-0324)]] <https://www.enersys.com/495d9a/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/_enersys/apac/apac-en-pg-ic-0324.pdf>.
- **Lines and construction:** flooded lead-acid; square tubular positive plates ("up to 84% more positive plate surface area"); Americas lines Deserthog, Loadhog and Superhog; Loadhog series E55L to E140 at 110 to 1,960 Ah per cell; the line spans 110 to 2,790 Ah (C6, 1.75 V per cell, 77 F) and 12 to 96 V on the Americas page; EMEA PzQ cells 276 to 2,064 Ah. Warranty "5+1 year limited" (Loadhog sheet, 2020). Sources: <https://enersys.com/en/products/batteries/ironclad/ironclad-americas>; <https://www.enersys.com/4a02bc/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/ironclad/amer/enersys_ironclad_loadhog_technical_data_sheet.pdf>; <https://enersys.com/492e6d/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/ironclad/apac/emea-en-td-ic-0524.pdf>
- **Claims:** up to 15 percent more power, 10 percent longer run time, 20 percent longer life, up to 120 percent of capacity per day with fast-opportunity charging and high-frequency chargers (APAC guide, maker claims). <https://www.enersys.com/495d9a/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/_enersys/apac/apac-en-pg-ic-0324.pdf>
- **Conflicts kept visible:** the dealer-sourced WorkHog variant above is not on the EnerSys page (C220); the page rates capacity at 1.75 V per cell, the Loadhog sheet at 1.70 V per cell (C221).
- **Design characteristics, with citations:**
  - [[Tubular Plate Construction]] (V): square tubular positive plates. <https://enersys.com/en/products/batteries/ironclad/ironclad-americas>
- **Offered with:** [[EnerSys Wi-iQ]] (V, APAC guide: monitoring option that works with NexSys+ chargers). <https://www.enersys.com/495d9a/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/_enersys/apac/apac-en-pg-ic-0324.pdf>
- **Options now with notes:** [[EnerSys BSI40]], [[EnerSys EZSelect Lite]] (the guide says "EZ Select"; C223), [[EnerSys LifeNetwork iQ]], [[EnerSys EnSite]].
- **Options still without notes:** E Connect app (IB-183).
- **Not stated:** cycle life count, watering and HydroFill options, grid alloy.
