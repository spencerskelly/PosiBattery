# Document - MMH Lithium-Ion Batteries Help Trim Fleet by a Third (Standard Distributing)

## Definition

Modern Materials Handling case article on a beer distributor that switched forklifts to lithium-ion batteries.

## Notes

Facts below were read through the fetch tool's summary of the page, not from the raw page.

- Customer: Standard Distributing Co. of Delaware, a beer distributor. The switch happened in April 2019; the page does not date the article. Vendor: OneCharge supplied the forklifts and batteries.
- Problems the customer side describes: a dirty battery charging area, daily lead-acid maintenance, frequent battery changes, eight-hour charging and cooling per battery, downtime for changes during shifts, and spare trucks kept on hand. The fleet went from 17 three-wheel trucks to 12 new 36 V trucks. No labor-hour figures.
- Customer statement (warehouse manager, named on the page): the charging area was dirty and lead-acid maintenance and charging were 'simply not needed' with lithium-ion. One customer, vendor-supplied, selected by the vendor for a case article.
