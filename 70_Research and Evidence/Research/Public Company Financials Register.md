# Public Company Financials Register

## Definition

Latest-fiscal-year financial figures for listed companies in or beside the battery-adjacent equipment space (truck makers, battery makers, fuel-cell and fleet-software vendors), each traced to a source note, with cautions where the sources disagree or are weak. Built 2026-10-18 to start sizing the market.

## Notes

**How to read it.** Figures are in each company's own currency and are not converted. Margins marked *derived* are calculated here from the cited figures; the company did not state them. Every source was read through a summarizing fetch tool, so numbers should be checked against the filing before use in a decision. Tiers follow the source notes (T1 company filing or release, T3 aggregator or automated summary).
