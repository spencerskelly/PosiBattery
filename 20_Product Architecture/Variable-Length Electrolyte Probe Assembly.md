# Variable-Length Electrolyte Probe Assembly

## Definition

Electrolyte-level probe assembly with adjustable probe length for adapting the sensing threshold to the target battery cell.

## Notes

- The PowerTrac 3 specification explicitly calls out a standard electrolyte sensor with a variable-length probe.
- The sensing principle, electrode material, excitation method, and connector details are not stated in the public specification.
