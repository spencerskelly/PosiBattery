# Load-Handling Image Storage

## Definition

Persistent storage used to retain captured load-handling images and associated metadata until review, transfer, or overwrite.

## Notes

- Physical implementation may be internal flash, removable media, solid-state storage, or another persistent medium.
- Capacity, retention policy, image compression, overwrite behavior, and data export are not published for the current product evidence.
