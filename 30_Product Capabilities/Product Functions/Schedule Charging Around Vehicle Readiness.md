# Schedule Charging Around Vehicle Readiness

## Definition

Plan when and where each vehicle or robot charges so it is ready when its work needs it.

## Notes

- **Solution link added 2026-10-14:** depends on [[Charge Scheduling and Prioritization Design]] (new software Design; owner asked that all aspects be modeled).
- Stated: Ampcontrol (geofence assignment by readiness), depotfinity (schedules and priorities), Seegrid Fleet Central (schedule charging), OTTO Fleet Manager (recharge in idle periods), WiBotic Commander (charge scheduling API), PowerFlex X Fleet+ (charging scheduler). Links are made only where a source states the behavior.
- No Requirement is linked.
