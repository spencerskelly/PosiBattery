# BMID Charger Communication

## Definition

Reusable logical interaction boundary between a supported BMID-family device and a compatible industrial battery charger for battery identity, condition, temperature, or other supported charging-related information.

## Notes

- This Port does not prescribe wired, Bluetooth, CAN, or another implementation.
- Charger power-control authority remains on the charger side unless a specific requirement states otherwise.
- Variant-specific protocols and physical connectors must be modeled separately when authoritative evidence exists.
