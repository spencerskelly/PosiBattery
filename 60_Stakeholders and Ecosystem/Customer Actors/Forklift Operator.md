# Forklift Operator

## Definition

A person who drives a forklift, pallet truck or similar warehouse truck in a distribution, manufacturing or cold-storage setting.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: source-stated.** No operator-side source (survey, interview, training material) is in the vault yet.
- **Role in the customer organization:** User of most truck accessories; also the person warned, restricted or assisted by the safety and assist functions.
- **Relation to the product (analyst classification, hypothesis):** user.
- **Needs this role takes part in:** see the Use Case notes that list this Actor as a participant (query `participants` in [[README_Customer Needs|Customer Needs]]); the product to need route is in [[Product to Customer Need Map]].
- **Evidence:**

Source: Crown RR/RD Series page and Godrej dealer page (T1/T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.crown.com/en-au/forklifts/reach-trucks/rr-rd-rider-deep-reach-truck.html>
