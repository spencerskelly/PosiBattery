# OSHA STD 1-11.4 - Charging-Only Areas Are Judged Under (g)(1) and (g)(8) to (g)(12)

## Definition

Per OSHA's 1978 enforcement instruction STD 1-11.4, an area where truck batteries are only charged (no maintenance, batteries not removed from trucks, no electrolyte handling) is not held to 1910.178(g)(2) but must meet (g)(1) and (g)(8) to (g)(12).

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It is an enforcement reading, 48 years old; whether it is still applied as written is unknown (C319).
- Lithium is not addressed; the "charging-only" test is plausibly easier to meet with lithium (no electrolyte handling), but no source says OSHA applies it that way.
- Second-hand reading; see [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]].
