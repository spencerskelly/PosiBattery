# Crown RC 5700 Series

## Definition

Crown three-wheel stand-up electric counterbalance forklift, 1.5 to 1.8 t, V-Force lithium-ion ready and InfoLink ready.

## Notes

- Crown's RC 5700 specification sheet lists models RC 5715, 5725, 5735 (1.5 t) and 5745 (1.8 t), 500 mm load centre, truck weight less battery 2,690 to 2,930 kg, and marks them V-Force lithium-ion ready and InfoLink ready. Source: Crown RC 5700 specification sheet (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.crown.com/content/dam/crown/pdfs/en-uk/specs/forklift-rc5700-spec-GB.pdf>
- The standard and optional equipment list includes cornering speed control that slows travel, travel speed reduction when forks are above free lift, ramp hold and ramp speed control, a forward tilt interlock, controlled tilt speeds, e-GEN variable regenerative motor braking with closed-loop traction control that holds the truck static on a grade and automatic electric parking brakes, three performance modes (P1, P2, P3), a battery discharge indicator with lift interrupt and re-key, hour meter, event code display and Access 1 2 3 diagnostics on the Crown display, pin code access capability, audible travel alarms, visual warning devices (floor spot lights, floor line lights, flashing lights), an entry bar with sensors that slows travel and sounds an alarm, and optional freezer and corrosion conditioning. Source: Crown RC 5700 specification sheet (in repo) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.crown.com/content/dam/crown/pdfs/en-uk/specs/forklift-rc5700-spec-GB.pdf>
