# Mitsubishi Logisnext Americas

## Definition

North, Central and South American arm of Mitsubishi Logisnext (Houston, Texas): maker or provider of Mitsubishi, Cat, UniCarriers and Rocla products and distributor of Jungheinrich, with an energy solutions business and the Promatch parts program.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Truck OEM, Brand Owner, Dealer or Distributor.
- Logisnext states it is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Mitsubishi Logisnext Co., headquartered in Houston, Texas, with a portfolio spanning five brands: Mitsubishi forklift trucks, Cat lift trucks, Rocla AGV Solutions, UniCarriers Forklifts and Jungheinrich warehouse and automation products. Source: Logisnext Americas release (2025-05-28) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisnextamericas.com/en/logisnext/news/mla-enersys-expand-power-solutions-for-material-handling-operations>
- It calls itself the exclusive manufacturer and provider of UniCarriers Forklifts across North, Central and South America; UniCarriers press releases carry a Marengo, Illinois dateline. Source: Logisnext Americas UniCarriers releases (2023) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisnextamericas.com/en/unicarriers/news/mla-launches-new-lithium-ion-battery-solutions>
- Its 'who we are' page uses the older name Mitsubishi Caterpillar Forklift America Inc. (Logisnext) and lists Mitsubishi, Cat and Jungheinrich as the three brands in Americas, 'combining the expertise of Caterpillar Inc., Mitsubishi Nichiyu Forklift Co., Ltd. and Jungheinrich AG'; GlobalData lists Mitsubishi Caterpillar Forklift America, Inc. and UniCarriers Americas Corporation as group subsidiaries (C91). Source: Logisnext Americas who-we-are page and GlobalData (T1/T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://logisnextamericas.com/en/logisnext/who-we-are>
- Logisnext Energy Solutions sells lithium-ion and lead-acid batteries, chargers and integrated lithium-ion forklifts; the Promatch parts program carries EnerSys and HAWKER batteries, chargers and monitors from 2025. Source: Logisnext energy solutions page and EnerSys partnership release (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisnextamericas.com/es-co/logisnext/solutions/energy-solutions>
- The EnerSys partnership covers EnerSys NexSys TPPL, NexSys+, Express, IMPAQ and Wi-iQ and HAWKER products (see [[EnerSys]]); Triathlon USA supplies the UniCarriers lithium-ion battery and charger; PowerFleet's telematics was resold from 2021 ([[Powerfleet Forklift Gateway]]); Lift Link is its own telematics from 2023 ([[Logisnext Lift Link]]); Phantom Auto teleoperation software was announced for UniCarriers and Mitsubishi electric trucks (name only here). Source: Logisnext Americas releases and Modern Materials Handling (T1/T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisnextamericas.com/en/logisnext/news/mla-enersys-expand-power-solutions-for-material-handling-operations>
