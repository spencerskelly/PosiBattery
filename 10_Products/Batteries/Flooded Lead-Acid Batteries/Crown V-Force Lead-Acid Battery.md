# Crown V-Force Lead-Acid Battery

## Definition

Crown V-Force flat plate and tubular lead-acid batteries introduced in 2020.

## Notes

- Crown added V-Force flat plate and tubular lead-acid batteries in October 2020, the tubular with a flexible bolt-on inter-cell connector. Source: Batteries International (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.batteriesinternational.com/?p=15261>
