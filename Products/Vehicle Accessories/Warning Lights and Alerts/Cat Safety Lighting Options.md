# Cat Safety Lighting Options

## Definition

Cat lighting options: road lights, amber strobes, red or blue warning spots and red lines that mark exclusion boundaries around the truck.

## Notes

- Cat's blog lists road lights, amber strobes, red or blue warning spots and red lines to highlight exclusion boundaries around the truck among options on the EP40-55N.
