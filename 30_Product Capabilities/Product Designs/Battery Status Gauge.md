# Battery Status Gauge

## Definition

Local gauge that presents battery status directly to a user without requiring a separate vehicle display or remote application.

## Notes

- [[Access Control Group CellVue]] is a verified example identified as a real-time battery gauge.
- [[Inventus Smart Battery Monitor SBM-01]] is a verified 52 mm panel monitor that receives battery-system data over CAN and displays SOC, SOH, runtime, voltage and faults.
- The retrieved source does not identify LCD, LED bar, analog pointer, mounting location, resolution, or electrical interface, so this Design is intentionally technology-neutral.
- [[Panel-Mount Gauge Form Factor]] remains a separate mounting/form-factor Design and is not applied to CellVue without evidence.
