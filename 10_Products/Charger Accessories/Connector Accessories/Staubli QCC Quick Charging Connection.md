# Staubli QCC Quick Charging Connection

## Definition

Staubli QCC: self-aligning quick charging connection for AGVs with over 100,000 mating cycles and data signals.

## Notes

- No current or voltage stated in the item; contacts stay covered until fully mated. Source: <https://electronicspecifier.com/cables-and-connecting/quick-charging-connector-system-for-autonomous-agvs> (T2).

- **Update 2026-10-14 (product page):** up to 1,500 V DC, 1,440 A peak and 670 A continuous, 2 power contacts + PE + 5-6 signal contacts, IP55, SAE J3105/3 (pin-and-socket, defined for transit buses at 250-1000 V). The connector rating far exceeds 80/96 V truck batteries, but no forklift use is stated; applications named are AGVs, buses, trucks, mobile machines, boats. Sources: <https://staubli.com/ch/en/electrical-connectors/products/multi-pole-connectors/qcc.html> (T1); <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAE_J3105> (T3).
