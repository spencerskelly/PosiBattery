# Doosan Bobcat 7-Series Plus Electric Forklifts

## Definition

Doosan Industrial Vehicle (Bobcat) 80 V 7-Series Plus electric forklifts with lead-acid or lithium-ion batteries and built-in safety systems.

## Notes

- The catalog entry says the 7-Series Plus (80 V) comes with lead-acid (quick side battery change) or lithium-ion batteries (fast charging) and standard Operator Sensing System, Hill Start Assist, Electric Parking Brake, Electric Foot Brake and mast sway control that cuts speed and stabilizes the mast at height; the 3-wheel version has an electro-magnetic automatic parking brake.
