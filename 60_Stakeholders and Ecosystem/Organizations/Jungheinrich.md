# Jungheinrich

## Definition

Truck maker that describes its lithium-ion battery, charging device and vehicle as developed together, with an integrated battery management system.

## Notes

- **Role (working label, not a governed property):** truck OEM and battery brand owner.
- A trade report says the Jungheinrich lithium-ion battery, charging device and vehicle were developed together and the battery is permanently monitored by an integrated battery management system, with 360 Ah cells reaching 50 percent in 40 minutes. Source: Warehouse News (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://warehousenews.co.uk/?p=45557>
- A Jungheinrich dealer shop lists an electrolyte level sensor with cable fixture and remote indication as a battery accessory for all battery types. Source: Jungheinrich shop (Slovenia) (T3), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.jungheinrich-shop.si/en/spare-parts-and-accessories/battery-accessories/electrolyte-level-sensor--51233095>
- **Unknown, do not infer:** who makes the cells or lead-acid batteries. Flux Power lists Jungheinrich among OEM relationships (self-reported, nature unstated).
- **Earlier offered-with list (round 5; superseded by the product notes linked from this note and by [[Offerings by Organization]]; kept as written for items not yet modeled):**
  - Trucks: the vehicle developed together with its battery and charger <https://warehousenews.co.uk/?p=45557>
  - Charger: charging device developed with the lithium-ion battery <https://warehousenews.co.uk/?p=45557>
  - Battery management: integrated BMS <https://warehousenews.co.uk/?p=45557>
  - Accessory: electrolyte level sensor <https://www.jungheinrich-shop.si/en/spare-parts-and-accessories/battery-accessories/electrolyte-level-sensor--51233095>
- **Forklift models (round 13):** see [[Forklift Offerings Matrix]] and the model notes linked by `makes` on this note.
