# Power Designers

## Definition

Maker of REVOLUTION modular chargers and the PowerTrac battery monitor family; appears as Power Designers Sibex and Power Designers USA LLC. Part of the [[Ampure]] group since its 2025 acquisition; analyzed in this vault as internal industrial portfolio, not as a PosiCharge competitor.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Charger Maker, Monitor Maker.
- Power Designers Sibex offers modular conventional, opportunity and fast chargers and battery monitoring, per its industry-association profile. Source: MHI member profile (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/members/17127>
- The vendor lists the REVOLUTION X series and the PowerTrac Monitor, PowerTrac 3, PowerTrac SP+ and PowerTrac DT products on one product menu. Source: Power Designers Sibex site (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.powerdesignerssibex.com/powertrac-sp/>
- POWER TRAC is a trademark of Power Designers, LLC, and a 2014 data sheet names Power Designers USA LLC in Madison, Wisconsin, while current pages name Power Designers Sibex in Crystal River, Florida. Source: Trademark record and PowerTrac SP+ data sheet (registry/T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://trademarks.justia.com/owners/power-designers-llc-1054278>
- **Ampure group (2025):** Ampure Charging Systems acquired Power Designers Sibex, a maker of industrial charging products and electronics manufacturer in Crystal River, Florida, citing added power configurations, ISO-certified manufacturing and final assembly, OEM relationships, and predictive-analytics and energy-demand-management software. Source: Ampure press release (T1), 2025-09-02. <https://www.ampure.com/press-releases/transom-capital-backed-ampure-acquires-power-designers-sibex>
- **Analysis treatment:** internal portfolio. Overlap with PosiCharge chargers, monitors and software is recorded in [[Ampure Industrial Portfolio Overlap and Synergy Map]], not in competitor cohorts. See [[Ampure Group Portfolio Context]].
- **Shared teams:** some teams serve both Power Designers Sibex and PosiCharge (vault owner statement, 2026-10-03).
- **Name caution (C32):** three corporate names appear (Power Designers LLC, Power Designers USA LLC, Power Designers Sibex). Their legal relationship is not established here. The 2025 acquisition names 'Power Designers Sibex' only; how the other two names relate is still open.
