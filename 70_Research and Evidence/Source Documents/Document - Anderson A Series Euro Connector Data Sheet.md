# Document - Anderson A Series Euro Connector Data Sheet

## Definition

Anderson A Series (A80, A160, A320) battery connector data sheet hosted by Farnell.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- A80/A160/A320; UL and CSA, 600 V AC/DC, dielectric 2200 V AC; A320 about 450 A (UL) on 120 mm2; -25 to 75 C; IP23; UL94 HB. Keys grey wet, green dry, yellow universal (male housing, vehicle side only), coded for 24, 36, 48, 72, 80, 96 V DC. Power contacts A80 10-35 mm2, A160 16-50 mm2, A320 50-120 mm2. Aux A80 1.5/2.5 mm2 upper and lower, A160 4 mm2 upper and 6 mm2 lower, A320 1.5/2.5 mm2; configuration codes A to F combine lower, upper or both aux sets. Mating force 68/170/71 N; power contact resistance 65/55/30 uOhm. Options: male (code 4) or female (code 5), key colour, low or high profile handle, kit or bulk packing; accessories latch plates, manual releases, battery brackets; crimp tooling per contact.
