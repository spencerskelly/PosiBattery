# Metric - Certifications and Standards

## Definition

Certifications and Standards: shared metric used for monitors, chargers, batterys.

## Notes

- **Code and class:** MM14, CM14, BM10 (monitor, charger, battery metrics merged by the note re-use rule). Unit or format: list.
- **Definition by class:**
  - monitor (MM14): Certifications and Standards: Safety, EMC and truck standards the device claims.
  - charger (CM14): Certifications: UL, cUL, CEC and similar marks.
  - battery (BM10): Certifications: UL 2580, UL 2271, UL Type EE and similar.
- **Comparability rules by class:**
  - monitor (MM14): List the exact standard identifiers; 'compliant' without a number is weak.
  - charger (CM14): California Energy Commission compliance is regional.
  - battery (BM10): UL 2271 and UL 2580 are different standards.
