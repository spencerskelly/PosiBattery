# Inductive Wireless Charging

## Definition

Charging by magnetic coupling between a ground or wall pad and a receiver on the vehicle or battery, with no contacts and no person plugging in.

## Notes

- Examples: [[EnerSys NexSys AIR Wireless Charger]], [[Wiferion etaLINK 3000 Wireless Charging System]], [[Wiferion etaTRAY Battery Tray]] (receiver built into a retrofit battery tray). The function [[Charge Battery Wirelessly]] already exists; this is the design that realizes it.
