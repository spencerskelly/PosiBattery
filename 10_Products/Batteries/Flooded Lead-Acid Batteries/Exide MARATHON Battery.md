# Exide MARATHON Battery

## Definition

Exide (GNB) flooded traction line, available with forced electrolyte circulation and extended watering intervals.

## Notes

- Exide's overview says MARATHON batteries can be fitted with AIR agitation, a forced electrolyte circulation system, an electrolyte level sensor and an automatic watering system are available, and MARATHON Excell reaches 16 weeks between watering with GNB chargers. Source: GNB motive power overview (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.exidegroup.com/eu/sites/default/files/2021-08/GNB_MP_Overview_EN_web.pdf>
- A catalog excerpt says watering intervals can extend to 40 and up to 200 working days depending on battery type with GNB charging. Source: Exide MARATHON catalog (DirectIndustry) (T3), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://pdf.directindustry.com/pdf/exide-technolgies/marathon/12301-575795.html>
