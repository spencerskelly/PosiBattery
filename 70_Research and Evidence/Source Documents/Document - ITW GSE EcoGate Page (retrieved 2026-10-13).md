## Notes

**Content recorded:**
- EcoGate is 'a design approach where GSE units work together as a highly efficient system' and 'not a single product'. No specifications.
- Power Share uses surplus grid power and distributes it to eGPUs or other eGSE during and between turnarounds. No specifications.
- Intelligent Power Management (IPM) is hosted in the 3500 PCA, allocates power to other units dynamically without exceeding available capacity; the 2400 GPU always takes priority.
- DC Charger: 60 kW per outlet, one or two outlets, CCS2 connector; can be connected to an ITW GSE 2400 GPU, which controls how much power the charger has. Voltage and current not stated.
- Claims 'no major airport infrastructure upgrade required' (unsubstantiated).
