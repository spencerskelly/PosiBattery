# Market History and Outlook Register

## Definition

Twenty-plus years of industrial truck volumes (global and North American), supplier revenue, battery-maker sales and battery cost, with every published forecast found and the published forecasts checked against what happened. Built 2026-10-18 for [[Market Size and Volume Register]] and [[Public Company Financials Register]].

## Notes

**Scope and honesty about the horizon.** History is well covered from 2002 (WITS) and, for supplier revenue, from 2009. Published forecasts reach about 2030 to 2035 (Interact Analysis to 2034, vendor reports to 2032 to 2035, BloombergNEF to 2030). **No source found looks to 2046.** The last table in section 8 extends the 2025 volume by stated growth rates for 2030 to 2046; it is arithmetic on assumptions, not a forecast, and should not be cited as one.

**Units.** WITS counts trucks, not dollars. "Orders" are used for the long series because WITS published orders for every year from 2002; shipments differ (see section 2). North America below means the WITS North America region; it is not the same as the Industrial Truck Association (ITA) series (section 3). All sources were read through a summarizing fetch tool.

### 3. North America and the United States

- WITS North America orders in section 1 run from 147,322 (2002) to a 2007 peak of 191,384 after 2006's 212,953, a 2009 low of 98,338, and 466,328 in 2022.
- The ITA reports a different, smaller figure: 347,677 units in 2021 and 344,330 retail orders in 2022 for classes 1 to 5 ([[Document - ITA Powered Industrial Trucks, a Robust Market (2023-03-08)|ita]]), against WITS North America orders of 458,082 and 466,328. The two series differ by about 24 to 25% in those years (derived); ITA's own note says its numbers are not comparable year to year because of member fluctuation ([[Document - ITA Market Intelligence page|itm]]). Both are kept; neither is preferred.
- Pre-2002 North America and Europe series (ITA, JIVA, FEM) are in [[Document - Hyster-Yale Exhibit 99 Lift Truck Market Data (2015)|hy15]]: North America 83,718 (1972), 105,508 (1990), 205,033 (2000), 145,967 (2001). Reporting basis varies.
- Electric share of North American orders: 72% (2021), 65% (2022) and 71% (2024), ITA via [[Document - ITA Powered Industrial Trucks, a Robust Market (2023-03-08)|ita]] and [[Document - Flux Power Form 10-K for Fiscal 2026 (2026-09)|fx10]].
- **Not found: a US-only series.** The ITA shipment tables are PDFs the fetch tool is blocked from reading. US demand has to be inferred from North America or Americas until those are read.

### 8. Extension to 2046 (arithmetic, not a forecast)

World orders from the 2025 WITS figure of 2,379,566, grown at fixed yearly rates. The three rates are a low case, the midpoint of Interact Analysis's 2023 shipment growth range, and the 2002 to 2023 order CAGR in section 1.

| Case | Rate | 2030 | 2035 | 2040 | 2046 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 2.0% | 2,627,233 | 2,900,678 | 3,202,583 | 3,606,628 |
| Interact Analysis shipment growth, midpoint of 4 to 5% | 4.5% | 2,965,372 | 3,695,393 | 4,605,132 | 5,997,080 |
| Long-run history, 2002 to 2023 order CAGR | 6.4% | 3,244,934 | 4,425,008 | 6,034,234 | 8,755,318 |

These ignore cycles. The history in section 1 includes drawdowns of 38 to 43% (2008 to 2009) and 11 to 16% over 2022 to 2023 (derived), and a Chinese-led surge in 2021 that has not repeated. Electric share would also have to be assumed (79% in 2025; Interact has lithium above 70% of electric sales only by 2034).
