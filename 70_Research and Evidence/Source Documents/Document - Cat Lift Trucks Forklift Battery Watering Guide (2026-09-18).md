# Document - Cat Lift Trucks Forklift Battery Watering Guide (2026-09-18)

## Definition

Truck-maker guide on when and how to water a flooded forklift battery and what over- and under-watering cause.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Water after charging; before charging only check that plates are covered and add enough distilled water to cover them if not (C340).
- Top to the battery maker's recommended level after charge; no measured level, no interval given.
- Water quality: distilled or deionized, pH 5 to 7; never untreated tap water (minerals such as calcium and chlorine named as causes of sulfation and corrosion).
- Overwatering: overflow, acid loss, corrosion, safety hazard, shorter life. Underwatering: exposed plates, overheating, lower capacity, sulfation, shorter life. No quantified effects.
- Logisnext Americas sells trucks, not the battery; the guide is a truck-maker page.
