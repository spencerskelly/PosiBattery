# Document - Deka MaxPowr Installation and Operation Manual (0608)

## Definition

Deka MaxPowr operating and maintenance instructions (0608).

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- Full-charge specific gravity 1.320 to 1.330 corrected to 77 F; pre-shift reading 1.310 to 1.330; rated capacity delivered to 1.155 to 1.175, below 1.155 can shorten life.
- Equalize before service and every one to four weeks; charge at end of shift; no more than two successive shifts before recharge; P60 5-plate cell 120 Ah at 6 h, finish rate 5 A.
- Opportunity charging, cooling and warranty records not addressed; points to the Deka Industrial Battery Service Manual (0656). Insulated spreader bar required for lifting.
