# Oshkosh AeroTech Aircraft Proximity Detection

## Definition

Optional Oshkosh AeroTech sensors on cargo loaders that help coordinate the aircraft, loader and personnel.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Optional Oshkosh AeroTech sensor system on cargo loaders that helps protect the aircraft, loader and personnel.

**Marketed features:**
- Optional APD sensors protect aircraft and loader
- Offered on Commander 30i-e and Ranger 15e electric loaders
- Component modules are detailed in the APD radar, bumper, wheel, cowling, wing/fairing and handrail notes
