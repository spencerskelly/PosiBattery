# Exide TENSOR xGEL Battery

## Definition

Exide gel traction battery that needs no watering.

## Notes

- Exide says Tensor Gel cells minimize heat evolution, are no-fill and no-spill with no watering, and suit replacement of flooded batteries in forklifts and order pickers. Source: Exide Tensor xGEL brochure (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://exidegroup.com/en/document/tensor-xgel-brochure>
