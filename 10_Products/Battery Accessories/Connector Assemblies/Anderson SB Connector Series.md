# Anderson SB Connector Series

## Definition

Anderson SB series power connectors (SB 50, 175, 350 and larger) used on forklift and pallet jack batteries, rated 600 V.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Anderson Power Products' genderless two-pole SB power connectors, the standard battery connector on forklifts and other motive-power equipment.

**Marketed features:**
- Genderless: mates when one connector is flipped 180 degrees
- Sizes SB 50 to SB 350, 50 to 500 A
- UL rated to 600 V
- Color-coded, mechanically keyed housings prevent cross-mating of different voltages
- Stainless steel springs and silver-plated contacts for very low resistance
- SB350: chemical-resistant PBT/PC option down to -40 C; same housings for wire and busbar contacts
