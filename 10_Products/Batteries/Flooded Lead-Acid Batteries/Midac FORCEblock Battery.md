# Midac FORCEblock Battery

## Definition

Midac 6 V, 8 V and 12 V monobloc batteries, 16 to 340 Ah, flooded or VRLA, welded internally.

## Notes

- On-board or off-board HF switching chargers. Source: <https://midacbatteries.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/1_CAT-INTEGRATED-SOLUTIONS_MOTIVE_EN_IT_DE_FR-01_2022-5011820-0967_21.pdf>. Filed as flooded provisionally because the catalogue lists VLA or VRLA (IB-190).
