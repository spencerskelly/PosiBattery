# Stretch Truck Run Time per Charge

## Definition

Customer need: Stretch Truck Run Time per Charge. The problem behind it: Less energy per move means fewer charge breaks and lower energy cost.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: need hypothesis.** The statements below are what makers and dealers say their products do for customers. No customer, user or buyer source in the vault confirms that customers hold this need or rank it. Per the ruleset, source research becomes a validated need only after customer-side evidence.
- **Problem solved (analyst wording):** Less energy per move means fewer charge breaks and lower energy cost.
- **Who has the problem:** [[Fleet Operations Manager]].
- **Operating segments (analyst crosswalk to [[PosiCharge Market Segments and Jobs-to-Be-Done]], hypothesis):** Material-handling fleets, Distributed and opportunity charging.
- **Realized by (specific functions; products link to these):** [[Recover Energy by Regeneration]], [[Deliver Constant Power Through Shift]].
- **Products reaching this need through those functions:** 6. Largest families: Forklifts/Class I Electric Rider Trucks (3), Fuel Cell Power Units/Hydrogen Fuel Cell Units (2), Forklifts/Class II Electric Narrow Aisle Trucks (1). Per-product list in [[Product to Customer Need Map]].
- **Vendor-stated evidence (by product):**
  - [[Raymond 7000 Series Reach-Fork Trucks]]: Raymond's catalog says the Model 7500 returns up to 10 percent of every amp to the battery with regenerative lowering technology and uses up to 21 percent less energy than the leading competition (vendor claims). Source: Raymond 7000 Series catalog (DirectIndustry) (T1 (catalog)), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://pdf.directindustry.com/pdf/raymond/7000-series-reach-fork-truck-universal-stance/14119-514315.html>
- **Gaps:** no customer-side source; each function in the list is realized by only the products that state it, so the product count is a lower bound; no Requirement is linked (the vault leaves requirements as an intentional gap).
