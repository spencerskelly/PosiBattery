# BMID - Provide Battery Identity to Compatible Charger

## Definition

The PosiCharge BMID family shall provide battery identity information to a compatible charging system when operating in a supported BMID charging configuration.

## Notes

- The charger retains charge-control authority.
- The identity payload and communication method may vary by BMID generation.
- This requirement does not prescribe the physical or link-layer interface.
