# CFC 309 - Battery Charging Is Not Allowed in Areas Open to the Public

## Definition

Under section 309.3 of the 2025 California Fire Code, battery charging for powered industrial trucks is not allowed in areas open to the public.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It constrains where a charger may be placed (for example a retail floor or a customer-accessible dock).
- Second-hand reading; see [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]]. Do not confuse with section 322 (powered micromobility devices), which the same site hosts and which is a different subject (C322).
