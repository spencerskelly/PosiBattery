# Wireless Communication Circuit

## Definition

Reusable radio communication electronics between a controller and an external charger, gateway, mobile device, or network.

## Notes

- BLE, LoRa, Wi-Fi, and custom RF are alternative/specialized solutions. A product link should be made only when evidence or a clearly documented engineering assumption supports the selected radio.
