# Document Battery Care for Warranty Compliance

## Definition

Customer need: Document Battery Care for Warranty Compliance. The problem behind it: Battery warranties depend on showing that charging, watering and equalization followed the maker's recommended practice.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: need hypothesis.** The statements below are what makers and dealers say their products do for customers. No customer, user or buyer source in the vault confirms that customers hold this need or rank it. Per the ruleset, source research becomes a validated need only after customer-side evidence.
- **Problem solved (analyst wording):** Battery warranties depend on showing that charging, watering and equalization followed the maker's recommended practice.
- **Who has the problem:** [[Fleet Operations Manager]], [[Dealer Service Technician]].
- **Operating segments (analyst crosswalk to [[PosiCharge Market Segments and Jobs-to-Be-Done]], hypothesis):** Material-handling fleets, Battery-room and centralized charging.
- **Realized by (specific functions; products link to these):** [[Log Battery Events and Usage]], [[Upload Battery Data to Cloud Portal]], [[Track Equalization]].
- **Products reaching this need:** 35 by function, design or option (owner decision, round 40: design and option routes count as reached); 32 of those perform a realizing function. Largest families by function: Battery Accessories/Monitoring Devices (22), Battery Accessories/Identification and Charge Interface Devices (5), Fleet Software and Platforms/Battery and Charger Management (3), Chargers/Industrial Modular Chargers (1), Battery Accessories/Telematics and Connectivity Devices (1). The metric route (hypothesis) adds 0 more. Per-product routes in [[Product to Customer Need Map]].
- **Vendor-stated evidence (by product):**
  - [[Raymond iBattery]]: Raymond's 2010 launch release says the iBattery module reports charge and discharge cycles, high and low temperatures and low water levels, helps prevent over-discharge through voltage and state-of-charge detection, can detect battery weight against truck specifications, and gives reports on charging intervals, temperature, watering and equalization for warranty compliance; DC Velocity says the module rests on the battery itself. Source: Raymond press release (2010) and DC Velocity (T1/T2 (dated)), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://raymondcorp.com/news/2010/ibattery-launch>
  - [[PosiCharge PosiLink]]: The PosiLink two-page sheet (Downloads/Posilink-2-Pager.pdf) calls it a fleet intelligence and battery management platform: plan and design with site data (including E-Meter and IC-Meter data), real-time alerts, lifecycle insights, usage tracked against recommended practices for warranty compliance, exceptions-based reporting, role-specific dashboards and asset drilldowns; energy management (facility power limits, time-of-use and scheduled charging) and integrated simulation tools are marked coming soon. Source: PosiLink sheet (read round 20) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://posicharge.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Posilink-2-Pager.pdf>
- **Gaps:** no customer-side source; each function in the list is realized by only the products that state it, so the product count is a lower bound; no Requirement is linked (the vault leaves requirements as an intentional gap).
