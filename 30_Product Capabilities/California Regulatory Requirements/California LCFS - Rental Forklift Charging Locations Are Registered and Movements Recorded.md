# California LCFS - Rental Forklift Charging Locations Are Registered and Movements Recorded

## Definition

Where a rental forklift fleet is used at several locations, each location shall be registered as a fuel supply equipment site under its first fuel reporting entity or designee, a new location shall be registered before volumes are reported for credit, and records shall show where forklifts were located and charging during the quarter.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** Records may include rental agreements, meter records if the serial number is recorded during charging, or geocoordinate data if available ([[Document - CARB LCFS Guidance 25-01 Forklift Fueling Reporting (2025-10)]], rental fleets section).
- Verifiers may ask for location records for a charging event.
- Product use (analyst reasoning): charging records that carry the charger or meter serial number help a rental fleet show which site the energy went to; no source says rental customers lack this today.
