# Document - Xpansiv British Columbia LCFS Market Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

A credit-market operator's summary of British Columbia's LCFS, including the 2024 expansion to heavy electric forklifts.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the North American programs survey. Market-information page from a credit-market operator; no date except a 2026 copyright line.

- **Forklifts:** electricity became an eligible fuel in 2021; in 2024 eligibility widened to electric equipment "such as heavy forklifts", and the eligible-asset list names heavy forklifts above 6,800 kg lifting capacity.
- **Credit generator:** under the 2024 amendments the entity named on the electric utility bill for the fuel supply equipment is the credit generator.
- **Program facts stated:** administered by the Ministry of Energy and Climate Solutions; annual compliance period; credits do not expire; book-and-claim renewable energy certificates accepted; verification listed as "None"; BC-LCFS is independent of the federal CFR and assets may take part in both.
- **Not stated:** credit price levels or trends, per-kWh or per-forklift values, and the legal basis. Some compliance wording is generic and inconsistent with a British Columbia page, so verify against the Ministry or portal.
- **Interest:** the host operates credit markets.
