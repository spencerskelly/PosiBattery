# Standalone Battery Management System

## Definition

BMS sold as a separate unit that a pack builder or vehicle maker fits to cells they assemble, instead of one built into a maker's pack.

## Notes

- Design characteristic found in products, not a decision by us. Links to products are made only where a source states it.
- Contrast with [[Integrated Battery Management System]].
