# Document - IRS Instructions for Form 8911 Alternative Fuel Vehicle Refueling Property Credit (2025-12)

## Definition

IRS instructions stating that the 30C refueling property credit ends for property placed in service after 2026-06-30.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- **End date:** the credit cannot be claimed for refueling property placed in service after 2026-06-30 (P.L. 119-21 moved the date up from 2032-12-31). As of 2026-10-17 it is unavailable for new installs.
- **Amount:** business property 6 percent of cost, or 30 percent if prevailing wage and apprenticeship rules are met; limit $100,000 per single item; cost reduced first by any section 179 deduction.
- **Location rule:** property placed in service after 2022 must be in an eligible census tract.
- **Forklifts:** the page does not mention forklifts or non-road equipment; whether forklift chargers ever qualified was not shown (IB-270).
