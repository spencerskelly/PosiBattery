# Conductive Auto-Contact Charging

## Definition

Charging through metal contacts that touch when the vehicle drives onto or against a station (vehicle-side collector and stationary base, or bumper or floor contacts), with no person plugging in.

## Notes

- Design characteristic found in product descriptions, not a decision by us. Examples: [[Vahle Charging Contacts]], [[Conductix-Wampfler Enduro+ Charging Contacts]]. Published standard ratings are limited to 60 V DC (custom higher at Vahle); see [[Charger-Vehicle Interface Register]].
