# Cat Electric Lift Trucks (North America Series List)

## Definition

Cat electric lift truck series sold in North America by Mitsubishi Logisnext Americas, 48 V and 80 V, cushion and pneumatic, 1,500 to 12,000 lb.

## Notes

- One family note listing series (capacity, max height): 2ET2500-2ET4000 (3-wheel, 2,500-4,000 lb, 256 in); 3EPC5000-3EP7000 (80 V, 5,000-6,600 lb, 275 in); EC15N-EC18LN (3,000-4,000 lb, 217 in); EC20Q-EC40Q (4,000-8,000 lb, 312 in); EC23N-EC30LN (4,500-6,500 lb, 221 in); ECS14N-ECS18N stand-up (3,000-4,000 lb, 276 in); EP15CB-EP35CB (1,500-7,000 lb); EP16PNT-EP20PNT (3-wheel, 3,000-4,000 lb, 216 in); EP25AN-EP35AN (5,000-7,000 lb); EP40N-EP55NH (9,000-12,000 lb); EPC3000-EP4000 (48 V, 3,000-4,000 lb, 255 in). 80 V on mid-size and large pneumatic, 48 V on EPC3000-EP4000 and a 3-wheel model.
