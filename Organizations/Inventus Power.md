# Inventus Power

## Definition

Maker of the S/M-48V60-TRX lithium batteries and the SBM-01 smart battery monitor.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Battery Maker, Monitor Maker.
- Inventus Power's SBM-01 data sheet lists a monitor kit for its S/M-48V60-TRX battery models with a 2 m shielded cable to the pack. Source: Inventus SBM-01 data sheet (08/2023) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://inventuspower.com/wp-content/uploads/TDS_Smart-Battery-Monitor_2023-Aug_V1.pdf>
