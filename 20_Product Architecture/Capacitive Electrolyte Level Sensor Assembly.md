# Capacitive Electrolyte Level Sensor Assembly

## Definition

Electrolyte-level sensor assembly that detects the presence or absence of electrolyte using a capacitive probe.

## Notes

- Flow-Rite states that the Eagle Eye sensor range uses patented capacitive sensing so no sensing current flows through the probe into the cell.
- The Elite IV implementation integrates its sensor with the watering-valve arrangement; the Essential IV uses an alloy probe that requires no trimming.
- The exact capacitance-measurement electronics are not published in the available product material.
