# Fleet Charging and Energy Management Software

## Definition

Software that schedules charging, shares limited site power among chargers or manages charger fleets, mostly for electric vehicles and robots.

## Notes

- Category note for navigation, added 2026-10-14. Members are adjacent-market software: the pages read show the vendors' own markets (robots, EV depots, warehouses), not forklift battery chargers.
