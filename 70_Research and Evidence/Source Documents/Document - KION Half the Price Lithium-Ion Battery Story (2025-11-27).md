# Document - KION Half the Price Lithium-Ion Battery Story (2025-11-27)

## Definition

KION story on a new in-house lithium-ion battery for Linde and STILL trucks, with modules made in China and final assembly in Hamburg, and a goal of halving cost.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Cost claims: goal of "reducing costs by half"; "up to 65 percent cheaper than previous models"; "several thousand euros" advantage per forklift for a medium battery (no per-kWh price, no baseline).
- Sizes: 48 V and 90 V (small and medium), a larger variant due in 2026; warranty claim of 2,000 charge cycles or five years; 28 vehicles field-tested.
- Made "from our own production": modules at the Jingjiang plant in China, final assembly, electronics and quality testing in Hamburg.
- Chargers: no model or specification; mentions faster charging and possible opportunity charging.
- Relevance: an OEM pushing its own battery price down is the pressure the third-party battery and charger makers would feel; the article itself does not discuss competitors.
