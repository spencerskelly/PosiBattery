# Document - Green Cubes SAFEFlex PLUS Datasheet (2026.03.18)

## Definition

Green Cubes SAFEFlex PLUS datasheet dated 2026.03.18, covering the 24, 36, 48 and 80 V classes.

## Notes

**How it was read.** Through a fetch tool that returns a model-written summary, not the raw text, on 2026-10-13. The tool itself said the table layout was garbled and that some column assignments were inferred, so per-class values below are unverified until the raw file is read (IB-168).

- Same class table as [[Document - Green Cubes Lithium SAFEFlex PLUS Product Page (retrieved 2026-10-13)]]: 25.6, 38.4, 51.2 and 83.2 V nominal; 420 to 630, 525 to 945, 525 to 840 and 315 to 735 Ah; 11 to 16, 20 to 36, 26 to 43 and 26 to 61 kWh.
- Peak discharge by class as read: 600, 1,200, 1,200 and 800 A. The summary assigned the 300 A (315 Ah) and 320 A (420 Ah and up) continuous values, and the 240 A and 320 A charge values, to the 36 V class by inference, which conflicts with the capacity ranges (315 Ah is only in the 80 V range) (C216).
- Same chemistry (LiFePO4), 4,000 cycles, temperature, IP 2X, BMS, communications (CAN bus, USB, Wi-Fi; separate CAN for truck and charger) and certifications (UL 2580:2020 MH66684, ISO 13849, UN 38.3 Rev 7, Type EE) as the product page.
- Not stated: dimensions, weight, warranty.
