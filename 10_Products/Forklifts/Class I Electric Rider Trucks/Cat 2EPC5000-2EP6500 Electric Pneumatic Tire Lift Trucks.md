# Cat 2EPC5000-2EP6500 Electric Pneumatic Tire Lift Trucks

## Definition

Cat electric pneumatic tire lift trucks with operator presence detection, travel speed reduction and a lithium-ion option.

## Notes

- A Cat brochure (in a trade-show directory) lists an operator presence detection system, travel speed reduction, an electric heater, a raised operator cab, a fully floating operator cabin and a lithium-ion battery package for the 2EPC5000-2EP6500 series. Source: Cat brochure (trade-show directory copy) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://expoproduction.thelogisticsworld.com/wp-content/themes/theme-summitexpo/directorio/assets/fichas/159.pdf>

  - [[Limit Truck Speed Automatically]] (V): <https://expoproduction.thelogisticsworld.com/wp-content/themes/theme-summitexpo/directorio/assets/fichas/159.pdf>

- **Update 2026-10-14:** a 2012 item calls them 80 V AC trucks with sealed wet-disc brakes and up to two shifts per charge; the current Americas series list shows 3EPC5000-3EP7000 at 80 V and 5,000 to 6,600 lb, so the successor naming is not stated (C243).
