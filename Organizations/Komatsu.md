# Komatsu

## Definition

Japanese maker with forklifts, thirteenth in the 2024 ranking.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Truck OEM.
- The ranking lists Komatsu thirteenth with about $605M in lift truck revenue. Source: Modern Materials Handling via Logistics Management (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/top_20_lift_truck_suppliers_2024>
- **Not researched:** models, battery and charger options.
- Komatsu's Australian site lists the FB series of battery-electric forklifts (2,500 or 3,000 kg) with a programmable controller, operator presence sensing and the KOMTRAX remote equipment and fleet monitoring system. Source: Komatsu Australia FB page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.komatsu.com/en-au/products/equipment/forklifts/standard-electric-powered/komatsu-fb>
