# Opportunity-Charge a Vehicle During a Work Break

## Definition

The operator uses an available work break or idle window to add energy to the vehicle and resume the required shift.

## Notes

- Primary context: [[Distributed and Opportunity Charging Area]].
- Typical sequence: reach an available break, connect to the charger, receive a partial/opportunity charge, disconnect when the available window ends, and resume work.
- Charge-control stages and thermal control remain product Functions rather than human workflow Steps.
