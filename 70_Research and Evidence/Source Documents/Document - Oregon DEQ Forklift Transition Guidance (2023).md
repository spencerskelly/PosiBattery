# Document - Oregon DEQ Forklift Transition Guidance (2023)

## Definition

Oregon DEQ's guidance for moving forklift electricity reporting in the Clean Fuels Program from estimates to direct metering, including who generates credits and how to register metering points.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the western-states clean fuel topic. DEQ guidance on moving forklift reporting from a transitional estimate to direct metering under the Clean Fuels Program (CFP). The summary found no publication date; its dates run from early 2023 to 2024-03-31.

- **Reporting entity:** the forklift's owner or operator is the eligible credit generator. Forklift fuel supply equipment (FSE) registration moves to the point where the energy is metered. For rental fleets the owner registers the forklift at its home base and coordinates with renters to avoid double counting.
- **Metering vs estimation:** DEQ adopted a transitional estimation method in early 2023 that ends with the Q3 2023 report; non-metered forklifts may estimate for Q3 2023 only. From Q4 2023 only direct-metered data is accepted: "Only direct metered electric forklifts will be able to generate credits." Metered data may not be adjusted, except to subtract charging claimed by another party such as a rental forklift's owner. A meter installed partway through Q4 counts only electricity measured after installation.
- **Dates:** 2024-01-10 Q3 2023 report deadline (DEQ deactivates all unmetered forklift FSE after it); 2024-02-15 deadline for Q4 2023 FSE registrations and re-registrations; 2024-03-31 Q4 2023 report deadline.
- **Registration by meter location:** a panel, circuit or wall meter is the FSE (one meter may cover several chargers only if the panel serves only eligible forklift chargers, other equipment is kept off it, and all forklifts charging there are the applicant's); for a charger or cable meter the charger is the FSE, with documentation of its data tracking and a written explanation of data collection; for a forklift-mounted meter the forklift is the FSE; telematics registers the forklift with letterhead documentation that data is collected; rental fleets register each rental forklift with a quarterly FSE Detail Sheet of locations.
- **Attestation:** written explanations go on the generator's letterhead with a signed and dated declaration that the information is true to the best of the signer's knowledge and belief.
- **Not stated:** charger efficiency or an energy economy ratio (EER) adjustment, metering accuracy or calibration standards, the conversion of metered energy to credits, the steps of DEQ's Call-In Process, and details of the estimation method.
- **Conflict:** the metered-data adjustment rule differs from CARB's charger-efficiency adjustment (C291); the date metering became mandatory differs from the May 2023 discussion draft (C292).
