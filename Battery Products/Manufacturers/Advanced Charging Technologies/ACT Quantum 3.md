# ACT Quantum 3

## Definition

ACT next-generation Quantum charger with a 7 inch touchscreen and 24 to 120 V range.

## Notes

- The spec sheet lists 24 to 120 V, peak efficiency over 96.4 percent, full-cycle over 96 percent, a 7 inch touchscreen, Wi-Fi to ACTview and Battview, and lithium-ion, VRLA and flooded lead-acid support.
