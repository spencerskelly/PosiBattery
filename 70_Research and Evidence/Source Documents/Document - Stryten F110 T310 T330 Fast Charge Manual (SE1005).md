# Document - Stryten F110 T310 T330 Fast Charge Manual (SE1005)

## Definition

Stryten fast charge manual SE1005 for F110, T310 and T330.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw PDF.

- Chargers must be approved in writing by Stryten Application Engineering before sale. Start rate 30% to 40% of nameplate Ah, taper to finish rate by 90% state of charge; no numeric finish rate given.
- Electrolyte not above 140 F (60 C); normal 60 to 125 F (15 to 52 C); throughput at most 160% of rated capacity per 24 hours; do not discharge below 80% of rated capacity; charge during breaks.
- Charger must read each battery's ID, accumulate Ah returned and record charge temperature peaks and averages; monthly records required for warranty.
