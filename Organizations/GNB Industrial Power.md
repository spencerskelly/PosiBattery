# GNB Industrial Power

## Definition

Former industrial battery division brand; Stryten Energy says it is formerly GNB Industrial Power and Exide's documents say GNB became Exide Technologies.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Battery Maker.
- Stryten Energy states it is formerly GNB Industrial Power. Source: Stryten release (2023-03-14) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.stryten.com/?p=207972>
- Exide brochures say GNB Industrial Power is replaced by the Exide Technologies name, a division of Exide Technologies. Source: Exide Tensor xGEL brochure (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://exidegroup.com/en/document/tensor-xgel-brochure>
- A 2000 trade item describes GNB Industrial Power as a division of Exide Technologies. Source: EE Power (T2 (dated)), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://eepower.com/new-industry-products/gnb-industrial-power-debuts-new-ims-battery-monitor/>
- **Open (C30):** the same predecessor is claimed by two successors; see the successorOf links.
