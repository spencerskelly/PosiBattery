# Document - Fabrico Fleet Maintenance Software for Material Handling Review (retrieved 2026-10-14)

## Definition

Fabrico's 2026 review of five maintenance tools for forklifts and AGVs.

## Notes

Facts below were read through the fetch tool's summary of the page, not from the raw page; vendor statements are claims.

- Written by a vendor that ranks itself first; the ratings have no stated method. Reviews Fabrico, Samsara, Fleetio, TotalTrax and MaintainX.
- Fabrico: recurring preventive tasks for battery watering and equalization charges, no telematics hardware. Samsara: gateway on the forklift detecting impacts and idle time; the article says Samsara does not talk to the plant maintenance system (competitor statement). Fleetio: fuel, tire and service costs; the article says it will not help with docking stations or chargers. TotalTrax: driver access control and indoor location, often integrated with WMS. MaintainX: manual checklists.
- None of the five products was read from its own page.
