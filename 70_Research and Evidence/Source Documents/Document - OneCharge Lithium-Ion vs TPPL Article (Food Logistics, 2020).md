# Document - OneCharge Lithium-Ion vs TPPL Article (Food Logistics, 2020)

## Definition

Food Logistics article (2020-11-17, modified 2023-03-21) by Alex Pisarev, with a byline link and image credits to OneCharge, comparing lithium-ion and thin plate pure lead (TPPL) batteries, read through a summarizing tool.

## Notes

Added for IB-233: only non-battery-maker statement found on why TPPL and lithium-ion differ in charge acceptance. Vendor-affiliated, so tier 3.

- **TPPL charge acceptance:** absorbs charge faster than standard AGM, fast up to 70 to 80 percent then slow. The author reports manufacturer claims of up to six times rated capacity and dead to full in under 30 minutes, while the article's own testing found the high rate lasts only briefly at about two times rated capacity and slows sharply near 70 percent. Heat at high rates is said to shorten life.
- **Lithium-ion:** said to take charge at any time for any length with no capacity loss; TPPL typically cannot be discharged below 40 percent state of charge without damage or warranty loss; incomplete recharges can cut TPPL capacity to about 50 percent.
- **Limits:** no charge efficiency, no temperature ranges, no flooded lead-acid detail. Vendor-affiliated and pro-lithium; the page never says what OneCharge sells.
