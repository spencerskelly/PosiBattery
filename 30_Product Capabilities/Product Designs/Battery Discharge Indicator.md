# Battery Discharge Indicator

## Definition

Dedicated operator-facing indication of battery discharge state or remaining battery capacity on the vehicle.

## Notes

- [[Hyster Power Cellect]] explicitly presents battery data on the factory Battery Discharge Indicator.
- [[Linde MT18 Multifunction Display]] explicitly includes a battery discharge indicator.
- This Design does not imply a particular visual technology, segmentation, state-of-charge algorithm, or communication transport.
