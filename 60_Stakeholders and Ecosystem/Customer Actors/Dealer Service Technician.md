# Dealer Service Technician

## Definition

A technician employed by a dealer or the maker who installs, retrofits, services and warranty-repairs trucks, batteries and chargers at the customer.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: source-stated.** Hyster states a size of service network, which says the role exists, not what the technician needs from products.
- **Role in the customer organization:** Installs retrofits and performs warranty service; distinct from the customer's own maintenance technician.
- **Relation to the product (analyst classification, hypothesis):** user and influencer.
- **Needs this role takes part in:** see the Use Case notes that list this Actor as a participant (query `participants` in [[README_Customer Needs|Customer Needs]]); the product to need route is in [[Product to Customer Need Map]].
