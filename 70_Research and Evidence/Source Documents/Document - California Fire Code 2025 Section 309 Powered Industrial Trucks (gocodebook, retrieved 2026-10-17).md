# Document - California Fire Code 2025 Section 309 Powered Industrial Trucks (gocodebook, retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

Third-party web reproduction of section 309 (powered industrial trucks and equipment) of the 2025 California Fire Code, the only adopted-code text read for forklift battery charging in this pass.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), from a reproduction site, not the Building Standards Commission publication. The page says it is not a substitute for the official publication. The page covers sections 309.1 to 309.7 only.

- **309.1 scope:** powered industrial trucks and similar equipment (the page names floor scrubbers and buffers) are operated and maintained under 309.2 to 309.7.
- **309.2:** trucks used in classified (hazardous) locations must be listed and labeled for that environment, per NFPA 505.
- **309.3 battery chargers:** chargers must be of an approved type; combustible storage is kept at least 3 ft from chargers; charging is not allowed in areas open to the public.
- **309.4 ventilation:** battery-charging areas need approved ventilation to prevent dangerous accumulation of flammable gases.
- **309.5 extinguishers:** battery-charging areas need a section 906 extinguisher rated at least 4-A:20-B:C within 20 ft of the charger.
- **309.6 and 309.7:** refueling (liquid fuel, LP-gas, hydrogen) and repairs are done outside buildings or in approved areas.
- **What it does not say:** no battery chemistry is named, so lithium is neither covered nor exempted by name; no signage rule appears; no detection or sprinkler rule appears.
- The page does not mention the International Fire Code. California's code is built on the IFC but the 2024 IFC section 309 text was not read (IB-274); do not assume they match.
