# Raymond Energy Essentials Lithium-Ion Battery

## Definition

Lithium-ion batteries sold as 'Energy Essentials Distributed by Raymond' and engineered to integrate into Raymond forklifts.

## Notes

- At ProMat 2023 Raymond displayed Energy Essentials Distributed by Raymond lithium-ion batteries engineered to integrate seamlessly into its forklifts. Source: Robotics 24/7 ProMat report (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.robotics247.com/article/raymond_shows_off_integrated_intralogistics_systems_promat_2023/Raymond_Corp>
- **Unknown:** who manufactures Energy Essentials batteries; the 'Distributed by' wording suggests another maker, which is not stated (C64).

- **Official sources found 2026-10-13 (read through a summarizing tool, IB-173):** [[Document - Raymond Introducing Energy Essentials Distributed by Raymond (2020-11-17)]] <https://www.raymondcorp.com/news/2020/introducing-energy-essentials-distributed-by-raymond>; [[Document - Raymond Lithium-Ion Batteries Press Release (2025-08-12)]] <https://www.raymondcorp.com/news/2025/raymond-introduces-lithium-ion-batteries-engineered-and-manufactured-for-peak-energy-demands>; [[Document - Raymond Energy Solutions Page (retrieved 2026-10-13)]] <https://www.raymondcorp.com/products/energy-solutions>; whitepaper landing page <https://prod-cms-raymondcorp.raymondcorp.com/information/raymond-literature/white-papers/lithium-ion-batteries-from-energy-essentials-distributed-by-raymond>.
- **2020 release (Energy Essentials Distributed by Raymond):** NMC lithium-ion; 1,056 Ah in an 18-inch compartment; fast charging at 500 A through a single connector; five years or 6,000 cycles; active cell balancing and thermal control; sealed; pairs with iWAREHOUSE Evolution. Source: Raymond press release (T1), retrieved 2026-10-13. <https://www.raymondcorp.com/news/2020/introducing-energy-essentials-distributed-by-raymond>
- **2025 release (Raymond lithium-ion batteries):** lithium iron phosphate; 24, 36 and 48 V; "IP rating of 69"; UL 2580 listed; compatible with Energy Essentials high-frequency lithium-ion chargers; integrates with [[Raymond Energy Asset Manager]]; fast and opportunity charging; sealed. Source: Raymond press release (T1), retrieved 2026-10-13. <https://www.raymondcorp.com/news/2025/raymond-introduces-lithium-ion-batteries-engineered-and-manufactured-for-peak-energy-demands>
- **Whitepaper page:** UL Listed; constant communication and data exchange between lift truck and battery; real-time state of charge, fault codes and maintenance data; remote diagnostics and upgrades. <https://prod-cms-raymondcorp.raymondcorp.com/information/raymond-literature/white-papers/lithium-ion-batteries-from-energy-essentials-distributed-by-raymond>
- **Conflicts kept visible:** chemistry and capacity differ between the 2020 and 2025 releases, and it is unclear whether they describe one product (C217). The maker is still not named (C64): the 2025 release lists "Energy Essentials Distributed by Raymond" as a trademark of The Raymond Corporation and is titled "Engineered, Manufactured", but names no plant or cell supplier.
