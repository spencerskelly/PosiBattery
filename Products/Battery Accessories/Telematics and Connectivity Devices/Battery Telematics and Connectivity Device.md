# Battery Telematics and Connectivity Device

## Definition

Device or software path that sends battery or charge data off the battery or charger, for example over cellular, Bluetooth or a site network.

## Notes

- **Locus:** varies; may be battery-mounted, charger-mounted, or cloud-only. Locus is not stated consistently in sources.
- PosiCharge describes Battery Rx with optional cellular connectivity and PosiNet collecting charge data for usage reports. Source: PosiCharge SVS 80/200/300 spec sheet (2019) (T1 (dated)), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.posicharge.com/source/files/PosiCharge_80_200_300-SpecSheet-04302019.pdf>
- Fronius Charge & Connect shows chargers, connected batteries, battery status and power use in one view. Source: Warehouse News report on Fronius (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://warehousenews.co.uk/2022/11/fronius-launches-range-of-lithium-ion-battery-chargers/>
- **Overlap warning:** Battery Rx appears in both this category and [[Battery Monitoring and Identification Device]]. One product can implement more than one family; classification is a modeling decision still open.
- **Gaps:** no battery-installed telematics products verified here, and no data-ownership, security or fleet-integration evidence.
