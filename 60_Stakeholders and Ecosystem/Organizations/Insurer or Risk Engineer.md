# Insurer or Risk Engineer

## Definition

Business role: a property insurer or its loss-prevention engineers, who set site conditions (separation, detection, ventilation, charging practice) that a customer must meet to keep or obtain cover or a good rate.

## Notes

- Added 2026-10-17 for [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]].
