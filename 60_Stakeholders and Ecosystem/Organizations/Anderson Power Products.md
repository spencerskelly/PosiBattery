# Anderson Power Products

## Definition

Maker of SB series power connectors widely used on electric forklift and pallet jack batteries.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Accessory Maker.
- An industrial knowledge-base article says Anderson Power Products SB connectors are the industry standard for battery connections in electric forklifts and pallet jacks, are rated 600 V AC/DC, have no male or female distinction and are color-coded by voltage (secondary source). Source: Industrial Monitor Direct knowledge base (T4), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://industrialmonitordirect.com/zh-hans/blogs/knowledgebase/anderson-sb-175-to-sb-350-battery-connector-adapter-guide>
- Crown's parts shop lists SB contacts, drive blocks, connecting handles and cable clamps in SB 50, 175, 350, 320 SBE and 350 SBX sizes. Source: Crown parts shop (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://shop.crown.com/crown/en/Batteries-and-Chargers/Battery-and-Charger-Parts-and-Accessories/Battery-Connectors/c/battery_connectors>
