# Convert Traction Battery Voltage for Onboard Systems

## Definition

Convert the traction battery voltage to a lower voltage for onboard systems.

## Notes

- **Need served (analyst reasoning, 2026-10-14):** [[Retrofit Safety and Telematics Onto Existing Trucks]]. Analyst reasoning: added telematics, lights and sensors run at 13 V or 24 V, but the truck battery is 24 to 80 V; a converter removes a second battery or a custom supply. Source: [[Delta MOOVon Onboard Charger and Converters]] (converters to 13 or 24 V at 400 or 600 W). Customer work: fitting accessories to a truck.
- Stated: MOOVon DC-DC converters to 13 V or 24 V at 400 or 600 W (category listing).
