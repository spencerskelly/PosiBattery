# Heli A3 Series Lithium Forklifts

## Definition

Heli A3 lithium forklifts (CPD18 at 80 V 150 Ah and CPD35 at 80 V 250 Ah) with a built-in 230 V charger, operator presence sensing and IPX4 sealing.

## Notes

- A Polish dealer says the CPD18-A3LiH4-M (1,800 kg, 80 V 150 Ah, up to 5.5 hours) and CPD35-A3LiH4-M (3,500 kg, 80 V 250 Ah) have a built-in charger for a standard 230 V socket, a factory OPS (Operator Presence Sensing) system, integrated side shift and IPX4 sealing; an external three-phase charger can charge the CPD35 in 2 hours. Source: Paleciaki dealer pages (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.paleciaki.info/en/electric-forklift-heli-cpd18-a3lih4-m-li-ion.html>
