# REMA Lipprandt

## Definition

Bonn-based maker (REMA Lipprandt GmbH & Co. KG) of twin plug and socket connectors for electric industrial trucks.

## Notes

- Per the data sheet, makes 80, 160, 320 and 400 A connector families to EN 1175-1 and DIN 43589 at 150 V. Source: [[Document - REMA Lipprandt Connector Data Sheet]] (T1), retrieved 2026-10-14. <https://assets.rs-online.com/v1698978199/Datasheets/18d662b92011f77aacf08eec76685c31.pdf>
