# PosiGuard - Support Local Service Configuration

## Definition

PosiCharge PosiGuard shall provide a supported local service interface that permits authorized users to configure supported settings and retrieve supported device information.

## Notes

- Current evidence identifies PosiConnect and Bluetooth as the supported service mechanism.
- The requirement states the service obligation without making Bluetooth or a specific app a permanent architecture constraint.
- Authentication, permissions, update security, supported operating systems, and offline behavior remain detailed requirement gaps.
