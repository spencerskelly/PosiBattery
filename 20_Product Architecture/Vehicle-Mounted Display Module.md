# Vehicle-Mounted Display Module

## Definition

Technology-neutral display module mounted on the vehicle and used to present battery or truck status to the operator.

## Notes

- [[Linde MT18 Multifunction Display]] explicitly provides a multifunction vehicle display including battery discharge state.
- [[Yale ERC050-060VGL]] explicitly shows state of charge and low-charge warnings on the truck display.
- Display size, LCD/OLED technology, resolution, interface bus, backlight, and supplier are not asserted unless a product source states them.
