# Document - Deka D-Series Installation and Operation Manual (0628)

## Definition

Deka D-Series operating and maintenance instructions (0628).

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- Full-charge specific gravity 1.280 to 1.295 at 77 F; average below 1.265 after equalizing means check the charger; equalize every one to four weeks; D100 5-plate cell 200 Ah with 10 A finish rate; discharge no more than 80% for longest life.
- Recharge at end of each shift, no more than two successive shifts; a battery above 1.230 can run another shift. Opportunity charging, cooling and warranty records not addressed.
