# Battery-Direct Charge Connection

## Definition

Charging by plugging the charger into a connector or port on the battery itself, whether the battery sits in the truck or is removed; current does not pass through a truck-mounted inlet.

## Notes

- Design characteristic found in product descriptions, not a decision by us. Counterpart of [[Truck Charging Port]] (alias: On-Vehicle Charge Inlet), where the socket is on the truck. Charging with a charger built into the truck is [[Battery Onboard Charger]]; its mains inlet is on the vehicle.
- A single truck can offer both (Hyster J-series lithium: battery-side port as standard, optional side charge inlet).
- No Requirement is linked (intentional gap).

## Aliases

- Direct-to-battery charging
- Battery-side charge port
