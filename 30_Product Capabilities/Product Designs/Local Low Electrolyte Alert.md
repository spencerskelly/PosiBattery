# Local Low Electrolyte Alert

## Definition

Low-electrolyte warning presented locally at or near the battery using a visual indicator, audible alarm, or both.

## Notes

- [[Flow-Rite Eagle Eye Elite IV]] provides local visual good/low indication with a remote-mountable light.
- [[Philadelphia Scientific SmartBlinky Pro]] provides multi-state LED indication and SmartBEEP audible warning.
- [[Crown Battery Acid Indicators]] include smart versions using LED and sound to indicate good electrolyte or watering need.
- The exact alert thresholds and internal logic are product-specific.
