# Exide Solition Material Handling Battery

## Definition

Exide Solition Material Handling lithium iron phosphate battery for forklifts, AGVs, ports, GSE and lifting platforms, launched October 2024.

## Notes

- LFP prismatic cells, up to 4,000 cycles in high discharge, over 98% Ah charge efficiency, fast and opportunity charging, modular for existing trays, EN 1175:2020, remote access/cloud monitoring option.
