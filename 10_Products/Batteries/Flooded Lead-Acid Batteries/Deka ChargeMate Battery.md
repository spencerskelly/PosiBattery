# Deka ChargeMate Battery

## Definition

Deka flooded battery with a built-in charger.

## Notes

- East Penn says ChargeMate combines a flooded battery and charger in one unit that connects to a 15 A, 120 V AC outlet. Source: East Penn forklift products page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.eastpennmanufacturing.com/forklift-products/>
