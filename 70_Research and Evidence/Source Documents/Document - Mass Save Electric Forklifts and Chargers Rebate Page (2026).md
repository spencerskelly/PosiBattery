# Document - Mass Save Electric Forklifts and Chargers Rebate Page (2026)

## Definition

Mass Save's 2026 rebate of $6,000 per electric forklift and $550 per high-frequency charger above 90 percent efficiency.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- **Forklift:** $6,000 per battery-powered electric forklift, no efficiency requirement listed; it must replace or be bought in place of a propane forklift and cannot replace an existing electric one.
- **Charger:** $550 per high-frequency battery charger with power conversion efficiency above 90 percent (at least 0.9 in the eligibility text); new equipment or a replacement for an SCR or ferroresonant charger; rated input power above 2 kW.
- **Dates:** purchase between 2026-01-01 and 2026-12-31; submit by 2027-01-31. Sponsors: Berkshire Gas, Cape Light Compact, Eversource, Liberty, National Grid, Unitil.
- **Not stated:** per-kW rates, lithium versus lead-acid distinction, caps. The page defers to the rebate form.
