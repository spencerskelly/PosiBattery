# Document - The Mobile Report T-Mobile to Begin LTE Phase-Out (2025-10-06)

## Definition

Blog report, based on a leaked internal T-Mobile document, that T-Mobile will re-farm LTE spectrum to 5G over two or more years, starting soon.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand). The leaked document appears only as an image, so every detail is the blog's own description and is unverified. No T-Mobile statement is cited.

- LTE bands re-farmed to 5G (band 2 to n2; band 4/66 to n66; bands 12 and some 71 later).
- Affected: LTE-only and 5G NSA-only devices.
- Reported timeline: most LTE gone around 2028; from January 2026 business customers need exceptions to activate LTE-only or NSA-only devices; a 5 MHz LTE channel stays nationwide until at least 2035, kept for IoT devices such as ATMs and vehicle infotainment; new business contracts would not promise LTE beyond 2035. LTE-M not mentioned.
- Disagrees with the vendor claims that LTE will last "a decade" or longer (C331).
