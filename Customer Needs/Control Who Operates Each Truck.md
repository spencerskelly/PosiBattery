# Control Who Operates Each Truck

## Definition

Customer need: Control Who Operates Each Truck. The problem behind it: Only trained, authorized people should run a truck, and use should be traceable to a driver.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: need hypothesis.** The statements below are what makers and dealers say their products do for customers. No customer, user or buyer source in the vault confirms that customers hold this need or rank it. Per the ruleset, source research becomes a validated need only after customer-side evidence.
- **Problem solved (analyst wording):** Only trained, authorized people should run a truck, and use should be traceable to a driver.
- **Who has the problem:** [[Fleet Operations Manager]], [[Site Safety Manager]], [[Forklift Operator]].
- **Operating segments (analyst crosswalk to [[PosiCharge Market Segments and Jobs-to-Be-Done]], hypothesis):** Material-handling fleets.
- **Realized by (specific functions; products link to these):** [[Control Operator Access]], [[Enforce Pre-Shift Checklist]], [[Report Truck Telemetry]].
- **Products reaching this need through those functions:** 26. Largest families: Fleet Software and Platforms/Truck Telematics (18), Forklifts/Class I Electric Rider Trucks (3), Vehicle Accessories/Access Control (2), Forklifts/Class III Electric Hand and Hand-Rider Trucks (2), Vehicle Accessories/Operator Assist and Stability (1). Per-product list in [[Product to Customer Need Map]].
- **Vendor-stated evidence (by product):**
  - [[Linde connect]]: Linde lists connect:ac access control (PIN or RFID), connect:an usage analysis, connect:dt crash detection, operating hours and trouble codes; impacts are recorded with time stamp, vehicle and driver reference and sent to the myLinde customer portal. Source: Linde dealer brochure, Linde retrofit page and KION story (T1/T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.kiongroup.com/en/Newsroom/Story-Categories/Innovation/Article/7-solutions-that-make-the-warehouse-safer.html>
  - [[STILL FleetManager]]: STILL says its FleetManager access control system offers reports and telematics applications that monitor the use of trucks and drivers on site, and access authorisation 'with Fleet Manager 4.x'. Source: STILL release (2021) and EXH-SF page (T1/T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.still.co.uk/company/news-press/news/detail/safe-safer-still.html>
