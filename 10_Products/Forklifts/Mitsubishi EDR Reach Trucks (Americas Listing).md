# Mitsubishi EDR Reach Trucks (Americas Listing)

## Definition

Mitsubishi EDR reach trucks in the Americas listing, for example EDR18LN, 3,500 lb, 36 V, sit/stand.

## Notes

- Listing shows 14 Mitsubishi reach trucks; only EDR18LN was read (wide chassis, sit/stand, 36 V, 73.1 in turning radius; the 450 in max lift looks wrong). European SENSiA reach trucks (RB series) are a different naming (C244). Source: <https://www.allmachines.com/forklifts/view-all?brand=mitsubishi&category=stackers&page=1&sortBy=default> (T3, second-hand).
