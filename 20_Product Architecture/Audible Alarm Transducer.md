# Audible Alarm Transducer

## Definition

Buzzer, beeper, or equivalent electro-acoustic component used to present an audible alert.

## Notes

- Alert cadence, sound pressure, tone, drive voltage, and transducer technology are product-specific.
- [[Philadelphia Scientific SmartBlinky Pro]] identifies its audible function as SmartBEEP; Crown also identifies sound on its smart battery acid indicators.
