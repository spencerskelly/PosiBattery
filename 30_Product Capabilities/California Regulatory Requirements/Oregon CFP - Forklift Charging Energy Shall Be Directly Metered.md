# Oregon CFP - Forklift Charging Energy Shall Be Directly Metered

## Definition

A party reporting electric forklift charging to Oregon's Clean Fuels Program shall, from the Q4 2023 report, report directly metered electricity, and shall not adjust the metered data except to subtract charging claimed by another party.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It binds the party named in the definition, not a charger. Product consequences are in the derived requirement REQ-00058 (analyst-proposed).
- Second-hand reading; see [[Western State Clean Fuel Programs for Forklift Electricity Register]] and the conflict register.
- **Status:** applies to parties who choose to generate credits; unmetered forklift FSE were deactivated after the 2024-01-10 Q3 2023 report deadline.
- Conflicts: C291 (no adjustment versus CARB's efficiency adjustment), C292 (date).
