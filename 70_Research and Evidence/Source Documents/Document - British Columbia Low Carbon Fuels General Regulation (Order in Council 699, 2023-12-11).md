# Document - British Columbia Low Carbon Fuels General Regulation (Order in Council 699, 2023-12-11)

## Definition

BC's 2023 Low Carbon Fuels General Regulation, naming use in a forklift as a prescribed purpose and setting an electricity threshold and allocation-agreement rules.

## Notes

**How it was read:** first-hand, from the raw file the owner added to the repo (`Downloads/BC_LCF_General_Regulation_2023.pdf`), with text extracted by pdftotext. Not a summary.

Added from the owner's downloads. Order of the Lieutenant Governor in Council No. 699, approved 2023-12-11, of the Low Carbon Fuels General Regulation under the Low Carbon Fuels Act (16 pages). The copy is hosted on a propane industry site, not the province; check against the BC Laws copy for currency (2024 amendments are mentioned by a trade source, C299).

- **Forklifts (section 2, applicable purposes):** the prescribed purposes of a fuel include use in cargo handling equipment, in ground support equipment and 'use in a forklift'. This is the legal basis for forklift electricity in BC-LCFS at the time of this order.
- **Electricity threshold (section 8):** the Act does not apply to the supply or export of electricity by a person in a compliance period if the person markets no other fuel and supplies 15,000 kWh or less in the period. A person who allocates responsibility under an allocation agreement is covered when the person who markets the electricity markets other fuel or at least 15,000 kWh.
- **Allocation agreements (sections 9 to 11 and 26):** responsibility for electricity supplied through final supply equipment can be allocated to another person; the agreement must identify each piece of equipment, the days of supply and the intended use of the electricity; section 11 restricts allocation of responsibility; section 26 sets reporting and record-keeping where responsibility is allocated.
- **Compliance period:** the calendar year (section 4). Type B fuels include hydrogen (section 3).
- **Not in this document:** the 6,800 kg heavy-forklift limit named by a market page, the utility-bill rule for the credit generator, credit prices, metering rules. Those may be in later amendments or the Act (C299, IB-260).
- **Conflicts:** the heavy-forklift limit and utility-bill rule are not in this text (C299).
