# Document - HELI H4 Series 1.5-3.8t Lithium Battery Forklift Brochure (FLH4002-EN)

## Definition

Manufacturer brochure for the Heli H4 series 1.5 to 3.8 t lithium-battery forklifts: truck data, battery options, charger and battery claims.

## Provenance

- **Local artifact:** `H4-1.5-3.8t-Lithium-battery-forklift-FLH4002-EN.pdf` in `Downloads`; an image-only PDF read by OCR on 2026-10-13 (8 pages) and checked against the page image for the battery and charger tables.
- **Author or publisher:** Heli (Hefei, China); the cover prints www.heliforklift.net.
- **Original web address:** the site address printed on the cover is used as `sourceUrl`; the address of the PDF itself is not known and was not fetched.
- **Evidence tier:** T1 (manufacturer brochure).

## Notes

**Battery and charger data (as stated; not independently verified):**
- Battery options for the truck family: 80 V in 125 to 560 Ah (Heli brand: 125, 150, 202, 250, 280, 404, 560 Ah) and 80 V in 125 to 604 Ah (CATL brand: 125, 150, 173, 202, 228, 271, 302, 346, 404, 456, 542, 604 Ah). Standard fit by truck: 1.5-1.8 t S 125 Ah; 1.5-1.8 t M, 2 t (X) S and 2-2.5 t S 150 Ah; 2-2.5 t M and 3-3.5 t S 202 Ah; 3-3.5 t M and 3.8 t S 250 Ah.
- Cells: square lithium iron phosphate cells and modules "used by large numbers of commercial vehicles"; modules in aluminium alloy plate frames.
- Claims: charge and discharge efficiency up to 98%; thermal runaway temperature over 600 C; more than 3,000 cycles or 5 years with capacity retention above 75%; charged in 2 hours with fast charging; heater fitted for low-temperature use (standard on one option); maintenance free (no water).
- Abuse tests shown as photos without results: seawater immersion, extrusion, heating, temperature cycling, drop, overcharge, short circuit.
- Truck tests: 15 min rainstorm to IPX4; 6 h alternating operation in a -20 C cold store then 12 h parked; 800 h reliability test.
- Charger D80V100ALI-438: 10 kVA, three-phase four-wire 380 V +/-15%, input under 20 A, output 5 to 100 A, indoor use, ambient -20 to +45 C, REMA 320 plug and socket.
- Full charging time = required charge capacity / charging current + 0.2 h. Worked example: 80 V 250 Ah battery, alarm at discharge, 100 A charger: 250 x 0.9 / 100 + 0.2 = 2.45 h (arithmetic checked: correct). The example implies the battery is charged from 10% state of charge.
- Fleet system 'HELI smart fleet management system (domestic basic edition)': positioning, remote diagnosis and monitoring, battery management, maintenance reminders; weight management and ID recognition optional.
- Battery weights and the truck table (OCR of the dense tables was unreliable for weights and dimensions; not recorded here).
