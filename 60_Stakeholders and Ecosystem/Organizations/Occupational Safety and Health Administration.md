# Occupational Safety and Health Administration

## Definition

US federal workplace safety agency; its powered industrial truck standard, 29 CFR 1910.178, includes the battery charging paragraphs (g) that apply to forklift charging areas.

## Notes

- Added 2026-10-17 for [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]]. Only the 1978 directive on charging-only areas was read ([[Document - OSHA Instruction STD 1-11.4 Battery Charging Stations for Fork Lifts (1978)]]); the standard text and any lithium letters of interpretation are unread (IB-280).
- A secondary source says OSHA has no lithium-specific standard as of mid-2025 (C319).
- Not linked to products: no relationship in the vocabulary fits a regulator.
