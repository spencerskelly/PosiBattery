# Battery Products

## What is here

Reusable definitions of products and product classes that connect to batteries: abstract categories, families, and commercial products grouped by manufacturer.

## What belongs here

- `Categories`: the root [[Battery-Connected Product]] and abstract product classes such as battery management, monitoring and identification, connectors, thermal management, protection, chargers and telematics.
- `Categories/Battery-Installed Devices`: the subset family defined by being installed on or integrated into the battery (carried from the seed work; scope is still open).
- `Categories/Traction Batteries`: [[Industrial Traction Battery]] and its chemistry families (flooded lead-acid, valve-regulated lead-acid, lithium-ion).
- `Manufacturers/<name>`: one note per commercial product, as a specialization of a category or family. Application use (a specific truck, battery or GSE pack) is not modeled here.
