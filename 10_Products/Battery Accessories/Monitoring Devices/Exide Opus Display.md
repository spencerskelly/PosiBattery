# Exide Opus Display

## Definition

Optional display for the Exide Sonnenschein Lithium battery that shows key information at a glance.

## Notes

- **Update 2026-10-14 (priority 5, unlinked-product review):** linked to [[Inform Users of Battery Condition]] because the Sonnenschein Lithium brochure says the display shows key battery information at a glance; what it shows is not stated, so only the general function is linked. Source: as cited on this note.
- The Sonnenschein Lithium brochure lists the Opus Display as an optional item that shows key battery information at a glance; the communication flow diagram links the battery and BMS, the High Output Charger and the battery display. Source: Exide Sonnenschein Lithium brochure (T1, read through a summarizing tool), retrieved 2026-10-13. <https://exidegroup.com/en/document/sonnenschein-lithium-brochure>
- **Not stated:** screen type, interfaces, shown values, mounting. The second Exide sheet read does not mention the Opus Display (IB-176). No function is linked yet because the sources do not say what it displays.
