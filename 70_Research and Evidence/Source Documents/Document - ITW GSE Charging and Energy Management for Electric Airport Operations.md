## Notes

**Content recorded (as stated by the source, not independently verified unless marked):**
- Names four ITW GSE solutions: Power Share (redistributes available gate power to charge electric GSE when capacity allows), EcoGate (system approach joining GPUs, pre-conditioned air units, chargers, cables and power infrastructure), DC Charger, and Intelligent Power Management (IPM).
- IPM is described as prioritizing the ground power unit while the pre-conditioned air unit is running; aircraft power and PCA keep priority and charging uses spare capacity.
- Challenges listed: too many GSE units competing for chargers at peak, limited gate power, simultaneous charging raising peak load and overload risk, chargers far from where equipment works, uncoordinated rollout.
- Approach: charge at gate or remote stand to cut towing and queues, opportunity charging during idle periods, centralized charging for planned or overnight needs, and a combination of fixed and mobile charging. Claims this is often possible 'without the need for major infrastructure upgrades'.
- No technical specifications on the page (no power, voltage, current or connector data).
