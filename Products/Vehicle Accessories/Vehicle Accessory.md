# Vehicle Accessory

## Definition

Devices added to a forklift, GSE vehicle or similar truck: detection, displays, assist, lights, power interfaces, convenience items.

## Notes

- Category note for navigation: the product folder for this category is `Products/Vehicle Accessories` .
- Owners are not part of the category; who offers a product is a relationship on the product note.
