# Battery Watering System

## Definition

Hardware that refills water in flooded lead-acid cells, from manual single-point systems to automatic systems controlled by a charger.

## Notes

- **Evidence is thin in this pass.** Only one first-hand source was retrieved here.
- PosiCharge lists automatic watering as an option on its SVS200 charger. Source: PosiCharge SVS200 page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.posicharge.com/svs200/>
- **Not verified by this pass:** Flow-Rite and Philadelphia Scientific products named in the ChatGPT seed branch.
- **Gaps:** valve and manifold hardware, flow indicators, charger integration method, and electrolyte-level sensing overlap with [[Battery Monitoring and Identification Device]].

## Aliases

- Single-point watering
- Automatic watering
