# UniCarriers MX2 and MXL Series

## Definition

UniCarriers 80 V four-wheel electric pneumatic forklifts of 5,000 to 12,000 lb, with a Triathlon lithium-ion option on the MXL.

## Notes

- Logisnext Americas launched the MX2 and MXL four-wheel electric pneumatic forklifts in June 2022 on an 80 V platform with capacities of 5,000-7,000 lb and 9,000-12,000 lb. Source: Logisnext Americas release (2022-06-21) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisnextamericas.com/en/logisnext/news/articles/unicarriers/2022/MLA-Launches-Two-New-UniCarriers-80V-Electric-Pneumatic-Forklifts>
- A trade report says the MXL is available with a lithium battery from Triathlon. Source: Supply Chain 24/7 ProMat report (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.supplychain247.com/article/unicarriers_and_rocla_reveal_5_forklifts>
- Logisnext says the MX2 Series is compact with a 5,000 to 7,000 lb capacity and the MXL Series offers 9,000 to 12,000 lb, both zero-emission with two-year warranties and the MXL available with a Triathlon lithium battery; a dealer-data page says the MX2-30L (electric pneumatic) has intelligent curve control that smoothly reduces cornering speed and can be customized with extra lights, awareness options and tire choices. Source: Logistics Management (ProMat) and AllMachines (T2/T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/unicarriers_and_rocla_reveal_5_forklifts>
