# Electronic Electrolyte Probe Assembly

## Definition

Electronic probe assembly installed in a flooded battery cell to determine whether electrolyte is at an acceptable level.

## Notes

- Philadelphia Scientific explicitly identifies the SmartBlinky Pro as using an electronic probe that senses electrolyte in a cell.
- The exact transduction principle and internal circuit are intentionally unspecified because the retrieved manufacturer information does not disclose them.
- The assembly is distinct from the cable-mounted LED/buzzer indication hardware.
