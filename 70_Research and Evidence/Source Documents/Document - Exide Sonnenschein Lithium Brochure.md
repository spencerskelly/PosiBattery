# Document - Exide Sonnenschein Lithium Brochure

## Definition

Exide GNB Sonnenschein Lithium tray battery brochure: voltage classes, capacity table, BMS, communication flow and options.

## Notes

**How it was read.** Through a fetch tool that returns a model-written summary, not the raw text, on 2026-10-13; values were not checked against the raw file (IB-173 to IB-178).

- Tray table (nominal V; capacity; energy): 24 V class 25.6 V, 80 to 440 Ah, 2.0 to 11.3 kWh; 36 V class 38.4 V, 80 to 440 Ah, 3.1 to 16.9 kWh; 48 V class 51.2 V, 110 to 552 Ah, 5.6 to 28.3 kWh; 80 V class 76.8 V, 110 to 966 Ah, 8.4 to 74.2 kWh. Arithmetic check: nominal voltage x capacity matches the stated energy in every class. Section headings name only 48 V and 80 V, so the 24 and 36 V rows come from the table alone.
- Discharge ratings: the 80 V 966 Ah model 1,960 A constant and 2,940 A peak; the 110 Ah model 220 A constant and 420 A peak.
- Ampere-hour charge efficiency "greater than 98%"; "ultra-fast" and "fast charge and opportunity charge" claimed with no times; works with recuperation systems; maintenance-free; classes I, II and III.
- BMS monitors voltage, current and temperature, controls cell balancing, calculates state of charge, controls the GNB Lithium Charger, has an internal communication bus between modules, runs thermal management if required and can integrate with the vehicle (optional). Communication flow: battery and BMS, High Output Charger, battery display.
- Options: Opus Display, HC-/LC-Logger. Chemistry stated only as lithium-ion. Cycle life: "superior", no count. Warranty: not stated. Certifications listed are ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO/TS 16949 for plants, not for the product.
