# Document - Stryten M-Series T330 Manual (SE1014)

## Definition

Stryten T330 installation and operation manual SE1014.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw PDF.

- Start rate up to 3 times finish rate, taper to finish by 85% state of charge; freshening charge 3 to 4 hours at finish rate; charger Ah within 10% of battery Ah; automatic termination.
- Equalize weekly (chargers can schedule, e.g. Sunday 08:00). Electrolyte not above 110 F (43 C); normal 60 to 100 F; allow about 8 hours cool-down. Specific gravity 1.295 +/- 0.005 at 77 F, not below 1.180. Check water weekly.
- Does not use the terms opportunity or fast charge; battery-mounted monitor checked monthly.
