# Class III Electric Hand or Hand-Rider Truck

## Definition

Electric motor hand trucks and hand/rider trucks: walkie pallet trucks, center-control and walkie/rider low-lift trucks.

## Notes

- Lift codes include low-lift walkie pallet, low-lift walkie/center and low-lift walkie/rider. Source: OSHA eTool and OSHA class definitions sheet (T1/T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://osha.prod.pace.dol.gov/etools/powered-industrial-trucks/types-fundamentals/types> and <https://www.navsup.navy.mil/Portals/65/NAVSUP%20ENTERPRISE/NAVSUP%20FLC%20Pearl%20Harbor/Documents/MHE_Class_Definitions_OSHA.pdf>
