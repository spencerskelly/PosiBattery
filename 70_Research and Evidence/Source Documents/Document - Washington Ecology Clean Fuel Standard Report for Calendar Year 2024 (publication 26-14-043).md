# Document - Washington Ecology Clean Fuel Standard Report for Calendar Year 2024 (publication 26-14-043)

## Definition

Washington Ecology's 2024 Clean Fuel Standard annual figures, including the average credit price.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the business case. Ecology report for 1 January to 31 December 2024 (the program's second full year); the exact title was not captured, so the title here is descriptive.

- **Price:** annual volume-weighted average $45.92 per credit; monthly averages ranged from $23.88 to $89.89.
- **Volumes:** electricity volumes by quarter, for example Q1 197,490,125 kWh and Q4 255,249,515 kWh; the credit bank reached 3,001,846 credits at the end of 2024.
- **Not stated:** credits generated by electricity or by forklifts.
