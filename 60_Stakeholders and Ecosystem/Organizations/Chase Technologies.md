# Chase Technologies

## Definition

South African supplier of modular lithium-ion packs for forklifts and other equipment, founded 2017 per one article.

## Notes

- Described as founded in 2017 and supplying lithium-ion packs for forklifts, cleaning equipment and access platforms; about 190 units installed in South Africa (company claim).
