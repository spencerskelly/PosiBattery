# Review Fleet Utilization and Idle Capacity

## Definition

A fleet manager opens the fleet software, reviews hours of use and peak periods by truck, operator and site, and decides whether to reallocate, add or remove trucks or change staffing.

## Notes

- Completion: the manager has a decision on fleet size or allocation, or a reason to leave it unchanged.
