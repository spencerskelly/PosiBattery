# GSE Front Body and Bumper

## Definition

Generic GSE vehicle part: Front frame and bumper, the edge that approaches an aircraft or load.

## Notes

- Abstract, shared by GSE vehicle types (baggage and tow tractors, belt loaders, pushback tractors, cargo loaders); not every type has every part (a tractor has no conveyor, a belt loader has no towing interface).
- Accessories map to this part in [[GSE Part Connection Register]]; the mapping is not written as frontmatter links (see that note).
