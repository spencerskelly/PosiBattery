# Document - BC Low Carbon Fuel Requirements Summary 2010-2025 (RLCF-007, updated 2026-06-29)

## Definition

BC's summary statistics showing electricity supplied and credits earned in 2024 and 2025, with no prices.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. Ministry summary of fuel supply, credits and the credit market for BC-LCFS.

- **Electricity credits (Table 9, net credits):** diesel-class electricity 160,553 (2024) and 165,048 (2025); gasoline-class electricity 276,980 (2024) and 351,306 (2025).
- **Electricity supplied (Table 2):** diesel class 210.2 million kWh (2024) and 222.1 million kWh (2025); gasoline class 292.3 million kWh (2024) and 381.6 million kWh (2025).
- **Analyst ratios:** diesel-class electricity earned 0.764 credits per MWh in 2024 and 0.743 in 2025; gasoline-class 0.948 and 0.921. The diesel class holds the heavy forklifts.
- **Forklifts:** mentioned only as one of the 'other purposes' added since 2024, with volumes described as relatively small; no forklift-specific numbers.
- **Market (Table 11):** debits from fuel supply 3,785,624 (2024) and 4,658,961 (2025); credits from fuel supply 4,069,263 and 4,076,263; credits from initiative agreements 440,224 and 313,492; surplus 723,863 (2024) and a deficit of 269,206 (2025).
- **Not stated:** any credit price, and any 2026 figure.
