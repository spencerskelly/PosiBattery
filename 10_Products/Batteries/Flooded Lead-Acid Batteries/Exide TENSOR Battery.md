# Exide TENSOR Battery

## Definition

Exide (GNB) high-power flooded TENSOR line for heavy duty with a fast-charge package (charger, air agitation, temperature probe).

## Notes

- Full recharge in about 4 hours with the package; GNB claims over 10,000 operating hours vs 6,000 to 7,000 for standard lead batteries (asterisked claim, basis not given). [[Charge Battery Fast]] (V): <https://www.exidegroup.com/eu/sites/default/files/2021-08/GNB_MP_Overview_EN_web.pdf>. Plate type not stated.
