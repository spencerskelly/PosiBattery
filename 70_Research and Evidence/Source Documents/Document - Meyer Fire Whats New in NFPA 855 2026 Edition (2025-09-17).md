# Document - Meyer Fire Whats New in NFPA 855 2026 Edition (2025-09-17)

## Definition

Fire-protection consultancy blog post on the changes in the 2026 edition of NFPA 855. Read here for the edition's release timing and the stored-energy threshold change; it does not mention forklifts.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand). Read once; the tool reported the post does not address scope, exclusions or forklift charging.

- **Release:** the 2026 edition was released "ahead of schedule" per the post (dated 2025-09-17); no effective or adoption date is given. The post says the standard is not outright adopted in most jurisdictions.
- **Thresholds:** the Chapter 1 threshold quantities table was updated to cover more battery technologies. The post says the maximum stored energy limits table was removed in 2026, so staying under those limits no longer avoids a hazard mitigation analysis; section 4.4.1 now requires one by default unless a technology-specific chapter modifies it.
- **Conflict:** [[Document - Energy-Storage.news NFPA 855 2026 Edition Updates (2026-04-29)]] says other chemistry thresholds remain the same and gives size examples below which the standard may not apply. The two are not necessarily incompatible (threshold quantities versus a stored-energy exemption), but they leave the exemption question unclear (C315).
- **Not stated:** any mention of industrial trucks, mobile equipment or battery charging areas.
