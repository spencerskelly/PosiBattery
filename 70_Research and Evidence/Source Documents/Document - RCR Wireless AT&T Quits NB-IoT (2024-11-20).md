# Document - RCR Wireless AT&T Quits NB-IoT (2024-11-20)

## Definition

Trade-press report that AT&T is ending NB-IoT plan sales and new device certification and retiring its US NB-IoT network, moving customers to LTE-M.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand). A [Light Reading](https://www.lightreading.com/iot/at-t-to-discontinue-nb-iot-but-t-mobile-and-verizon-keep-the-faith) article of the same day was also read and is treated as part of this record.

- AT&T stops selling NB-IoT data plans, stops certifying new NB-IoT devices and plans to retire its NB-IoT network, "as early as the first quarter" of 2025; devices migrate to LTE-M. No exact shutdown date. RedCap is mentioned as a possible later successor, not the migration target.
- No AT&T statement on how long LTE-M or LTE will last.
- Light Reading: T-Mobile says it "currently has no plans to shut down its NB-IoT network"; Verizon says its NB-IoT runs on its 700 MHz LTE network with "no imminent plans to turn that down". An analyst (Mobile Experts) says few Western operators will keep NB-IoT.
- Relevance: only matters if a product uses NB-IoT; none of the PosiCharge or OEM monitor sources read names the cellular technology (C329).
