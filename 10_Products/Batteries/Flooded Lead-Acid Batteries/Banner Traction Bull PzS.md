# Banner Traction Bull PzS

## Definition

Banner Traction Bull PzS/PzB flooded lead-acid traction battery, certified explosion-safe, with an option to replace water automatically.

## Notes

- Banner's brochure describes the Traction Bull PzS/PzB as a lead-acid battery with liquid electrolyte, 'certified, explosion-safe' and the most cost-efficient, best-proven solution for industrial trucks and other e-vehicles; the text on automatic water replacement is cut off in the retrieved copy. Source: Banner Energy Solutions brochure (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://bannerbatterien.com/energysolutions/upload/files/FolderAGS_GB.pdf>
- **Not stated in the retrieved text:** voltages, capacities, cycle life and warranty.

- **Official brochures found 2026-10-13 (read through a summarizing tool, IB-180):** [[Document - Banner Traction Bull PzS DIN Cells Folder (1709002006)]] <https://bannerbatterien.com/energysolutions/upload/files/FolderTractionBullNassDINZellen_gb.pdf>; [[Document - Banner Energy Solutions Traction Bull PzS and PzB Folder (1709000515)]] <https://bannerbatterien.com/energysolutions/upload/files/FolderAGS_GB.pdf>. The cut-off text about water replacement is resolved: an optional filling system replaces water consumed automatically; no components are described.
- **Construction and ratings:** positive tubular plates, liquid lead-acid, bolted DIN cells 2 PzS to 10 PzS, 120 to 1,550 Ah (60 to 155 Ah per positive plate at C5, 30 C), 8.4 to 81.3 kg per cell, "1,500 cycles accord. to DIN/EN 60254-1, IEC 254-1". Source: Banner Traction Bull PzS folder (T1). <https://bannerbatterien.com/energysolutions/upload/files/FolderTractionBullNassDINZellen_gb.pdf>
- **Options named:** electrolyte circulation, water refill system, battery controller, explosion protection, cold store version; none is named as a product. 
- **Conflict kept visible:** one folder calls the battery "certified, explosion-safe", while the cell folder lists explosion protection as an option and names no certification (C222). The folders carry codes that may date them to 2017 and 2008 (inferred).
- **Design characteristics, with citations:**
  - [[Tubular Plate Construction]] (V): positive tubular plates. <https://bannerbatterien.com/energysolutions/upload/files/FolderTractionBullNassDINZellen_gb.pdf>
- **Not stated:** nominal voltage, warranty, grid alloy, separators.
