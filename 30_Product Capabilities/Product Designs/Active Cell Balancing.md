# Active Cell Balancing

## Definition

Cells are equalized by moving charge from higher to lower cells instead of burning it as heat.

## Notes

- Neutral definition: energy shuffling between cells ([[Document - Wikipedia Battery Management System (retrieved 2026-10-13)]], [[Document - MathWorks Battery Management System Overview (retrieved 2026-10-13)]]).
- The REC page describes the REC Active BMS as managing 4 series cells with 'active bi-directional balancing'; no balancing current is stated ([[Document - REC BMS Product Page (retrieved 2026-10-13)]]).
- No Requirement is linked (intentional gap).
