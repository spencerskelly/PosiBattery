# Banner Traction Bull Filling System

## Definition

Optional filling system for Banner Traction Bull wet batteries that automatically replaces water lost by electrolysis.

## Notes

- **Working name given by the analyst; Banner's pages give no product name.** Page wording: filling system for "quick, safe and clean refilling". Source: Banner Traction Bull page (T1, second-hand), <https://www.bannerbatterien.com/en/Products/Industrial-Batteries/Traction-Bull/244-Wet>
- The DIN cells folder lists "water refill system" as an option; whether it is the same system is assumed, not shown (IB-185). Float valves, hose, indicators and supply method are not described.
