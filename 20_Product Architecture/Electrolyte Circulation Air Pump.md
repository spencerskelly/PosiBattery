# Electrolyte Circulation Air Pump

## Definition

Air pump that supplies low-pressure airflow to flooded-battery cells for electrolyte circulation during charging.

## Notes

- HAWKER explicitly identifies an air pump in its electrolyte-circulation option.
- Midac explicitly identifies a charger-mounted air pump feeding in-cell tubing.
- For other products, pump presence is not asserted unless supported by evidence or an explicit engineering-confidence assumption.
- Pump technology, voltage, flow, pressure, noise, service life, and control method are not specified by the currently modeled sources.
