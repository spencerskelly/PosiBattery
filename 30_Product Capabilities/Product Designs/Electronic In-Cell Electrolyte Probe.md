# Electronic In-Cell Electrolyte Probe

## Definition

Electrolyte-level sensing using an electronic probe inserted into a battery cell, with the exact electrical sensing principle left unspecified when the manufacturer does not publish it.

## Notes

- Philadelphia Scientific explicitly describes [[Philadelphia Scientific SmartBlinky Pro]] as an electronic probe that senses electrolyte in a cell.
- The manufacturer calls the sensing method patented Smart Sensing Technology but does not identify it as conductive, capacitive, optical, or another physical principle in the retrieved public material.
- This Design therefore captures the verified physical arrangement without converting "electronic probe" into an unsupported sensing-technology claim.
