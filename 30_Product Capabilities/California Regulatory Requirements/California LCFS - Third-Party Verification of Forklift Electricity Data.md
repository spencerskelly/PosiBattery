# California LCFS - Third-Party Verification of Forklift Electricity Data

## Definition

Forklift electricity data reported for LCFS shall be third-party verified starting with the 2026 data year, with verification statements due 31 August (or the next business day) from 2027.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It binds the party named in the definition, not a charger. Product consequences are the derived requirements REQ-00051 to REQ-00053 (analyst-proposed).
- Second-hand reading; see C-items in [[Battery Product Landscape Conflicts and Open Questions]] and [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]].
- **Status:** applies to parties who claim credits. Records to keep include meter serial numbers and rental agreements.
- What a verifier needs beyond that was not found (IB-247).
