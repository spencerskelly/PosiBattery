# Document - OSHA Summaries of Selected Forklift Fatalities (retrieved 2026-10-14)

## Definition

OSHA library page summarizing selected forklift fatalities investigated in 1997 and 1998.

## Notes

Government safety agency case summaries; they show the hazard exists, not how often or how much a feature reduces it.

- The page gives case summaries only, with no totals or rates (read through the fetch tool's summary, second-hand).
- Tip-overs on turns, ramps and uneven ground; one 1998 unloaded forklift tipped on a 13 percent ramp slope and the operator, trying to jump clear, was killed by the overhead guard.
- Seat belts absent or unused in several cases, including the 1998 ramp tip-over ("not wearing the supplied seatbelt") and a 1997 case where the head was caught under the overhead cage.
- Pedestrians struck by forklifts whose loads blocked the driver's view (two 1998 cases).
- Loads falling or workers falling from pallets on the forks (1997 and 1998 cases).
