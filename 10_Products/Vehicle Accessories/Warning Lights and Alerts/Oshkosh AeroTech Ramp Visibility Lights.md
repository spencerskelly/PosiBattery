# Oshkosh AeroTech Ramp Visibility Lights

## Definition

Customer Care kit items to make a loader easier to see and operate on the ramp: operator cab beacon, aircraft position light and cab work light, with LED lighting life extended to 10 years.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Oshkosh AeroTech Customer Care lighting upgrades that make cargo loaders easier to see and operate on the ramp.

**Marketed features:**
- HID operator lighting kits for C15i/C30i variants
- LED operator cab beacon (P/N 628-5729), aircraft position light (628-5733) and cab work light (628-5734)
- LED turn/side markers, taillights and platform work lights
- Kits include hardware and wiring
