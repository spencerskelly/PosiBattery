# BSL Lithium Forklift Battery

## Definition

BSL lithium (LiFePO4) batteries sold by Windsor Materials Handling for retrofit to any make of forklift, with own BMS and remote monitoring.

## Notes

- **Update 2026-10-17 (customer traceability review):** hasDesign [[Integrated Battery Management System]] because the dealer item says the batteries have their own BMS (T2). Remote monitoring is stated but its channel is not, so no communication Function is linked.
- Working name analyst-given (`unnamed-in-source`): the item names no model. Up to 5 h runtime; about 10% in 20 min, full in 2 h (vendor figures). Source: <https://www.mhwmag.com/products/uk-forklift-battery-dealer-touts-bsl-battery/> (T2).
- Voltages, capacities, warranty and compatible trucks are not stated (open, IB-197).
