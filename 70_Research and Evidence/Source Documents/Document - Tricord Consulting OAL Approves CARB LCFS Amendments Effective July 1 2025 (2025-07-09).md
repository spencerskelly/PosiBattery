# Document - Tricord Consulting OAL Approves CARB LCFS Amendments Effective July 1 2025 (2025-07-09)

## Definition

A consultancy's report that California's amended LCFS took effect on 2025-07-01 with a diesel benchmark of 81.70.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. Consultancy article of 2025-07-09.

- **Adoption:** the Office of Administrative Law announced approval on 2025-06-27; the amendments took effect 2025-07-01; Q1 and Q2 2025 follow the earlier regulation and Q3 2025 onward the amended one.
- **Benchmarks:** gasoline 85.77 to 76.60 gCO2e/MJ; diesel 86.64 to 81.70.
- **Other:** automatic acceleration mechanism from 2027-05-15; CA-GREET4.0 for new pathways; a 20 percent cap on soybean, sunflower and canola renewable diesel credits from 2028.
- **Not stated:** electric forklifts, EER, metering, FSE registration.
- **Interest:** the author sells consulting services.
