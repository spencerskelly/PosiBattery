# Panacea Aftermarket Co.

## Definition

Aftermarket forklift safety supplier of a camera DVR with impact sensors, a fingerprint starter and a UL-listed blue light.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Accessory Maker.
- DC Velocity says Panacea's Cam-DVR has four cameras and integrated g-force sensors, the Smart Start is a fingerprint starter, and the UL-listed blue light has aluminum construction, Cree LEDs and an IP68 rating. Source: DC Velocity (T2 (dated)), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.dcvelocity.com/articles/28818-spotlight-on-forklift-safety-products>
