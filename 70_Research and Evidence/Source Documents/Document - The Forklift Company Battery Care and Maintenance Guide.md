# Document - The Forklift Company Battery Care and Maintenance Guide

## Definition

Care and maintenance guide for lead-acid motive-power batteries from a UK forklift service company (Atherstone, Warwickshire).

## Provenance

- **Local artifact:** `Battery-Care-Maintenance.pdf` in `Reference Files`; image-only PDF read by OCR on 2026-10-13.
- **Author or publisher:** The Forklift Company (UK service company; the guide prints a service-office address and email, not a web address). Reseller or service company, not the battery maker.
- **Original web address:** not captured in the saved copy.
- **Evidence tier:** T4 (see [[Landscape Evidence and Modeling Conventions]]).

## Notes

- A battery cycle is one full discharge and recharge at 80% depth of discharge, about 5 h of continuous use, roughly one 8 h shift. Example given: a 500 Ah battery should be discharged to 80%, so 400 Ah is usable.
- Do not over-discharge; shallow discharge wastes capacity per cycle; opportunity charging (at breaks and lunch) should be avoided.
- Charging is recommended after the battery is discharged to 80% as shown by the battery discharge indicator; do not interrupt a charge cycle.
- Continuous use above 38 degrees reduces battery life (unit not printed; read as Celsius). Over-topping loses electrolyte and lowers acid density.
- Topping up: only when fully charged, only with de-mineralised or deionised water, never acid. Battery fill system (Autofil BFS): topping after charge, about once a week, white-eye level indicator; options are a pump cart, a gravity-feed bottle, or charger-controlled automatic topping near end of charge with a solenoid valve and a water supply on a timer 2 days a week.
- Electrolyte described as dilute sulphuric acid or potassium hydroxide. Weekly and daily check lists, charger monthly checks, spill and chemical-safety procedures.
