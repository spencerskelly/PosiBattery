# Toyota Traigo48

## Definition

Toyota Europe electric counterbalance forklift range in three- and four-wheel versions, 1.5 to 2.0 t, with lead-acid or lithium-ion batteries and an integrated-battery Traigo_i model.

## Notes

- Toyota Material Handling Europe says the Traigo48 range covers 1.5 to 2.0 t, lift heights up to 7.5 m and up to 20 km/h, with lead-acid or lithium-ion batteries, a side panel door for quick lead-acid battery changes, and the Traigo_i model with an integrated lithium-ion battery and charging port in the counterweight. Source: AviationPros product item (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.aviationpros.com/ground-support-worldwide/ground-handling/product/55274834/new-toyota-traigo48-electric-forklift-range>
