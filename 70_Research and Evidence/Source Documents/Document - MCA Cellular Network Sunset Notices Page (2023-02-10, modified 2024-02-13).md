# Document - MCA Cellular Network Sunset Notices Page (2023-02-10, modified 2024-02-13)

## Definition

Vendor page listing past and announced US cellular network shutdown dates; the only source for the backlog's "AT&T has committed to LTE only through the end of 2027".

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- US carriers only; no Canada, Europe, LTE-M, NB-IoT or 5G dates.
- Table: AT&T 2G 2016-12-31; AT&T 3G 2021-03-01; **AT&T LTE 2027-12-31** ("AT&T has committed to operating its LTE network through the end of 2027"); Verizon 2G 2019-12-31 and 3G 2022-12-31; T-Mobile 2G partial 2012 and full 2024-04-02, 3G 2022-07-01; Sprint 3G 2022-03-31 and LTE 2022-06-30.
- The page gives no source for the AT&T LTE line. Other sources read disagree with it (C328) and with its AT&T 3G date (C330).
- The publisher (Mobile Communications America) sells connectivity; the 2023 date and 2024 modification make this at least two years old.
