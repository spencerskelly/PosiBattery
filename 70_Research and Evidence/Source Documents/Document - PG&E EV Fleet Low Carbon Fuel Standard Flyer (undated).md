# Document - PG&E EV Fleet Low Carbon Fuel Standard Flyer (undated)

## Definition

A utility's flyer giving a worked example of LCFS credits for one electric forklift.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the business case. Flyer from Pacific Gas and Electric (an electric utility; the earlier summary wrongly named it Portland General Electric) on LCFS credits for fleets. Undated; it references 2021 trades and a 2022 average value.

- **Forklift example:** "Forklift + 2 hrs daily operation = 17 metric tons CO2 reduced x $80 per MT = $1,360", for a 5,000 lb capacity truck, 50 weeks at 5 days a week.
- **Transport refrigeration example:** 40,150 kWh a year = 33 metric tons reduced x $80 = $2,640 (about $0.07 per kWh by the analyst's division, not stated).
- **Price assumption:** $80 per metric ton under a heading about the 2022 annual average; the flyer says credit value fluctuates with supply and demand.
- **Who earns:** the charging-infrastructure owner may generate credits; the utility helps fleets set up a dedicated meter. EER values and the calculation method are not stated.
- **Conflict:** the forklift example's credit volume cannot be reconciled with current California factors without a stated kWh per year (C297).
