# Get Charging Areas Approved by the Fire Authority and Insurer

## Definition

The site wants its forklift charging area accepted by the local fire authority and its insurer, so it can operate and keep cover without rework or delay.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: analyst hypothesis, drafted 2026-10-17.** No customer statement was seen. The need is inferred from code text and one insurer's sheet; see [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]].
- Not evidenced: how often approval blocks or delays a lithium charging area, who bears the work, and whether the charger is ever the deciding item (IB-279).
- Realization link is an analyst hypothesis: an external interlock input (REQ-00076) is the only charger-side feature the sources suggest; other needs on this topic are site-side.
- Related existing need that this challenges: [[Charge Without a Ventilated Battery Room]] (C318).
