# Document - Li et al. Data-driven health estimation and lifetime prediction of lithium-ion batteries A review (RSER 2019)

## Definition

Peer-reviewed version (Queen's University Belfast portal) of a 2019 Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews review of data-driven SOH and lifetime methods (a second identical copy named Estimation (1).pdf is also in Downloads).

## Provenance

- **Source address:** https://pureadmin.qub.ac.uk/ws/files/180469370/Estimation.pdf
- **Repo file:** Downloads/Estimation.pdf (61 pages), added by the owner on 2026-10-18. Text was extracted from the file and the passages below were read; this is a first-hand read of the file, but not every section was read in full.
- **Evidence tier:** not covered by T1 to T5 in [[Landscape Evidence and Modeling Conventions]] (paper, standard or catalogue record).
- **Source key in the SOC and SOH notes:** S31.

## Notes

- Lithium-ion scope. Lead-acid appears in two cited items (a remaining-useful-life study for valve-regulated lead-acid, and a float-life reference).
- States that cells are replaced when capacity falls below 80 percent of initial (EV context), another 80 percent source for C349.
