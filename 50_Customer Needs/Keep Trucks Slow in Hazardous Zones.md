# Keep Trucks Slow in Hazardous Zones

## Definition

Customer need: Keep Trucks Slow in Hazardous Zones. The problem behind it: Some places (crossings, pedestrian areas, aisle ends) need lower truck speed than the rest of the site, whatever the operator does.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: need hypothesis.** The statements below are what makers and dealers say their products do for customers. No customer, user or buyer source in the vault confirms that customers hold this need or rank it. Per the ruleset, source research becomes a validated need only after customer-side evidence.
- **Problem solved (analyst wording):** Some places (crossings, pedestrian areas, aisle ends) need lower truck speed than the rest of the site, whatever the operator does.
- **Who has the problem:** [[Site Safety Manager]], [[Pedestrian Near Trucks]].
- **Operating segments (analyst crosswalk to [[PosiCharge Market Segments and Jobs-to-Be-Done]], hypothesis):** Material-handling fleets.
- **Realized by (specific functions; products link to these):** [[Limit Vehicle Motion by Location Zone]], [[Limit Truck Speed Automatically]].
- **Products reaching this need:** 30 by function, design or option (owner decision, round 40: design and option routes count as reached); 23 of those perform a realizing function. Largest families by function: Vehicle Accessories/Proximity and Object Detection (12), Vehicle Accessories/Operator Assist and Stability (5), Forklifts/Class I Electric Rider Trucks (4), Fleet Software and Platforms/Truck Telematics (1), Forklifts/Class II Electric Narrow Aisle Trucks (1). The metric route (hypothesis) adds 0 more. Per-product routes in [[Product to Customer Need Map]].
- **Vendor-stated evidence (by product):**
  - [[Linde Safety Guard Zone Marker]]: The brochure says the Zone Marker sets maximum travel speeds in defined zones, reduces material damage and noise and defuses accident hot spots; the KION catalog says it defines speed-restriction areas. Source: Linde Rhein-Ruhr safety brochure and KION North America catalog 2023 (T3/T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.lmh-rr.de/media/Linde-Rhein-Ruhr/02_PDF/Safety/broschuere_safety.pdf>
  - [[Raymond iWAREHOUSE Real-Time Location System]]: [[Limit Vehicle Motion by Location Zone]] (V): <https://www.dcvelocity.com/material-handling/raymond-showcases-products-that-better-connect-operator-and-forklift-truck>
- **Independent evidence that the problem exists (not that the product is wanted):**
  - BLS fact sheet 'Occupational Injuries, Illnesses, and Fatalities Involving Forklifts' (June 2019, 2017 data, dated) (G, US government statistics): 614 workers died in forklift-related incidents from 2011 to 2017; of 74 fatal forklift injuries in 2017, 9 were pedestrian vehicular incidents; of 9,050 nonfatal cases with days away from work in 2017, 1,850 involved pedestrians while the forklift was in transportation use, and those pedestrian cases had the highest median days away (20). Retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.bls.gov/iif/factsheets/fatal-occupational-injuries-forklifts-2017.htm>
  - Secondary pages repeat different fatality and injury figures; see conflicts C101 to C104 in [[Battery Product Landscape Conflicts and Open Questions]]. Only the government sources above are used here.
- **Gaps:** no customer-side source; each function in the list is realized by only the products that state it, so the product count is a lower bound; no Requirement is linked (the vault leaves requirements as an intentional gap).
