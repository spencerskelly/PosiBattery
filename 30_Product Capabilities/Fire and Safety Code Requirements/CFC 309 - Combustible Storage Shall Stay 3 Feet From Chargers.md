# CFC 309 - Combustible Storage Shall Stay 3 Feet From Chargers

## Definition

Under section 309.3 of the 2025 California Fire Code, combustible storage shall be kept at least 3 ft from battery chargers used with powered industrial trucks.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It binds the site layout, not the charger.
- Distances differ widely by source and purpose: this 3 ft is a clearance to storage; the insurer sheet asks 10 m from buildings and storage for lithium truck charging (REQ-00072). They are different rules from different kinds of sources, not a contradiction of one number (C321).
- Second-hand reading; see [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]].
