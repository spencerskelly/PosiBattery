# Exide Technologies

## Definition

Lead-acid battery maker whose GNB Industrial Power division supplies motive-power batteries and, through Aker Wade, chargers and battery monitoring.

## Notes

- **Role (working label, not a governed property):** battery maker and monitor seller.
- Exide announced it acquired Aker Wade Power Technologies, adding its chargers to the GNB Industrial Power battery portfolio; the combined chargers and battery monitoring solutions are to be developed and marketed under both the Aker Wade and GNB Industrial Power brands. Source: EE Power news report (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://eepower.com/news/exide-expands-motive-power-offering-with-aker-wade-acquisition/>
- Exide sells the Motion+ EasyMonitor battery monitoring system, and an earlier GNB PRO 2.0 brochure uses near-identical wording. Source: Exide EasyMonitor page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.exidegroup.com/en/product/easymonitor>
- **Not found:** private-label motive-power supply to a named truck OEM.
