# Logisnext Americas Product and Accessory Inventory

## Definition

Inventory of the products and accessories named for Mitsubishi Logisnext Americas (renamed Logisnext Americas in 2026), with the evidence for each and whether a vault note exists.

## Notes

- **Name:** the company was renamed Logisnext Americas (announced 2026-03-11, effective 2026-04-30) and the Mitsubishi Forklift Trucks line becomes Logisnext Forklifts; vault notes keep the old name (C245). Source: <https://www.mmh.com/article/mitsubishi_logisnext_americas_will_rebrand_as_logisnext_americas>
- **Completeness limit:** the official model pages do not load and dealer listings expose about nine models per read, so this is a series and category inventory, not a model-by-model list. Claimed totals: Mitsubishi 153 models, UniCarriers 117, Cat 168 (AllMachines, T3). Remaining work is IB-200.
- **Rule:** an item without a note is named only; it becomes a note when a source says what it is.
