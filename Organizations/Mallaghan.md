# Mallaghan

## Definition

Northern Irish ground support equipment maker whose SkyBelt is a fully electric belt loader with lithium-ion batteries and collision avoidance.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Truck OEM.
- AviationPros reports Mallaghan's SkyBelt is its first fully electric standard belt loader, with electric tri-action drive control, lithium-ion battery technology, a patented lifting mechanism, a collision avoidance system and auto-level, and sensor technology and telematics powered by Adveez. Source: Ground Support Worldwide (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.aviationpros.com/ground-support-worldwide/gse/article/55018081/mallaghan-expands-into-the-belt-loader-market>
