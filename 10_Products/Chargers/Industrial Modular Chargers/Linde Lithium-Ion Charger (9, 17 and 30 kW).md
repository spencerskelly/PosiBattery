# Linde Lithium-Ion Charger (9, 17 and 30 kW)

## Definition

Linde chargers in 9, 17 and 30 kW sizes for its 90 V lithium-ion batteries.

## Notes

- **Update 2026-10-14 (priority 5, unlinked-product review):** linked to [[Charge Battery]], [[Charge Lithium-Ion Battery]] because Linde says the chargers are for its 90 V lithium-ion batteries. Source: as cited on this note.
- Linde says the 90 V lithium-ion batteries are compatible with 9, 17 and 30 kW chargers and that the charger size depends on how quickly energy must be restored during opportunity charging. Source: Logistics Business and Warehouse News (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://warehousenews.co.uk/?p=62842>
