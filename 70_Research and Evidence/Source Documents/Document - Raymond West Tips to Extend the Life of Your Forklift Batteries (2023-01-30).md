# Document - Raymond West Tips to Extend the Life of Your Forklift Batteries (2023-01-30)

## Definition

Dealer blog with tips on watering, charging and cleaning forklift batteries.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Watering: never let electrolyte fall below the tops of the plates; if low, add distilled water just to cover plates before charging, then fill to the approved level after charging; automatic watering suggested (C340).
- Charging: a fully saturated charge of 14 to 16 hours once a week for conventional charging; an extended equalizing charge weekly for opportunity or fast charging (C338, C344).
- Cleaning: keep clean and dry, check terminals for corrosion, wipe with a damp cloth, brush grime.
- No cycle life, depth of discharge, temperature or equalize detail. Author not shown; the page never names the battery type (lead-acid is implied).
