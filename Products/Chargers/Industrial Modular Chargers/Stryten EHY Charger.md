# Stryten EHY Charger

## Definition

Stryten software-controlled heavy-duty charger family for conventional charging.

## Notes

- Stryten says the EHY family are software-controlled heavy-duty chargers for conventional charging, controlled by a central microprocessor with an alphanumeric display and keypad. Source: Stryten lineup sheet (09/2024) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/14502/133723547947804003.pdf>
- **Functions performed, with citations** (V = verified this pass):
  - [[Charge Battery Conventionally]] (V): <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/14502/133723547947804003.pdf>
- Stryten's EHY brochure (SE1057, 2/23) says the family is based on a high-efficiency, software-defined charge control architecture for flooded-cell batteries, controlled by a central microprocessor with an alphanumeric display and keypad that can query charge history logger (250 cycles), programmable real-time clock and calendar and audible alarms, with an optional profile for the M-Series T310 90-day watering interval. Source: Stryten EHY brochure (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.stryten.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Stryten_M-Series_ProductBrochure_EHY_SE1057_DIGITAL.pdf>
- Stryten's EHY brochure (SE1057, web copy read in round 19) lists 40 to 240 A output at nominal 24, 36, 48, 72 and 80 VDC; single-phase 208, 240 or 480 VAC and three-phase 208, 240, 480 or 600 VAC at 60 Hz; CEC-compliant models above 90 percent efficiency; power factor above 90 percent single-phase and above 97 percent three-phase; modified WSa profile with an optional T310 90-day watering interval profile, programmable weekly equalization and off-peak hours; forced ventilation, under 65 dBA at 1 m, IP21; -10 to +50 C operation; 250-cycle data logging; UL 1564 and CSA 22.2 107.2-01 with CEC marking; cabinets EHY1 19.9 x 14.1 x 27.2 in and EHY2 19.9 x 17.4 x 35.5 in. Source: Stryten EHY brochure SE1057 (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.stryten.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Stryten_M-Series_ProductBrochure_EHY_SE1057_DIGITAL.pdf>
