# Toyota 7FBCU35-55 Electric Pneumatic Forklifts

## Definition

Toyota 8,000 to 12,000 lb electric pneumatic-tire counterbalanced forklifts (7FBCU35, 45, 55), 36 V or 48 V.

## Notes

- **Battery integration and charging interface (2026-10-14):** optional battery slide-out; standard gray SB 350 connector; lithium option and charge inlet not stated. Source: <https://www.summithandling.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/2019_Large-Electric_Spec-Sheet_Digital.pdf> (T2).
- **Design characteristics, with citations:**
  - [[Drop-In Battery Cavity]] (V, optional slide-out): <https://www.summithandling.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/2019_Large-Electric_Spec-Sheet_Digital.pdf>
- The 8-series 4,000-5,000 lb trucks offer an optional compact 27 in compartment at 36 or 48 V (change method not stated). Source: <https://www.materialhandling247.com/product/compact_battery_compartment_option_for_8_series/Lift_Trucks> (T3).
