# Document - CARB ZEF Brochure for State and Local Government Fleets (2026-04)

## Definition

CARB's brochure for state and local government fleets on the Zero-Emission Forklift regulation: scope, model-year rules, phase-out window, reporting dates and labels.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic; this is the CARB brochure the Knowledge Base Backlog (2026-10-04) cited for the 2028 to 2038 phase-out. It is written for state and local government fleets only.

- **Status:** the regulation took effect for state and local government fleets on 2026-01-01. The brochure does not mention EPA authorization or enforcement pauses.
- **Covered forklifts:** Class IV LSI of all capacities and Class V LSI of 12,000 pounds or less (propane, natural gas or gasoline).
- **Phase-out:** runs from 2028 to 2038 by model year and class, oldest first; large fleets (26 or more forklifts) start in 2028; small fleets (25 or fewer) may use a slower schedule that starts in 2029. The full timelines are in a QR-linked resource that was not read; the dated table is in [[Document - CARB ZEF Government Fleets Overview Fact Sheet (2026-01)]].
- **Reporting:** after an initial report, annual reporting is due 30 April (large fleets) or 30 September (small fleets); a free online tool called DOORS is used for reporting.
- **Exemptions and extensions:** low-use, dedicated emergency use and in-field forklifts are listed as exemptions; extensions cover delays outside the operator's control and need a CARB application. Forklifts under an exemption or extension need an Equipment Identification Number label within 30 calendar days of receiving the number.
- **Gaps:** the item about 2026 to 2028 model-year Class V forklifts rented from a rental agency is garbled in the summary; confirm it in the regulation text.
- **Correction to the 2026-10-04 backlog wording:** the 2028 to 2038 phase-out described here applies to state and local government fleets, not to private fleets (C285).
