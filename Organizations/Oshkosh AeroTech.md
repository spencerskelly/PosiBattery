# Oshkosh AeroTech

## Definition

Maker of B-series baggage tractors and pushbacks, Commander and Ranger cargo loaders and other ground support equipment, listed by a distributor as Oshkosh AeroTech (JBT).

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Truck OEM.
- A GSE distributor lists Oshkosh AeroTech (JBT) products including the Tempest deicer, B250, B350, B350E, B650, B650E and B950 pushbacks, the B80 gasoline and B80E electric baggage tractors, the Commander cargo loader and the Ranger cargo loader with a Ranger 15E lithium-ion version. Source: Fortbrand GSE product page (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://fortbrand.com/gse-products/>
- **Open (C71):** how JBT AeroTech and Oshkosh AeroTech are related is not stated in the retrieved text; EPRI lists the electric maker as JBT.
