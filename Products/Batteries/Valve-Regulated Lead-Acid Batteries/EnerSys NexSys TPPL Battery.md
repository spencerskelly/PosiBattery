# EnerSys NexSys TPPL Battery

## Definition

EnerSys thin plate pure lead battery line, described as virtually maintenance-free.

## Notes

- EnerSys says NexSys TPPL is a virtually maintenance-free technology that Hyster-Yale approved for integration with Hyster Power Cellect and Yale Power Key. Source: EnerSys release (2023-07-19) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.enersys.com/de/about-us/news/fleet-managers-get-powerful-flexibility-combining-enersys-technology-breadth-with-yale-power-key-and-hyster-power-cellect/>
