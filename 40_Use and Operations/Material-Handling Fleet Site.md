# Material-Handling Fleet Site

## Definition

Operating context for industrial trucks, batteries, chargers, operators, maintenance activity and shared work areas in warehouses, distribution centers or manufacturing sites.

## Notes

- Use this context when an operational scenario occurs in a material-handling fleet rather than at an airport or another vehicle domain.
- Typical surrounding equipment includes industrial trucks, [[Industrial Traction Battery|traction batteries]], [[Industrial Battery Charger|industrial battery chargers]], battery-monitoring devices, fleet software, pedestrians, racks, loads and facility infrastructure.
- Derived from the material-handling fleet segment in [[PosiCharge Market Segments and Jobs-to-Be-Done]].
