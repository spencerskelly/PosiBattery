# Crown Battery Manufacturing

## Definition

US battery company, listed among forklift battery makers, whose site shows an EVOLUTION charger series with the same text as the Power Designers REVOLUTION series.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Battery Maker, Brand Owner.
- Crown Battery's Power Line page describes an EVOLUTION Series of modular chargers with the same specification table as the REVOLUTION Series and says combined with the PowerTrac it becomes multi-voltage (24/36/48), and refers to a PowerHouse EV. Source: Crown Battery power line page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://crownbattery.com/lp-power-line>
- A supplier list describes Crown Battery as a maker of lithium-ion, deep cycle, starter and lead-acid batteries along with chargers. Source: Foxtron Power Solutions list (secondary, dated) (T4), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.foxtronpowersolutions.com/forklift-battery-manufacturers/>
- **Name caution (C27):** unrelated to Crown Equipment as far as retrieved sources show; no link is established either way.
