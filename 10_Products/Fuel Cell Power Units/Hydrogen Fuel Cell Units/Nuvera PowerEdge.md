# Nuvera PowerEdge

## Definition

Nuvera hybrid fuel cell power unit (C Series for Class I, R Series for Class II) with sealed batteries, compressed hydrogen storage and computerized controls.

## Notes

- Nuvera's PowerEdge replaces lead-acid batteries, includes the needed counterweight, requires no truck modification or derating, is refueled in 2 minutes or less, provides constant voltage, and has automated controls and remote monitoring for fleet management.
