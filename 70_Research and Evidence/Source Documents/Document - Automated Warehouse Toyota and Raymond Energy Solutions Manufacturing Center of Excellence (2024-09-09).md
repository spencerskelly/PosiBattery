# Document - Automated Warehouse Toyota and Raymond Energy Solutions Manufacturing Center of Excellence (2024-09-09)

## Definition

Trade-press report of a Toyota Material Handling and Raymond battery manufacturing facility in Broome County, New York, which makes lithium-ion and thin-plate pure-lead batteries for both brands.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Facility: "Energy Solutions Manufacturing Center of Excellence"; makes lithium-ion batteries and thin-plate pure-lead battery technology for Toyota and Raymond products; no outsourcing is described.
- Not stated: chargers, volumes, investment, jobs, cell source, opening date beyond the announcement.
- Company-wide figures quoted: nearly 2.5 million sq ft of manufacturing space in six locations; more than 1,500 forklifts built per week.
- Contradicts the vault's gaps "Not found: who makes Toyota's lithium-ion batteries" ([[Toyota Material Handling]]) and "maker not stated" for Raymond Energy Essentials ([[Industrial Battery Supply and Private-Label Relationships]]); see C324. Whether the packs sold today under the Raymond and Toyota brand come from this plant is not stated.
