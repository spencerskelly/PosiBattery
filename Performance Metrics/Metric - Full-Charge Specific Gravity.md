# Metric - Full-Charge Specific Gravity

## Definition

Full-Charge Specific Gravity: kg/l or g/cc at a stated temperature

## Notes

- **Code and class:** BM16; battery metric. Unit or format: kg/l or g/cc at a stated temperature.
- **Comparability rule:** Specific gravity depends on the electrolyte design and the reference temperature (25 C or 30 C); compare only like designs.
- **Direction:** not ranked.
- **Values on file (as stated in each product note; n/s means not stated):**
  - [[HAWKER Perfect Plus Battery]]: 1.29 kg/l nominal at 30 C (reached within the first 10 cycles); correction -0.0007 kg/l per C
  - [[Deka D-Series Battery]]: 1.280 to 1.295 temperature-corrected to 77 F (25 C)
  - [[Deka MaxPowr Battery]]: 1.310 to 1.330 temperature-corrected (reference temperature cut off in the retrieved text)
- **Source rule:** the product note holds the source URL for each value; this note copies the value for comparison. If they differ, the product note wins and this note is fixed.
- **Gaps and to-do:** Exide Industries gives 1.275 to 1.285 g/cc at 30 C (snippet, no product note).
