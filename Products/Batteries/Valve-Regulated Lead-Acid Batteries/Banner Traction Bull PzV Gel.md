# Banner Traction Bull PzV Gel

## Definition

Banner Traction Bull PzV/PzVB maintenance-free gel traction battery.

## Notes

- Banner's brochure describes the Traction Bull PzV/PzVB as a maintenance-free gel technology drive battery; the Bloc DB is a sealed valve-regulated gel battery and the Bloc range also offers AGM Deep Cycle.
