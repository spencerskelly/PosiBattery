# Document - Anderson IBC 320 Data Sheet (DS-IBC320 Rev 1)

## Definition

Anderson IBC 320 data sheet (DS-IBC320 Rev 1).

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- Covers only the IBC 320 (no IBC 80 or 160 products listed). Housings: plug, receptacle, panel-mount receptacle; cartridges Air Tube, 4 Aux, 10 Aux with a supplemental holder for receptacle use. Seven coding pins: grey wet, green dry, yellow universal, blue male/female (Type II dry), red male/female (Type II wet). Handles: latching (plug only) and non-latching (plug and receptacle). IP68 seal kits for 4 Aux and 10 Aux, grommet sold separately. NTC sensor upgrade kits for 4 Aux, Air Tube and 10 Aux. Cable diameter kits for power wire about 11.5-18.0 mm and signal cable 8.5-10.5 mm. Modular tool-less contact cartridges.
