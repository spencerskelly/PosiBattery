# Crown Battery EVOLUTION Series

## Definition

Crown Battery charger series whose page repeats the Power Designers REVOLUTION text under the EVOLUTION name.

## Notes

- Crown Battery's Power Line page gives the EVOLUTION Series the same wording and specification table as REVOLUTION (3.9 to 31.2 kW, 480 V AC three-phase) and refers to the PowerTrac and a PowerHouse EV. Source: Crown Battery power line page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://crownbattery.com/lp-power-line>
- **Inference (C33):** identical text suggests a rebrand of REVOLUTION. No source states the supply relationship.
