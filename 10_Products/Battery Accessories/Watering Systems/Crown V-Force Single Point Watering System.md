# Crown V-Force Single Point Watering System

## Definition

Crown watering systems and kits for lead-acid batteries: single point watering, float system kits, direct fill tanks, carts, deionizers, testers, guns and spider injection.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Crown single-point watering system for lift-truck batteries, part of Crown's battery watering range.

**Marketed features:**
- Waters lift-truck batteries safely
- Less time and labor than manual watering
- Range also lists float kits, direct-fill tanks, carts, deionizers, testers and guns (per existing note)
