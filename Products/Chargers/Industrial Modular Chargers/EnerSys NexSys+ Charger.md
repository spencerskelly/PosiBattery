# EnerSys NexSys+ Charger

## Definition

EnerSys programmable modular high-frequency charger for mixed lead and lithium fleets that works with the Wi-iQ monitor.

## Notes

- The NexSys+ guide says the chargers are programmable with profiles for NexSys TPPL and other EnerSys technologies, are Wi-iQ enabled for battery type and voltage, compensate for temperature automatically when a Wi-iQ is present, and use high-frequency modular technology. Source: EnerSys IMPAQ and NexSys+ guide (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.enersys.com/490f6e/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/nexsys/modular-charger/amer/impaq-nexsys-plus-modular-charger-product-guide-1020.pdf>
- Product guides describe NexSys+ chargers as suited to mixed-fleet battery management with an outdoor option; the APAC guide says outdoor charging needs the optional NexSys+ Outdoor model with an IP-54 enclosure. Source: EnerSys EMEA and APAC motive power guides (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://enersys.com/496812/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/_enersys/apac/apac-en-pg-0423.pdf>
