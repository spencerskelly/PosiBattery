# Company Financial History Register

## Definition

Multi-year public financial series and company statements on market position, collected 2026-10-18 from public filings and aggregators. Extends [[Public Company Financials Register]] (latest year only) with 2010 to 2025 history and adds what EnerSys, Hyster-Yale and Electrovaya say about shares, competitors and mix. **All data in the vault's market work comes from public sources; no internal PosiCharge or Ampure data is used.**

## Notes

**Source quality.** Filings and company releases are T1 ([[Document - EnerSys Form 10-K for Fiscal 2026 (Business section)|ens26]], [[Document - EnerSys Motive Power Update investor presentation (2023-12-18)|ensmp]], [[Document - EnerSys Fourth Quarter and Full Year Fiscal 2020 earnings release (Form 8-K exhibit 99.1)|ens20]], [[Document - Hyster-Yale Form 10-K for Fiscal 2025 (Business section)|hy26]], [[Document - Electrovaya Reports Fiscal Year 2025 Results (2025-12)|elv]]). The multi-year revenue series for KION, Jungheinrich and Hyster-Yale come from aggregators (T3: [[Document - KION Group revenue and net income by year (roic.ai, 2010 to 2025)|kgr]], [[Document - Jungheinrich revenue and net income by year (roic.ai, 2010 to 2025)|jgr]], [[Document - Hyster-Yale revenue by year (macrotrends, 2010 to 2023)|mtr]]) and print no currency or basis; the 2025 values match the companies' own releases, and Hyster-Yale 2023 and 2024 match the Modern Materials Handling list, which supports but does not prove the rest. All were read through a summarizing fetch tool.

**What this shows.** Hyster-Yale (5.0% a year) and EnerSys (5.7%) grew more slowly than world truck orders (7.6% a year, 2010 to 2025, derived); KION (8.1%) and Jungheinrich (7.7%) grew about as fast. KION's growth includes acquisitions, so the companies are not like for like. KION fell 5.3% in 2020; Jungheinrich 6.5%; Hyster-Yale 14.6% (all derived), while world truck orders rose 8.7% in 2020 (derived). Hyster-Yale revenue fell 12.5% in 2025 (derived: 3,769.3 against 4,308.2) while world orders rose 10.2% (derived). Revenue is not tracking units, which fits the mix explanation in [[Market Size and Volume Register]] (W7): more low-priced warehouse trucks in volume.

Product-line (Motive Power) sales for FY2011 to FY2020 are still not found: the fetch tool stops before the 10-K segment note.

Named competitors in motive power: East Penn Manufacturing, Exide Technologies (Stryten), Hoppecke, Eternity, Midac, Sunlight and TAB, plus a number of Chinese manufacturers ([[Document - EnerSys Form 10-K for Fiscal 2026 (Business section)|ens26]]). The company supplies no share for any of them.

### 4. Electrovaya (lithium for material handling)

Revenue USD 63.8M in FY2025 against 44.6M, a 43% rise (derived); gross margin 30.8%; net profit USD 3.3M; backlog about USD 100 to 125M ([[Document - Electrovaya Reports Fiscal Year 2025 Results (2025-12)|elv]]). It is one of two public lithium material-handling battery makers found, with [[Flux Power]] (FY2026 revenue USD 42.1M, down 37%, [[Public Company Financials Register]]). The two moved in opposite directions in their latest fiscal years.
