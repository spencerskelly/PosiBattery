# Washington CFS - Credit Designation Contracts From 2026 Use Prescribed Waiver Language

## Definition

An eligible credit generator that designates another entity by written contract shall, for contracts made on or after 2026-01-01, use the waiver sentence prescribed in WAC 173-424-220(3)(c), provide its electricity data to the designated entity, and the designated entity shall report total credit revenue for the previous year to the first fuel reporting entity.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** Customers who hand credits to an aggregator or vendor in Washington (the case the New Mexico brief also describes) sign such contracts.
- **Read first-hand:** [[Document - WAC 173-424-220 Washington Legislature Certified Copy (2025-11-12)]], subsections 3(a) to 3(d). The prescribed sentence waives credit generation under the CFS, allocates credits to the designated entity, and has the first entity agree to provide electricity data.
- Contrast: California allows a designation contract with similar content for forklifts, but no prescribed sentence was found ([[Document - CARB LCFS Second 15-Day Changes Attachment A-1 Proposed Regulation Order (after 2024-08-12)]], proposed text, C283).
- Product use: a data layer that exports electricity data to a designee supports the data-provision clause; this is analyst reasoning, no source says customers use it.
