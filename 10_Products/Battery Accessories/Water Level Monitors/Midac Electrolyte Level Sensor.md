# Midac Electrolyte Level Sensor

## Definition

Optional or standard electrolyte level sensor on Midac flooded batteries.

## Notes

- **Working name given by the analyst; the catalogue says "electrolyte level sensor".** Optional on MDL-MBS and MDLblock, standard on MLM, MLMx and MHE. [[Sense Electrolyte Level]] (V).
