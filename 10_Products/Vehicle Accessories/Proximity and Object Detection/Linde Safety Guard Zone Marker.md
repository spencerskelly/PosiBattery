# Linde Safety Guard Zone Marker

## Definition

Safety Guard module that defines zones with a maximum travel speed.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Linde Safety Guard marker-point module that sets maximum driving speeds in defined zones.

**Marketed features:**
- Sets maximum driving speed in defined zones
- Reduces material damage and noise
- Mitigates accident hotspots
