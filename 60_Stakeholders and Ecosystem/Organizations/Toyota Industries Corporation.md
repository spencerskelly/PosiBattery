# Toyota Industries Corporation

## Definition

Parent of the Toyota and Raymond lift truck brands, first in the 2024 world lift truck ranking.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Truck OEM, Brand Owner.
- The 2024 lift truck supplier ranking puts Toyota Industries Corporation first, listing the Toyota and Raymond brands, headquartered in Kariya-shi, Aichi, Japan. Source: Modern Materials Handling ranking via Supply Chain 24/7 (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.supplychain247.com/article/top_20_lift_truck_suppliers_2024>
- Toyota Material Handling North America is described as bringing together the strengths of the Toyota and Raymond brands to offer integrated energy solutions. Source: TMHNA release on its lithium-ion battery series (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://themachinemaker.com/news/toyota-material-handling-introduces-advanced-lithium-ion-batteries-to-boost-efficiency-and-productivity/>
