# GS Yuasa

## Definition

Japanese battery maker whose traction range for forklifts, pallet trucks and cleaning machines was launched in European sizes in 2021.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Battery Maker.
- A trade report says GS Yuasa describes itself as a world leader in vehicle and industrial batteries with over 100 years of Japanese battery pedigree, and that its traction batteries are the preferred choice in the Asian material handling market. Source: Warehouse News and Logistics Business (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisticsbusiness.com/?p=40376>
- **Region:** Japan (tag from the 'Japanese battery pedigree' wording; headquarters city not stated).
