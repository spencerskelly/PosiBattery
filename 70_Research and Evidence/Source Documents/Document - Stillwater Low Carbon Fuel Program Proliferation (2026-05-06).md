# Document - Stillwater Low Carbon Fuel Program Proliferation (2026-05-06)

## Definition

A trade page that classifies North American low-carbon fuel programs as active, enacted, in the 2026 legislative session or earlier-stage.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the North American programs survey. Trade-publication page dated 2026-05-06 (modified 2026-08-05 per metadata) that sorts jurisdictions by program status.

- **Active:** British Columbia (BC-LCFS), California (LCFS), Oregon (CFP), Washington (CFS), Canada federal (Clean Fuel Regulations), New Mexico (CTFP).
- **Enacted, awaiting implementation:** Hawaii.
- **Legislation active in the 2026 session:** Illinois, Massachusetts, Minnesota, New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, Vermont.
- **Previously proposed, considering introduction or in a working group:** Colorado, Michigan. Mexico is not listed.
- **Not stated:** dates, whether any bill passed, and whether any program covers electricity, off-road equipment or forklifts. The text says eight state legislatures but lists seven for 2026 and does not explain the difference. Only British Columbia is listed among Canadian provinces.
