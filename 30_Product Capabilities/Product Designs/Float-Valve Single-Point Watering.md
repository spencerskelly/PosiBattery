# Float-Valve Single-Point Watering

## Definition

Single-point battery watering using cell-level automatic shutoff valves connected by common tubing to a single external water connection.

## Notes

- [[Philadelphia Scientific Stealth Watering System]] explicitly uses float valves and common tubing.
- [[Flow-Rite Maverick Battery Watering System]] explicitly uses automatic shut-off valves and tubing replacing the vent caps.
- Crown explicitly markets float-system kits within its V-Force watering range.
- HAWKER Aquamatic is allocated at the method level because its manual identifies automatic water refilling and operating pressure but does not expose the valve construction in the current source.
