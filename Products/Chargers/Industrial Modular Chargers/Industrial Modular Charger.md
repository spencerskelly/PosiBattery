# Industrial Modular Charger

## Definition

High-frequency industrial chargers for forklifts and similar trucks, usually modular and multi-voltage (the default charger category).

## Notes

- Category note for navigation: the product folder for this category is `Products/Chargers/Industrial Modular Chargers` .
- Owners are not part of the category; who offers a product is a relationship on the product note.
