# Document - CARB LCFS Electric Forklift Charging Claimed by EDU FAQ (2021-09-20)

## Definition

CARB's 2021 FAQ on how electric utilities claim LCFS credits for forklift charging electricity that no one else has reported.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic. CARB explanation of how a utility earns LCFS credits for forklift electricity; posted 2021-05-28 and updated 2021-09-20. It is a non-binding explanation; the regulation governs. Whether the method still applies after the amendments effective 2025-07-01 is not shown (C288).

- **Who generates:** the electrical distribution utility (EDU) can earn credits for electricity supplied to electric forklifts in its territory when no other eligible entity has claimed it; the fleet operator is not the generator under this method.
- **How:** each quarter CARB estimates total forklift electricity use in the EDU's territory and subtracts the electricity other reporting entities reported for forklift fueling there; the difference is the unclaimed electricity that becomes the credit basis. Credits are issued after the quarterly deadline and are exempt from quarterly reporting deadlines. From Q2 2021 CARB applies a four-quarter average of the forklift vehicle application types (post-2010 and pre-2011).
- **Inputs:** a statewide estimate of the electric forklift population, a per-forklift annual usage estimate, the EDU's share of non-residential electricity consumption from a California Energy Commission dataset, and electricity reported by others. No numeric values were captured in the summary.
- **Not stated:** metering requirements, separate meters or charger-level data, and the energy economy ratio.
