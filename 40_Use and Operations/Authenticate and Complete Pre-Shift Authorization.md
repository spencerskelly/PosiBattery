# Authenticate and Complete Pre-Shift Authorization

## Definition

The operator identifies themselves and completes required pre-use checks so the vehicle may be authorized for operation.

## Notes

- Primary context: [[Material-Handling Fleet Site]].
- Typical sequence: authenticate the operator, complete the required checklist or inspection responses, then receive permission or denial to operate.
- The system's enforcement or lockout behavior remains a Function.
