# Anderson SBE320-SBX350 Charger-Truck Side Bracket Kit

## Definition

Anderson bracket and hardware kit (SKU 994G1) for the charger or truck side of the SBE320/SBX350 manual-release connector system.

- The page says it works with the battery side to ease mating and unmating; current rating, voltage and release mechanism are not on the page. Source: <https://www.andersonpower.com/product/sbe320-sbx350-manual-release-charger-truck-side/> (T1, second-hand).
