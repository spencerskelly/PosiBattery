# Start and Stop Charge from an External Control

## Definition

Let a person or site system start and stop a charge from outside the charger, such as a button or signal.

## Notes

- **Manual check (2026-10-14):** the DVS and MVS manual describes a STOP button on the front panel and no start button; charging starts when the battery is connected. It does not describe an external control input. The Function therefore has one performer, the Fronius range.
- Added 2026-10-14 from the owner's account that some sites require a start and stop button at the beginning and end of a charge ([[Drive Up, Plug In and Walk Away]], U). The owner says the practice is a habit left from old chargers that sparked on connection (see [[Avoid Arcing at the Charge Connection]]), kept although current chargers stop charging automatically. Owner (2026-10-14, U): only a small number of customers in production warehouses still require it, so it is a minor variant, not the general case.
- Fronius says its Selectiva 4.0 96 V and 120 V range offers 'external start/stop to increase user safety' (flyer, T1, second-hand); the flyer does not say what the control is, and does not mention sparking (C275).
- **Need (analyst link, hypothesis):** realizes [[Connect Chargers and Batteries Safely at the Site]].
- No Requirement is linked (intentional gap).
