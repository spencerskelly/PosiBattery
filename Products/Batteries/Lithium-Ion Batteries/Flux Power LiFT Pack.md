# Flux Power LiFT Pack

## Definition

Flux Power lithium packs for material handling, starting with a 24 V 250 Ah unit.

## Notes

- Flux introduced the LiFT Pack line with a 250 Ahe, 24 V first product, and its accessories include stand-alone battery management, stackable chargers, programming software and display systems. Source: Flux Power release (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://ir.fluxpower.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/78/flux-power-introduces-new-line-of-advanced-lithium-battery>
