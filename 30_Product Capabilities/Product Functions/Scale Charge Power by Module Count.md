# Scale Charge Power by Module Count

## Definition

Raise or lower total charge power by the number of installed power modules.

## Notes

- **Solution link added 2026-10-14 (reviewed, owner "y"):** depends on [[Modular Power Modules]] (parallel power modules so output scales). One performer, Delta MOOVbase; 12 other chargers carry the Design but no source ties them to this Function.
- Stated: 3-5 PM1350 modules give 1-6.75 kW; 1-10 PM3000 modules give 3-32 kW.
- Links to products are made only where a source states the behavior. No Requirement is linked yet.
