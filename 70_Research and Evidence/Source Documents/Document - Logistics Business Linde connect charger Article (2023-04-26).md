# Document - Logistics Business Linde connect charger Article (2023-04-26)

## Definition

Logistics Business article on the Linde connect:charger module (published 2023-04-26, modified 2025-07-25).

## Notes

Facts below were read through the fetch tool's summary of the page, not from the raw page; vendor statements are claims.

- Linde Material Handling connect:charger, part of its fleet management solution. A central hardware unit links up to 50 chargers and controls power demand by predefined rules; operators set maximum power levels, fixed charging time slots and priorities (for example a battery below 50 percent charges first).
- Dashboard shows when a truck is ready and each charger's energy use. Launch version is wired; a wireless version, dynamic charge management, a cloud application and integration into an energy management system are described as planned. Retrofit works if chargers have an integrated interface (2018 model year onward).
- Claims lower electricity cost with high availability; no savings figure. A second article with the same content (Logistics Matters, 2023-09-07, <https://www.logisticsmatters.co.uk/?p=1953>) was also read; both say the launch is timed for LogiMAT and neither gives its year (C266).
