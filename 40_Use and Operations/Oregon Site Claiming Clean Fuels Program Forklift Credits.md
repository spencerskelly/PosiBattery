# Oregon Site Claiming Clean Fuels Program Forklift Credits

## Definition

Operating context: a charging location in Oregon whose forklift owner or operator reports metered charging electricity to DEQ's Clean Fuels Program to earn credits. Participation is optional.

## Notes

- **Evidence:** only directly metered electric forklifts can generate credits since the Q4 2023 report ([[Document - Oregon DEQ Forklift Transition Guidance (2023)]], [[Document - Oregon DEQ Fuel Supply Equipment Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)]]). Eligibility of forklifts is also stated by [[Document - 3Degrees Revenue Streams from Electric Forklifts Under Clean Fuel Standards (2025-09-25)]] (T4).
- Participants are stand-ins as in [[California Site Claiming LCFS Forklift Electricity Credits]]; the compliance owner is not identified (IB-252).
- Register: [[Western State Clean Fuel Programs for Forklift Electricity Register]].
