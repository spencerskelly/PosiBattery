# Magnetic Current Measurement Assembly

## Definition

Battery-current measurement assembly using a magnetic sensor around or within the conductor path.

## Notes

- [[Hall-Effect Current Sensor]] is the explicit sensor component for the Hall-effect Design.
- The sensor may be integrated, aperture-style, busbar-mounted, or a complete module.
- Product-specific current range, conductor geometry, offset compensation, and output format remain open.
