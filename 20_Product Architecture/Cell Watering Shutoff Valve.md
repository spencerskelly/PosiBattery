# Cell Watering Shutoff Valve

## Definition

Cell-level automatic watering valve that stops water flow when the intended electrolyte fill level is reached.

## Notes

- Philadelphia Scientific Stealth explicitly uses float valves.
- Flow-Rite explicitly describes automatic shut-off valves in its motive-power watering systems.
- This reusable Object does not require a specific float geometry, seal material, vent-cap interface, or pressure rating.
