## Notes

**Content recorded:**
- Sponsored by a lithium battery maker, so treated as marketing. Lead-acid standard models USD 2,950 to 5,000 (the intro says USD 2,000 to 6,000); lithium-ion USD 6,000 to 25,000+ depending on voltage and capacity. No prices by forklift class.
- Lead-acid replacement every 3 to 5 years under heavy use; lithium 8 to 10+ years; multi-shift lead-acid sites buy 2 to 3 batteries per forklift. Lithium payback claimed 2 to 4 years (two or three shifts) and 4 to 6 years (single shift). Charge efficiency about 75% lead-acid, about 99% lithium (the article's own figures).
