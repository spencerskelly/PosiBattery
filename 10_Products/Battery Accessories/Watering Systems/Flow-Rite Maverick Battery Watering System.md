# Flow-Rite Maverick Battery Watering System

## Definition

Flow-Rite battery-installed single-point watering system built around Maverick watering valves for motive-power lead-acid batteries.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Flow-Rite single-point battery watering system built around Maverick valves for motive-power lead-acid batteries.

**Marketed features:**
- Single-point watering valves
- Lets batteries be watered in the equipment without swapping
- Saves time where equipment runs around the clock
- Standalone kit or bundled with Eagle Eye Essential or Elite sensors
