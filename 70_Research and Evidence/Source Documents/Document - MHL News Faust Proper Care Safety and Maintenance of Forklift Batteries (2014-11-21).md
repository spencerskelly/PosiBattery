# Document - MHL News Faust Proper Care Safety and Maintenance of Forklift Batteries (2014-11-21)

## Definition

Maker-authored trade article listing daily, weekly and monthly care tasks for lead-acid forklift batteries.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Author Brian Faust, general manager of Douglas Battery, an EnerSys brand; no one else is quoted.
- Daily: connect to an approved charger, open cover or hood to ventilate, switch the charger off before disconnecting. Weekly: check water in every cell and fill to about 1/4 inch below the bottom of the vent well, adding water while gassing or at end of charge; give an equalize charge (C338, C340, C341). Monthly: hydrometer reading on every cell after charge, inspect leads and connectors, wipe the top with a neutralizing cleaner (C343).
- Charger sizing: an undersized charger can sulfate the positive plates; over-discharging can dissolve the negative plate; an oversized charger can shed and burn the positive plates.
- Not covered: temperature limits, depth of discharge, storage, specific gravity targets, equalize frequency or duration.
