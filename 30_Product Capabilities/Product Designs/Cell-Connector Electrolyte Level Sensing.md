# Cell-Connector Electrolyte Level Sensing

## Definition

Electrolyte-level sensing performed by a multi-function sensor assembly mounted around a battery cell connector.

## Notes

- [[Exide Motion+ EasyMonitor]] explicitly states that its 3-in-1 sensor wraps around a cell connector and monitors electrolyte level, temperature, and voltage symmetry.
- [[Wrap-Around Cell Connector Probe]] remains the mounting Design; this note captures the electrolyte-level sensing role of the same physical assembly.
- The public source does not disclose the physical principle by which the external cell-connector assembly determines electrolyte level.
