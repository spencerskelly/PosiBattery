# TUG 660 Belt Loader

## Definition

Textron TUG belt loader in electric (660E) and lithium-ion (660 Li) versions, with the Smart Sense anti-collision system.

## Notes

- Textron launched the TUG 660 Li belt loader with a lithium-ion electric drivetrain that accommodates a range of commercially available lithium-ion battery packs. Source: Textron GSE release (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://textrongse.com/about-us/news-events/textron-gse-launches-tug-660-li-belt-loader>
- Smart Sense is available on the TUG 660 and 660E electric belt loaders. Source: Airport Industry Review company insight (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://airport.h5mag.com/air_dec18/textron_company_insight>
- Textron GSE says the TUG 660 has multi-position E-stop buttons along the conveyor, a wet-throttle interlock for belt operation, retractable side guides and collapsible safety rails, return-to-neutral and shift inhibit, regenerative braking on the electric model, a lithium option with a battery management system, and options of Smart Sense, CE certification, OSHA-compliant folding handrails and an indicator that shows when the wheels are straight to help align with an aircraft's cargo hold. Source: Textron GSE pages and TUG 660 Li release (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://textrongse.com/products/belt-loaders/tug-660>
- **Source note:** the Textron product page names Samsung lithium among the powertrain choices.
