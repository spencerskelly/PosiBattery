# Document - Oregon DEQ Rule Attachments OAR 340-253-8010 Tables 4 7 and 9 (effective 2025-01-09)

## Definition

Oregon's rule tables giving an electric forklift EER of 3.8 and pathway carbon intensities from 0 for renewable power to 150 for the average mix.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. Attachment PDF for OAR 340-253-8010 (rule version DEQ 2-2025, filed and effective 2025-01-09). The fetch tool's text did not show column alignment.

- **Table 7 EER forklift rows:** Electricity/Electric Forklift 3.8; Hydrogen/Fuel Cell Forklift 2.1; Propane/Propane Forklift 0.9. No lift-capacity split and no model-year split appear for these rows.
- **Table 4:** solar, wind and renewable power pathways (ORELEC100, 101, 200) carry 0 gCO2e/MJ; the table had no Oregon average electricity row.
- **Table 9:** 'Oregon average electricity mix' ORELEC600T 150.00 (units and year not shown in the extract).
- **Conflict and difference:** Oregon's single 3.8 differs from California's proposed 2.4 for trucks under 12,000 lb (C307). The 0 and 150 values bracket what a site's electricity pathway could be worth in Oregon (C308).
- **Not found:** Oregon's diesel standard row for heavy-duty/off-road (Tables 1 and 2) was not read.
