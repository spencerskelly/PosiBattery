# Triathlon USA

## Definition

Triathlon-branded battery and charger supplier named by Mitsubishi Logisnext Americas for its UniCarriers lithium-ion option.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Battery Maker, Charger Maker.
- Mitsubishi Logisnext Americas launched Triathlon lithium-ion battery and charger solutions as a factory-installed option on most UniCarriers Class I and III models, and a Triathlon USA vice president says Triathlon is working alongside Logisnext to develop an integrated lithium-ion solution. Source: Logisnext Americas release (2023-03-20) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisnextamericas.com/en/unicarriers/news/mla-launches-new-lithium-ion-battery-solutions>
- **Open (C62):** the relationship of Triathlon USA to [[Triathlon Battery Solutions]] (UK, a Sunlight Group company) is not stated in retrieved sources.
