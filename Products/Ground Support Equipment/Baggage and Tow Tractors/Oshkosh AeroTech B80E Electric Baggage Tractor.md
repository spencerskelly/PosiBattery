# Oshkosh AeroTech B80E Electric Baggage Tractor

## Definition

Oshkosh AeroTech electric baggage tractor with 5,000 lb drawbar pull and a governed 20 mph top speed.

## Notes

- A GSE distributor says the B80E offers 5,000 lb drawbar pull and a governed 20 mph top speed, powered exclusively by electricity; the gasoline B80 is listed at 5,750 lb and 20 mph. Source: Fortbrand GSE page (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://fortbrand.com/gse-products/>
