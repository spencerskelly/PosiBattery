# Charlatte T135 Neo 25T

## Definition

Charlatte electric baggage tractor (T135 Neo 25T).

## Notes

- A GSE distributor lists the T135 Neo 25T as a highly efficient electric baggage tractor for baggage and cargo operations; specifications are not in the retrieved text. Source: Fortbrand GSE page (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://fortbrand.com/gse-products/>
- **Verification 2026-10-03 (round 40, verified):** the Charlatte T135 NEO brochure (French, via the UGAP catalogue) lists three braking levels, the first being electric regenerative braking, plus heavy-duty front and rear disc brakes and a manual or automatic parking brake (multi-disc option), traction and push capacity up to 25 tonnes, drawbar force 18,000 N, 25 km/h, and customizable drive parameters. Source: Charlatte T135 NEO brochure (T1, hosted by a buyer's catalogue) <https://www.ugap.fr/images/media-wp/2023/10/rfart_3963241_ft-t135_neo_brochure_a4_fr.pdf.avif>. A dealer listing for the older T-137/135 gives 3,333 lb drawbar pull; models differ, see conflict C109.
- **Not linked:** automatic or manual parking brake (no electric parking brake stated); pressure accumulator for energy savings is hydraulic (no function).
