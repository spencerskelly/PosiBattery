# CLARK SMART Telematics

## Definition

CLARK SMART cellular/Wi-Fi telematics unit sold through the Clark Aftermarket program for Clark and other trucks.

## Notes

- Launched 2022-09-21 with the separate Clark Smart Fleet service for National Accounts. Five years of data stored, no monthly fee, install in two hours or less by an Authorized Clark Dealer, fits "virtually any truck" (vendor claim). Dashboard shows impact reports, pre-shift inspections and utilization. Source: <https://liftandaccess.com/article/clark-material-handling-company-releases-telematics-and-fleet-management-offerings> (T2, second-hand).
