# Stryten M-Series T300 Battery

## Definition

Stryten premium flooded battery for everyday conventional use that is also opportunity-charge capable.

## Notes

- Stryten's lineup sheet describes T300 as premium, cost-efficient, durable and ideal for everyday use. Source: Stryten lineup sheet (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/14502/133723547947804003.pdf>
- Stryten says T300 is more durable and dependable than traditional flat-plate designs, ideal for everyday conventional use and opportunity-charge capable. Source: Stryten lineup sheet (09/2024) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/14502/133723547947804003.pdf>
- **Related-product note (round 12):** the EHF charger brochure names this battery among its algorithms; other pairings are not named. The earlier 'no charger linked' statement is replaced by this link.
- Stryten's T300 installation and operating instructions say the starting charge rate will be 3 times the finish charge rate indicated on the battery and should taper to the finish rate by the time the battery is 85 percent charged, equalize once a week, allow the battery to cool or warm to room temperature before charging or adding water, treat an average battery temperature above 125 F as a condition for action (the sentence is cut off) and check specific gravities after a charge. Source: Stryten T300 installation and operating instructions (SE1012, search snippet) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.stryten.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/T300_Installation-Operation_Manual_SE1012.pdf>
