# Document - EnerSys Maintenance and Service Instruction Sheet Water Less Perfect Plus Ironclad (EMEA 0724)

## Definition

EnerSys maintenance and service instruction sheet for Water Less, Perfect Plus and Ironclad flooded batteries.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Weekly equalizing charge; topping-up table with charging factors from 1.04 to 1.2 and topping-up intervals from 2 weeks (three shifts, 50 Hz, factor 1.2) to 20 weeks (one shift, HF, Wi-iQ, factor 1.04); the table was partly garbled in the fetch (C341).
- Water: do not add before charging, only if the level is below the electrodes; top up 20 minutes before end of charge or immediately after; fill to the max mark; water purity not specified (C340).
- Temperature: let electrolyte over 45 C cool before measuring; the temperature warning LED blinks red above 55 C.
- Other checks: cell voltage monitoring called essential for health and lifespan; insulation at least 50 ohm per volt; replace the Aeromatic filter and check the pump. Discharge limit, storage and commissioning not specified.
- A different sheet from [[Document - HAWKER Perfect Plus Instructions for Use (EMEA)]], which holds the charge parameters.
