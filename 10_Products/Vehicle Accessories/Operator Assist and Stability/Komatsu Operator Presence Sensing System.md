# Komatsu Operator Presence Sensing System

## Definition

Komatsu operator presence sensing on the FB series electric forklifts.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Komatsu interlock system that stops travel and lifting when the operator is not correctly seated.

**Marketed features:**
- Disables travel and lifting when the operator is unseated
- Requires operator seated and direction lever in neutral before travel ('double safety measure')
- Alarm buzzer if the operator leaves the seat while traveling
- Interlock state shown on the display/color monitor
- ISO 3691-1 compliant
- Cuts traction only; does not apply the brakes
