# Exide 2100 HP Charger

## Definition

GNB 2100 HP high-output charger, 300 to 600 A, for fast and opportunity charging of the largest batteries.

## Notes

- [[Charge Battery Fast]] and [[Charge Battery by Opportunity]] (V): <https://www.exidegroup.com/eu/sites/default/files/2021-08/GNB_MP_Overview_EN_web.pdf>. The 2100 Li High Output page says that model is based on the 2100 HP range. Voltage, kW and efficiency not stated. Source: GNB motive power overview 2021-08 (T1, second-hand; parts garbled), <https://www.exidegroup.com/eu/sites/default/files/2021-08/GNB_MP_Overview_EN_web.pdf>
