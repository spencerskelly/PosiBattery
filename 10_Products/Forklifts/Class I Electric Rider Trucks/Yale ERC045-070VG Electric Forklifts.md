# Yale ERC045-070VG Electric Forklifts

## Definition

Yale 36, 48 and 80 V electric counterbalanced forklifts for 4,500 to 7,000 lb with lead-acid standard and lithium-ion optional.

## Notes

- **Battery integration and charging interface (2026-10-14):** compartments 27 to 40 in; side extraction with rollers and lift-out extraction listed; charging connectors by option (gray or blue SB350, black SBE320 conventional; two EBC320 DIN female connectors for fast charging); lithium-ion optional. Charge-inlet location not stated, so no charge-access design is linked.
