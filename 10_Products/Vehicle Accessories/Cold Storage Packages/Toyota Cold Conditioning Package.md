# Toyota Cold Conditioning Package

## Definition

Toyota cold storage option with cold storage oil, greasable steer bearing and linkage bushings and stainless steel linkage pins.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Toyota cold-storage option package that adapts trucks for freezer and cold-store work.

**Marketed features:**
- Cold-storage-specific (freezer) oil
- Greasable steer bearing and linkage bushings
- Stainless steel linkage pins
- Dealer source adds: up to 30 min use at -4 F (-20 C), extra grease fittings, enclosed drive motor, rust inhibitor, tilt cylinder boots and heaters
