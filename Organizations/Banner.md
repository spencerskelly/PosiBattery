# Banner

## Definition

Austrian battery maker (since 1937) with the Traction Bull range for traction, small traction and stationary uses.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Battery Maker.
- Banner's brochure says it has been a battery technology pioneer since 1937 and lists the Traction Bull PzS/PzB (liquid electrolyte, certified explosion-safe), PzV/PzVB (maintenance-free gel), Bloc GiS (flat plate), Bloc DB (gel), Bloc AGM Deep Cycle and Bloc PzF (tubular) ranges. Source: Banner Energy Solutions brochure (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://bannerbatterien.com/energysolutions/upload/files/FolderAGS_GB.pdf>
- A 2026 trade report calls Banner an Austrian maker; its new Traction Bull Bloc Lithium series is a 12 V LiFePO4 line for motorhomes, caravans and boats, not for forklifts. Source: openvan.camp and Banner release (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.bannerbatterien.com/group/en/posts/messepremieren-im-dreierpack>
- **Region:** Europe (Austria; stated by trade press).
