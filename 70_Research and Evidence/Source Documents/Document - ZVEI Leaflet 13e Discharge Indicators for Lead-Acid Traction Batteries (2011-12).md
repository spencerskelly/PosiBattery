# Document - ZVEI Leaflet 13e Discharge Indicators for Lead-Acid Traction Batteries (2011-12)

## Definition

ZVEI leaflet 13e on requirements for discharge indicators and lift cut-offs on lead-acid traction batteries.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Deep discharge is more than 80 percent of nominal capacity (C5); for PzS, electrolyte density below 1.13 kg/l at 30 C or open-circuit voltage below 1.97 V per cell. Operating capacity is 80 percent of nominal.
- Example cut-off voltages for counterbalance trucks (PzS, range B): 1.86 V per cell at 20 A per 100 Ah (5 h) and 1.79 V per cell at 30 A per 100 Ah (3 h). Voltage-based indicators are only approximate; low temperature and ageing lower the voltage and reduce precision.
- Open-circuit voltage per cell equals density plus 0.84, after at least a 2-hour rest; density correction -0.0007 kg/l per K.
- Warning or lift cut-off must occur at 80 percent depth of discharge; stepwise current reduction instead of a clean cut-off is called a critical fault; a reset needs confirmed charging and must not be possible by unplugging.
- Deep discharge effects: more lead sulphate stress and corrosion, longer charge, repeated deep discharge gives irreversible sulphation and sludging; every deep discharge needs an equalising charge. A 100 percent depth of discharge cuts service life by about one third against discharging only the operating capacity.
- Mentions BATTERIEBUS controllers that compute state of discharge from current, temperature and range.
