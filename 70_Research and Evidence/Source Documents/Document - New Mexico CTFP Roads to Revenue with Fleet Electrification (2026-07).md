# Document - New Mexico CTFP Roads to Revenue with Fleet Electrification (2026-07)

## Definition

New Mexico Environment Department's outreach document describing how fleets earn credits, including electric forklifts as special equipment.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the western-states clean fuel topic. Program outreach document from the New Mexico Environment Department's Clean Transportation Fuel Program (CTFP); the date is taken from the upload path (2026/07).

- **Forklifts:** lists "electric forklifts" as "special equipment" with electric cargo handling equipment, electric ground support equipment, electric transport refrigeration units and public transit, and gives "electric fork lift eGSE" as an eligible example.
- **Metering and reporting:** fleets must "report metered electricity dispensed (in kilowatt-hours) to fleet vehicles or special equipment" each quarter and register fuel supply equipment in ARCS (the program's reporting system).
- **Who generates credits:** fleets that own chargers and dispense electricity to their fleet are "eligible to be first in line to claim credits"; vendors that own chargers and aggregators under contract can also participate.
- **Dates and money:** "Effective April 1, 2026, the CTFP is a policy that:"; revenue tables estimate 2027 revenue at $100 per credit (a projected price, not a market price).
- **Not stated:** credit issuance timing, the kWh-to-credit conversion, carbon intensity values, fee amounts, compliance period length, and rule citations.
- **Conflict:** see C294 (versus the Trinity article) and C295 (reporting entity).
