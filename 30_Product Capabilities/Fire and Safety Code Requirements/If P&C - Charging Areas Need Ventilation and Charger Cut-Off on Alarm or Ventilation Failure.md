# If P&C - Charging Areas Need Ventilation and Charger Cut-Off on Alarm or Ventilation Failure

## Definition

One insurer's hazard sheet says a lithium charging area needs air exchange of at least one room volume per hour (or natural openings of at least 400 cm2 per 50 m3 at floor and ceiling levels), with chargers that cut power on ventilation failure and shut off when the fire alarm sounds.

## Notes

- **Insurer guidance, not a code.** Together with REQ-00067 it challenges the vault's assumption that lithium removes the ventilation need (C318).
- The charger-side consequence is an interlock input; see REQ-00076 (analyst-proposed).
- Second-hand reading; see [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]].
