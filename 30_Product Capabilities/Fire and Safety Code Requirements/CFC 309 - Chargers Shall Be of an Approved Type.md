# CFC 309 - Chargers Shall Be of an Approved Type

## Definition

Under section 309.3 of the 2025 California Fire Code, battery chargers used with powered industrial trucks shall be of an approved type.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It binds whoever installs or operates the charging area. The code text read does not say what "approved" means (listing to a named standard or approval by the fire authority); that is the fire authority's call.
- Second-hand reading of a reproduction site; see [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]]. No battery chemistry is named, so lithium is neither covered nor exempted by name (C314).
- Product consequence (analyst-proposed): REQ-00078.
