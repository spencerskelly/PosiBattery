# Godrej Multi-Ion Forklift Battery

## Definition

Godrej Multi-Ion forklift battery claiming up to 5,000 cycles, operation above 45 C ambient and a 7-year warranty.

## Notes

- A 2026 trade report says the Multi-Ion battery, developed with a deep-tech partner, delivers up to 5,000 cycles (about 60 percent above standard lithium-ion), is built for ambient temperatures above 45 C, has over 5,000 hours of real-world validation and a 7-year warranty. Source: pv magazine India (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/?p=15032>
- **Not stated:** chemistry label beyond 'Multi-Ion', voltages and capacities; the partner's name is cut from the retrieved text.
