# Wet Dusty or Outdoor Operating Environment

## Definition

Operating context exposed to rain, wash-down, dust, dirt, corrosion or outdoor weather that affects equipment enclosure, mounting, connector and maintenance needs.

## Notes

- Use this as a condition on vehicle operation, charging, installation and maintenance scenarios.
- Supported by [[Keep Equipment Working in Cold, Wet and Dusty Conditions]] and source-backed product evidence for outdoor- and ingress-rated equipment.
- This context does not duplicate the product-controlled Function [[Operate in Wet or Dusty Conditions]].
