## Notes

- Says forklift batteries are generally lead-acid or nickel-iron (nickel-iron is not recorded elsewhere in the vault as a forklift chemistry; treated as a statement by a safety talk, not as market data).
- Charging can emit oxygen and hydrogen; ventilated, signed and striped charging area, eyewash and shower, spill kit with acid neutralisation, PPE (gloves, goggles and face shield, apron, steel-toe boots).
- Inspection points: fluid level, cables and connectors, contact posts for crystallisation or corrosion, casing cracks. Charger off before connecting or disconnecting; vent caps in place; no smoking or sparks.
- Batteries can weigh up to 2,000 pounds; use a rated battery cart or forklift and strap the battery.
