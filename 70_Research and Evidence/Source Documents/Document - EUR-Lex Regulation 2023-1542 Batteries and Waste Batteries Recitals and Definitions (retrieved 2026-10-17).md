# Document - EUR-Lex Regulation 2023-1542 Batteries and Waste Batteries Recitals and Definitions (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

EUR-Lex page for Regulation (EU) 2023/1542; only the recitals and the first definitions could be read, so the operative articles on the passport and state of health are unread.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand). Three attempts (two addresses, one with an offset) returned the preamble and Articles 1 to 3 up to definition (22) only; **Article 77 (passport), Article 14 (state of health and BMS), Article 13 (labelling) and Annex XIII were not read.** Every operative-article statement elsewhere in this register is therefore second-hand from the Commission FAQ or secondary sources (IB-298).

- **Definition 13, industrial battery:** "a battery that is specifically designed for industrial uses", plus other non-vehicle batteries over 5 kg (Recital 15 gives the 5 kg rule).
- **Recital 44:** "The QR code should give access to a battery's product passport."
- **Recital 46:** read-only access to battery management system data should be given to the person who purchased the battery; data updated at least daily (for stationary storage, LMT and EV batteries with a BMS).
- **Recital 126:** the passport "should be based on a decentralised data system, set up and maintained by economic operators."
- Recitals are not binding; they only signal intent.
