## Notes

**Content recorded:**
- Section 30C (alternative fuel vehicle refueling property): not allowed for property placed in service after 2026-06-30.
- Section 45W (qualified commercial clean vehicle): not allowed for vehicles acquired after 2025-09-30; acquired means a written binding contract plus a payment; the credit can still be claimed when placed in service later.
- The article does not cover sections 48, 48E, 45V or 45X, so the fuel cell and hydrogen credits are not verified here. It states that IRS FAQs are general information and do not control over the law.
