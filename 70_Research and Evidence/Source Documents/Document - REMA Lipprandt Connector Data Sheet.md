# Document - REMA Lipprandt Connector Data Sheet

## Definition

REMA Lipprandt data sheet for twin plug and socket connectors for electric industrial trucks.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- EN 1175-1 and DIN 43589, 150 V; 80, 160 and 320 A families plus a 400 A series (400 A continuous, 450 A for 15 min, compatible with the 320 A DIN series); conductors 80 A 16 and 25 mm2, 160 A 25, 35 and 50 mm2, 320 A 50, 70 and 95 mm2, 400 A 120 mm2; variants with 2 pilot, 2 auxiliary, both, or air supply; optional 4 auxiliaries on 80/160/320 A sockets; hexagonal wet/dry coding pin set for six voltages 24, 36, 48, 72, 80, 96 V visible through a window; pin colours grey (wet + wet), green (dry), yellow (wet + dry); housings black, acid-proof grey, red (80 and 160 A only). Mating cycles not stated.
