# Larson Explosion-Proof Blue LED Forklift Light

## Definition

Blue LED forklift warning light rated for hazardous areas, 2,250 lumens at 25 W, 9 to 60 V.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Larson Electronics compact, waterproof, explosion-proof blue LED forklift warning light for hazardous locations.

**Marketed features:**
- 2,250 lumens while drawing 25 W (stated as 150 W equivalent)
- IP66 waterproof
- Class I, II, III Division 1 and 2 hazardous-location ratings
- 9-60 V universal input
- Compact for tight mounting spaces
