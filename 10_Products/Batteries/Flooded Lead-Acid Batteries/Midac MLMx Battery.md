# Midac MLMx Battery

## Definition

Midac flooded traction battery, 100 to 1,550 Ah, topping up up to 8 weeks, up to 1,500 cycles, open to third-party chargers.

## Notes

- **Update 2026-10-17 (customer traceability review):** offeredWith [[Midac Electrolyte Level Sensor]] (catalogue: level sensor standard) and [[MIDATRON BMU]] (catalogue: BMU optional), matching the links already on [[Midac MLM Battery]]. Single-point watering (standard) and air mixing (optional) are not linked because the catalogue does not name which Midac product supplies them.
- Single-point watering and level sensor standard; air mixing optional. Any compliant HF charger (MIDATRON HE optional, BMU optional); third-party chargers need approval and IUIa profile, at least 92% efficiency, charging factor 1.08 or less, ripple 4% or less.
