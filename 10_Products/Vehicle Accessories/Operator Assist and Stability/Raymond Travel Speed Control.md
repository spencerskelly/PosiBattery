# Raymond Travel Speed Control

## Definition

Raymond configurable option that limits truck travel speed to a preset value.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Raymond configurable option that limits travel speed once the forks are raised out of free lift.

**Marketed features:**
- Configurable travel-speed limit from 1 to 6 mph
- Applies when the forks exit free lift
