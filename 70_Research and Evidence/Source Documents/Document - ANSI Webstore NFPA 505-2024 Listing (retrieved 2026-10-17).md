# Document - ANSI Webstore NFPA 505-2024 Listing (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

Catalog entry for NFPA 505-2024, the fire safety standard for powered industrial trucks. Read for its scope statement only; the standard text was not read.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), from a standards-seller catalog page, so only metadata and the abstract are known. A second product page was read for the scope list: <https://www.fire-police-ems.com/NFPA505-2024.shtml>.

- **Title and edition:** NFPA 505, Fire Safety Standard for Powered Industrial Trucks Including Type Designations, Areas of Use, Conversions, Maintenance, and Operations; 2024 edition, revising the 2018 edition.
- **Scope (abstract):** mitigates fire and explosion hazards of powered industrial trucks: fork trucks, tractors, platform lift trucks, motorized hand trucks and other specialized industrial trucks powered by electric motors or internal combustion engines.
- **Scope list on the second page:** hazardous (classified) locations, dual-fuel trucks, truck conversions, maintenance, fuel recharging, marking and operation, portable fire extinguishers.
- **Lithium and charging areas:** neither page mentions lithium-ion batteries or battery charging areas by name; "fuel recharging" is the closest. Whether the 2024 edition has lithium provisions is unknown (IB-275). The California Fire Code section 309 reproduction refers to NFPA 505 only for classified-location trucks.
- **Not stated:** publication date (the page shows only a 2026 ANSI copyright line); amendments.
