# Linde Load Management Advanced

## Definition

Linde assistance system for high-lift pallet trucks that warns near the load limit, locks the lift above it and adapts travel speed and acceleration to lift height and load weight.

## Notes

- The brochure says Load Management Advanced warns by light and sound when the mast moves near the load limit, locks the lift function automatically when it is exceeded, adjusts travel speed and acceleration to lift height and load weight, shows load and lift on the display and is factory-fitted on the L14 to L20 AP high-lift pallet trucks (series 1173). Source: Linde Rhein-Ruhr safety brochure (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.lmh-rr.de/media/Linde-Rhein-Ruhr/02_PDF/Safety/broschuere_safety.pdf>
