# Exide Motion+ Premium Charger

## Definition

Exide compact high-frequency charger (SP3) for small electric vehicles, cleaning machines and pallet trucks, for lead-acid and lithium-ion batteries.

## Notes

- Exide says the Motion+ Premium Charger (SP3) succeeds the 2100SP series, is compatible with lead-acid and lithium-ion batteries with profiles tailored to capacity and type, is manufactured in Europe by Exide, uses high-frequency conversion, has CAN-board functionality for lithium-ion batteries using CAN, and has an optional USB data download. Source: Exide news release (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.exidegroup.com/en/news/exide-technologies-unveils-new-motion-premium-charger>
- A trade report quotes Exide's product manager saying the charger was made in-house with Exide controlling its design, development, technology and manufacture, with charging curves matched to Exide's own lead-acid and lithium light traction and small tray batteries. Source: Automotive World (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.automotiveworld.com/?p=452187>
