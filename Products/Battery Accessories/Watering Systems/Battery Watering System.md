# Battery Watering System

## Definition

Battery-installed fluid-distribution hardware that enables multiple flooded lead-acid cells to be filled from a common connection while controlling cell fill level.

## Notes

Water supply equipment that is not installed on the battery is adjacent infrastructure; the valves, tubing, manifolds, couplings, and battery-resident portions are within this family.
- **Scope difference (open):** an earlier draft of this category (merged here) defined watering more broadly, from manual single-point systems to automatic systems controlled by a charger. This family is limited to battery-resident hardware; charger-side or charger-controlled elements are adjacent and are not modeled yet.
- **Verification 2026-10-02 (re-verified):** PosiCharge lists automatic watering as an option on its SVS200 charger. Source: PosiCharge SVS200 page (T1) <https://www.posicharge.com/svs200/>
- **Gaps:** valve and manifold hardware, flow indicators, charger integration method; overlap with [[Battery Water Level Monitor]].
