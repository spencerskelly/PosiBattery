# Flux Power

## Definition

US lithium-ion forklift and ground support battery maker that discloses private-label OEM relationships without naming the partners.

## Notes

- **Role (working label, not a governed property):** battery maker (lithium); supplies private-label packs.
- Flux Power reported in March 2019 that a major global forklift manufacturer selected it to supply lithium-ion batteries for that manufacturer's walkie pallet jack line under a private label OEM relationship; the manufacturer is not named. Source: Flux Power Form 8-K (2019-03-29) (T1 (SEC filing)), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1083743/000165495419003678/flux_8k.htm>
- Flux Power announced on 2024-09-12 a partnership with a leading forklift OEM for a private label program using its UL Type EE S-Series batteries so the OEM can offer them under its own brand; the OEM is not named. Source: Business Wire release (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240912587367/en>
- A 2019 Flux presentation lists sales and relationships with Toyota, Raymond, Kion, Jungheinrich, Hyster and Crown Equipment, and says its pack was tested and approved by Toyota, Raymond and Crown. It does not say any of these are private-label arrangements. Source: Flux Power company presentation (Oct 2019) (T1 (self-reported)), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.fluxpower.com/hubfs/investors/Flux-Power-Company-Presentation-October-2019.pdf>
- Flux says it sells primarily to lift equipment OEMs, their dealers and battery distributors. Source: Flux Power Q3 FY2019 results exhibit (T1 (SEC filing)), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1083743/000165495419005804/flux_ex991.htm>
- A fiscal-2020 call transcript says Flux secured supply relationships with more forklift OEMs, such as Clark Material Handling. Source: Earnings call transcript (secondary host) (T4), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.roic.ai/quote/FLUX/transcripts/2020-year/4-quarter>
- **Unknown, do not infer:** which OEM is the 2019 or 2024 private-label partner. The named OEMs above are sales relationships, not identified white-label customers.
