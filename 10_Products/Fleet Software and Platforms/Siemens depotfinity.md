# Siemens depotfinity

## Definition

Siemens depot management platform for electric fleets with charge scheduling and load management in three tiers.

## Notes

- Basic, Advanced and Premium tiers: remote control and monitoring; scheduling and load-managed depot operation; dynamic load management and day-ahead optimization. Vehicle types named are eBus and eTruck. Source: [[Document - Siemens depotfinity Page (retrieved 2026-10-14)]] (T1). <https://www.siemens.com/en-us/products/emobility/depotfinity>
