# Avoid Arcing at the Charge Connection

## Definition

Avoid sparks or arcing when a charger is connected to or disconnected from a battery or vehicle.

## Notes

- **Manual evidence (2026-10-14):** the PosiCharge DVS300 and MVS400 and MVS800 manual says the stations are designed to stop the charge automatically on a hot disconnection 'to minimize arcing', and elsewhere that disconnecting without STOP makes the charger stop immediately 'and prevent any arcing' (C276). This is the same product line as the SVS100 listing.
- Added 2026-10-14 from the owner's account of the plug-in workflow ([[Drive Up, Plug In and Walk Away]]): old chargers sparked on connection unless switched off first (owner statement, U). Only one product states the behavior; whether it works by cutting output before the connector parts is not stated.
- **Need (analyst link, hypothesis):** realizes [[Connect Chargers and Batteries Safely at the Site]], the need whose problem statement covers a safe connection.
- No Requirement is linked (intentional gap).
- **Sources** (product, evidence level, web page):
  - [[PosiCharge SVS100]] (V): <https://posicharge.com/products/svs100/>
  - [[PosiCharge DVS300 Series]] (V): [[Document - PosiCharge DVS and MVS GSE Operation and Maintenance Manual (5914769 rev C03)]], sections 1.2 and 4.4 (stops the charge automatically on a hot disconnection)
  - [[PosiCharge MVS400 and MVS800]] (V): [[Document - PosiCharge DVS and MVS GSE Operation and Maintenance Manual (5914769 rev C03)]], sections 1.2 and 4.4 (stops the charge automatically on a hot disconnection)
