# Cat VISR

## Definition

Cat VISR integrated telematics for lift trucks: control unit, display, RFID reader, impact sensors, portal and API; factory-fit or dealer retrofit, with a universal kit for other brands.

## Notes

- Brochure OEBC2563 (06/26) on the Cat Lift Trucks EAME site. Features: GPS, access control with certification checks, digital pre-operational checks that can immobilise the truck, maintenance tracking, impact detection with optional lockout or slowdown, utilisation reports, standard API. Applies to almost any forklift; a universal retrofit kit exists for other brands. Cameras not mentioned. Relation to [[Logisnext Lift Link]] (Americas) is not stated (C242). Source: <https://www.catlifttruck.com/sites/default/files/documents/cat-visr-brochure-oebc2563.pdf> (T1, second-hand).
