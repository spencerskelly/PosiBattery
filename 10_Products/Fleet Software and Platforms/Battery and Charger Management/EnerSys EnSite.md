# EnerSys EnSite

## Definition

EnerSys modeling software that recommends battery chemistry, battery and charger specifications for a lift truck fleet from operating data.

## Notes

- Inputs: vehicle count, size, type, schedules, charging needs. Outputs: right-sized battery and charger fleet specification. Chemistries compared: NexSys iON, NexSys PURE, hybrid. Source: trade article relaying EnerSys (T3, second-hand), <https://www.mhwmag.com/?p=85539>
- A sizing and selection tool, not an operating-time monitor; it sits in this class only for lack of a better one (candidate class: fleet sizing tool). No EnerSys page found (IB-183).
