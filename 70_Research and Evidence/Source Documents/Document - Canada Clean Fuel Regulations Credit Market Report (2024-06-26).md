# Document - Canada Clean Fuel Regulations Credit Market Report (2024-06-26)

## Definition

Canada's June 2024 credit market report with average credit prices near CAD 133 and early charging-credit volumes.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. Environment and Climate Change Canada report published 2024-06-26 covering credit creation for 2022 and 2023 and transfers from 2023-06-30 to 2024-05-31.

- **Prices (CAD per credit, volume weighted, transfers with a price):** 2022 average 141.80 (range 9.77 to 300.00, 1,222,594 credits); 2023 average 127.30 (range 6.75 to 300.00, 1,780,206 credits); both years 133.20 on 3,002,800 credits. Each credit equals one tonne CO2e.
- **Creation (t CO2e):** low-carbon fuels 6,806,598; advanced vehicle technology 625,930; carbon intensity reduction projects 1,116,918; rollover 2,774,461; total 11,323,907.
- **Charging credits:** 2023 charging-site hosts (10 organizations) 3,814,006 kWh and 4,437 credits; general public 250,335,841 kWh and 314,052 credits; residential 58,364,106 kWh and 72,367 credits. Analyst ratio: charging-site hosts 1.16 credits per MWh in 2023. All used provincial grid carbon intensities.
- **Limits stated:** early-stage market, pricing not yet economically robust. No forklift data.
- **Conflict:** a vendor claims credits traded above 400 each in early 2026 ([[Document - 7Gen CFR Credit Price in 2026 (2026-06-11)]]); this report ends in May 2024 and its maximum was CAD 300 (C309).
