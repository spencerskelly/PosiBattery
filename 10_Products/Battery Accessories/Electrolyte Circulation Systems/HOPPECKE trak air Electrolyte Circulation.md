# HOPPECKE trak air Electrolyte Circulation

## Definition

HOPPECKE trak | air electrolyte circulation used on trak | uplift air batteries.

## Notes

**Summary:**
HOPPECKE electrolyte circulation system that blows air into each cell during charging to prevent acid layering.

**Marketed features:**
- Air into each cell during charging prevents acid stratification
- Eliminates time-consuming weekend equalization charges
- Reduces energy and water consumption and charging time
- Fewer replacement batteries needed; longer battery life
