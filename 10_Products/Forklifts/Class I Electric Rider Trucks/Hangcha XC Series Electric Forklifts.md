# Hangcha XC Series Electric Forklifts

## Definition

Hangcha XC series lithium iron phosphate electric forklifts (1.5 to 5 t) with quick-change packs, backup camera, FIMS and seat-leave protection.

## Notes

- Hangcha's brochures say the XC series (1.5 to 3.5 t, and 4 to 5 t) uses LiFePO4 batteries with options of quickly replaceable (roller type) packs, a backup camera and packs with low-temperature electric heating, the Hangcha FIMS system with real-time truck and driver information, a dedicated lithium charger (Titan or Schneider on the 4 to 5 t edition), and protection when the driver leaves the seat. Source: Hangcha XC series brochures (2024 to 2026 editions) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.hcforklift.com/upload/files/b8ede554fd4dbc7af35f288f0c9991f2.pdf>
- A North American dealer lists XC series Core (3-wheel), Lite (4-wheel) and Mid (FB40Li, 8,000 lb, 80 V) models with a PIN code system, fork-descent buffering, emergency cut-off switches, a controller that slows the truck in corners, and a backup camera option. Source: Summit Handling pages (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.summithandling.com/summit-product/hangcha-xc-series-mid-electric-outdoor-lithium-ion-forklift/>
