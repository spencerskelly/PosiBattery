# Electrolyte Air Circulation Assembly

## Definition

Reusable assembly that supplies air to flooded-battery cells during charging to mix electrolyte and reduce stratification.

## Notes

- Typical architecture consists of an air source plus distribution tubing/manifold to each equipped cell.
- The assembly may be battery-mounted, charger-mounted, or split across the charger and battery depending on the product.
- Exact pump type, flow rate, tubing material, manifold arrangement, check valves, connectors, and control interface remain product-specific.
