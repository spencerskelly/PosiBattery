# Document - CARB LCFS 15-Day Notice of Modified Text (2024-08-12)

## Definition

CARB's August 2024 notice of modified text for the LCFS amendments, giving the reasoning for a forklift EER change and the delay of forklift metering to 2026 reporting.

## Notes

**How it was read:** first-hand, from the raw file the owner added to the repo (`Downloads/CARB_15day_notice.pdf`), with text extracted by pdftotext. Not a summary.

Added from the owner's downloads. CARB notice of public availability of modified text for the proposed LCFS amendments (public availability 2024-08-12, comment deadline 2024-08-27, hearing 2024-11-08). It lists attachments A-1 to A-2.1 and summarizes each change.

- **Forklift EER (Table 5 in section 95486.1(a)):** staff proposes updating the EER for electric forklifts under 12,000 lb lift capacity, and for hydrogen fuel cell forklifts under 12,000 lb. Reason given: in the 2010 baseline year about half of that forklift population was already electric, so the EER averages 1 (against electric forklifts) and the original 3.8 (against combustion forklifts). 2.4 follows from that averaging; the notice states the method, not the number.
- **Metering (section 95491(d)(3)(E)):** the new metered reporting requirement for electric forklifts is delayed to 2026 reporting, to give FSE owners time to buy metering equipment and set up procedures. Reporters may keep existing methods for 2025.
- **Registration:** the notice also touches registration requirements for electric forklifts (a passage on the definition of equipment).
- **Status:** these are proposals, not the adopted order. The adopted order was not read (IB-242).
- **Resolves in part:** C296 (the 2.4 value is now on a CARB document, not only a trade blog) and C286.
