# Review BMID Battery History and Exceptions

## Definition

Responsible personnel review BMID-supported battery history, usage, and exception information to decide whether service, operating changes, warranty follow-up, or replacement planning is needed.

## Notes

- Primary product family: [[PosiCharge BMID]].
- Cloud upload and remote dashboards are variant/system dependent and are not assumed for every BMID.
- Completion: the reviewer has identified relevant battery-history or exception information and an appropriate follow-up decision.
- Manufacturer-specific warranty acceptance criteria remain outside this generic product use case.
