# PC Service Tool Interface

## Definition

Desktop or laptop service-tool interface used to configure a battery-connected device, inspect status, or retrieve diagnostic and logged data.

## Notes

- The connection may use Bluetooth, USB, serial, NFC reader hardware, or another local service interface.
- Crown explicitly supports laptop/tablet connection to its V-Force BMID over Bluetooth Class 1.
- HOPPECKE publishes PC software access for trak collect.
- The exact application name, operating system, transport stack, file format, and service workflow remain product-specific unless published.
