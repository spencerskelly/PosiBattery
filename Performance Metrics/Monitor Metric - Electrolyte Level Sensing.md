# Monitor Metric - Electrolyte Level Sensing

## Definition

Electrolyte Level Sensing: Method for sensing electrolyte level in flooded cells.

## Notes

- **Code and class:** MM06; monitor metric. Unit or format: method; cells covered.
- **Comparability rule:** Capacitive and conductive probes differ in corrosion and calibration; one probe usually covers one cell.
- **Direction:** n/a.
- **Values on file (as stated in each product note; n/s means not stated):**
