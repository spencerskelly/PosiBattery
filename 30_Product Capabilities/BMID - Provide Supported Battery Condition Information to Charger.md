# BMID - Provide Supported Battery Condition Information to Charger

## Definition

The PosiCharge BMID family shall make supported battery-condition information available to a compatible charger when required by the supported BMID charging configuration.

## Notes

- The exact data set may differ by BMID generation.
- This requirement does not imply that current, electrolyte level, state of charge, CAN data, or any other specific signal exists on every BMID product.
- Child-product requirements may refine the required data set.
