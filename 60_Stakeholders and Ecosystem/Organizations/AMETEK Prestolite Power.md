# AMETEK Prestolite Power

## Definition

Troy, Ohio maker of industrial battery chargers (Eclipse II, ULTRA) and the BID and WBID battery data devices; formerly the Hobart Brothers battery charger division.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Charger Maker, Monitor Maker.
- A trade profile says Prestolite Power, formerly the Hobart Brothers Battery Charger Division, has made industrial battery chargers for material handling since 1917, from Battery-Mate single-shift models to Eclipse II high-frequency opportunity and fast chargers. Source: New Equipment Digest company profile (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://newequipment.com/55096937>
- East Penn Canada's charger page, as shown by dealer Johnstone Equipment, says East Penn offers AMETEK's full line of chargers, from the LTD-Ferro to the Eclipse II series. Source: Johnstone Equipment dealer page (T3), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.johnstoneequipment.com/forklifts/batteries-solutions/battery-and-chargers/chargers>
