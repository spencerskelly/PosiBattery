# Textron GSE

## Definition

Textron business selling TUG, Douglas, Premier and Safeaero ground support equipment, including lithium-ion belt loaders, baggage tractors and pushbacks.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Truck OEM, Brand Owner.
- Textron says it offers conventional and towbarless pushbacks, baggage tractors, tow tractors, belt loaders, deicers, ground power units, air starts and air conditioning units under the TUG, Douglas, Premier and Safeaero brands, as part of Textron Specialized Vehicles. Source: Textron GSE release (TUG 660 Li) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://textrongse.com/about-us/news-events/textron-gse-launches-tug-660-li-belt-loader>
- A trade profile lists lithium options TUG Endurance (new baggage tractor), TUG ALPHA 1 (pushback) and TUG 660 (belt loader), with CE-certified models. Source: The Flying Engineer listing (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://theflyingengineer.com/?p=25152>
