# Linde Load Management Advanced

## Definition

Linde assistance system for high-lift pallet trucks that warns near the load limit, locks the lift above it and adapts travel speed and acceleration to lift height and load weight.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Linde assistance system for high-lift pallet trucks that warns near the load limit, locks lifting above it and adapts travel to lift height and load.

**Marketed features:**
- Visual and acoustic load-handling signals
- Warning near the load-capacity limit
- Automatic lift lock-out above the capacity limit
- Travel speed and acceleration adapted to lift height and load weight
- Display shows load and lift situation and possible capacity
- Factory option on L14-L20 AP (series 1173)
