# Document - ANL ESD 08-3 Full Fuel-Cycle Comparison of Forklift Propulsion Systems

## Definition

A national laboratory report giving 7.5 kWh per hour purchased for a 5,000 lb electric forklift and typical annual runtimes of 500 to 3,500 hours.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- **Electricity per hour:** 7.5 kWh per hour purchased for a 5,000 lb electric forklift (cited to EPRI 2001) at $0.077 per kWh; 1.56 kWh purchased per 1 kWh delivered to the wheels.
- **Efficiencies:** typical charger 84 percent, forklift battery 76 percent, so about 64 percent of grid electricity does useful work; only 80 percent of battery charge is usable without damage.
- **Annual runtime:** 500 to 3,500 hours for battery forklifts (cited to GTI 1995); Class I and II shifts: 69 percent one shift, 16 percent two, 15 percent three; average 1.5 shifts for 5 days a week.
- **Derived (analyst):** 7.5 kWh per hour x 500 to 3,500 hours = 3.75 to 26.25 MWh a year per truck purchased; an average of 1.5 shifts x 5 days x 52 weeks x about 6 hours is about 17.6 MWh.
- **Not in the document:** measured electric forklift data (its measurements cover propane trucks); a per-class annual kWh figure. Figures are from 1990s and 2001 sources and old.
