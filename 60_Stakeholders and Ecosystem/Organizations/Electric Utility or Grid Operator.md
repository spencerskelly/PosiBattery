# Electric Utility or Grid Operator

## Definition

Business role: the party that supplies electricity to a site, sets tariffs or demand-response programs, and may signal the site to reduce or shift load.

## Notes

- **Layer:** L8, site to grid or utility, in [[Interface and Protocol Layer Reference]]. This is an entry only; deeper work is planned later (owner decision 2026-10-13).
- **Protocols named in the vault:** OpenADR for demand-response signals (encyclopedia only; versions 1.0 and 2.0 covered); IEEE 2030.5 is unread. OCPP and ISO 15118 are L7 charging protocols.
- **Link to PosiCharge products:** none evidenced. The vault has research notes on energy management ([[Energy Management Systems for Warehouses and Production Facilities]], [[Cost Driver 08 Utilities and Energy]]) but no PosiCharge product note states a utility interface.
- **Open:** whether any PosiCharge product or PosiLink or SkyLink exchanges data with a utility or aggregator; this needs owner input and is not in public sources.
