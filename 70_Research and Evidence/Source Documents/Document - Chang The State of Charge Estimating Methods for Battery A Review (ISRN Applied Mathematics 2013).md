## Notes

- Names lead-acid, Ni-MH, Ni-Cd and Li-ion as industrial chemistries, then treats mostly Li-ion models; no traction duty or voltage range.
- States the lead-acid OCV is approximately linear in SOC (VOC = a1 SOC + a0) and that OCV is proportional to SOC after disconnection for longer than two hours, which it calls too long to be practical. This conflicts with Khan et al. 2024 on hysteresis and with rest times elsewhere (C350).
- The OCV-versus-SOC figure shown is a Li-ion curve from a cited source and reaches 320 to 380 V; it is a pack-level figure for an unstated pack.
