# Water Injector Shutoff Assembly

## Definition

Injector-tip assembly that senses target fill level and automatically stops water flow during battery watering.

## Notes

- Philadelphia Scientific explicitly states that the precision level-sensing valve shuts off at the injector tip and operates without floats.
- The internal sensing mechanism and valve construction are not disclosed.
