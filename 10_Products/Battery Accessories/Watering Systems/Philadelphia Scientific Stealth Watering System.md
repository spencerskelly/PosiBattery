# Philadelphia Scientific Stealth Watering System

## Definition

Philadelphia Scientific Stealth watering system for industrial batteries.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Philadelphia Scientific low-profile float-based single-point watering system that fills all cells to the same level at once, aimed at light-duty batteries.

**Marketed features:**
- Fills a battery in one-tenth the time of hand watering; about USD 7,000/year labor saving for 25 batteries at USD 20/h (maker figure)
- Lowest-profile float valve in the industry (0.6 in / 15 mm)
- Barbed fittings and Push Speed Tubing; snap-fit rotatable base; compatible with other float systems
- Float Guard against moss shield and separator oil; visible fill-level indicator
- 3-45 psi operating pressure; fits 5- and 7-plate cells
- For forklifts, scrubbers, aerial, buggies and marine
