# Airport Ground-Support Operating Area

## Definition

Airport ramp and ground-support operating context where GSE, aircraft, ground crew, chargers and batteries interact under mission-critical outdoor conditions.

## Notes

- Use this context for baggage, tow, belt-loader, cargo-loader, pushback and related eGSE scenarios.
- Surrounding equipment includes [[Ground Support Equipment]], aircraft, ground crew, battery/charging equipment and ramp infrastructure.
- Derived from the Airport eGSE fleet segment in [[PosiCharge Market Segments and Jobs-to-Be-Done]] and the aircraft-protection customer need.
