# Midac MDLblock Battery

## Definition

Midac 24 V DIN-B/BS block battery, 130 to 250 Ah, welded internally, 1,500+ cycles.

## Notes

- Pairs with MIDATRON HF; BMU optional; single-point watering and level sensor optional; no air mixing. Electrolyte type (flooded or VRLA) is not stated in the summary, so it is filed as flooded provisionally (IB-190).
