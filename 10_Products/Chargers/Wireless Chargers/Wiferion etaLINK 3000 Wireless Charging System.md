# Wiferion etaLINK 3000 Wireless Charging System

## Definition

Wiferion etaLINK 3000, a 3 kW inductive charging system (6 kW with two in series) with CAN bus, IP65, for AGVs, AMRs, forklifts and tugger trains.

## Notes

- The etaLINK range is listed from 1 kW to 36 kW; efficiency, air gap and tolerance are not stated. Source: <https://www.wiferion.com/us/products> (T1, second-hand).
