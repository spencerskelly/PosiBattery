# Raymond 4000 Series Counterbalanced Trucks

## Definition

Raymond stand-up and sit-down electric counterbalanced trucks including the 4460, 4750, 4800 and 4810.

## Notes

- Raymond's literature page lists the 4000 Series stand-up counterbalanced trucks and the 4460 and 4750 sit-down forklifts (3,000 to 3,500 lb and 8,000 to 12,000 lb versions). Source: Raymond literature page (test host, caution) (T1 (caution)), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://test-raymondcorp.raymondcorp.com/information/lift-truck-literature>
- At ProMat 2023 Raymond showed the 4800 and 4810 sit-down electric counterbalanced trucks. Source: Robotics 24/7 ProMat report (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.robotics247.com/article/raymond_shows_off_integrated_intralogistics_systems_promat_2023/Raymond_Corp>
- A dealer-data page for the 4810 C90 and C110 sit-down pneumatic trucks lists an operator presence system and multiple cabin options. Source: AllMachines listings (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.allmachines.com/forklifts/the-raymond-corporation-4810-c110>
- **Functions performed, with citations:**
  - [[Stop Vehicle When Operator Is Out of Position]] (V): <https://www.allmachines.com/forklifts/the-raymond-corporation-4810-c110>
  - [[Shelter Operator from Weather]] (V): <https://www.allmachines.com/forklifts/the-raymond-corporation-4810-c110>

- **Update 2026-10-14:** the 4460 page and sell sheet give 36 V or 48 V, 4460 3,000 to 4,000 lb, 4750 3,000 to 12,000 lb, lithium-ion option (LFP and NMC mentioned), optional right-hand battery rollout, cold-storage control protection, UL EE, slope sensing. **Conflict-visible (C241 below):** the older literature-page text above gives 3,000 to 3,500 lb for the sit-down 4460/4750 versions. Sources: <https://www.johnstonequipment.com/-/media/raymond/literature/truck-literature/counterbalanced-trucks/raymond_4000_series_sit_down_sell_sheet.pdf>; <https://raymondcorp.com/lift-trucks/counterbalanced-trucks/4460-sit-down-forklift>
- Accessories added: [[Raymond Right-Hand Battery Rollout]], [[Raymond Slope Sensing]].

- **Battery integration (2026-10-14):** the 4460 page offers an optional right-hand battery rollout for faster changes and says batteries can be shared with an existing fleet. Source: <https://raymondcorp.com/lift-trucks/counterbalanced-trucks/4460-sit-down-forklift>
- **Design characteristics, with citations:**
  - [[Drop-In Battery Cavity]] (V, battery rollout option): <https://raymondcorp.com/lift-trucks/counterbalanced-trucks/4460-sit-down-forklift>

- **Update 2026-10-14 (4750 sell sheet):** 36 or 48 V; battery change method, lithium and charger not stated on this sheet (the 4460 page states the optional right-hand side rollout); Performance Stability System (active mast control and rear-axle swing lock) and attachments (multiple load handler, layer picker, carton clamps, rotating forks) named. Source: <https://www.carolinahandling.com/-/media/raymond/literature/truck-literature/counterbalanced-trucks/raymond-counterbalanced---4750-8k-10k-12k-sell-sheet.pdf> (T2)
