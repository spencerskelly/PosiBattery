# Toyota Carriage-Mounted Camera

## Definition

Toyota camera on the carriage that helps the operator position forks at height.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Toyota carriage-mounted camera that gives the operator a visual aid when positioning forks at height.

**Marketed features:**
- Additional visual aid for the operator
- Helps position forks at high lift heights
- Part of the Toyota Assist portfolio
