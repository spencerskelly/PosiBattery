# Document - Wu et al Energy Consumption Analysis of High-Voltage Lithium Electric Forklifts (Applied Sciences 2025)

## Definition

A 2025 paper measuring a high-voltage lithium 3-ton forklift prototype at 1.11 kWh per hour.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- **Prototype:** 3-ton (3,000 kg) forklift retrofitted to 320 V, 100 Ah, 32 kWh LiFePO4; peak current 44.5 A; maximum output power 14.3 kW; tested to JB/T 3300-2024 on a flat paved surface with a 3,000 kg load.
- **Result:** 1.11 kWh per hour (6.16 kWh over 5.56 h) against about 4.05 kWh per hour the paper gives for traditional low-voltage 3-ton trucks; energy split 53.1 percent full-load lifting and 36.7 percent travel; 37 cycles averaged 108.81 s.
- **Limits:** one prototype, lab conditions, charger efficiency not reported, no multi-shift data. Battery-side energy, not wall energy (C310).
