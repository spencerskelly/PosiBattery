# Report Metered Forklift Charging Energy for LCFS Credits

## Definition

A site owner measures the energy delivered to forklifts, converts it to reportable kWh, registers each charging location and files quarterly reports, so credits can be earned and verified.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: analyst hypothesis, drafted 2026-10-17.** The rules are from [[Document - CARB LCFS Guidance 25-01 Forklift Fueling Reporting (2025-10)]] (second-hand reading). The steps and the effort involved are reasoned, not observed.
- Steps (analyst wording): choose a meter point (upstream of the charger with an efficiency adjustment, at the charger output, or on the forklift); keep the meter within 5 percent accuracy; register each charging location as fueling supply equipment; collect quarterly kWh per location; apply the charger efficiency (85 percent if none is listed); file by the quarterly due dates; keep meter serial numbers and rental records for verification from the 2026 data year.
- Completion: reports filed on time and a third-party verifier accepts the data.
- **Not evidenced:** whether customers pursue credits at all (participation is optional), what the credits are worth (IB-248), and who does this work inside a customer.
