# Document - ZVEI Leaflet 8e Service Life of Traction Batteries (2014-02)

## Definition

ZVEI leaflet 8e on what determines the service life of traction batteries and how it is estimated.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Lab baseline (EN 60254-1, end at 80 percent of nominal capacity): PzS typically 1500 cycles, PzV about 900 cycles; lab results do not transfer directly to practice.
- Depth of discharge: maximum 80 percent of nominal capacity; more is a deep discharge with severe effects. PzV at 60 percent reaches a life comparable to PzS at 80 percent; PzV at 80 percent has about two thirds of the indicated life.
- Temperature: nominal 30 C, maximum 55 C (PzS) and 45 C (PzV). In the leaflet's examples an average of 45 C instead of 30 C cut expected life from 60 to 29 months, but those examples differ in other parameters, so the figure is not a clean temperature effect.
- Charging: unmatched characteristics and obsolete chargers cause under- or overcharging; opportunity charging without electrolyte mixing or without regular equalizing charges causes acid stratification, corrosion and mass damage; very high rapid-charge currents raise temperature and corrosion; regenerative braking adds transient high charge currents.
- High discharge currents and mechanical stress also age the battery. Does not cover watering, maintenance routines or PzB.
