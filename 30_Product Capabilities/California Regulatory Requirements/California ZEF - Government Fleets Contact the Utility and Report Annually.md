# California ZEF - Government Fleets Contact the Utility and Report Annually

## Definition

A state or local government fleet operator shall contact its electric utility by 2026-03-31 for each separately metered location with affected forklifts, and shall file annual reports (due 30 April for large fleets, 30 September for small fleets).

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It binds the party named in the definition, not a charger. Product consequences are the derived requirements REQ-00051 to REQ-00053 (analyst-proposed).
- Second-hand reading; see C-items in [[Battery Product Landscape Conflicts and Open Questions]] and [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]].
- **Status:** in force for government fleets. The 30 April and 30 September report dates come from the register's reading of CARB's government-fleet brochure; confirm in the raw document.
