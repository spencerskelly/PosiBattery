# Anderson IBC Industrial Battery Connector

## Definition

Anderson IBC: DIN-style industrial battery connector with battery monitoring and durability features and an IP68 waterproof kit named in a news item.

## Notes

- The landing page gives no current rating, voltage or truck fit. Source: <https://www.andersonpower.com/in/en/material-handling.html> (T1, second-hand). Open: IBC datasheet.

- **Update 2026-10-14 (IBC 320 sheets):** only the IBC 320 is documented (no IBC 80 or 160 found); 600 V UL/CSA, 150 V EN1175; current by wire size 290-460 A (UL); cartridges Air Tube, 4 Aux, 10 Aux (gold, up to 10 signal wires); latching or non-latching handle; IP68 kit (IP23 without); NTC temperature sensing; panel-mount; seven coding pins including Type II dry and wet; EN 1175:2020 excluding the 10 Aux signals. See [[Connector Configuration Options Register]]. Sources: <https://www.mouser.lt/pdfDocs/af358045-4432-4ad4-8159-7c8ce2014460_SS-IBC320.pdf>; <https://www.mouser.com/catalog/specsheets/Anderson%20Power%20Products_09-08-2025_IBC-Series.pdf> (T1, second-hand).
