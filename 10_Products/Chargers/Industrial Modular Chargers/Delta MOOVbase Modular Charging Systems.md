# Delta MOOVbase Modular Charging Systems

## Definition

Delta MOOVbase wired charging family: 1 phase (three to five PM1350 modules, 1-6.75 kW), 3 phase (one to ten PM3000 modules, 3-32 kW) and a multi-port system charging up to three batteries of different voltages from one AC connection.

## Notes

- PM3000: 3 kW or 70 A per module, CAN bus. Voltage range, efficiency and battery types are not on the pages read; the category page lists both phases as '1 to 32 kW' (C263). Sources: <https://www.deltaww.com/en-US/products/Industrial-Battery-Charging>; <https://www.deltaww.com/en-US/products/Industrial-Battery-Charging/MOOV-base-3-Phase-Modular-Charging-System>; <https://www.deltaww.com/en-US/products/Industrial-Battery-Charging/MOOV-base-1-Phase-Modular-Charging-System>; <https://www.deltaww.com/en-US/products/Industrial-Battery-Charging/5779> (T1, second-hand).
