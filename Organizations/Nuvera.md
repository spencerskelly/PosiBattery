# Nuvera

## Definition

Hyster-Yale fuel cell business whose PowerEdge hybrid units replace lead-acid batteries in Class I and II trucks.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Battery Maker.
- The 2024 ranking lists Nuvera among the Hyster-Yale brands. Source: Modern Materials Handling via Logistics Management (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/top_20_lift_truck_suppliers_2024>
- Nuvera's PowerEdge hybrid fuel cell product replaces standard lead-acid batteries with a hydrogen fuel cell system, sealed maintenance-free batteries, compressed hydrogen storage and a computerized control system, in C Series (Class I) and R Series (Class II) models. Source: Lift and Access news item (dated) (T2 (dated)), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.liftandaccess.com/news/hybrid-fuel-cell-forklifts-introduced>
