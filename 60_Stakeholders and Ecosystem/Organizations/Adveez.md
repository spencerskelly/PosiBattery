# Adveez

## Definition

Vendor of an airside telematics and asset monitoring system for ground support equipment.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Software Vendor.
- Adveez's Asset and Operations Monitoring System is described as a telematics solution engineered for airside environments and airport GSE, with live GPS and maintenance data. Source: Fortbrand GSE page (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://fortbrand.com/gse-products/>
- Mallaghan says the SkyBelt's sensor technology and telematics data are powered by Adveez. Source: Ground Support Worldwide (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.aviationpros.com/ground-support-worldwide/gse/article/55018081/mallaghan-expands-into-the-belt-loader-market>
