# Linde Rotating Operator Workstation

## Definition

Linde rotating driver's workstation that turns toward the work; on 10 to 32 t trucks the whole cabin turns 180 degrees left and 65 degrees right.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Linde rotating operator workstation that turns the seat or whole cabin toward the work so the operator need not twist.

**Marketed features:**
- Reduces musculoskeletal strain and fatigue (BGHW test to DIN EN 1005-4)
- Better view for large and hard-to-see loads
- On 10-32 t trucks the cab rotates 180 degrees left and 65 degrees right
- Factory or retrofit on E25-E50 (387-388) and H20-H80 (391-396)
