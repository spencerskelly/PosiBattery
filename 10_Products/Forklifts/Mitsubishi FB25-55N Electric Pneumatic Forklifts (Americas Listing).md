# Mitsubishi FB25-55N Electric Pneumatic Forklifts (Americas Listing)

## Definition

Mitsubishi FB electric pneumatic and cushion forklifts named in dealer listings (FB25AN, FB45N, FB50N, FB50NH, FB55NH, FB50CN), 5,000 to 11,000 lb.

## Notes

- Listing data only: FB25AN 5,000 lb pneumatic, 171.5 in, 12.4 mph; FB50N 11,000 lb pneumatic, 130 in, 11.0 mph; other model names appear in links. Battery voltage, options and lithium availability not read (IB-200). Source: <https://www.allmachines.com/forklifts/view-all?brand=mitsubishi&category=stackers&page=1&sortBy=default> (T3, second-hand).
