# Cell Air Distribution Tubing

## Definition

Tubing and distribution path that routes circulation air from the air source into the individual flooded-battery cells.

## Notes

- Midac explicitly describes tubes in each cell supplied by a charger-mounted pump.
- HOPPECKE explicitly describes air being blown into each cell; cell-level air distribution hardware is therefore required at **>=95% engineering confidence**, though the exact tubing/manifold construction is not published.
- HAWKER's air-pump circulation system strongly implies a cell-distribution path, but the current source does not describe its construction.
- This reusable Object does not assume a specific manifold, quick connector, check valve, or tube material.
