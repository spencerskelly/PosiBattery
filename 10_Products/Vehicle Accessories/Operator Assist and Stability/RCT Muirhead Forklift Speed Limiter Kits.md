# RCT Muirhead Forklift Speed Limiter Kits

## Definition

RCT Muirhead generic forklift speed limiter kits for cable-throttle (12 V, 24 V) and fly-by-wire trucks.

## Notes

- Kits 6000 (12 V), 7000 (24 V) with VDO E-Gas actuator; 7290 generic fly-by-wire dual TPS (voltage not listed). Interfaces with RCT speed zone systems. Fault-safe overspeed circuit. The 12 V and 24 V kits point at internal-combustion truck wiring; use on 36-80 V electric trucks is not stated (C239). Source: <https://rct-global.com/datasheet/forklift-speed-limiters> (T1, second-hand).
