# Cost Drivers

## Purpose

This folder contains the migrated warehouse and MHE cost-driver research series. The notes synthesize public evidence, product landscapes, KPI frameworks, ROI logic, risks, and product-adjacency hypotheses.

These are **research notes**, not canonical model facts. Product opportunities, functions, architectures, needs, metrics, and recommendations inside them remain hypotheses until separately supported and modeled.
