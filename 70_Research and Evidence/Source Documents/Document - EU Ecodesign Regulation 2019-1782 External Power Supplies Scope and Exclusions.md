# Document - EU Ecodesign Regulation 2019-1782 External Power Supplies Scope and Exclusions

## Definition

Document - EU Ecodesign Regulation 2019-1782 External Power Supplies Scope and Exclusions.

## Provenance

- **Original web address:** <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2019/1782/data.html>.
- **Author or publisher:** UK-retained copy of Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/1782, plus energy-efficient-products.ec.europa.eu product page.
- **Period or date:** Applies from 2020-04-01; replacement by Regulation (EU) 2025/2052 from 2028 (date inconsistent on the Commission page).
- **Evidence tier:** T2 ([[Landscape Evidence and Modeling Conventions]]).
- **Read:** 2026-10-18, through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

## Notes

**Region:** EU.

**Content recorded:**
- Scope is external power supplies up to 250 W for household, office, consumer and leisure equipment (Annex I list). Art. 1(2)(c) excludes battery chargers without power supply function.
- Industrial or traction battery chargers are not mentioned, so no ecodesign charger-efficiency duty for forklift chargers is shown. This is the fetch tool's reading of the text, not a statement in it [S].
- Commission page says the Regulation is replaced by 2025/2052 from 2028-01-01 while a link on it says 14/12/2028: unresolved, read the official text.
- Consequence for the regional brief: no EU equivalent to the US federal and California charger efficiency rules was found for forklift chargers. Absence not proven.
