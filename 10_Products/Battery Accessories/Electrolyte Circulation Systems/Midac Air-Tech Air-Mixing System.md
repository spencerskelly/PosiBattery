# Midac Air-Tech Air-Mixing System

## Definition

Midac air-mixing system that fits the MDL-MBS lids.

## Notes

- Catalogue: lids accept push-in plugs and the Air-Tech air-mixing system; air mixing is an optional VLA feature. Whether Air-Tech is the same product as the dealer-listed [[Midac EUW Electrolyte Circulation System]] is not stated (C228).
