# Aircraft Service Envelope

## Definition

Immediate operating area around an aircraft where ground-support equipment approaches, docks, services and withdraws while avoiding aircraft contact and ground-crew hazards.

## Notes

- Use this context for GSE approach, docking, proximity indication, automatic slow/stop and equipment-stow interlock scenarios.
- Representative evidence includes [[TLD Aircraft Safety Docking]] and other aircraft-proximity products.
- This is narrower than [[Airport Ground-Support Operating Area]] and may be used as a specialized operational context.
