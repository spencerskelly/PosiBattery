# Document - Apache Electric Vehicles How Much Electricity Does a Forklift Use in a Year (blog)

## Definition

A vendor blog stating that electric forklifts use 10,000 to 20,000 kWh a year, with an internally inconsistent table.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- **Headline:** most electric forklifts use 10,000 to 20,000 kWh a year. By shifts (8 or 16 hours x 250 days): single about 6,500 to 8,000 kWh; double 13,000 to 16,000; continuous 19,000 to 25,000 or more.
- **Cost:** $1,200 to $2,800 a year; example 15,000 kWh at $0.14 per kWh = $2,100. The $960 line in its table is below its own $1,200 minimum.
- **Internal inconsistency:** the article's heavy-duty rate of 12 to 15 kWh per hour would give 24,000 to 30,000 kWh for one shift, above its table (C310).
- **Interest:** the author sells golf carts and promotes Liftron forklifts; no independent data cited.
