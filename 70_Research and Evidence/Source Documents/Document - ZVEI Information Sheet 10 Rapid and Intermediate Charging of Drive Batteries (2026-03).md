# Document - ZVEI Information Sheet 10 Rapid and Intermediate Charging of Drive Batteries (2026-03)

## Definition

2026 ZVEI information sheet on rapid and intermediate charging of industrial truck drive batteries, covering lead-acid and other chemistries.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Title and scope differ from the 2011 leaflet 10e (all drive batteries in intralogistics, not only lead-acid traction); the text read does not mention the 2011 edition (C339).
- Lead-acid: usual optimal state-of-charge window 20 to 80 percent (maker-specific); regular intermediate charging at high states of charge can shorten life.
- Maximum charge current as a rule 0.2 C for standard lead-acid and 0.5 C for advanced lead-acid (CN = C5 per IEC 60254-1).
- Temperature-controlled charging recommended for intermediate charging of lead-acid; electrolyte circulation recommended for liquid electrolyte; elevated temperature reduces life, active cooling can mitigate at a cost in efficiency.
- Refers to IEC TS 61044:2021 (opportunity charging of lead-acid traction batteries), ZVEI sheet 14 and IEC 62485-3 for details. Contains no gassing voltage, equalizing rule, water or stratification text.
