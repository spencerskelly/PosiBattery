# Ampure Industrial Portfolio Overlap and Synergy Map

## Definition

Evidence-led map of where PosiCharge and Power Designers Sibex offers complement, overlap or could share a platform. It records intersections and open questions; it does not decide product rationalization.

## Notes

### Classification values

| Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Complementary | Different customer jobs, segments or ranges; together they widen coverage |
| Overlapping | Same customer job and segment; customer or channel confusion is possible |
| Migration candidate | One offer could plausibly replace the other over time (question, not decision) |
| Shared-platform candidate | Common hardware, firmware, communications, cloud or manufacturing could serve both |
| Unresolved | Evidence is not sufficient to classify |

### Record fields

Offer space · PosiCharge offer · Power Designers Sibex offer · Segment and customer job · Classification · Evidence · Open question · Next action.

### Questions this map must answer

1. Which offers address the same customer jobs?
2. Where does each family sit by segment, chemistry, charging strategy, voltage and power, environment and channel?
3. What is the path for PosiLink and PowerCharge.NET?
4. Which group assets close gaps already recorded for PosiCharge?
5. Where could brand or channel overlap confuse customers or cannibalize profitable offers?
6. Which external competitors should be compared with the combined portfolio rather than PosiCharge alone?

### Guardrails

- Keep overlaps and conflicts visible; do not resolve them by deleting or merging records.
- A row moves out of Unresolved only with linked evidence; log the change in [[Research Change and Decision Tracker]].
- Automotive and Aftermarket EVSE offers are not entered here unless a documented industrial intersection exists. See [[Ampure Group Portfolio Context]].
