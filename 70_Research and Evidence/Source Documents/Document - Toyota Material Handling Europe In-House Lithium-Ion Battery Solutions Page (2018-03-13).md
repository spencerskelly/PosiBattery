# Document - Toyota Material Handling Europe In-House Lithium-Ion Battery Solutions Page (2018-03-13)

## Definition

Toyota Material Handling Europe news page announcing an "in-house lithium-ion battery solution" for its electric trucks at the Logimat fair, March 2018.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand). The URL given by a search hit redirected from a CDN host to the main site; the main-site address was read.

- Calls the battery "in-house" and says it applies "Toyota's group competence"; it does not say who makes the cells, packs or chargers.
- Modular sizes; opportunity charging; extended warranty of 7 years; chemistry not named; no voltages or capacities; no compatibility statement; no mention of third-party batteries.
- Dated 2018: sets the earliest in-house claim among the OEMs read other than Jungheinrich (2017). It shows the in-house move is not new (C323), and says nothing on whether it is current.
