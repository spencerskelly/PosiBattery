# Start and Stop Charge Automatically

## Definition

Begin and end a charge without an operator plugging or unplugging.

## Notes

- **Solution link added 2026-10-14 (reviewed, owner "y"):** three alternative Designs, any one enough: [[Inductive Wireless Charging]] (5 of 8 performers: Wiferion, Resonant Link, EnerSys NexSys AIR), [[Conductive Auto-Contact Charging]] (2: Raymond Courier AutoCharge, Grenzebach) and [[Automated Connector Charging]] (a mechanism mates the connector; carried by Staubli QCC, not by a performer). Seegrid Auto Charge states no Design.
- Stated: NexSys AIR fully automated charging; etaLINK starts when the vehicle is on the station (etaTRAY in under one second); Resonant Link start and stop automatically (CEO statement); AutoCharge triggers by schedule, low battery or inactivity; Grenzebach automatic intermediate charging.
- Links to products are made only where a source states the behavior. No Requirement is linked yet.
