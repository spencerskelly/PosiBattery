# EnerSys Aeromatic Circulation System

## Definition

Optional electrolyte circulation with an Aeromatic diaphragm pump for HAWKER Perfect Plus batteries.

## Notes

- Pumps low-rate air into each cell to prevent stratification; pump filter replaced at least yearly; charge factor 1.07 with circulation. Source: HAWKER Perfect Plus instructions (T1, second-hand), <https://enersys.com/4a6cd5/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/hawker/perfect-plus/emea/hawker-perfect-plus-instruction-for-use-english.pdf>
