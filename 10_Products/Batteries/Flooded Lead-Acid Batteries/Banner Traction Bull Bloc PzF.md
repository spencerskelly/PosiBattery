# Banner Traction Bull Bloc PzF

## Definition

Banner Traction Bull Bloc PzF 6 V and 12 V block batteries with positive tubular plates for small traction, up to 1,200 cycles at 80 percent discharge.

## Notes

- Banner's data sheet says the Bloc PzF has liquid electrolyte, positive tubular plates and negative grid plates, 6 V and 12 V blocks, a polypropylene casing, high antimony content and up to 1,200 cycles at 80 percent K5 discharge, for lifting platforms, golf carts, small stackers and cleaning machines. Source: Banner Bloc PzF data sheet (2016) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://bannerbatterien.com/energysolutions/upload/files/Folder_TB_Bloc_PzF_gb_2016.pdf>
- **Not stated:** the full capacity range (the sheet lists types PzF 36 to PzF 320).
