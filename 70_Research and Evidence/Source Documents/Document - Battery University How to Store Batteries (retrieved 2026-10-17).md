# Document - Battery University How to Store Batteries (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

Battery University storage guidance, including lead-acid state of charge, temperature and recharge thresholds.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Store lead-acid charged, cool and dry; recommended 15 C, extreme range -40 to 50 C.
- Recharge when the battery falls to 70 percent state of charge: 2.07 V per cell open circuit (12.42 V on a 12 V pack, specific gravity about 1.218); thresholds quoted for starter batteries (specific gravity below 1.225). Traction cells have a nominal 1.29 specific gravity, so these thresholds do not transfer (C345).
- Retention of a full-charged lead-acid battery: about 97 percent after one year at 0 C, 90 percent at 25 C, 62 percent at 40 C, 38 percent after six months at 60 C (no source cited).
- Low charge causes sulfation, partly reversible by a topping charge if caught early. No date or author shown.
