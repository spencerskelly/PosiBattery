# Site Safety Manager

## Definition

A person accountable for forklift and ground-vehicle safety on a site: operator training, access, near-miss and impact follow-up, and pedestrian separation.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: hypothesis.** HYPOTHESIS. No product source names a safety role. The only support is that OSHA's truck standard puts duties (operator competence, inspection) on the employer, so someone on site holds them. Confirm with customer sources before promoting.
- **Role in the customer organization:** Candidate owner of the safety needs; no source in the vault names this role.
- **Relation to the product (analyst classification, hypothesis):** buyer or influencer.
- **Needs this role takes part in:** see the Use Case notes that list this Actor as a participant (query `participants` in [[README_Customer Needs|Customer Needs]]); the product to need route is in [[Product to Customer Need Map]].
- **Evidence:**
