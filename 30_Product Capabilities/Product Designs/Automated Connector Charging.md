# Automated Connector Charging

## Definition

A powered or robotic mechanism mates a connector to the vehicle, so the connector has contacts but no person plugs it in.

## Notes

- Examples found so far: [[Staubli QCC Quick Charging Connection]] (self-aligning connector with misalignment tolerance). The vault holds no robot-arm charging product for industrial trucks yet (IB-206).
