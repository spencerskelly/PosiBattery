# Document - SRP E-Tech Forklift Program Flyer (program year 2022-23)

## Definition

SRP's 2022-23 forklift rebate flyer, which required rebated chargers to be network-enabled and demand-response capable.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- **Forklifts:** up to $2,000 per forklift replacing an internal combustion truck; up to $500 per additional electric forklift; Class 3 pallet jack $200; small-business bonus (under 145,000 annual kWh) $500 for up to two forklifts.
- **Chargers:** up to $150 per high-frequency charger replacing an SCR or ferroresonant unit; 24 to 48 V; at least 89 percent power conversion efficiency; **network-enabled and capable of demand response**.
- **Period:** May 2022 to April 2023 (expired); cap $75,000 per customer.
- **Use:** the only source read that ties a charger rebate to network-enabled demand-response capability (C312).
