# Charge Battery from Standard Power Outlet

## Definition

Charge from an ordinary wall socket through a built-in charger, so no dedicated charging supply is needed.

## Notes

- Behavior found in product descriptions. Product links only where a source states the behavior.
- No Requirement is linked (intentional gap).
