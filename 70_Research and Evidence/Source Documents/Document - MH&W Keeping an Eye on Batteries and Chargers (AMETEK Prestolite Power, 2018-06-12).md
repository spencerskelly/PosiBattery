# Document - MH&W Keeping an Eye on Batteries and Chargers (AMETEK Prestolite Power, 2018-06-12)

## Definition

Material Handling and Warehousing article by AMETEK Prestolite Power on battery and charger safety.

## Notes

Facts below were read through the fetch tool's summary of the page, not from the raw page.

- Written in the voice of AMETEK Prestolite Power for National Forklift Safety Day. Cites NIOSH (most common forklift injuries are workers struck, crushed or pinned) and OSHA (heavy suspended batteries, sulfuric acid, hydrogen gas that can ignite, short circuits and arcing). Training and preparation are urged by a vendor business manager.
- No customer voice, incident counts, costs or downtime figures. It restates hazards that regulators describe; it does not show that customers rank them.
