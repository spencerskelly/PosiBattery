# Linde 90 V Lithium-Ion Battery

## Definition

Linde 90 V lithium-ion battery family for 2 to 8 t trucks (24.1, 36.2, 60.3, 64.7, 66.3, 84.6 and 118.4 kWh versions).

## Notes

- Linde says its lithium-ion battery versions for E20 to E35 trucks are 24.1, 60.3 and 84.6 kWh, for 3.5 to 5 t trucks 36.2 and 66.3 kWh, and for 6 to 8 t trucks 64.7 and 118.4 kWh, with the new 90 V batteries compatible with 9, 17 and 30 kW chargers. Source: Logistics Business (2019-10-17) (T2 (dated)), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisticsbusiness.com/materials-handling-warehousing/forklift-technology/linde-material-handling-expands-lithium-ion-portfolio/>
