## Notes

**Content recorded:**
- North America classes 1 to 5: 347,677 units in 2021 (record), 344,330 retail orders in 2022. Electric (classes 1 to 3) about 72% of the market in 2021 and 65% in 2022; internal combustion (classes 4 and 5) about 28% and 35%. ITA regular members make and sell more than 80% of powered industrial trucks sold in North America.
