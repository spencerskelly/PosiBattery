# Mid-Battery Voltage Tap

## Definition

Extra voltage connection at the middle of the battery (a balance wire) so the device can compare the two halves.

## Notes

- Stated for Wi-iQ4 (gray balance wire, with fuse) and Exide EasyMonitor (middle voltage tap detects imbalances).
- Citations are listed under Sources below. A design characteristic found in products, not a decision by us.
