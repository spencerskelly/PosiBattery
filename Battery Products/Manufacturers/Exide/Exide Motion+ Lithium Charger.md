# Exide Motion+ Lithium Charger

## Definition

Exide charger controlled by the Solition lithium battery management system.

## Notes

- The Solition Light Traction Battery leaflet lists control of the Motion+ Lithium Charger among the functions of its battery management system. Source: Exide Solition leaflet (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://exidegroup.com/us/en/document/solition-light-traction-battery-leaflet>
