# Wiferion etaTRAY Battery Tray

## Definition

Wiferion etaTRAY: a forklift retrofit lithium-ion battery tray with an integrated etaLINK 3000 receiving unit, 24-48 V, 60-180 A.

## Notes

- Modular capacity sizes; the receiver is in the tray, so the charge path is the wireless pad rather than a plug. Source: <https://www.wiferion.com/us/products> (T1, second-hand).

- **Update 2026-10-14 (etaTRAY page):** 3-6 kW, 15-60 V charging, 60-120 A, 100-240 Ah, 12/24/48 V packs, air gap 15-40 mm, +/-30 mm, IP65/IP68; replaces the lead-acid (PzS) tray; page names STILL, Jungheinrich and Linde as compatible without models. This differs from the products page values in the definition above (24-48 V, 60-180 A) - see C258. Sources: <https://www.wiferion.com/en/products/inductive-forklift-battery-charging-batterytray-etatray/> (T1); <https://www.plantautomation-technology.com/products/wiferion/etatray> (T3).
