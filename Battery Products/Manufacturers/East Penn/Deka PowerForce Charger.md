# Deka PowerForce Charger

## Definition

East Penn charger designed for Deka motive power batteries, lead and lithium, with an integrated touchscreen.

## Notes

- East Penn describes Deka PowerForce as an exclusive, fully integrated charging system for all Deka motive power batteries, usable with lead and lithium, charging flooded or gel, about 95 percent efficient, fast, opportunity and conventional, with a color touchscreen, 14 x 14 x 16 in, and an integrated breakaway connector. Source: East Penn news item (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.eastpennmanufacturing.com/?p=6135>
- **Unknown:** who builds the PowerForce hardware; East Penn describes it as Deka's own system without stating the manufacturer.
