# Philadelphia Scientific Blinky Watering Monitors

## Definition

Philadelphia Scientific Blinky line (Smart Blinky Remote, Smart Blinky, Blinky Basic) of LED watering monitors, announced December 2015.

## Notes

- One family note; variants: **Smart Blinky Remote** (green/red, remote edge-mountable LED), **Smart Blinky** (red/green flashing), **Blinky Basic** (green only, off when water is needed). All UL Classified. Relation to [[Philadelphia Scientific SmartBlinky Pro]] is not stated (C240). Source: <https://www.mhlnews.com/new-products/forklift-battery-watering-monitors-new-products> (T2, second-hand).
