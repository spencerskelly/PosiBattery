# Jungheinrich ETV C16 and C20

## Definition

Jungheinrich electric sit-down moving-mast reach trucks distributed in North America by Mitsubishi Logisnext.

## Notes

- The ETV C16 and C20 reach trucks with solid pneumatic tires reach lift heights up to 24 ft, carry up to 4,400 lb and work in aisles as narrow as nine feet; they are distributed in North America by Mitsubishi Caterpillar Forklift America (the earlier name of the Logisnext Americas group). Source: Supply Chain 24/7 (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.supplychain247.com/article/jungheinrich_lift_trucks_undergo_product_upgrades>
