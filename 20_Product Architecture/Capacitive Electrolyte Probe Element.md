# Capacitive Electrolyte Probe Element

## Definition

Physical probe element whose capacitance changes with electrolyte presence and is interpreted by the surrounding level-sensing electronics.

## Notes

- Flow-Rite publicly identifies capacitive sensing for its Eagle Eye sensor range and states that no sensing current flows through the probe into the cell.
- This component-level abstraction does not claim a specific dielectric geometry, electrode construction, capacitance range, or supplier part number.
