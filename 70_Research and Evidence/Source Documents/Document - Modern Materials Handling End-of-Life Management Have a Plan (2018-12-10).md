## Notes

**Content recorded:**
- Toyota Material Handling: depreciation schedules can mismatch use, e.g. seven-year depreciation 'when effectively the usable life is over in five years'; a fleet running 4,000 hours a year depreciated over 7 to 10 years. Customers who once retired trucks at 12,000 hours now run them to 15,000 to 20,000 hours.
- UniCarriers Americas: trucks staying 8 to 10 years with large, intensive users; customers decided to keep internal combustion forklifts 8 years and electric 10 years; replacement used to be closer to every 5 years; a five-year fair-market-value lease may be returned with another good five years or 10,000 hours left.
- Author's statement, no source quoted: 60% to 70% of forklifts are leased. No fleet average age stated.
