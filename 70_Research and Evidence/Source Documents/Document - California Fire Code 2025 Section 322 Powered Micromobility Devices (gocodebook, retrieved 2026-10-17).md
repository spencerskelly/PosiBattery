# Document - California Fire Code 2025 Section 322 Powered Micromobility Devices (gocodebook, retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

Third-party web reproduction of section 322 of the 2025 California Fire Code, kept to record that its battery-charging-area provisions concern powered micromobility devices and not forklifts.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), from a reproduction site. The page says it is not a substitute for the official publication.

- **Why this note exists:** a search for "IFC 2024 battery charging areas lithium-ion forklift" returned a hit titled "2024 International Fire Code (IFC) - 322.4 Battery charging areas." That section number belongs to the micromobility section, so a reader could mistake it for a lithium forklift rule (C322).
- **322.1 scope:** powered micromobility devices that use lithium-ion or lithium metal batteries. The page gives no size, weight or speed thresholds. Exceptions: personally owned devices in homes, and charging a single device in any occupancy when its owner does it. The text does not mention forklifts or powered industrial trucks.
- **322.2 and 322.3:** charge per the listing and maker instructions with original or listed equipment; devices listed to UL 2272 or UL 2849 as applicable.
- **322.4 charging rooms (where approved):** listed devices and chargers only; direct receptacles, no extension cords or power taps; no combustibles or hazardous materials stored; not in or blocking egress; no stacking or enclosed-cabinet charging of removable batteries unless the cabinet is approved for it; 18 in between removable batteries during charging unless separated by fire-resistant material; the room has a fire alarm using air-aspirating smoke detection or radiant energy-sensing detection.
- **322.5:** the fire safety plan includes emergency response for fires in these batteries.
- **Use:** these values are not forklift requirements and are not copied into any Requirement note. They show the kind of lithium-specific charging-room text that an adopted code can contain, and that the section 309 text read for forklifts has none of it.
