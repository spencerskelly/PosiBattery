# Oshkosh AeroTech Commander 30i Cargo Loader

## Definition

Oshkosh AeroTech cargo loader with optional aircraft proximity detection and iOPS equipment monitoring; an electric version has its own page.

## Notes

- Oshkosh AeroTech says the Commander 30i offers optional Aircraft Proximity Detection sensors and iOPS equipment monitoring technology with diagnostics, and has an electric cargo loader page (30i-E). Source: Oshkosh AeroTech Commander 30i page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://oshkoshaerotech.com/products-and-services/ground-support-equipment/cargo-loaders/commander-30i-e-electric-cargo-loader>
- Oshkosh AeroTech says the Commander offers dual-language controls and PLC options, JetDock automatic docking options, optional APD sensors and optional iOPS monitoring, solid maintenance-free tires and a lithium-ion or lead-acid battery; the Customer Care catalog adds field kits such as APD, a center lift module, brake upgrades, bumper skirts and lighting. Source: Oshkosh AeroTech product pages and Customer Care catalog (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://oshkoshaerotech.com/products-and-services/ground-support-equipment/cargo-loaders/commander-30i-e-electric-cargo-loader>
