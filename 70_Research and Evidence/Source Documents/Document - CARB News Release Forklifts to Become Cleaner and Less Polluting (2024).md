# Document - CARB News Release Forklifts to Become Cleaner and Less Polluting (2024)

## Definition

CARB's adoption announcement for the Zero-Emission Forklift regulation, with the statewide count of spark-ignited forklifts and CARB's benefit estimates.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic. CARB news release announcing adoption of the Zero-Emission Forklift regulation. The release date is not shown in the text read; the advisory and the fact sheet give the adoption month as June 2024.

- **Population:** more than 89,000 spark-ignited forklifts operate in California (the only statewide count found so far; it covers private and public fleets and is not split by class or fleet size).
- **Claimed benefits (CARB):** more than 2 tons of nitrogen oxide avoided per day by 2031; $7.5 billion in health benefits from 2026 to 2043; $2.7 billion in net fleet cost savings from lower operating costs.
- **Rule as announced:** from 2026 manufacturers cannot produce or sell Class IV and V LSI forklifts for California use; large fleets (26 or more) begin phasing out spark-ignited forklifts in 2028 and small fleets in 2029; no forklift has to be phased out before it is 10 years old.
- **Conflict:** the manufacturer sales prohibition from 2026 is not in effect as announced, because CARB's 2025 advisory says it will not stop manufacturers, dealers or rental agencies from producing or selling LSI forklifts (C280, [[Document - CARB ZEF Implementation Update Advisory (2025-05-29)]]).
