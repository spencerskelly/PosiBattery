# Document - Arianee Battery Passport for Material Handling Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

Passport-software vendor page on how the battery passport applies to material handling (forklift) batteries; the only source read that speaks to lead-acid traction batteries.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand). The vendor sells passport services, so scope claims are its own reading of the regulation.

- The obligation falls on the economic operator placing the battery on the EU market (manufacturer or importer), also for rental batteries; rental companies and users hold usage data that must feed the passport.
- Replacement batteries placed on the market after 2027-02-18 are in scope; retrofits are not addressed separately.
- Says **32 of 71** data points are mandatory for industrial batteries on 2027-02-18 (identifiers, manufacturer, chemistry, capacity, voltage, service life, state of health and usage history for legitimate-interest readers, disassembly instructions). The Commission news item gives no per-category count; a trade article gives 56 for EV and 59 for LMT and no industrial count (C335).
- Says a lead-acid traction battery above 2 kWh is an industrial battery in scope of the passport (regime depends on use, not chemistry).
- Due diligence 2027-08-18 with an exemption under EUR 40 million turnover (C334); recycled content declaration 2028-08-18.
- State of health must stay current after placement; suggests batch imports, BMS feeds or field scans.
