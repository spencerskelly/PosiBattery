# Godrej Multi-Ion Forklift Battery

## Definition

Godrej Multi-Ion forklift battery claiming up to 5,000 cycles, operation above 45 C ambient and a 7-year warranty.

## Notes

- A 2026 trade report says the Multi-Ion battery, developed with a deep-tech partner, delivers up to 5,000 cycles (about 60 percent above standard lithium-ion), is built for ambient temperatures above 45 C, has over 5,000 hours of real-world validation and a 7-year warranty. Source: pv magazine India (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/?p=15032>
- **Not stated:** chemistry label beyond 'Multi-Ion', voltages and capacities; the partner's name is cut from the retrieved text.

- **Official source found 2026-10-13:** [[Document - Godrej Multi-Ion Press Release (2026-05-14)]] <https://www.godrejenterprises.com/newsroom/press-releases/godrej-introduces-india-s-first-multi-ion-battery-technology-for-forklifts-advancing-clean-and-efficient-supply-chains> (read through a summarizing tool, IB-177). Launched 2026-05-14; up to 5,000 cycles ("nearly 60% higher than standard lithium-ion"); 7-year warranty; ambient above 45 C; 5,000 hours of validation; fast and opportunity charging; zero maintenance; up to 25 percent lower total cost of ownership; developed with an unnamed deep-tech battery innovator.
- **Conflict kept visible:** [[Godrej Lithium-Ion Forklift Battery]] already claims 5,000 cycles with cells developed with Log9 Materials, so "nearly 60% higher than standard lithium-ion" and Godrej's own 5,000-cycle lithium-ion figure cannot both be the baseline (C218). The Multi-Ion partner is not named; it is not assumed to be Log9.
