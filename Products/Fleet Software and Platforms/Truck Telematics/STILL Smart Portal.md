# STILL Smart Portal

## Definition

STILL portal that brings fleet information together, from operator management and impact detection to access control.

## Notes

- STILL's EXH-SF page says the Smart Portal brings together fleet information from operator management and impact detection to access control, and that PIN access is also available without it.
