# Lithium Battery Solutions (BSL)

## Definition

UK lithium forklift battery supplier, a wholly owned subsidiary of Windsor Materials Handling, selling BSLBATT-cell batteries for retrofit.

## Notes

- Managing director named as Stephen Burton; Windsor MH is its customer and a distributor; BSL plans a select dealer network. The cells' maker is called BSLBATT in the item; whether BSL and BSLBATT are one company is not stated.
