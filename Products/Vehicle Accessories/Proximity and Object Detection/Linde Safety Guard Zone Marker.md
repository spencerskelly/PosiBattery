# Linde Safety Guard Zone Marker

## Definition

Safety Guard module that defines zones with a maximum travel speed.

## Notes

- The brochure says the Zone Marker sets maximum travel speeds in defined zones, reduces material damage and noise and defuses accident hot spots; the KION catalog says it defines speed-restriction areas. Source: Linde Rhein-Ruhr safety brochure and KION North America catalog 2023 (T3/T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.lmh-rr.de/media/Linde-Rhein-Ruhr/02_PDF/Safety/broschuere_safety.pdf>
