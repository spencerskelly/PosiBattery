# OTTO Motors

## Definition

Waterloo, Ontario maker of autonomous mobile robots (OTTO 100, OTTO 1500) and the OTTO Lifter autonomous forklift, acquired by Rockwell Automation.

## Notes

- Relevant here as a battery and charging customer, not a battery product maker: its published case study ties charging strategy to missions per charge (3 to 4 missions for a previous AGV system against up to 36 for the OTTO 1500, which returns to charge at about 70 percent battery). Source: OTTO case study (T1); see C184.
- Rockwell Automation called OTTO its 'latest acquisition'; no deal date in the retrieved text. Source: Inside Logistics (T2).
