# Battery-Connected Product

## Definition

Reusable family for any product connected to a battery. Organizes the specialization family only.

## Notes

- Classification root for the landscape survey. It is not a product. It covers anything electrically, mechanically or informationally connected to a battery, whether installed on the battery or adjacent to it.
- Where a product sits (battery-mounted, vehicle-mounted, charger-side, site infrastructure) is recorded per product and per category as locus, not as a subtype, because the same definition can be used in different places.
- Working axes used in this survey (not governed properties): locus, chemistry, voltage class, application. See [[Battery Product Landscape]].

## Aliases

- Battery-adjacent product
