# Dispatch Vehicles to Tasks

## Definition

Assign tasks to vehicles or robots by availability and location, and in some products battery level.

## Notes

- **Solution link added 2026-10-14:** depends on [[Vehicle Task Dispatch Design]] (new software Design; owner asked that all aspects be modeled).
- Stated: OTTO Fleet Manager (battery level is one factor), MiR Fleet (location and availability; battery not named), Seegrid Fleet Central (dispatch), FleetRabbit (nearest suitable free truck; vendor article). Blue Yonder claims multi-vendor robot orchestration and labor interleaving, which is a looser match.
- No Requirement is linked.
