# Exide MARATHON Excell Battery

## Definition

Exide (GNB) MARATHON Excell PzS cells with low-antimony alloy: DIN EPzS 120-1,240 Ah and BS EPzB 110-800 Ah.

## Notes

- Watering interval 16 weeks (80 cycles), up to 24 weeks (120 cycles) with AIR; options electrolyte level sensor and automatic watering. [[Sense Electrolyte Level]] (V), [[Extended Watering Interval]] (V): <https://www.exidegroup.com/eu/sites/default/files/2021-08/GNB_MP_Overview_EN_web.pdf>. A catalog excerpt gives 40 to 200 working days (C233). Part of the family in [[Exide MARATHON Battery]]. Source: GNB motive power overview 2021-08 (T1, second-hand; parts garbled), <https://www.exidegroup.com/eu/sites/default/files/2021-08/GNB_MP_Overview_EN_web.pdf>
