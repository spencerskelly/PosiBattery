# Crown V-HFM3 Charger Stand

## Definition

Crown stand option for the V-HFM3 charger.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Floor-anchored stand for installing Crown V-HFM3 chargers at the point of use.

**Marketed features:**
- Multiple configuration options
- Installs chargers at the point of use, freeing warehouse floor space
- Anchored to the floor
- Places chargers at optimal working height
