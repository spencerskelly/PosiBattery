# Document - ZVEI Leaflet 23e Definition of Different Life Time Terms for Batteries (2013-08)

## Definition

Association leaflet (ZVEI, August 2013) defining life time terms so that stated lifetimes are compared like with like.

## Provenance

- **Repo file:** Downloads/23e_Definition_of_different_life_time_terms_for_batteries_2013-08.pdf (2 pages), added by the owner. Text was extracted from the file and read directly on 2026-10-18 (first-hand).
- **Source address:** https://www.zvei.org/ (the wishlist row gave the publisher address; the exact download address was not recorded where this shows only a site root).
- **Evidence tier:** T2, industry association leaflet, not a manufacturer sheet or a standard.

## Notes

- Generic term: life time. Three uses: design life (in theory; deduced from design of components and life-limiting parameters from endurance tests; the test standard or specification must be indicated); endurance (in the laboratory; measured under defined, partly standardized and partly accelerated conditions; subtopics endurance in cycles, in overcharge, in float service); service life (in practice; established from field experience under optimized conditions, the time in which a specified capacity or power can be used; optimum application and operation conditions must be specified; subtopic expected service life, calculated from field experience and endurance tests, with application and storage parameters stated).
- Terms the leaflet says to replace: expected life, endurance life time, cycle life time, float life time, service life expectation, practical service life.
- The leaflet states that only comparable facts should be assessed and that these definitions will be used in new and revised ZVEI leaflets. It gives no numbers.
- Relevance: Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 Annex IV asks for expected lifetime in cycles and calendar years under reference conditions ([[Document - EUR-Lex Regulation 2023-1542 Full Text as Published 2023-07-28 (Articles 3, 10, 13, 14, 77, 78 and Annexes IV, VII, XIII read)]]); the ZVEI terms are a possible vocabulary for that declaration (analyst reasoning, the regulation does not cite the leaflet).
