# Exide GNB Lithium Battery 2.0

## Definition

GNB lithium-ion battery launched at MODEX 2020 with a BMS and an option for remote communication to GNB Cloud.

## Notes

- The 2020 release says the battery has a specialized electronic BMS that monitors the status and health of each battery, an option for remote communication into GNB Cloud, and no watering.
