# Document - ZVEI Leaflet 6e Cleaning of Batteries (2006)

## Definition

ZVEI leaflet 6e on cleaning lead-acid traction batteries.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Purpose: prevent accidents, damage and loss of life; electrolyte residue forms a conductive layer (leakage current, self-discharge, sparking). Insulation resistance at least 50 ohm per volt nominal (EN 50272-3).
- Method: remove the battery from the vehicle, keep cell plugs in and closed, clean plastic parts only with water or wet cloths, no cleaning agents, no dry cloths, dry with compressed air or damp antistatic cloth (C343).
- High-pressure cleaning allowed with limits: at most 50 bar, nozzle at least 30 cm away, not more than 3 seconds on one spot, surface not above 60 C, machine setting at most 140 C.
- No cleaning frequency given.
