## Notes

**Content recorded:**
- Large spark-ignition (LSI) forklift population about 95,000 (Class V up to 12,000 lb 54%, Class IV 44%, Class V over 12,000 lb 2%); 45% of California's forklift population already zero-emission; in 2020 about 80,000 zero-emission forklifts operated in California, about 70,000 of them lead-acid. 89,000 LSI forklifts expected to be phased out.
- Dates: purchase restriction on LSI forklifts from 2026-01-01; phase-out of targeted LSI forklifts begins 2028-01-01 and extends to 2038. Estimated cumulative net fleet cost savings through 2043 USD 2.7bn; health benefits USD 7.5bn; social cost of carbon savings USD 0.25 to 1bn. The slides do not give battery prices, per-forklift costs or energy use.
