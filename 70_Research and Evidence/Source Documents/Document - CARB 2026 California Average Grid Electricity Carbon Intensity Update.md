# Document - CARB 2026 California Average Grid Electricity Carbon Intensity Update

## Definition

CARB's 2026 grid electricity carbon intensity of 65.07 gCO2e/MJ used for LCFS electricity reporting.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the business case. CARB document setting the grid electricity carbon intensity (CI) for 2026 LCFS reporting.

- **Value:** the average CI for California grid electricity for use in 2026 reporting is 65.07 gCO2e/MJ (pathway code ELC000L00072026), down from 70.05 gCO2e/MJ used for 2025.
- **Smart charging:** hourly values by quarter range from 1.44 gCO2e/MJ to 123.36 gCO2e/MJ.
- **Not stated:** any forklift-specific CI. Whether forklift charging uses the annual average is an assumption in the business case.
