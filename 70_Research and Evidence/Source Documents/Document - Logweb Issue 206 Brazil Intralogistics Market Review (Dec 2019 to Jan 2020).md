## Notes

**Content recorded:**
- Brazilian forklift and pallet-truck units sold grew about 12% in 2019 (KION South America); other manufacturers and dealers quote 10% to 17%.

- Electric gaining share against LPG; warehouse equipment numerically passed combustion models in 2019 (rental firm); CLARK delivered 50% of its 2019 sales with lithium-ion; low-cost Chinese brands taking share (Tradimaq). No absolute unit sales, fleet size or rental fleet size.
