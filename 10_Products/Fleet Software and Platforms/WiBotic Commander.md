# WiBotic Commander

## Definition

WiBotic fleet energy management software for robot and drone fleets: charge scheduling, battery health data, charger status and bulk updates.

## Notes

- Launched 2021-02-10; an API controls charging per robot schedule, per-cycle data supports battery analysis, and settings and firmware are pushed fleet-wide. Source: [[Document - WiBotic Commander Robot Report Article (2021-02-10)]] (T2). <https://www.therobotreport.com/wibotic-commander-offers-energy-management-robot-fleets/>
- Supported chargers and integrations are not stated; WiBotic's charger notes are listed on [[WiBotic]].
