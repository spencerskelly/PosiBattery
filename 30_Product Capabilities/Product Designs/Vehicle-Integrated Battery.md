# Vehicle-Integrated Battery

## Definition

A battery built into the truck as part of its structure or sealed system, not designed to be lifted out and swapped by the operator or dealer as a unit.

## Notes

- Design characteristic found in product descriptions, not a decision by us. Counterpart of [[Drop-In Battery Cavity]]. Only vendor wording ("integrated", "permanently integrated", "built-in") is used; whether a vendor's "integrated" pack can still be unbolted is usually not stated (C246).
- No Requirement is linked (intentional gap).

## Aliases

- Built-in battery
- Integrated battery pack
