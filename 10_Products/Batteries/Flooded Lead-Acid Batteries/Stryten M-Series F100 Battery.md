# Stryten M-Series F100 Battery

## Definition

Stryten economical flooded flat-plate battery family for light to medium duty.

## Notes

- Stryten's lineup sheet describes F100 as economical flooded flat-plate batteries for consistent, reliable power in light to medium duty applications, optimized for conventional charging. Source: Stryten lineup sheet (09/2024) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/14502/133723547947804003.pdf>
