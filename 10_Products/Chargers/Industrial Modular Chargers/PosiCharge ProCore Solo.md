# PosiCharge ProCore Solo

## Definition

PosiCharge single-phase ProCore charger listed under material handling charging.

## Notes

- **Owner statement 2026-10-14 (U):** when connected and the battery drops below a voltage, charging brings it to the optimal ready-state voltage. Recorded as [[Voltage-Triggered Ready-State Top-Up]]. The sheet gives no thresholds (IB-238).
- PosiCharge's Resources page lists 'ProCore Solo - Single Phase' under eMHE chargers with a spec sheet file (5x7_ProCore-Solo-1.pdf); no ratings are in the retrieved listing. Source: PosiCharge Resources page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://posicharge.com/product-resources/>
- **Still needed:** ratings, voltages, input, chemistries; read the spec sheet (see [[Document Wishlist]]).
- The ProCore Solo sheet (Reference Files/5x7_ProCore-Solo-1.pdf) says it is a single-phase charger for 120 V and 240 V with 1 and 2 module cabinets; models PCS-24-120 (24 V, 40 A), 24 V 90 A on 240 V, PCS-48-120 (36/48 V, 26 A) and PCS-48-240 (36/48 V, 52 A); 1.2 kW at 120 V and 2.7 kW at 240 V; efficiency up to 95 percent; 11.9 x 11.9 x 5.2 in; 25 lb (1 module) or 30 lb (2 modules) maximum; UL and cUL listed and CEC compliant; plug and go, float charge, no battery monitor required. Source: ProCore Solo sheet (read round 20) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://posicharge.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/5x7_ProCore-Solo-1.pdf>
