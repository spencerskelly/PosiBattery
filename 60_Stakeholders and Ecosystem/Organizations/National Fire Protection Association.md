# National Fire Protection Association

## Definition

Standards body that publishes NFPA 505 (fire safety standard for powered industrial trucks) and NFPA 855 (stationary energy storage systems), among other fire and electrical codes.

## Notes

- Added 2026-10-17 for [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]]. NFPA's own pages could not be read (permission not answered), so everything here comes from catalog and press sources.
- Adoption of NFPA standards depends on the local authority; many places enforce earlier editions (per [[Document - Energy-Storage.news NFPA 855 2026 Edition Updates (2026-04-29)]]).
- Not linked to products: no relationship in the vocabulary fits a standards body.
