# Godrej Enterprises Group

## Definition

Indian industrial group whose Godrej and Boyce material handling business launched lithium-ion forklifts with its own battery management system.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Battery Maker.
- Godrej and Boyce's 22 August 2024 release says its material handling business launched India's first lithium-ion forklifts with an indigenously developed battery management system, developed with Log9 Materials (cells 30 percent localized), and targets full localization of cells. Source: Godrej Enterprises release (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.godrejenterprises.com/newsroom/press-releases/godrej-and-boyce-launches-india-s-first-lithium-ion-powered-forklift-truck-with-fully-indigenous-battery-management-system>
- A 2026 trade report says Godrej introduced a Multi-Ion battery for forklifts with up to 5,000 cycles, operation above 45 C ambient and a 7-year warranty, developed with a deep-tech partner whose name is cut from the retrieved text. Source: pv magazine India (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/?p=15032>
- **Region:** India (Mumbai release, group history since 1897).
- **Also:** a Godrej page presents Crown RR/RD reach trucks, so Godrej appears to sell Crown trucks in India (dealer relationship not modeled yet).
