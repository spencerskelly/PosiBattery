# Midac MHE Battery

## Definition

Midac flooded traction battery, 250 to 1,000 Ah, welded connections, up to 1,800 cycles, claimed up to 30% lower CO2.

## Notes

- MIDATRON HE only and BMU required; single-point watering and level sensor standard; air mixing not available. CO2 claim basis not stated. Source: <https://midacbatteries.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/1_CAT-INTEGRATED-SOLUTIONS_MOTIVE_EN_IT_DE_FR-01_2022-5011820-0967_21.pdf>
