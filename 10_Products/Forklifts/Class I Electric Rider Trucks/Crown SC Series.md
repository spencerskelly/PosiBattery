# Crown SC Series

## Definition

Crown three- and four-wheel sit-down electric counterbalance forklifts.

## Notes

- Crown lists the SC Series as 3-wheel and 4-wheel sit-down electric counterbalance forklifts. Source: Crown forklift range page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.crown.com/en-la/forklifts>
- **Not retrieved:** capacities, voltage and battery options.

- **Battery integration and charging interface (2026-10-14, gap fill):** spec sheet: 48 V DIN 43531 compartment, side extraction and seat-deck top access, optional rollers, SB350 connector, optional Li Ready; optional QuickCharge adds one or two seat-deck connectors.
