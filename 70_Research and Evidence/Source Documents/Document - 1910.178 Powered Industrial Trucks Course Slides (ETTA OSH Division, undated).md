# Document - 1910.178 Powered Industrial Trucks Course Slides (ETTA OSH Division, undated)

## Definition

Course slides summarizing 29 CFR 1910.178, used here as the only available paraphrase of the battery charging paragraphs (g) because the standard text itself was not read.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand). Origin is uncertain: the file is hosted on the New Mexico Environment Department site, while its credit line names "ETTA, OSH Division" with a 919 area-code phone number (919 is a North Carolina area code, from general knowledge, not stated in the file).

- **(g)(1):** battery charging installations must be located in designated areas.
- **(g)(2):** facilities for flushing and neutralizing spilled electrolyte; adequate ventilation to disperse fumes from gassing batteries; protection of charging apparatus from damage by trucks.
- **(g)(4):** conveyor, overhead hoist or equivalent equipment for handling batteries.
- **(g)(10) and (g)(11):** smoking prohibited in the charging area; precautions against open flames, sparks and electric arcs.
- **Not covered:** signage; eyewash; any lithium-ion provision. The slides' battery wording is about electrolyte, gassing and vent caps, which is lead-acid language (C319).
- **Caveat:** paragraph numbers are as the slides give them and are not checked against the current standard text (IB-280). Training slides can drift from the rule.
