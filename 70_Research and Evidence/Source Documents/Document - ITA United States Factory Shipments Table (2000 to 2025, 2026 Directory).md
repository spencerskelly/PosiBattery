## Notes

**Content recorded:**
- Title 'United States Factory Shipments'; columns electric rider (Classes 1 and 2), motorized hand (Class 3), internal combustion engine (Classes 4 and 5); rows 2000 to 2025. The ITA page describing it says 'since 1983'; the file read starts in 2000.
- Electric rider: 2019 78,243; 2020 73,183; 2021 78,030; 2022 85,071; 2023 96,652; 2024 97,050; 2025 88,461.
- Motorized hand: 2019 66,618; 2020 61,980; 2021 71,091; 2022 75,928; 2023 87,815; 2024 75,049; 2025 79,081.
- Internal combustion: 2019 77,588; 2020 61,161; 2021 55,770; 2022 78,964; 2023 113,592; 2024 82,260; 2025 63,855.
- Earlier years, same table: 2000 56,090 / 49,121 / 85,993; 2009 trough 28,409 / 28,635 / 28,740; 2015 65,042 / 57,329 / 76,075.
- No footnote in the file on what 'factory shipments' covers (exports, members only). The ITA page says annual figures are not directly comparable because membership changes.
- Totals derived: 2023 298,059; 2024 254,359; 2025 231,397 (down 9.0% and 22.4% against 2024 and 2023). Electric share of the total: 61.9% (2023), 67.7% (2024), 72.4% (2025).
