# Customer Actors

## Purpose

Generalized customer-side roles (operator, technician, manager, dealer, installer and others) that take part in needs. Roles are by function in an organization, not named people.

## Contents

- Actor notes. Each states its evidence status: source-stated, source-implied or hypothesis. Specific operator roles are subtypes of [[Vehicle Operator]].
