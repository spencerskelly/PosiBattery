# IRIS 860 Sensor Pack

## Definition

Sensor pack that recognizes reflective tape on safety gear and warns the forklift operator, with a detection zone of up to 8 m.

## Notes

- A dealer says the IRIS 860 sensor recognizes reflective tape on safety gear worn by other workers, has a customizable detection zone up to 8 m, can be paired with other sensors, works in direct sun glare and darkness, and comes with a 4 m power cable and stainless steel mounting bracket. Source: Holt of California page (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://holtlift.com/?p=4466>
