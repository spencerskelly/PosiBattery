# Forklifts

## What is here

Forklift and powered industrial truck families, grouped by the seven Industrial Truck Association classes, with who makes them and the batteries, chargers, telematics and programs sold with them.

## What belongs here

- `Categories`: [[Powered Industrial Truck]] and the seven class notes
- `Manufacturers/<name>`: one note per model family (not per model), plus telematics and option packages that a truck maker sells with its trucks
- batteries and chargers offered with trucks stay in `Battery Products`; the link between them is `offeredWith`
