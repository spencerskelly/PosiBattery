# Heli G Series Lithium Forklifts

## Definition

Heli G2 and G3 series lithium-ion electric forklifts (from 1.3 t up to 5 t) with a fleet management system and automatic speed reduction in turns.

## Notes

- A Polish dealer lists G2 (1.3 to 1.5 t, lithium-ion, HELI fleet management with remote diagnostics, monitoring and battery management), G3 (2 to 3.5 t and 4 to 5 t, driver absence warning) and H3 and H4 series; another dealer lists the CPD50 G2A11LI with a 153.6 V, 230 Ah battery, a 4-year (48-month) battery warranty and automatic speed regulation in turns. Source: FLT Grupa and Paleciaki dealer pages (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://fltgrupa.pl/en/heli-forklifts/>
- An Australian listing says G2 trucks come in S, M and H configurations, with auto steering deceleration, a three-speed mode and lithium batteries that heat automatically in the cold. Source: Lifts Today listing (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.liftstoday.com/listing/for-sale/259364865/2026-heli-cpd15-pneumatic-tire-forklifts?print=1>
- **Conflict (C90):** the dealers give different capacity ranges for the G2 series (1.3 to 1.5 t, 1.5 to 3.8 t, and a 5 t CPD50 G2A11LI).
