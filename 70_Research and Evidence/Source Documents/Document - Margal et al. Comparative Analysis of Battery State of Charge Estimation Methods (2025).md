# Document - Margal et al. Comparative Analysis of Battery State of Charge Estimation Methods (2025)

## Definition

Peer-reviewed conference paper comparing extended Kalman filter, adaptive observer and NARX neural-network SOC estimators in simulation (E3S Web of Conferences 601, 00033, 2025, open access).

## Provenance

- **Source address:** https://doi.org/10.1051/e3sconf/202560100033 (DOI link taken from the journal record cited in [[SOC and Health Estimation Literature Review]]; the link was not fetched in this session, the reviewed copy is the PDF in `Downloads`).
- **Reviewed copy:** local PDF `Comparative_Analysis_of_Battery_State_of_Charge_Es.pdf` in `Downloads`, read in full during the literature review.
- **Evidence tier:** not covered by T1 to T5 in [[Landscape Evidence and Modeling Conventions]] (journal or conference paper). Treated as peer-reviewed research evidence; its figures come from laboratory data, not from a product.

## What this Document supports

- **Accuracy basis ([[Estimate State of Charge and Health - Declare Accuracy Basis]]):** the abstract and Table 5 give figures ten times apart, the error is measured against a Coulomb-counted reference rather than a true SOC, and the cell, cycle and error definition are not stated together (see C148 and C149 in [[Battery Product Landscape Conflicts and Open Questions]]). That is the case for declaring the basis.
- Not supported: any forklift or lead-acid claim; the cycle is a passenger-vehicle schedule.
