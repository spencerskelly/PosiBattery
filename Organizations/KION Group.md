# KION Group

## Definition

German truck group, second in the 2024 ranking, whose brands include Linde Material Handling, STILL and Baoli.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Truck OEM, Brand Owner.
- The 2024 ranking puts KION Group second with $8.96B in lift truck revenue, down 4.2 percent from 2023. Source: Modern Materials Handling 2025 ranking via Supply Chain 24/7 (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.supplychain247.com/article/top-20-lift-truck-suppliers-2025>
- KION North America says its brand companies Linde Material Handling and Baoli serve the US, Canadian and Mexican markets, and its sister company Dematic does automation. Source: KION North America release (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.mheda.org/news/kion-north-america-launches-new-electric-forklift-the-linde-series-1293/>
- **Not researched:** STILL models and options.
