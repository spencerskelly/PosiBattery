# Pick Orders on Foot Alongside the Truck

## Definition

An order picker walks along an aisle picking items and the truck follows without the picker returning to drive it each time.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: analyst hypothesis, drafted 2026-10-14.** The steps are not an observed customer procedure; they are reasoned from vendor descriptions of the functions. Only one product states the follow behavior. Please correct with real workflow.
- Primary context: [[Material-Handling Fleet Site]].
- Steps (analyst wording): start the follow mode; walk the aisle and pick; the truck keeps pace and stops with the picker; return to manual driving at the end.
- Completion: the order is picked with fewer boarding and driving actions.
- Basis: the function Follow Operator Automatically and its one performer's source; the need is analyst-derived.
