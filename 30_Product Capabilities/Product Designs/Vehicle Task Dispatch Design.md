# Vehicle Task Dispatch Design

## Definition

Software that chooses which vehicle gets a task from position, availability and sometimes battery state.

## Notes

- Robot fleets are far better evidenced than manually driven forklift fleets.
- A design characteristic found in products, not a decision by us. Added 2026-10-14.
