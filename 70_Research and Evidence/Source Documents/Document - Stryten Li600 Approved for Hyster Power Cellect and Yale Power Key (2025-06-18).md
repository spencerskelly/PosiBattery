# Document - Stryten Li600 Approved for Hyster Power Cellect and Yale Power Key (2025-06-18)

## Definition

Stryten Energy release, relayed by a financial news site, that its M-Series Li600 lithium-ion battery is certified as an approved battery for trucks with Hyster Power Cellect or Yale Power Key.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Li600: LFP chemistry, Classes I to III, optional cold storage to -40 C, touch screen and remote monitoring.
- With an approved battery, Power Cellect and Power Key set up a CAN handshake that gives real-time state of charge, warnings, protection and tracking in Hyster Tracker or Yale Vision.
- Charger: "compatible with M-Series X-3 and X-7 chargers" (maker not stated on the page; the Class III model has an optional onboard charger). So the approved battery is paired with the battery maker's chargers, not Hyster's.
- A Hyster-Yale representative says Stryten has supplied its Aftersales Program for many years.
- Not stated: how customers order it, voltages, warranty.
- Shows that Hyster-Yale approved third-party lithium power in mid-2025, the year it announced its own battery and charger program (C323).
