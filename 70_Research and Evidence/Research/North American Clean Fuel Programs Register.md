# North American Clean Fuel Programs Register

## Definition

Register of North American low-carbon and clean fuel programs, their 2026 status and whether forklift charging electricity can earn credits. Evidence sits on the Document notes; disagreements are numbered C291 onward in [[Battery Product Landscape Conflicts and Open Questions]].

## Notes

- **Reading rule:** every source was read through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand). Trade, consultancy and vendor sources are T4; agency sources sit outside T1 to T5. "Analyst reasoning" marks a conclusion no source states. Status as of 2026-10-17.
- **Scope:** which North American low-carbon or clean fuel programs exist, their status, and whether forklift electricity is covered. Detailed state rules are in [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]] and [[Western State Clean Fuel Programs for Forklift Electricity Register]]; economics are in [[Forklift Clean Fuel Credit Business Case]].

### Bottom line (analyst summary)

1. **Six programs are active** per a May 2026 trade page: California, Oregon, Washington, New Mexico, British Columbia and the Canadian federal Clean Fuel Regulations ([[Document - Stillwater Low Carbon Fuel Program Proliferation (2026-05-06)]], T4).
2. **Forklift coverage is stated for all six, with differing strength:** California, Oregon, Washington (rules read, see the linked registers), British Columbia (heavy forklifts above 6,800 kg, [[Document - Xpansiv British Columbia LCFS Market Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)]], T4), the Canadian federal program (electric forklifts listed as opportunities, [[Document - 3Degrees Canada Clean Fuel Regulations FAQ (modified 2026-01-22)]], T4) and New Mexico (state outreach document, [[Document - New Mexico CTFP Roads to Revenue with Fleet Electrification (2026-07)]]). New Mexico rule text, the BC regulation and the Canadian regulation text were not read.
3. **Seven more jurisdictions have 2026 legislation and one has an enacted bill** awaiting implementation; none has an operating program. The sources do not say whether any would cover forklifts.
4. **Mexico** is not listed on the survey page and was not searched separately.

### Differences that matter for a product team

- **Reporting party:** California facility owner, Oregon owner or operator, Washington fleet owner, New Mexico charger-owning fleet (vendors and aggregators also allowed), British Columbia the name on the utility bill (C295, C299).
- **Metering and verification:** California and Oregon require metered data (California with a charger-efficiency adjustment, Oregon with none, C291); BC's source lists verification as none and the federal program requires third-party verification (T4 sources, unverified).
- **Stacking:** BC-LCFS is independent of the federal program and assets can take part in both ([[Document - Xpansiv British Columbia LCFS Market Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)]]).
