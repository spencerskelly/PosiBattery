# STILL Curve Speed Control

## Definition

STILL assistance function that reduces travel speed automatically according to the steering angle when cornering.

## Notes

**Summary:**
STILL steering-angle-dependent speed limiter that automatically sets a safe cornering speed.

**Marketed features:**
- Automatic travel-speed regulation by steering angle
- Reduces the risk of tip-over in corners
- Brakes only as much as needed, keeping cornering as fast as safely possible
- Available on various forklifts and warehouse trucks
