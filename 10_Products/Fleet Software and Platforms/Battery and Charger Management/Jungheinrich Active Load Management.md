# Jungheinrich Active Load Management

## Definition

Jungheinrich load management that holds a group of networked lithium-ion chargers to an overall power limit.

## Notes

- Source: [[Document - Jungheinrich Active Load Management Page (retrieved 2026-10-14)]] (T1). SLH 700i chargers, charger software and the SLC 700 control centre; peak shaving, prioritisation by state of charge, weekly schedules, fallback rules. Optional higher-level energy management system integration stated, protocol not named.
- SLH 700i and SLC 700 are named in the source but have no vault notes.
