# Jungheinrich ISM Online

## Definition

Jungheinrich web-based truck fleet management system for fleets of any size and for mixed fleets.

## Notes

- Jungheinrich says ISM Online manages a whole truck fleet regardless of size or complexity; a German trade report says it manages, analyzes and optimizes fleets, handles mixed fleets, can require a pre-operation check before driving, and then ran in 17 European countries (dated). Source: Jungheinrich safety page and Industrial Production (T1/T2 (dated)), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.industrial-production.de/wirtschaft---unternehmen/jungheinrich-verbessert-staplermanagement--neue-moeglichkeiten.htm>
- A 2019 report says every Jungheinrich truck from 2019 has an integrated telematics box, existing trucks can be retrofitted, and a manufacturer-independent fleet management system from a partner is offered alongside ISM Online. Source: Logistics Matters (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://logisticsmatters.co.uk/ZoneControl-to-assist-safety>
