# Batrium CellMate-K9

## Definition

Cell monitor of the Batrium BMS for 3 to 16 series cells; requires the WatchMonCORE and daisy-chains for larger strings.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Cell monitor of the Batrium BMS for 3 to 16 series cells; requires the WatchMonCORE and daisy-chains for larger strings.

**Marketed features:**
- Monitors 3s to 16s cells, 0.50 V to 5.50 V at 0.001 V resolution
- Optional fan and a 25 W heatsink support stronger balancing
- Two or more can be daisy chained; multistring setups supported
- Requires the WatchMonCORE
