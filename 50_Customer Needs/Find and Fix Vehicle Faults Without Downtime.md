# Find and Fix Vehicle Faults Without Downtime

## Definition

Customer need: Find and Fix Vehicle Faults Without Downtime. The problem behind it: a vehicle that is down, or about to be, costs the shift; the people who fix it need fault data and service prompts before they reach the vehicle.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: need hypothesis.** Statements below are what makers and dealers say their products do. No customer-side source confirms the need. Added in round 40 (gap review).
- **Problem solved (analyst wording):** Faults found late cost shift time; technicians arrive without knowing the cause.
- **Who has the problem:** [[Maintenance Technician]], [[Dealer Service Technician]], [[Fleet Operations Manager]].
- **Operating segments (analyst crosswalk to [[PosiCharge Market Segments and Jobs-to-Be-Done]], hypothesis):** Material-handling fleets; Airport eGSE fleets.
- **Realized by (specific functions; products link to these):** [[Diagnose Vehicle Remotely]], [[Indicate Maintenance Due]], [[Report Truck Telemetry]], [[Display Truck Status to Operator]].
