# Charlatte CBT350 AC Tow Tractor

## Definition

Charlatte self-propelled battery-powered AC tow tractor for regional baggage operations.

## Notes

- Charlatte America describes the CBT350 AC Tow Tractor as a self-propelled battery-powered GSE tow vehicle; a related Charlatte tractor with a 40 hp AC motor drive axle tows more than 20 tons and the TE.208 is a battery-powered four-wheel baggage and tow tractor.
