# Document - Midac MDL-MBS Motive Power Catalogue (02-2023)

## Definition

Midac MDL and MBS motive power catalogue (02/2023).

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw PDF.

- MDL: DIN range, IEC 60254-2 series L-E 2 V cells, C5 100 to 1,550 Ah; MBS: British Standard range with PzB plates, 110 to 1,050 Ah. Technologies VLA and VRLA; design life VLA 1,500+ cycles, VRLA 1,200+ cycles; maintenance weekly (VLA), yearly (VRLA).
- MDL positive tubular gauntlet plates, die-cast pin grids; microporous polyethylene separators; M10 inserts; lids take push-in plugs and the Air-Tech air-mixing system. Charge time 7 to 12 h with LF or HF chargers (MIDATRON MTB, MTT, HF/HE).
- VLA options: single-point watering, air mixing, electrolyte level sensor, welded inter-cell connections; BMU optional.
- Not in the document: warranty, charge voltages and factor, the words Aquamatic, EUW, end leads.
