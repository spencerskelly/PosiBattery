# Document - Trinity Consultants New Mexico Becomes Fourth State to Adopt a Clean Fuel Program (2026-02-27)

## Definition

A consultancy's summary of New Mexico's adoption of a clean transportation fuel program, with key dates.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the western-states clean fuel topic. Trinity Consultants article dated 2026-02-27 (page metadata shows an update on 2026-08-06).

- **Adoption:** the Environmental Improvement Board voted unanimously on 2026-01-22 to adopt 20.2.92 NMAC. The reporting period begins 2026-04-01, with the initial compliance period through 2027-12-31. Targets: 20 percent reduction by 2030, rising to 30 percent by 2040. Third-party verification starts 2028-09-15.
- **Electricity and forklifts:** electricity is not addressed as a fuel category (EV electrification is called the primary compliance driver); forklifts are not mentioned.
- **Not stated:** who must report, when credit generation begins, and whether forklifts generate credits.
- **Conflict:** silence on forklifts versus the state's own program document that lists electric forklifts (C294).
