# Document - W-Carbon Low Carbon Fuel Credits Refresh (2025-05-16)

## Definition

A newsletter's April 2025 monthly average credit prices for California, Oregon and Washington.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the business case. Newsletter post dated 2025-05-16, abridged and partly paywalled; figures are April 2025 monthly averages.

- **California LCFS:** about $64 per tonne in April 2025 (up about 5 percent from March, down 4.5 percent from $67 a year earlier).
- **Oregon CFP:** $47.98 per tonne (up 12 percent from March's $43.00; down 59 percent from $117.37 in April 2024).
- **Washington CFS:** $28.86 per tonne (up 28 percent from March's $22.60; down 53 percent from $61.85 in April 2024).
- **Not stated:** British Columbia, Canada CFR and New Mexico prices. These are 2025 prices and stale for a 2026 decision; see the CARB weekly figure for California in 2026.
