# Document - OSHA Instruction STD 1-11.4 Battery Charging Stations for Fork Lifts (1978)

## Definition

OSHA's 1978 enforcement instruction on how 29 CFR 1910.178(g)(2) applies to areas where industrial truck batteries are only charged.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw directive. Read once.

- **Purpose:** clarifies how (g)(2) applies to areas where power industrial truck batteries are charged only; supersedes Field Information Memorandum 74-63 (1974-08-06); effective 1978-10-30 until canceled or superseded.
- **Background:** an inspector told an employer that charging stations in a warehouse under construction had to meet (g)(2); industry then asked the national office.
- **Reading of what inspectors check (from the summary, not a checklist in the text):** whether the area is charging-only (no maintenance, batteries not removed from the trucks, no electrolyte); a charging-only area is not held to (g)(2), but must meet (g)(1), (8), (9), (10), (11) and (12). Protective equipment is required when and where applicable.
- **Lithium:** not addressed. The directive refers to power industrial truck batteries generally and predates lithium use in these trucks.
- **Caveat:** whether this directive is still applied as written, and how it reads for lithium (no electrolyte handling, little gassing), is unknown (C319).
