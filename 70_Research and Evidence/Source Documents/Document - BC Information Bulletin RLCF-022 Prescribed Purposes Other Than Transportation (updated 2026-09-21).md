# Document - BC Information Bulletin RLCF-022 Prescribed Purposes Other Than Transportation (updated 2026-09-21)

## Definition

BC's bulletin on prescribed purposes, setting forklift eligibility at over 6,800 kg or rough terrain and an electricity EER of 3.9 for forklifts.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. Ministry bulletin on fuel use in prescribed non-transportation purposes; originally issued May 2024, last updated 2026-09-21 per the page.

- **Forklift eligibility:** forklifts are covered only if their lifting capacity is over 6,800 kg (about 15,000 lb) or they are rough-terrain forklifts. Equipment not named in the bulletin's Table 1 cannot be reported under these categories.
- **EER (Table 1):** electricity 3.9 for forklifts and 2.5 for cargo handling and ground support equipment; the comparison fuels are 0.9. Electric yard trucks use a battery truck EER of 3.2.
- **Start:** these purposes apply from the 2024 compliance period.
- **Not stated:** credit generator, metering, price.
- **Why it matters (analyst reasoning):** 6,800 kg is above most counterbalance trucks in typical warehouses, so BC covers only the heavy end of the forklift population. California's split is at 12,000 lb (about 5,450 kg) and Oregon has no split (C306, C307).
