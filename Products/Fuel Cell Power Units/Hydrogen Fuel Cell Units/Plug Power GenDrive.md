# Plug Power GenDrive

## Definition

Plug Power hydrogen fuel cell power unit that replaces the battery in electric material handling trucks, refuels in about 2 to 3 minutes and keeps voltage constant.

## Notes

- Plug Power says GenDrive is a drop-in replacement for batteries, delivers constant voltage, communicates with the lift truck to monitor stack and system performance, keeps performing at -22 F, has a fuel level gauge, stores 0.7 to 3.4 kg of hydrogen depending on model, and can be refueled in approximately 3 minutes (Series 1000 sheet) or approximately 2 minutes (GD2000 sheet). Source: Plug Power spec sheets (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://plugpower.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/GenDrive-Series-1000-Spec-Sheet.pdf>
- A Plug Power FAQ says operators refuel at a dispenser in under 3 minutes, and a press item says refueling can take as little as 180 seconds. Source: Plug Power FAQ and Manufacturing.net (T1/T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.plugpower.com/faq-from-potential-gendrive-customers>
- **Conflict-visible (C67):** the refuel time is stated as about 3 minutes, about 2 minutes, under 3 minutes and 180 seconds in different Plug Power documents; battery change is stated as 15 minutes per shift (material handling page) and 20-30 minutes (FAQ). Sources: <https://www.plugpower.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/2018_GD2000SpecSheet_F1Digi.pdf>; <https://www.plugpower.com/applications/material-handling/>.
