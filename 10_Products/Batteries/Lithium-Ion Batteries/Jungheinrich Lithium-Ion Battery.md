# Jungheinrich Lithium-Ion Battery

## Definition

Jungheinrich lithium-ion battery developed together with its charger and trucks, with an integrated BMS.

## Notes

- A trade report says the battery with 360 Ah cells reaches 50 percent in 40 minutes and full charge in 80 minutes, targets up to 3,000 full cycles versus 900 to 1,200 for lead-acid, and is permanently monitored by an integrated BMS. Source: Warehouse News (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://warehousenews.co.uk/?p=45557>

The same figures are confirmed by BEST Magazine (2017-03-27) and an Operations Engineer article: 48 V, 360 Ah and 480 Ah packs, 50% in 40 minutes, full charge in 80 minutes, up to 3,000 full cycles vs 900 to 1,200 for lead-acid, -20 to 55 C. Warranty: free 5 years on self-made lithium batteries (24, 48, 80 V), below 65% capacity triggers repair or exchange; also quoted as five years or 10,000 hours (eMove360, 2017-06-08). Sources: <https://www.bestmag.co.uk/?p=26740>; <https://www.operationsengineer.org.uk/content/news/jungheinrich-launches-stronger-faster-lithium-ion-battery>; <https://emove360.com/?p=59644>
