# Integrated LCD Display

## Definition

LCD on the monitoring device showing state of charge, voltage, temperature and other readings at the battery.

## Notes

- Stated for Wi-iQ4 (turns off after 15 minutes idle) and Exide Motion+ EasyMonitor.
- Citations are listed under Sources below. A design characteristic found in products, not a decision by us.
