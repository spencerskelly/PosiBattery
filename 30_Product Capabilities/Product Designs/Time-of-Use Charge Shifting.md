# Time-of-Use Charge Shifting

## Definition

Charging is moved to cheaper or lower-demand time windows by fixed slots, weekly schedules or tariff rules.

## Notes

- Linde states fixed time slots, Jungheinrich weekly schedules, Flip Turn time-of-use shifting, ACT shifting away from peak-rate periods.
- A design characteristic found in products, not a decision by us. Added 2026-10-14.
