# Crown V-Force Lead-Acid Battery

## Definition

Crown V-Force flat plate and tubular lead-acid batteries introduced in 2020.

## Notes

- Crown added V-Force flat plate and tubular lead-acid batteries in October 2020, the tubular with a flexible bolt-on inter-cell connector. Source: Batteries International (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.batteriesinternational.com/?p=15261>
- **Design characteristics, with citations:**
  - [[Tubular Plate Construction]] (V): <https://www.batteriesinternational.com/?p=15261>
  - [[Flexible Bolt-On Intercell Connector]] (V): <https://www.batteriesinternational.com/?p=15261>

- **Update 2026-10-13:** 2020-10-06 release: flat plate (five-layer wrap, heavy grid) and tubular (flexible bolt-on inter-cell cable connector, high-capacity cells); can ship preconfigured with watering kits, "Blinky" electrolyte monitors and battery monitoring identification devices, linked here to [[Crown Battery Acid Indicators]], [[Crown V-Force Single Point Watering System]] and [[Crown V-Force BMID]] (the release does not name those exact products; the match is the analyst's). No voltages, capacities or warranty found (IB-189). Source: <https://www.crown.com/en-us/newsroom/articles/product-news/power-solutions-for-every-forklift-application.html>
