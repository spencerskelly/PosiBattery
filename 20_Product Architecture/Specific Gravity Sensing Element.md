# Specific Gravity Sensing Element

## Definition

Technology-neutral sensing element within an electrolyte probe that responds to electrolyte density or another physical property used to determine specific gravity.

## Notes

- A sensing element is required for TruBID's published continuous specific-gravity measurement, but the transduction principle is not disclosed.
- No optical, pressure, buoyancy, conductivity, acoustic, refractive-index, or other sensing technology is asserted.
- Exact materials, geometry, calibration range, supplier, and part number remain unknown.
