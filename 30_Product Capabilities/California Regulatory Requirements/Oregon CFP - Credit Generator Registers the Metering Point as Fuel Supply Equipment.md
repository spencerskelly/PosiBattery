# Oregon CFP - Credit Generator Registers the Metering Point as Fuel Supply Equipment

## Definition

The forklift owner or operator, as credit generator, shall register the metering point as fuel supply equipment with proof of ownership or operation and that the forklift and metering equipment are operational; the registered item depends on where the meter sits (panel or circuit, charger or cable, forklift, telematics).

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It binds the party named in the definition, not a charger. Product consequences are in the derived requirement REQ-00058 (analyst-proposed).
- Second-hand reading; see [[Western State Clean Fuel Programs for Forklift Electricity Register]] and the conflict register.
- **Status:** applies to parties who generate credits. A charger-level meter requires documentation of the charger's data tracking and a written explanation of data collection.
- Rental forklifts are registered by their owner at the home base, with a quarterly detail sheet of locations.
