# Linde Motion Detection

## Definition

Linde assistance system that detects movement behind a stationary truck and warns the driver.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Linde assistance system for counterbalance forklifts that detects movement behind a stationary truck and prevents it from moving off.

**Marketed features:**
- Detects people or vehicles moving behind the stationary truck and blocks move-off
- Driver warning on the display; optional acoustic warning to pedestrians
- Driver must return the pedal to neutral before continuing
- Works regardless of lighting; detection area adjustable to the site
- No continuous reversing tone, avoiding extra warehouse noise
- Factory option on new Linde counterbalance series; retrofit for existing fleets
