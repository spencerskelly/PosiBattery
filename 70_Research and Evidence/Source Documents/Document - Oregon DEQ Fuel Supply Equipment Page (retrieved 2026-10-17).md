# Document - Oregon DEQ Fuel Supply Equipment Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

Oregon DEQ's FSE registration page, stating that forklift reporting must use actual metering from the Q4 2023 report.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the western-states clean fuel topic. DEQ's Clean Fuels Program page on fuel supply equipment (FSE) registration.

- **Forklift metering:** the estimation method was "only approved for use in the Q1 through Q3 2023 reports"; "starting with the Q4 2023 report, reporting must be through actual metering". A transition guidance document is linked ([[Document - Oregon DEQ Forklift Transition Guidance (2023)]]).
- **Registration:** each piece of FSE must be registered by a credit generator, showing ownership and that it is operational; new FSE applications must be submitted in the first 45 days of the reporting period; the Expansion 2022 rulemaking registration rules took effect 2023-01-01.
- **Not stated:** forklifts are not named in the FSE examples (EV chargers, hydrogen stations, natural gas and propane equipment); the page does not give metering equipment, accuracy or method.
- **Conflict:** the Q1 to Q3 2023 estimation window differs from the transition guidance's Q3 2023-only wording for non-metered forklifts (C292).
