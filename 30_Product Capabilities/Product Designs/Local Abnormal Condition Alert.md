# Local Abnormal Condition Alert

## Definition

Abnormal-condition warning presented directly on the monitoring device through LEDs, an audible alarm, an integrated display, or a combination of those outputs.

## Notes

- [[EnerSys Wi-iQ]] explicitly combines LCD, LEDs and a low-voltage buzzer with battery warnings and alarms.
- [[EnerSys iQ Mini]] explicitly shows over-temperature, low-electrolyte and over-discharge alerts on the unit.
- [[Philadelphia Scientific eGO!Mini]] and [[Philadelphia Scientific eGO!pro]] explicitly provide local LED/audible alert behavior.
- This Design describes alert delivery; the threshold/evaluation implementation is modeled separately.
