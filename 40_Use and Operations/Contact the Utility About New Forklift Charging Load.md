# Contact the Utility About New Forklift Charging Load

## Definition

A government fleet manager tells the electric utility about forklift charging load at each separately metered location, so the utility can plan service.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: analyst hypothesis, drafted 2026-10-17.** The deadline (2026-03-31, each separately metered location with affected forklifts) is from [[Document - CARB ZEF Government Fleets Overview Fact Sheet (2026-01)]]; what the utility needs from the fleet, and what a charger could supply, is not stated in any source read.
- Related: [[Electric Utility or Grid Operator]], [[Charge Within Site Power Limits and Utility Costs]].
