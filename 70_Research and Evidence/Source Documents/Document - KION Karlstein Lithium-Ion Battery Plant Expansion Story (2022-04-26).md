# Document - KION Karlstein Lithium-Ion Battery Plant Expansion Story (2022-04-26)

## Definition

KION story on its battery plant in Karlstein am Main, Germany, which builds lithium-ion batteries for Linde and STILL trucks.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Production hall of 4,000 m2 has built "thousands" of 48 V and 80 V batteries per year since 2019 for counterbalanced trucks; a second line is to make 24 V batteries for warehouse trucks, with up to 30,000 batteries per year once running; at least 80 staff in production and R&D.
- Output: lithium-ion battery systems assembled from cells and components, including a "technology container" for software and intelligent charging; reconditioning of rental-fleet batteries.
- Cell source is not stated; chargers are not named as a product; no third-party suppliers named.
- The order-intake record of 87 percent electric trucks (fiscal 2021) is a truck share, not a share fitted with KION batteries.
