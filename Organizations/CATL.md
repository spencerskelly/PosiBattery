# CATL

## Definition

Battery cell maker named as the cell and module source for Hangcha's lithium forklift battery packs in earlier brochure editions.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Battery Maker.
- Hangcha's 2024 and 2025 XC series brochures say the lithium-ion technology was developed jointly by Hangcha and CATL and that the battery cells and modules are from CATL, exclusively for Hangcha. Source: Hangcha XC series brochures (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://hcforklift.com/upload/files/XC%20Series%204-W%20Electric%20Forklift%20Truck%20With%20Lithium%20Iron-Phosphate%202.0-3.5t.pdf>
- **Not stated in the retrieved text:** CATL's full name, headquarters and regions served (Coverage Plan rule 5 gap). The 2026 edition of the brochure does not repeat the statement (C88).
