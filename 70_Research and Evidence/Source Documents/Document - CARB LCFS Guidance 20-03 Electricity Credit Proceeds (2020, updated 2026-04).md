# Document - CARB LCFS Guidance 20-03 Electricity Credit Proceeds (2020, updated 2026-04)

## Definition

CARB's guidance on how electric utilities spend LCFS electricity credit proceeds, with a passing list of electric forklifts among reported uses.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic because the Knowledge Base Backlog (2026-10-04) pointed to it as support for forklift metering. It does not set metering rules (C284).

- **Subject:** how electrical distribution utilities (EDUs) must spend proceeds from LCFS electricity credits; released March 2020 and updated April 2026; guidance that creates no new legal requirement.
- **Forklifts:** electric forklifts are listed among the uses whose electricity data must go to the designated reporting entity, citing sections 95483.2(b)(8), 95491 and 95491.1 without explaining them; incentives for buying or leasing electric forklifts are given as an example of qualifying spending.
- **Numbers (not forklift-specific):** Clean Fuel Reward contributions from 2025-07-01 are 50 percent for large investor-owned utilities, 25 percent for large publicly owned utilities, 10 percent for medium publicly owned utilities and 0 percent for small ones; minimum holdback equity share is 75 percent for large and medium investor-owned utilities and 50 percent for others; administrative costs for holdback equity projects are capped at 10 percent; annual compliance reports are due 30 April.
- **Not covered:** the forklift energy economy ratio, FSE registration and metering standards.
