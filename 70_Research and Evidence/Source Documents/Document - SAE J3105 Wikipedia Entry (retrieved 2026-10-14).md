- SAE recommended practice for conductive automated DC charging, mainly heavy-duty transit buses, first issued January 2020. Parts: J3105-1 cross rail (overhead contacts lowered onto roof rails), J3105-2 bus-up pantograph, J3105-3 pin-and-socket (marketed by Staubli as QCC). DC 250-1000 V; level 1 up to 600 A (350 kW), level 2 up to 1200 A. Page does not give the current revision.
