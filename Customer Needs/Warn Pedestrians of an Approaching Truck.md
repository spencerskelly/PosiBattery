# Warn Pedestrians of an Approaching Truck

## Definition

Customer need: Warn Pedestrians of an Approaching Truck. The problem behind it: People on foot in a working area need to know a truck is coming before it reaches them.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: need hypothesis.** The statements below are what makers and dealers say their products do for customers. No customer, user or buyer source in the vault confirms that customers hold this need or rank it. Per the ruleset, source research becomes a validated need only after customer-side evidence.
- **Problem solved (analyst wording):** People on foot in a working area need to know a truck is coming before it reaches them.
- **Who has the problem:** [[Pedestrian Near Trucks]], [[Site Safety Manager]].
- **Operating segments (analyst crosswalk to [[PosiCharge Market Segments and Jobs-to-Be-Done]], hypothesis):** Material-handling fleets.
- **Realized by (specific functions; products link to these):** [[Warn Pedestrians of Approaching Truck]], [[Alert Operator of Hazards]].
- **Products reaching this need through those functions:** 37. Largest families: Vehicle Accessories/Proximity and Object Detection (17), Vehicle Accessories/Warning Lights and Alerts (12), Vehicle Accessories/Operator Assist and Stability (5), Forklifts/Class I Electric Rider Trucks (2), Vehicle Accessories/Operator Displays (1). Further products reach it by design, option or metric routes; see [[Product to Customer Need Map]].
- **Vendor-stated evidence (by product):**
  - [[Linde Safety Guard]]: KION says Linde Safety Guard (introduced 2018) detects trucks and pedestrians approaching too closely, warns pedestrians through interactive warning vests, and can define zones with reduced speed. Source: KION Group story (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.kiongroup.com/en/Newsroom/Stories/Innovation/Safety-in-the-warehouse-comprehensive-protection-under-all-circumstances.html>
  - [[STILL Warning Zone Light]]: STILL says the warning zone light projects two strips of light to the left and right of the truck and the Warning Zone Light Plus projects a semi-circle around the rear, complementing the Safety Light's blue cone at the front. Source: STILL Safety Assist page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.still.co.uk/forklift-trucks/driver-assistance/safety-assist.html>
- **Independent evidence that the problem exists (not that the product is wanted):**
  - BLS fact sheet 'Occupational Injuries, Illnesses, and Fatalities Involving Forklifts' (June 2019, 2017 data, dated) (G, US government statistics): 614 workers died in forklift-related incidents from 2011 to 2017; of 74 fatal forklift injuries in 2017, 9 were pedestrian vehicular incidents; of 9,050 nonfatal cases with days away from work in 2017, 1,850 involved pedestrians while the forklift was in transportation use, and those pedestrian cases had the highest median days away (20). Retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.bls.gov/iif/factsheets/fatal-occupational-injuries-forklifts-2017.htm>
  - Secondary pages repeat different fatality and injury figures; see conflicts C101 to C104 in [[Battery Product Landscape Conflicts and Open Questions]]. Only the government sources above are used here.
- **Gaps:** no customer-side source; each function in the list is realized by only the products that state it, so the product count is a lower bound; no Requirement is linked (the vault leaves requirements as an intentional gap).
