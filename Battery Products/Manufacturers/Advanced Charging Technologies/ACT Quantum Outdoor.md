# ACT Quantum Outdoor

## Definition

ACT NEMA 3R outdoor Quantum charger, also sold as Quantum GSE for ground support equipment.

## Notes

- The spec sheet lists 24 to 96 V, NEMA 3R outdoor rating, peak efficiency over 94 percent and 93.5 percent total cycle efficiency, with lithium-ion, VRLA and flooded lead-acid support. Source: ACT Quantum Outdoor sheet (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/41607/133717591287578074.pdf>
- A GSE trade article says the outdoor Quantum GSE is UL certified Type 3R and communicates with BATTview, uploading to ACTintelligent. Source: Airside International (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.airsideint.com/issue-article/act-moves-into-the-gse-battery-charging-business/>
