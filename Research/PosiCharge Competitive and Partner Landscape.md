# PosiCharge Competitive and Partner Landscape

## Purpose

Classify organizations relative to a specific PosiCharge offer space. This is not a generic company list: every classification must identify the relevant offer, market segment, relationship type, evidence, and confidence.

## Relationship classes

| Classification | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| Ampure group / internal portfolio | The organization is an Ampure business ([[Power Designers]], [[Ampure Automotive and Aftermarket EVSE]]). Never an external competitor; overlap is recorded in [[Ampure Industrial Portfolio Overlap and Synergy Map]] |
| Direct competitor | The organization offers a substitutable product for the same customer job and operating context |
| Partial competitor | The organization overlaps with one part of the offer or segment but is not a full substitute |
| Partner | The organization can enable delivery, integration, distribution, service, supply, or customer access without direct overlap in the defined offer |
| Competitor and partner | The organization overlaps in one offer space and collaborates, supplies, distributes, integrates, or co-sells in another |
| Channel partner | The organization principally provides sales, distribution, installation, or service reach |
| Technology or integration partner | The organization provides interoperable technology, data, connectors, batteries, software, or systems integration |
| Customer or operator | The organization operates the target fleet or buys/uses the offer |
| Adjacent ecosystem participant | Relevant to the customer environment but neither a meaningful substitute nor a current partnership target |
| Not comparable | No defined overlap, relationship, or strategic relevance has been established |

## Required record fields

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Link to the organization record |
| PosiCharge offer space | Intelligent charging, monitoring/identification, connected operations, or MHE/eGSE energy operations |
| Compared offer | Specific competitor, partner, or ecosystem offering |
| Target segment | Defined fleet, battery, charging, or operating context |
| Classification | One or more relationship classes from the table above |
| Reason | Why the classification applies within this offer space |
| Evidence | Source-document, organization, product, or research links |
| Confidence | High, medium, or low, with reason |
| Open question or next action | Validation needed before changing the classification |

## Initial classification policy

The cohorts above are research candidates, not final classifications. A forklift or eGSE OEM must not be treated as a direct PosiCharge competitor solely because it manufactures vehicles. Assign a competitive relationship only when a directly comparable offer is documented. An Ampure business is never assigned a competitive relationship; see [[Ampure Group Portfolio Context]].

External competitors should, once the overlap map is complete, be compared with the combined Ampure industrial portfolio as well as with PosiCharge alone.

## Evidence workflow

1. Identify the PosiCharge offer and customer job.
2. Identify the other organization’s specific offer in the same space.
3. Compare functions, designs, metrics, deployment model, channel, and target segment.
4. Classify the relationship and confidence.
5. Record conflicts and missing evidence in [[PosiCharge Opportunity Backlog]] or [[Investigation Backlog]].
