# Linde Safety Pilot

## Definition

Linde driver assistance system that shows load weight, centre of gravity, lift height and tilt against limits and intervenes in lift functions to prevent overturning.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Linde driver-assistance system that displays load and stability data against limits and actively intervenes in lifting and tilting to prevent overturning.

**Marketed features:**
- Monitors load center of gravity, lift height and driving speed
- Actively intervenes in driving, lifting and tilting when instability threatens
- Displays load weight, tilt angle, maximum and current lift height and load center
- Two variants: 'active' (with intervention) and 'select' (display only)
- Factory-only on E12-E50 (386-388) and H14-H80 (391-396)
