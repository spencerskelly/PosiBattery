# Midac MBS Traction Battery

## Definition

Midac British Standard (BS) range of traction battery cells with PzB plates, 110 to 1,050 Ah (C5).

## Notes

- Catalogue: MBS is the BS range, PzB plates, C5 110 to 1,050 Ah, series 55, 65, 75, 85, 100, 105; 2 to 10 cells per block; VLA and VRLA; shares the MDL-MBS catalogue options. Source: Midac MDL-MBS catalogue (T1, second-hand), <https://midacbatteries.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/CAT-MDL-MBS_MOTIVE_EN_IT-02_2023-5011742-0312-00.pdf>
- Plate geometry for MBS is named "PzB" only; no design link made.
