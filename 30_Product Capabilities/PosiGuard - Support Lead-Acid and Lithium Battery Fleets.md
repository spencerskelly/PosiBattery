# PosiGuard - Support Lead-Acid and Lithium Battery Fleets

## Definition

PosiCharge PosiGuard shall support its defined monitoring and integration role for supported lead-acid and supported lithium industrial battery applications.

## Notes

- This requirement is scoped to PosiGuard and is not generalized to BMID 1, BMID 3, or Battery Rx.
- Current public evidence states support for lead-acid and lithium batteries.
- Supported lithium BMS protocols, CAN messages, and responsibility boundaries remain open detail requirements.
