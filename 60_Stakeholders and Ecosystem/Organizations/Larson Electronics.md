# Larson Electronics

## Definition

Maker of an explosion-proof blue LED forklift light.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Accessory Maker.
- DC Velocity's 2017 forklift safety products item says Larson introduced a blue LED forklift light of 2,250 lumens drawing 25 W, rated for Class I, II, III and Division 1 and 2 hazardous areas, for 9 to 60 V systems. Source: DC Velocity (T2 (dated)), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.dcvelocity.com/articles/28818-spotlight-on-forklift-safety-products>
