# Document - Warehouse News Fronius PV Connect Article (2023-06-13)

## Definition

Warehouse News article on Fronius PV Connect in the Charge & Connect portal.

## Notes

Facts below were read through the fetch tool's summary of the page, not from the raw page; vendor statements are claims.

- PV Connect is a function in Fronius Perfect Charging's cloud portal Charge & Connect. Chargers, the PV system and a Fronius inverter are connected; charging is planned from PV yield and weather forecasts with a load balancing charging algorithm. Modes: PV-maximum (charge as many batteries as PV power allows) and PV-optimum (raise the share of PV power in the power used).
- Interfaces and protocols are not stated. Claims lower energy cost, fewer load spikes and maximum forklift availability; no figures.
