# California LCFS - Forklift Charging Energy Shall Be Metered

## Definition

A party reporting electric forklift charging for LCFS credits shall, from Q1 2026 reports, report kWh measured by equipment accurate within 5 percent, with meter placement upstream of the charger (efficiency-adjusted), at the charger output, or on the forklift.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It binds the party named in the definition, not a charger. Product consequences are the derived requirements REQ-00051 to REQ-00053 (analyst-proposed).
- Second-hand reading; see C-items in [[Battery Product Landscape Conflicts and Open Questions]] and [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]].
- **Status:** applies only to parties who choose to claim credits. Estimates under CARB's earlier method were accepted through Q4 2025 reports.
- CARB does not approve meters for forklift compliance; no meter standard or revenue-grade requirement was found in the summary.
- Open: whether charger-reported data qualifies, and the adopted EER (C283, C286, C287).
