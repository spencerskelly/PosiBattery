# Battery Cabinet Cooling Fan

## Definition

A fan unit that fits inside the vehicle's battery cabinet to cool the battery during fast charging.

## Notes

- Design characteristic found in a product, not a decision by us. Added 2026-10-14 (IB-236).
- **Basis:** owner statement 2026-10-14 (U): the PosiCharge Cooling Fan Box is for the battery and fits into the battery cabinet in the vehicle. PosiCharge's public accessories page calls the box "state-of-the-art thermal management for battery fast charging" and says it is for ProCore chargers (T1, marketing claim, no mechanism, no figures). What the fan moves, how it is controlled and whether the charger commands it are not stated anywhere yet (IB-237).
- **Sources** (product, evidence level, basis):
  - [[PosiCharge Cooling Fan Box]] (U): owner statement 2026-10-14; <https://posicharge.com/accessories/> (V, marketing line only)
