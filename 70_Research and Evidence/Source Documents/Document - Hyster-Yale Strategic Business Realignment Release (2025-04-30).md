# Document - Hyster-Yale Strategic Business Realignment Release (2025-04-30)

## Definition

Hyster-Yale investor release that downsizes Nuvera's fuel cell program and moves lithium-ion battery module, charger and battery management system work into the company's own development and manufacturing.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- **Batteries and chargers:** Hyster-Yale Materials Handling will "develop, manufacture, and commercialize" lithium-ion battery modules, battery chargers, battery management systems and energy management activities at Nuvera's Billerica facility; next-generation batteries are expected to be used broadly in its electric trucks.
- **Battery program sales:** expected to "accelerate substantially from 2024 levels in 2025 and in following years" (no figures).
- **Other:** fuel cell program "very significantly downsized"; a mobile modular charging platform (HydroCharge) with first sales expected in the second half of 2025; direct annualized cost reductions $15 to $20 million from the second half of 2025.
- This is the only maker-side source read that states Hyster-Yale itself makes the chargers as well as the batteries.
