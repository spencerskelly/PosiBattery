# EnerSys NexSys TPPL Battery

## Definition

EnerSys thin plate pure lead battery line, described as virtually maintenance-free.

## Notes

- EnerSys says NexSys TPPL is a virtually maintenance-free technology that Hyster-Yale approved for integration with Hyster Power Cellect and Yale Power Key. Source: EnerSys release (2023-07-19) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.enersys.com/de/about-us/news/fleet-managers-get-powerful-flexibility-combining-enersys-technology-breadth-with-yale-power-key-and-hyster-power-cellect/>
- EnerSys's forklift page lists NexSys TPPL and NexSys TPPL Bloc batteries. Source: EnerSys forklift page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.enersys.com/en/industries/logistics-warehousing/forklifts-pallet-trucks/>
- **Design characteristics, with citations:**

- **Related products and how they differ (offeredWith):**
  - [[EnerSys NexSys+ Charger]]: profiles exist for NexSys TPPL; the iON profile is truncated in the retrieved text.
  - [[EnerSys Wi-iQ]]: no difference stated in the sources.
- Listed in the Logisnext Promatch parts program (2025). Source: Logisnext Americas release (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisnextamericas.com/en/logisnext/news/mla-enersys-expand-power-solutions-for-material-handling-operations>
- A trade report says NexSys Fast TPPL batteries recharge in under four hours from 60 percent depth of discharge and take opportunity charges from 40 to 80 percent state of charge in one hour and to 98 percent in two hours, with chargers up to 94 percent efficient and a low charging factor giving up to 30 percent energy saving against standard batteries; the EnerSys charging poster says to keep the battery above 40 percent charge, that temperatures above 60 C inhibit charging and that opportunity charging maximizes life. Source: Logistics Matters and EnerSys charging poster (EMEA 0324) (T2/T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisticsmatters.co.uk/?p=14776>

- **Update 2026-10-13:** product page: 12 to 80 V, US capacity 560 to 840 Ah (C6, 1.75 V per cell), developed for fast and opportunity charging, no cycle life or warranty stated (<https://enersys.com/en/products/batteries/nexsys/nexsys-tppl-amer>). The 2 V cell sheet lists 510 to 1,020 Ah (C6) in NXSA and NXSS series, charge rates 0.2 to 0.25 C6 standard and 0.26 to 0.4 C6 fast (<https://www.enersys.com/4aa968/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/nexsys/pure-2v/amer/amer-en-td-nex-tppl-2v-1023.pdf>). The two ranges differ (C226). The 12 V monoblocs are modeled in [[EnerSys NexSys TPPL Bloc Battery]].
