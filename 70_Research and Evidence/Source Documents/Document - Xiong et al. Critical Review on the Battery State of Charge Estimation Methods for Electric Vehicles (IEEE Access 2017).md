# Document - Xiong et al. Critical Review on the Battery State of Charge Estimation Methods for Electric Vehicles (IEEE Access 2017)

## Definition

IEEE Access review of SOC methods for electric vehicle batteries, with pack inconsistency treatment (a second identical copy, with the suffix (1), is also in Downloads).

## Provenance

- **Source address:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2017.2780258
- **Repo file:** Downloads/Critical_Review_on_the_Battery_State_of_Charge_Estimation_Methods_for_Electric_Vehicles.pdf (12 pages), added by the owner on 2026-10-18. Text was extracted from the file and the passages below were read; this is a first-hand read of the file, but not every section was read in full.
- **Evidence tier:** not covered by T1 to T5 in [[Landscape Evidence and Modeling Conventions]] (paper, standard or catalogue record).
- **Source key in the SOC and SOH notes:** S29.

## Notes

- Dates: received 2017-11-11, published 2017-12-06 with a version of 2018-02-14; the wishlist row's 2018 and page numbers were taken from a search record and were not checked here.
- Scope is EV lithium-ion; lead-acid appears only in the reference list (for example lead-acid SOC and SOH observers). No forklift or traction content.
- Abstract notes that pack inconsistency in cell capacity, resistance and voltage matters and discusses a monomer-selection and bias-correction method for packs; this applies to multi-cell traction packs, not tested here.
