# Document - HAWKER Perfect Plus Instructions for Use (EMEA)

## Definition

HAWKER Perfect Plus instructions for use (EMEA).

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- DC charging only; nominal 2.0 V per cell; float 2.27 V per cell; equalizing current not above 5 A per 100 Ah; charge factor 1.07 with circulation; start charging only below 45 C, 55 C upper limit; end of charge when gravity and voltage constant for 2 hours; rated gravity 1.29 at 30 C; discharge no more than 80%; no watering in the first 10 cycles; insulation resistance at least 50 ohm per volt.
- Optional: Aquamatic water refilling (0.2 to 0.6 bar, max 20 cells in series), electrolyte circulation with Aeromatic diaphragm pump, Wi-iQ monitoring (24 to 80 V, stores 2,555 cycles, Wi-iQ Report and E-Connect app).
