# Linde Dynamic Mast Control

## Definition

Linde system for reach trucks whose electric thrust drive counters mast oscillation and deflection at height.

## Notes

- The brochure says Dynamic Mast Control uses an electric thrust drive that makes fast, precise counter-movements to reduce mast swing and deflection, available only on new R14 to R20 HD/N/W reach trucks (series 1120). Source: Linde Rhein-Ruhr safety brochure (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.lmh-rr.de/media/Linde-Rhein-Ruhr/02_PDF/Safety/broschuere_safety.pdf>
