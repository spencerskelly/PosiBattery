# Jungheinrich easyPILOT

## Definition

Jungheinrich belt-worn remote that moves an order picker truck with the operator, with a Follow mode that makes the truck follow the operator.

## Notes

- A 2016 report describes easyPILOT as a remote control worn on a belt: a tap sets the truck moving, it moves in step with the operator and stops at a tap or the next picking position; a 2024 trade article says easyPILOT Follow lets certain Jungheinrich trucks sold by Mitsubishi Logisnext Americas follow the operator automatically. Source: Logistics Matters and Supply Chain 24/7 (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://logisticsmatters.co.uk/page_724637.asp>
