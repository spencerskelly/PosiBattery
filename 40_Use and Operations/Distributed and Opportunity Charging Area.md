# Distributed and Opportunity Charging Area

## Definition

Charging context where chargers are located near work areas and vehicles receive short or opportunistic charging sessions during the operating shift.

## Notes

- Use this context for break-time charging, point-of-use charging and charging outside a centralized battery room.
- Typical surrounding equipment includes [[Industrial Battery Charger|industrial chargers]], vehicles, [[Industrial Traction Battery|traction batteries]], charging cables or ports, and nearby work-area infrastructure.
- Derived from the Distributed and opportunity charging segment in [[PosiCharge Market Segments and Jobs-to-Be-Done]].
