# Load Cell Weight Sensor

## Definition

Force transducer, typically a strain-gauge bridge load cell or load pin, used to convert battery-supported force into an electrical signal.

## Notes

- Capacity, overload margin, bridge resistance, excitation voltage, mechanical mounting, environmental rating, and accuracy are product-specific.
- Multiple sensors may be summed where battery support geometry requires more than one load point.
- No current market-product allocation is asserted.
