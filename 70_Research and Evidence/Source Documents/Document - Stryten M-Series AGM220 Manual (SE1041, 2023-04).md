# Document - Stryten M-Series AGM220 Manual (SE1041, 2023-04)

## Definition

Stryten installation and operating manual SE1041 (April 2023) for the AGM220 integrated battery and charger.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw PDF.

- AGM VRLA with onboard charger, "previously sold as Marathon Pow'r Pak LD".
- Stryten-approved HF charger with CC-CV-CC profile and automatic charge control; constant-voltage portion 2.37 V per cell; do not discharge below 1.90 V per cell (80% depth of discharge); initial equalizing charge before first use; never add water; connect battery to charger before AC.
- Not covered: Ah rating, indicators, communication, opportunity charging rules, input power.
