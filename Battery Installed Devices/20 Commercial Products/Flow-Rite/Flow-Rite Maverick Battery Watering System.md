# Flow-Rite Maverick Battery Watering System

## Definition

Flow-Rite battery-installed single-point watering system built around Maverick watering valves for motive-power lead-acid batteries.

## Notes

- Manufacturer: Flow-Rite Controls
- Market evidence checked: 2026-10-02
- Flow-Rite's motive-power watering systems use automatic shut-off valves and tubing that replace battery vent caps, with a common quick connection to the external water supply.
- The Maverick product is presented for batteries charged while remaining in material-handling equipment and is available as a kit or bundled with Eagle Eye level sensors.
- The external water supply is adjacent infrastructure; the battery-resident valves/tubing/manifold are the installed system modeled here.
- Evidence: https://www.flow-rite.com/battery-care/
