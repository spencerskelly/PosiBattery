## Notes

**Content recorded (as stated by the source, not independently verified unless marked):**
- Voltage classes: 24 V light duty (walkie pallet jacks, small riders); 36 V mid-range sit-down and some order pickers; 48 V most common for 1.5 to 5 ton counterbalanced trucks; 72 or 80 V high capacity, reach trucks, heavy duty. A '48 V' LFP pack charges to 54 V and runs at 51.2 V nominal.
- Capacity formula: Required Ah = (kW x shift hours x 1,000) / nominal voltage x 1.2. Worked example: 10 kW, 8 h, 48 V gives about 2,000 Ah, then says a 608 Ah 48 V pack gives about 31.1 kWh usable and suits medium-duty single shift (C183).
- LFP preferred over NMC or NCA for thermal stability and wide temperature range. BMS limits quoted: over-charge cut-off typically 3.65 V per cell, over-discharge typically 2.5 V per cell, charge suspended below 0 C and above 45 C, operating range -20 to 50 C.
- Claims 3,000+ cycles for Grade A cells from EVE versus degradation after 800 for a poor BMS and cells; IP67 recommended minimum; lithium typically 30 to 40 percent lighter than equivalent lead-acid (C180).
- BlitzNXT 48 V 608 Ah: 38.15 x 32.60 x 22.64 in (969 x 828 x 575 mm), about 864 lb (392 kg), REMA 320A and Anderson 350A connectors, 350 A maximum discharge.
- Warranty benchmark: 5 years minimum, 10 years premium, with a capacity retention commitment such as 80 percent. A 10-year illustrative total-cost table is marked by the author as illustrative.
- References IEC 62619 for secondary lithium cells in industrial applications.

**Checks made:**
- Arithmetic: 10 x 8 x 1000 / 48 x 1.2 = 2,000 Ah; 608 Ah x 51.2 V = 31.1 kWh nominal. The 31.1 kWh is nominal energy but is called usable (C178). Cell limits 3.65 V and 2.5 V per cell are consistent with common LFP data sheets, not verified here.
