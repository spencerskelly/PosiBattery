# Raymond Energy Asset Manager

## Definition

Raymond cloud-based system that gives access to battery performance data from anywhere in a facility.

## Notes

- **Update 2026-10-14 (IB-226):** Raymond's energy solutions page (read on its prod-cms mirror host, <https://prod-cms-raymondcorp.raymondcorp.com/products/energy-solutions>, second-hand) says only that the system gives access to battery performance data from anywhere in the facility; it does not mention state of charge, dashboards or alerts. Not linked to [[Show Battery and Charger State in Fleet Software]].
- Raymond describes Energy Asset Manager as cloud-based, giving access to battery performance data from anywhere in a facility to support proactive energy management; its 2025 lithium release says the batteries integrate with it. Source: Raymond energy solutions page and 2025 release (T1, read through a summarizing tool), retrieved 2026-10-13. <https://www.raymondcorp.com/products/energy-solutions>; <https://www.raymondcorp.com/news/2025/raymond-introduces-lithium-ion-batteries-engineered-and-manufactured-for-peak-energy-demands>
