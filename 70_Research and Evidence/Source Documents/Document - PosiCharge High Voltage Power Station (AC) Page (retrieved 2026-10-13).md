# Document - PosiCharge High Voltage Power Station (AC) Page (retrieved 2026-10-13)

## Definition

Official PosiCharge product page for the High Voltage Power Station (AC): headline, key features, a technical specifications tab, a downloads tab and the link to the product card.

## Notes

**How it was read.** The page was read twice through a fetch tool that returns a model-written summary of the page, not the raw page text. Values below are what that summary reported; each was compared with [[Document - PosiCharge High Voltage Power Station Product Card (2026)]] and the differences are listed. The matching DC page (<https://posicharge.com/products/high-voltage-power-station-dc/>) was read the same way on the same day and reported the same table.

- Page title: High Voltage Power Station (AC). Main heading: High Voltage Power Station. Sub-heading: 20kW DC Fast Charger for eGSE + EVs. Third line: 30kW quick charge via CCS1 + NACS.
- Specification values reported match the card for input voltages and currents, output power (20 kW via DC bus, 30 kW via AC input), output voltage (250 to 920 Vdc), efficiency, connectors, cable, display, network, temperatures, humidity, altitude, enclosure, impact, weight, mounting and certifications.
- **Difference from the card:** the page's protocol row was reported as DIN 70121 and LTE, Wi-Fi, and the page text was reported to contain no mention of OCPP; the card lists DIN 70121 and OCPP 1.6J compliant in one row and cellular 4G LTE, Wi-Fi in a separate network row (C210).
- Reported page inconsistencies: the hero line says 20kW while the features and specifications give 30 kW via AC input (see C81); a humidity label appears twice, and the certification row was reported under a humidity label; the connector label is misspelled. These come from the summary and are not independently checked.
- Reported navigation inconsistency: some site menus link High Voltage Power Station (DC) to the AC address while others link to the DC address. Reported by the fetch tool only; not independently checked.
- The page links the English specification sheet at the card's address and the resources hub <https://posicharge.com/product-resources/>. No warranty terms were reported.
