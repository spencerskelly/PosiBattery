# Battery Thermal Management Device

## Definition

Device that heats or cools battery cells to keep them in an allowed temperature range, usually controlled by the BMS.

## Notes

- **Locus:** battery-integrated. All retrieved evidence is lithium-ion cold-storage heating; cooling and lead-acid thermal devices were not researched.
- Toyota Material Handling Europe describes per-module heating that activates only when a module is too cold, with a cold-store version rated for use down to -30 C. Source: Toyota Material Handling Europe blog (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://blog.toyota-forklifts.eu/forklifts-with-lithium-ion-batteries-in-cold-store-is-that-a-thing>
- BSLBATT describes an individual heater on each module for continuous heating 'up to -40 C' and an internal heater designed to operate during overnight charging in the truck. Source: MH&W magazine and SC Xchange case study (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.mhwmag.com/?p=105883>
- Stromcore describes per-module heating that uses about 2 percent of battery capacity and says modules were tested idle for up to 14 hours at -40 F. Source: Stromcore (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.stromcore.com/cold-storage>
- ROYPOW describes self-heating batteries rated to -40 C with in-freezer charging typically achievable to about -30 C, and advises buyers to ask whether the heater runs off the charger or the battery cells. Source: ROYPOW blog (T4), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.roypow.com/blog/cold-storage-forklift-batteries/>
- A Yale listing says its integrated lithium-ion trucks come with battery heaters installed for cold storage. Source: AllMachines listing for Yale ERC050VGL (T3), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.allmachines.com/forklifts/yale-erc050vgl>
- **Open:** temperature figures describe different things (operating, storage, charging) and are not directly comparable; heater power source differs by vendor. See conflicts note items C6 and C7.
