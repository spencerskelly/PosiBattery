# Document - IETA Business Brief California Low Carbon Fuel Standard (2025-09-19)

## Definition

An industry brief confirming the adopted 2025 diesel benchmark of 81.70 and giving California credit price history and the credit bank.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. Industry-association brief of September 2025 on the amended California LCFS.

- **Benchmarks from Q3 2025:** gasoline from 85.77 to 76.60 gCO2e/MJ; diesel from 86.64 to 81.70 gCO2e/MJ (matching the 2025 diesel value in the proposed order, C298). No 2026 value given.
- **One-time step-down:** described as 9 percent; the diesel change is 5.7 percent and the gasoline change 10.7 percent by the analyst's arithmetic (C300).
- **Targets:** 30 percent below a 2010 baseline by 2030.
- **Prices:** cap $200 per credit (inflation adjusted); high $196.5 per tonne on 2021-03-19; low $39.5 on 2024-05-16; $40.25 on 2025-06-10; spot rose to $50 after approval. **Bank:** 40.22 million MT at the end of Q1 2025.
- **Not stated:** forklifts or electricity EER.
- **Interest:** an emissions-trading association whose members trade credits.
