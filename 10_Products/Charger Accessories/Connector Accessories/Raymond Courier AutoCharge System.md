# Raymond Courier AutoCharge System

## Definition

Raymond's floor- or wall-mounted charging station that charges Courier automated trucks through two vehicle plates, as a factory option or retrofit.

## Notes

- Station supply 120 V, 150 A maximum current; TPPL batteries recommended; vehicle voltage not stated. Courier truck models (3010, 3020, 3220, 3030) are not yet notes in the vault. Sources: <https://raymondcorp.com/Products/-/media/raymond/Literature/Truck-Literature/Courier/SIPB-1093-Courier-AutoCharge-Sell-Sheet_Final> (T1); <https://www.mhlnews.com/new-products/product/55251828/automatic-charging-system> (T2).
