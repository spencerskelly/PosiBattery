# Document - C and S Companies Port of Portland eGSE EER Tier 2 Pathway Report (2022-01)

## Definition

Consultant report for the Port of Portland supporting a request that Oregon add energy economy ratios for electric ground support equipment to the Clean Fuels Program.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- In January 2022 electric GSE could not yet earn Oregon credits: GSE were "not currently listed in Table 7"; the Port wanted EERs added so it and the airlines could earn credits.
- Proposed EERs: generic 2.73 against diesel and 3.56 against gasoline; baggage tractor 2.59 or 3.56, belt loader 2.88 or 3.56, narrow-body pushback 2.73 or 3.56. Earlier figures cited: California cargo handling equipment 2.7; a California Airports Council estimate of 4.2 (gasoline) and 2.9 (diesel).
- Energy per unit is modeled from FAA default horsepower-hours, not measured; no metering rule for eGSE is given.
- The vault's later read of Oregon Table 7 lists electricity for ground support equipment at 3.2 ([[Document - Oregon DEQ OAR 340-253-8010 Tables 1 2 3 4 7 and 9 Standards PDF (undated)]]), so the request appears to have been adopted with a different value (C348).
