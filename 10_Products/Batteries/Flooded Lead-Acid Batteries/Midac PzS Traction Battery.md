# Midac PzS Traction Battery

## Definition

Midac tubular plate traction battery with optional watering, electrolyte circulation and monitoring module.

## Notes

- A dealer says Midac PzS cells use tubular plate technology for trucks, tow tractors and cleaning machines, with Aquamatic, EUW electrolyte circulation, a battery monitoring module and end leads as optional accessories. Source: Batterie Siems dealer page (T3), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.batterie-siems.de/en-gb/2-pzs-280-2mdl140>
