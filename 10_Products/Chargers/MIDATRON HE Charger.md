# MIDATRON HE Charger

## Definition

Midac premium 100 kHz high-frequency charger with IUIa profile and BMU integration, about 8 h or faster in FAST mode.

## Notes

- Required for MHE and MLM; with BMU it configures itself from the BMU. Source: <https://midacbatteries.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/1_CAT-INTEGRATED-SOLUTIONS_MOTIVE_EN_IT_DE_FR-01_2022-5011820-0967_21.pdf>
