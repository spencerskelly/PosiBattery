# Document - Smart Charging Technologies Letter to Oregon DEQ on Forklift Metering Options (2023-05-15)

## Definition

A metering-device vendor's May 2023 letter to Oregon DEQ about forklift metering options and timing.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the western-states clean fuel topic. This is not a DEQ letter: it is a 2023-05-15 letter from Smart Charging Technologies LLC to DEQ's Clean Fuels Program staff, hosted on DEQ's site. It describes the company's concerns and metering products and does not state DEQ requirements or DEQ's response.

- **Meter locations proposed by the company:** CHARGlink units at the output terminals of industrial chargers; IoTEmeter units at AC panels or subpanels feeding chargers, or at each charging station; IoTAh units on individual forklifts. The letter asks whether DEQ must track new versus old trucks.
- **Requests:** a 12-month grace period before enforcing the metering requirement; the company estimates permitting alone could take 3 to 9 months.
- **Not stated:** DEQ's requirement, any accuracy class or tolerance, who may report, a compliance deadline, or whether the grace period was granted.
- **Use:** shows that a vendor read the Oregon rule as allowing meters on the charger side, the panel or the truck. It is the same company as the author of [[Document - Smart Charging Technologies Comment Letter on Electric Forklift EER (2024-05-09)]].
