# Document - Jungheinrich Free 5-Year Lithium-Ion Battery Warranty Release (2017-06-01)

## Definition

Jungheinrich press release from 2017 giving a free 5-year warranty on its self-manufactured lithium-ion batteries.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Warranty: five years or 10,000 operating hours (the page's wording implies whichever comes first), covering repair or exchange if capacity falls below 65 percent of nominal.
- Maker: Jungheinrich says it makes the batteries in-house ("manufacturing depth ... unique in the market") and plans to expand; it does not say whether cells are in-house.
- Chargers: Jungheinrich "also develops and manufactures the charging systems"; batteries communicate with both the vehicle and the charger.
- Versions: 24 V, 48 V and 80 V; trucks with lead-acid batteries "can also easily be converted" to lithium-ion.
- Not stated: whether the warranty requires a Jungheinrich charger or truck; third-party vehicles.
- Dated 2017: an early example of an OEM bundling battery, charger and warranty (C323).
