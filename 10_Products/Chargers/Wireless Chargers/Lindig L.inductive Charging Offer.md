# Lindig L.inductive Charging Offer

## Definition

Lindig's German dealer offer of Wiferion etaLINK 3000 inductive charging (L.inductive), integrated into new Linde manned electric forklifts and tugger trains or retrofitted with the etaTRAY.

## Notes

- Dealer-level offer, not a Linde Material Handling factory option as far as the article states; Linde pages in the vault do not mention it. Sold by [[Lindig]]. Source: <https://www.industrial-production.de/materials-handling/lindig-relies-on-battery-charging-system-from-wiferion.htm> (T2).
