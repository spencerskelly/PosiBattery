# MIDATRON BMU

## Definition

Midac battery monitoring unit mounted on the battery for its whole life, measuring current, voltage, inter-group voltage balance, temperature and electrolyte level.

## Notes

- Temperature input is required for FAST and OPPORTUNITY charging; with a MIDATRON HE charger the BMU sets the charger configuration; data over a mobile app or Service Tool. Required for MLM and MHE. Source: Midac integrated solutions catalogue (T1, second-hand), <https://midacbatteries.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/1_CAT-INTEGRATED-SOLUTIONS_MOTIVE_EN_IT_DE_FR-01_2022-5011820-0967_21.pdf>
