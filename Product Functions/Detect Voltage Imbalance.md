# Detect Voltage Imbalance

## Definition

Detect imbalance between the two halves of the battery or between cells, usually from a mid-battery voltage reading.

## Notes

- Stated for Wi-iQ (half-battery voltage, unbalance LED), Exide Motion+ EasyMonitor (middle voltage tap) and Truck iQ (displays cell imbalance).
