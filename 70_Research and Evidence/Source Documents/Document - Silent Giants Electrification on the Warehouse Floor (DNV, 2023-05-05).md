## Notes

**Content recorded:**
- Nearly 1.5 million forklifts sold a year worldwide; 60% of forklifts sold are electric. Energy use per truck at 40 hours a week for a year: electric 103 MMBtu, diesel 495 MMBtu (the article says 4.5 times less; the numbers give 4.8). ICE delivers about 20% of fuel energy to work, electric 65 to 70%. 103 MMBtu is about 30,000 kWh (conversion by the vault, not in the article).
