# Industrial Battery Connector Glossary and Differences

## Definition

Terms used for the connectors that join industrial truck batteries, trucks and chargers, with the source for each. This is a working glossary built from maker pages and standards listings, not a standards text.

## Notes

- **Power contact / pole:** the current-carrying contacts; DIN-type and SBE/SBX carry two (positive and negative). Source: Anderson catalog, SBE page.
- **Auxiliary contact:** small contact beside the power contacts for battery monitoring and charger communication (EBC up to 4, IBC up to 10, SBE/SBX up to 8). Source: Anderson EBC and IBC pages, SBE page.
- **Pilot contact:** auxiliary contact used as a signal line; REMA lists variants with 2 pilot contacts, EBC has a lower pilot contact. Source: REMA data sheet, EBC data sheet.
- **Last-mate / first-break:** an auxiliary contact that connects after the power contacts and disconnects before them, used for power switching, CAN and interlock loops. Source: Anderson SBE/SBX page.
- **Genderless:** either half mates with the other when rotated 180 degrees (SB family). Source: Anderson SB page.
- **Plug and socket:** the male and female halves of DIN-type connectors (listed as male and female by distributors). Source: McMaster listing.
- **Housing, contact:** housings are the moulded body; wire or busbar contacts are fitted into them (SB350 uses one housing for both). Source: Anderson SB350 page.
- **Coding pin / voltage key:** the feature that stops cross-voltage mating: replaceable hexagonal pins in DIN-type (REMA six voltages 24-96 V), moulded colour keys in SB-type. Source: REMA data sheet, Anderson pages.
- **Wet / dry pin colour:** grey wet-cell, green dry-cell, yellow universal (vehicle or male side). Source: Anderson EBC data sheet, REMA data sheet.
- **Hot plug:** mating under load; the EBC data sheet lists 5 cycles at 200, 400 and 800 A at 96 V for the 80, 160 and 320 sizes. Source: EBC data sheet.
- **Touch-safe:** housings that limit contact with live parts (SBE/SBX to IEC 60950 and IEC 60529). Source: Anderson SBE/SBX page.
- **Air tube:** a connector option carrying air for electrolyte circulation (SBE, SBX, DIN, EBC to 0.8 bar). Source: Anderson catalog, EBC data sheet.
- **Handle, latch, manual release bracket:** accessories for mating force and retention. Source: Anderson catalog; see [[Anderson SBE320-SBX350 Charger-Truck Side Bracket Kit]].
- **Battery side, charger / truck side:** Anderson names the two ends of an SBE320/SBX350 pair this way for its bracket kit. Source: Anderson 994G1 page.
- **Emergency switching off:** DIN VDE 0623-589 states its types are suitable for emergency switching off per EN 1175-1 except type 640. Source: VDE Verlag listing.

Specifications and the differences between families are in [[Industrial Battery Connector Specification Register]].
