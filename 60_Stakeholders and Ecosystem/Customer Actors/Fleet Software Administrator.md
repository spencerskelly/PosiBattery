# Fleet Software Administrator

## Definition

A person who sets up and maintains a customer's access to fleet, battery or charger software: adds users, assigns roles and sets alert thresholds and views.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: source-stated, thinly.** Vendors name the function, not the job title. [[Fleet Manager and Admin Digital Access Evidence]] records three: Toyota I_Site ('colleagues can be added and assigned to roles', F3), Keytroller Infomatics ('roles such as admin, local admin and supervisor', F14) and Flux SkyEMS 3.0 ('role-based user management', F6).
- **Open question (hypothesis only):** whether this is a separate person, the [[Fleet Operations Manager]], a site supervisor or the customer's IT team. No source says who holds the role.
- **Not evidenced:** how many administrators a customer has, what they are called, and whether the dealer or the maker performs setup instead.
