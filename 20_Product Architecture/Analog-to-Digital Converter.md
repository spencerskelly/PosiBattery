# Analog-to-Digital Converter

## Definition

ADC resource that converts conditioned analog measurement signals into digital samples.

## Notes

- May be integrated into the [[Control Circuit]] MCU or implemented as an external ADC.
- Resolution, reference, input range, sampling rate, and accuracy remain product-specific.
