# Convert a Government Forklift Fleet on the ZEF Phase-Out Schedule

## Definition

A government fleet manager replaces LSI forklifts with electric ones by the dates in the phase-out schedule, and does not acquire new LSI forklifts unless an exception applies.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: analyst hypothesis, drafted 2026-10-17.** The steps are reasoned from the rule text in [[Document - CARB ZEF Government Fleets Overview Fact Sheet (2026-01)]]; no customer procedure was read. Please correct with real workflow.
- Steps (analyst wording): list the forklifts by class, model year and location; find each one's phase-out date; plan replacement within the 50 percent (large fleet) or 25 percent (small fleet) cap at the first date; choose electric trucks, batteries and chargers; ask the utility for service (see [[Contact the Utility About New Forklift Charging Load]]); report each year.
- Completion: no forklift is in service past its date and the annual report is filed.
- **Where charging products enter (analyst reasoning):** every replaced truck needs charging at a separately metered location; the vault has no source on which battery or charger types government fleets pick.
