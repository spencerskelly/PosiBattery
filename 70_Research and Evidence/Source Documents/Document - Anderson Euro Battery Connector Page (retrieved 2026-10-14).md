# Document - Anderson Euro Battery Connector Page (retrieved 2026-10-14)

## Definition

Anderson page for the legacy Euro Battery Connector (EBC).

## Notes

- DIN-style EBC in sizes 80, 160 and 320, up to 450 A, meets EN 1175:2020, DIN 43589-1 and VDE 0623-589, up to four auxiliary contacts (battery monitoring and charger communication), colour-coded hexagonal keys; being phased out in favour of the Industrial Battery Connector (IBC).
