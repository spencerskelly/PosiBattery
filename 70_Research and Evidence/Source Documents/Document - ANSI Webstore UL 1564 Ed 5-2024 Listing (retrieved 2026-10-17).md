# Document - ANSI Webstore UL 1564 Ed 5-2024 Listing (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

Catalog entry for UL 1564, the standard for industrial battery chargers. Read for edition, date and scope; the standard text was not read.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), from a standards-seller catalog page.

- **Title and edition:** UL 1564, Standard for Industrial Battery Chargers, Edition 5 (2024), revising Edition 4 (2015); published 2024-02-13.
- **Scope:** chargers rated 600 V or less that charge industrial storage batteries for motive equipment such as material handling trucks, tractors and personnel carriers; cord-and-plug or permanently connected. Excludes marine, fire protection signaling, household and automotive chargers, among others.
- **Lithium:** the page says "industrial storage batteries" only; whether the edition addresses lithium-ion is unknown.
- **Conflict:** a panelist quoted in [[Document - MHI Blog Navigating Third-Party Safety Standards for Lithium Batteries in Lift Trucks (2024-10-08)]] says UL no longer maintains UL 1564. A new edition published eight months before that article contradicts "no longer maintains", though the panelist may have meant something narrower (C316).
- **Existing vault use:** [[Metric - Certifications and Standards]] records UL 1564 for the Stryten EHY charger without an edition; the edition a given charger is certified to is generally not stated in vault sources.
