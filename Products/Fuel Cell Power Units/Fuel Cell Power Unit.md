# Fuel Cell Power Unit

## Definition

Reusable family for hydrogen fuel cell power units that replace the battery in an industrial truck.

## Notes

- The US Department of Energy describes fuel cell power units for Class 1, 2 or 3 trucks, refueled from a hydrogen dispenser in about two minutes. Source: US DOE presentation (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/2014/03/f10/ciotto_infrastructure_for_emerging_markets.pdf>
- Alternative to [[Industrial Traction Battery]]; kept in `Forklifts/Categories` while the survey stays with electric trucks (Q13).
