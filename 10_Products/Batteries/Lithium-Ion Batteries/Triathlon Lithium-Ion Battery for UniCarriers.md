# Triathlon Lithium-Ion Battery for UniCarriers

## Definition

Triathlon lithium-ion battery offered as a factory-installed option on most UniCarriers Class I and III forklifts.

## Notes

- Logisnext Americas says the Triathlon lithium-ion battery solutions are a factory-installed option on most UniCarriers Class I and III models across North and Latin America, with no battery changeover during shifts, fast and opportunity charging, and no gassing. Source: Logisnext Americas release (2023-03-20) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisnextamericas.com/en/unicarriers/news/mla-launches-new-lithium-ion-battery-solutions>
- **Unknown:** voltage, Ah and cell maker; not stated.
- The March 2023 release says the Triathlon lithium-ion battery and charger are a factory-installed option on most UniCarriers Class I and III models across North and Latin America, backed by UniCarriers dealers and Triathlon USA; the listed benefits are consistent performance even at lower state of charge, no battery changeover during shifts, fast and opportunity charging, lower operating and maintenance cost and no gassing. Source: Logisnext Americas release and MH&L summary (T1/T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisnextamericas.com/en/unicarriers/news/mla-launches-new-lithium-ion-battery-solutions>
