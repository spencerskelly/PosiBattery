# Daihen D-Broad Wireless Charging System

## Definition

Daihen D-Broad wireless power transfer for AGVs using WiTricity magnetic resonance technology, up to 4 kW per a supplier-copied list.

## Notes

- The 4 kW and 40 mm gap figures come from a supplier-copied list (T4); the MHL item gives no numbers.
