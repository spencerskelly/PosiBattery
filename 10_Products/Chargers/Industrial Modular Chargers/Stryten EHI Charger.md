# Stryten EHI Charger

## Definition

Stryten software-programmable charger family that receives battery temperature as data over the power line.

## Notes

- Stryten says the EHI family offers software-programmable customized charging profiles, temperature from the battery is transmitted to the charger as data over power line to adjust to the shortest optimal profile, and data stored in the charger can be downloaded wirelessly to a PC management application. Source: Stryten lineup sheet (09/2024) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/14502/133723547947804003.pdf>
- **Unknown:** what battery-side device sends the temperature over the power line; not named on the sheet.
