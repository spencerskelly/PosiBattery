# Raymond 7000 Series Reach-Fork Trucks

## Definition

Raymond reach-fork truck family on 36 V batteries, including the 7500 universal stance and dockstance, 7530 high capacity and 7700 sit/stand models.

## Notes

- A Raymond dealer lists the 7530 high-capacity reach truck at 4,500 lb with 542 in maximum elevated height on 36 V, the 7500 universal stance and dockstance at 3,500 to 4,500 lb with 444 in on 36 V, the 7700 sit/stand at 3,500 to 4,500 lb on 36 V, and the 7200, 7300 and 7310 models, with telematics built into the truck control system. Source: Carolina Handling reach forklift page (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.carolinahandling.com/forklifts/reach-forklifts>
