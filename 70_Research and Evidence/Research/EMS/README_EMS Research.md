# EMS Research

## Purpose

This folder contains the migrated Energy Management System (EMS) research set for warehouses, production facilities, charging infrastructure, building systems, industrial controls, distributed energy resources, and related interfaces.

These are **research notes and research-support records**, not approved product requirements, architecture, protocol support claims, or compliance conclusions.

## Current notes

- [[Energy Management Systems for Warehouses and Production Facilities]] — primary EMS synthesis covering objectives, product categories, interfaces, control functions, MHE charging, metrics, integration, opportunities, roadmap hypotheses, and risks.
- [[Source Links]] — source/link register for standards, protocols, cybersecurity references, grid/DER material, CAN/MHE material, and vendor documentation.
- [[EMS 2]] — acquisition and verification plan identifying the standards, manuals, register maps, protocol documents, and compliance references needed for defensible command-level analysis.
- [[EMS 1]] — preserved research-status note explicitly recording that exact commands, protocol versions, and compliance claims were not yet verified.

## Evidence status

The set intentionally preserves unresolved verification work.

Do not assume that a protocol, command, register, object, topic, compliance standard, or vendor feature listed in these notes is supported by a specific product unless an authoritative source verifies it.

Source Links.md is a research support register, not a replacement for curated Source Document records.

## Maintenance

Preserve distinctions among:

- official standards and vendor documentation;
- general protocol capabilities;
- product-specific command support;
- research interpretation;
- product opportunity hypotheses;
- approved model requirements or architecture.

Extraction into Requirements, Functions, Designs, Interfaces, Product Architecture, or planning records requires separate evidence-backed modeling work.
