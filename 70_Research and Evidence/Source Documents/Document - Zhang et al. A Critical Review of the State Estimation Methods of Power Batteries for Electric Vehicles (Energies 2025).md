# Document - Zhang et al. A Critical Review of the State Estimation Methods of Power Batteries for Electric Vehicles (Energies 2025)

## Definition

Open-access 2025 review of multi-state estimation (SOC, SOE, SOH, SOP, SOT, SOS) for EV power batteries.

## Notes

- EV scope; no lead-acid, forklift or traction content found by text search.
- Says battery life is considered ended when capacity drops to 80 percent of nominal (for EV lithium-ion), another source for the 80 percent threshold in C192 and C349 in a different chemistry and context.
