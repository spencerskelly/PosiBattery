# Document - Energy-Storage.news NFPA 855 2026 Edition Updates (2026-04-29)

## Definition

Trade-press article on what changed in the 2026 edition of NFPA 855 for stationary energy storage projects. Read here only for scope and thresholds; it does not mention forklifts.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand). Read once.

- **Applicability:** depends on chemistry and energy capacity (kWh). The article gives, as examples, under 20 kWh for lithium-ion and under 70 kWh for valve-regulated lead-acid as sizes below which NFPA 855 may not apply, and says other listed chemistry thresholds remain the same in 2026. It presents these as examples and does not say they changed.
- **Structure and chemistries:** the 2023 edition grouped technologies in Table 1.3; the 2026 edition lists energy storage systems in order and adds named chemistries (hybrid supercapacitors, iron-air, lithium metal, zinc bromide).
- **Adoption:** many jurisdictions still enforce codes based on earlier editions, such as the language in the 2024 International Fire Code; confirm local adoption with the authority having jurisdiction.
- **Not stated:** publication or effective date; any exclusion list; any mention of industrial trucks, mobile equipment, forklifts or battery charging areas.
- **Disagrees with** [[Document - Meyer Fire Whats New in NFPA 855 2026 Edition (2025-09-17)]] on whether the stored-energy exemption still avoids a hazard mitigation analysis (C315).
