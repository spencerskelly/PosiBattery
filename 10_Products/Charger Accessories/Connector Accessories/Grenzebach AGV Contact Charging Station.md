# Grenzebach AGV Contact Charging Station

## Definition

Grenzebach 48 V DC charging station with spring contacts rated to 200 A and up to 80 A charge rate for its AGVs.

## Notes

- Charges AGVs 15-80% in about 21-30 minutes; sold with Grenzebach's own AGVs, not as a stand-alone connector. Source: <https://www.grenzebach.com/fileadmin/Grenzebach_Group/Intralogistics/Grenzebach_Intralogistics_Data_Sheet_Charging-Energy-Management_en.pdf> (T1, second-hand).
