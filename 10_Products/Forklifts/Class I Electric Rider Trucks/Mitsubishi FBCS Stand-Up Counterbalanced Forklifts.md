# Mitsubishi FBCS Stand-Up Counterbalanced Forklifts

## Definition

Mitsubishi FBCS16N and FBCS18N stand-up counterbalanced forklifts with dual operator presence pedals and an adjustable anti-fatigue floorboard.

## Notes

- Dealer data repeating Mitsubishi's text says dual operator presence pedals require the standing operator to be in the normal position to activate travel or hydraulics, the suspended floorboard adjusts to operator weight, electric power steering is standard and lithium-ion batteries are offered.
