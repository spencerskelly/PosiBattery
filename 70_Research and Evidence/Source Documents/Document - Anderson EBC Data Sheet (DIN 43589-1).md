# Document - Anderson EBC Data Sheet (DIN 43589-1)

## Definition

Anderson Euro Battery Connector (EBC) data sheet hosted by RS.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- Anderson EBC 80/160/320 per DIN 43589-1: certifications UL, CSA, EN1175-1:1998; voltage 150 V AC/DC (EN1175), 600 V (CSA and UL); dielectric 2000-2200 V AC; -25 to 75 C; IP23; acid resistance 16 h at 1.40 g/cm3; air tube max 0.8 bar. Current by wire size (EN1175/CSA/UL, A): 80 A class 10 mm2 95/70/90, 16 mm2 125/90/120, 25 mm2 145/110/140, 35 mm2 165/120/160; 160 A class 16 mm2 115/90/120, 25 mm2 160/120/160, 35 mm2 185/140/180, 50 mm2 225/160/220; 320 A class 50 mm2 225/165/220, 75 mm2 260/200/260, 95 mm2 350/260/340. Contact resistance power/aux (uOhm): 80 A 176/1100, 160 A 95/920, 320 A 77/1000. Mating cycles no load >5000; hot plug at 96 V 5 cycles at 200/400/800 A. Mating force 68/170/71 N; contact retention 134 N min (80), 445 N min (160, 320). Aux wire 1.5-2.5 mm2 (80), 4-6 mm2 (160, 320); upper aux and lower pilot contacts; on EBC 80 lower pilot and air tubes share a location. Housing PBT/PC, copper alloy contacts with 6 um silver. Coding pins: grey wet cell, green dry cell, yellow universal (male housing only).
