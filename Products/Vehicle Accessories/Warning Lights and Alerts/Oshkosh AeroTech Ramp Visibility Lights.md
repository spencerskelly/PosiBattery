# Oshkosh AeroTech Ramp Visibility Lights

## Definition

Customer Care kit items to make a loader easier to see and operate on the ramp: operator cab beacon, aircraft position light and cab work light, with LED lighting life extended to 10 years.

## Notes

- Oshkosh AeroTech's Customer Care Service and Kit Catalog (Summer 2024 and 2026 editions) lists an Operator Cab Beacon, Aircraft Position Light and Cab Work Light to make the loader easier to see and operate on the ramp, and a lighting upgrade that extends lighting service life to 10 years; the retrieved text cuts off the model coverage. Source: Oshkosh AeroTech Customer Care Catalog (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://oshkoshaerotech.com/hubfs/images/Customer%20Care%20Catalog%20-%20Summer2026.pdf?hsLang=en>
