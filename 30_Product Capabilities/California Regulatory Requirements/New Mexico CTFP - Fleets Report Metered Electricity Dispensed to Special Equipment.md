# New Mexico CTFP - Fleets Report Metered Electricity Dispensed to Special Equipment

## Definition

A fleet that dispenses electricity to fleet vehicles or special equipment (including electric forklifts, per the state's outreach document) shall report the metered kWh quarterly and register its fuel supply equipment in the program's reporting system, from the reporting period beginning 2026-04-01.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It binds the party named in the definition, not a charger. Product consequences are in the derived requirement REQ-00058 (analyst-proposed).
- Second-hand reading; see [[Western State Clean Fuel Programs for Forklift Electricity Register]] and the conflict register.
- **Status:** adopted 2026-01-22 as 20.2.92 NMAC; third-party verification begins 2028-09-15 ([[Document - Trinity Consultants New Mexico Becomes Fourth State to Adopt a Clean Fuel Program (2026-02-27)]]). The rule text was not read, so the forklift provision rests on the outreach document (C294).
