# Document - Smart Charging Technologies Comment Letter on Electric Forklift EER (2024-05-09)

## Definition

A 2024 comment letter that reports CARB's proposal to halve the electric forklift energy economy ratio and cites the effect of metering on credits in Oregon.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic. Public comment letter to CARB dated 2024-05-09 by Ma'n Altaher, Director, Regulatory & Program Management, Smart Charging Technologies LLC, writing for the company and its clients. The company earns revenue from forklift credits, so this is an interested party; every figure below is the author's claim and was not checked.

- **Proposal described:** CARB proposed to cut the electric forklift energy economy ratio (EER) from 3.8 to 1.9, a 50 percent drop; the author says this reduces credits by 66 percent under the LCFS credit formula.
- **Evidence the author cites:** Oregon DEQ fourth-quarter 2023 credit data said to show a drop of more than 90 percent in electric forklift credits between 4Q22 and 4Q23 after metering requirements began there; a December 2022 paper by Dr. Colin Murphy on fractional displacement; CARB's own rationale document.
- **Requests:** a more gradual approach; reassessment after studying the effect of metering on credit numbers; removing Class III forklifts (about 20 percent of forklifts, per the letter) from the baseline; fractional displacement crediting later; clear phase-out criteria applied equally to all fuels; collaboration with aggregators.
- **Status:** this is the proposal stage. The adopted forklift EER was not found (C286); the metering date in the amendments is in [[Document - CARB LCFS Guidance 25-01 Forklift Fueling Reporting (2025-10)]].
