# Air Injection Electrolyte Circulation

## Definition

Forced electrolyte circulation that injects air into flooded lead-acid cells during charging to mix electrolyte and reduce acid stratification.

## Notes

- This Design is a specific realization of [[Forced Electrolyte Circulation]].
- HOPPECKE explicitly describes blowing air into each cell during charging.
- Midac explicitly describes in-cell tubes supplied by a charger-mounted air pump.
- HAWKER explicitly describes an electrolyte-circulation system with an air pump.
- Exide AIR and GS Yuasa Air-Mix establish air-agitation/circulation behavior but do not disclose the complete pump/tubing architecture in the currently modeled sources.
- No claim is made here about air-flow rate, pressure, duty cycle, valve topology, or charger-control strategy unless a product source states it.
