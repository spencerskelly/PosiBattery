# Document - Hyster Lithium-Ion Batteries Page (EMEA, retrieved 2026-10-14)

## Definition

Hyster EMEA web page on its lithium-ion forklift batteries, read through a summarizing tool.

## Notes

Added for IB-233: the page states that the battery's BMS sets the charge current the charger delivers.

- **What it says about charging:** the battery management system monitors cell voltage, module temperature and pack current, talks to the charger over CAN to adjust charge current, and opens the charge contactor if a cell nears an unsafe limit. With two chargers on a dual-charge battery the battery caps total current (example: 400 A battery limit, two 300 A chargers, 400 A flows).
- **What it does not say:** nothing on fast or opportunity charge times or rates; its temperature bands tie to warranty (75 percent state of health for 60 months, 3,000 cycles or 10,000 hours at or below 35 C average), not to charging.
