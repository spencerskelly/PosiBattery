# Place a Load on a Rack or Floor

## Definition

An operator lowers a pallet onto a rack beam or the floor and the lowering is cushioned so the load and the rack are not hit.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: analyst hypothesis, drafted 2026-10-14.** The steps are not an observed customer procedure; they are reasoned from vendor descriptions of the functions. No source describes damage rates; the need and the steps are reasoning. Please correct with real workflow.
- Primary context: [[Material-Handling Fleet Site]].
- Steps (analyst wording): lift and position the load; lower; the final lowering slows; release the load.
- Completion: the load is placed without an impact on the load or the rack.
