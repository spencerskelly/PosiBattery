# Ampure Automotive and Aftermarket EVSE

## Definition

Ampure's automotive and aftermarket business, which develops EVSE for vehicle OEMs, dealers, fleets and drivers. In this vault it is context only: a source of transferable capability, not a PosiCharge comparator.

## Notes

- **Business unit of [[Ampure]]** (vault owner statement, 2026-10-03). No relationship field is written: the vocabulary has no business-unit link and no public source names this unit. See follow-up in [[PosiCharge Opportunity Backlog]].
- **Scope boundary:** do not compare its products against PosiCharge industrial charging unless the same customer job, industrial operating context and buying alternative are documented. See [[Ampure Group Portfolio Context]].
- Ampure Go 2 is a portable EVSE with SAE J1772 or NACS connectors, up to 32 A. Source: Ampure product and shop pages (T1). <https://www.ampure.com/products/ampure-go-2>; <https://shop.ampure.com/products/ampure-go-2>
- Ampure markets Go 2 at 9.6 kW and TurboConnect at 11.5 kW with OCPP integration and cybersecurity features to fleets, dealers and automotive OEMs. Source: Ampure industry pages (T1). <https://www.ampure.com/industries/fleets>; <https://www.ampure.com/industries/automotive-oem>
- TurboConnect lists Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and OCPP 1.6, with remote monitoring, dynamic load management and energy reporting. Source: Ampure product page (T1). <https://www.ampure.com/products/turboconnect>
- **Capability areas that may transfer to industrial work (analyst view, not verified):** connector and cable design, J1772 and NACS knowledge, safety and certification, cybersecurity, OCPP and back-end connectivity, load management, OEM quality processes, sourcing, and dealer and aftermarket channels.
- **Name:** 'Automotive and Aftermarket EVSE' is the vault owner's working name. The official unit name is not recorded.
