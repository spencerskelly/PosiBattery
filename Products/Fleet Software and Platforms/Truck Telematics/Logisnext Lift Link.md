# Logisnext Lift Link

## Definition

Mitsubishi Logisnext Americas telematics with access control, enforced pre-operation inspections, impact detection, hour meters and optional speed monitoring, factory-fitted or retrofit across brands and competitors' trucks.

## Notes

- Logisnext says Lift Link limits use to authorized operators by badge or PIN, enforces pre-operation inspections, uses impact detection and management, captures hour meters and usage in real time, offers two-way communication and, optionally, speed monitoring (select equipment), an external alarm, location tracking and load sensing; it is factory-installed or field retrofit across Cat, Mitsubishi, Jungheinrich and UniCarriers trucks and competitive equipment. Source: MHL News and Logisnext release (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.mhlnews.com/new-products/article/21271747/forklift-telematics-solution>
- The Lift Link sheet (not opened by the research tool) is reported to list a built-in RFID reader, embedded reader options (proximity, iButton, Indala), electronic safety checklists on a built-in display with a 20-key keypad, and sensors including GPS, impact, speed and distance, and loaded or unloaded. Source: Logisnext Lift Link spec sheet (search snippet) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisnextamericas.com/-/media/logisnext/sites/public/documents/unicarriers/sales-literature/lift%20linkspecsheet%20final%2072523.pdf>
