# REMA Euro DIN Battery Connector

## Definition

REMA Euro DIN forklift battery plug and socket family (80, 160 and 320 A seen in a retailer catalog), with colour coding pins for wet, dry and universal batteries.

## Notes

- A retailer lists a 320 A plug for 50 mm2 cable, grey coding pin (wet battery), described as exceeding DIN 43589 and EN 1175-1. REMA's own datasheet has not been read, so ratings are retailer data (T2). Source: <https://akkusys.shop/en/batteries-by-application/forklifts/accessories/2257/rema-plug-euro-din-320a-50mm2-coding-pin-grey-main-contact-strain-relief>
- ROYPOW names REMA 320 A (male and female) as a charge connector on its packs ([[ROYPOW LiFePO4 Forklift Batteries]]).

- **Update 2026-10-14 (REMA data sheet):** REMA Lipprandt makes 80, 160, 320 A and a 400 A series (400 A continuous, 450 A 15 min), 150 V, EN 1175-1 and DIN 43589; coding pin set covers 24, 36, 48, 72, 80, 96 V; pin colours grey wet, green dry, yellow wet plus dry; mating cycles not stated. Source: <https://assets.rs-online.com/v1698978199/Datasheets/18d662b92011f77aacf08eec76685c31.pdf> (T1, second-hand).
