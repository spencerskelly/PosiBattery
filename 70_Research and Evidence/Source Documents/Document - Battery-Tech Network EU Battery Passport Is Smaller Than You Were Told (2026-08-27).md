# Document - Battery-Tech Network EU Battery Passport Is Smaller Than You Were Told (2026-08-27)

## Definition

Trade-media commentary on how many of the 71 passport data points are filled in February 2027 and which are hard to source.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- 71 data points in total; 10 are not to be filled in February 2027 (two duplicates, seven awaiting delegated or implementing acts, one on hold pending a further omnibus); counts given for EV (56) and LMT (59) only, none for industrial batteries.
- State of health is split by category; for industrial batteries it is "marked if applicable across the whole block"; "several cycle-life and efficiency fields apply to industrial batteries" (not listed).
- Composition and dismantling data are called the hardest, mandatory for every category; access rules for "legitimate interest" readers are undefined and the access-rights act is overdue.
- Publisher offers paid media and business development services; the piece is commentary, not a legal source.
