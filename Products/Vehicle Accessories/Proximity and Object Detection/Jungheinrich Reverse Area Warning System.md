# Jungheinrich Reverse Area Warning System

## Definition

Jungheinrich rear-area device that warns by light and sound, stronger as a detected object gets closer.

## Notes

- Jungheinrich says the rear-area warning device monitors the space behind the vehicle and gives an optical and acoustic signal that varies in intensity and volume with the distance of the detected object. Source: Jungheinrich assistance systems page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://jungheinrich.co.uk/products/new-forklifts/jungheinrich-assistance-systems-723794>
