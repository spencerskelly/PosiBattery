# Document - Trinity Consultants California Commits to Repealing ACF and Locomotive Rules While Pursuing ZEF

## Definition

Trinity Consultants' article on California's non-enforcement position for the Zero-Emission Forklift rule.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic. Consultancy article; no publication date appears in the text read.

- It says CARB agreed not to enforce the Zero Emission Forklifts rule until U.S. EPA grants a waiver, and treats this as CARB backtracking from the position in its Final Statement of Reasons that the rule sets no standards preempted by the Clean Air Act.
- It places CARB's current position in the ZEF Advisory of 2025-05-29 and says the advisory states CARB has not yet sought EPA authorization under section 209(e)(2)(A). It gives no timeline or steps for seeking it.
- It names no settlement for the ZEF rule and gives no date for the non-enforcement agreement; the named settlement it describes concerns the Advanced Clean Fleets rule.
- **Wording difference:** it says non-enforcement lasts until EPA grants a waiver; CARB's advisory says until EPA authorizes or determines authorization is unnecessary (C282).
