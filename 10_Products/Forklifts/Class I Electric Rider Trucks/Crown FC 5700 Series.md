# Crown FC 5700 Series

## Definition

Crown four-wheel sit-down electric counterbalance forklift handling loads up to 6,500 lb with the V-Force Integrated Lithium-Ion System and InfoLink.

## Notes

- Crown's brochure says the FC 5700 handles loads up to 6,500 lb in tight spaces, fits a larger battery in a smaller truck through its steering geometry, is offered with the V-Force Integrated Lithium-Ion System, and has InfoLink wireless fleet and operator management and InfoPoint on-truck feedback. Source: Crown FC 5700 brochure (08/22) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.crown.com/content/dam/crown/pdfs/apac/brochures/forklift-truck-fc5700-brochure-GB.pdf>
- A dealer-data site says one V-Force lithium-ion battery can handle multiple shifts on the FC 5700 and lists InfoLink for inspection compliance. Source: AllMachines listing (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.allmachines.com/forklifts/crown-fc-5715-40>
- The brochure lists Access 1 2 3 programmable performance (speeds and acceleration), system-controlled hydraulics that control tilt and limit sideshift speed, a tilt interlock that stops the forks and mast rising above free lift when tilted forward more than two degrees, ramp speed control, hill hold that maintains truck position on a hill when no travel input is given, travel and corner speed control that limits speed in turns by load weight, lift height and steering angle, on-demand power steering, e-GEN braking with an automatic parking brake, and dual traction motors. Source: Crown FC 5700 brochure (in repo, 08/22) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.crown.com/content/dam/crown/pdfs/apac/brochures/forklift-truck-fc5700-brochure-GB.pdf>
