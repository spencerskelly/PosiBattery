# Hyster J2.5-3.0XNL Integrated Lithium-Ion Forklifts

## Definition

Hyster 80 V 420 Ah lithium-ion counterbalanced forklifts with the battery integrated in the truck.

## Notes

- **Battery integration and charging interface (2026-10-14):** the sheet says the battery is integrated and no exchange is needed, with an external charge socket. Source: <https://www.briggsequipment.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/hysterxnldemotrucks-public-new-landscape-en.pdf> (T2).
