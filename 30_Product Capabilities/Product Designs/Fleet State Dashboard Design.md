# Fleet State Dashboard Design

## Definition

A software view that shows battery state, charger status, readiness or energy use for a fleet.

## Notes

- What each shows differs: state of charge for Fronius, readiness and energy use for Linde, availability for Exide.
- A design characteristic found in products, not a decision by us. Added 2026-10-14.
