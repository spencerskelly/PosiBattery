# Panacea Blue Warning Light

## Definition

Panacea UL-listed blue warning light with aluminum construction, Cree LEDs and IP68 rating.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Panacea UL-listed blue forklift warning light built for heavy-duty applications.

**Marketed features:**
- Heavy aluminum construction with toughened glass
- Cree LEDs
- IP68 water protection
- 12-48 V multivoltage
- Also available in red; lifetime warranty
