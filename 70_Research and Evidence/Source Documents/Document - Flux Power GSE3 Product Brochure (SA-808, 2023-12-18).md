# Document - Flux Power GSE3 Product Brochure (SA-808, 2023-12-18)

## Definition

Flux Power GSE3 product brochure (80 V, 315 Ah, LiFePO4): ratings, charge current, temperature range, IP56, SkyBMS Telematics, cycle life.

## Notes

**How it was read.** Through a fetch tool that returns a model-written summary of the file, not the raw text, on 2026-10-13. The direct download from the workspace was refused by the network proxy, so no value was checked against the raw PDF (IB-166).

- Model GSE3 (G-Series); 80 V (76.8 V) nominal; 315 Ah (24.2 kWh); LiFePO4.
- Maximum continuous external charge current 150 A (the 2021 brochure gives 300 A for the GSE3, C213). Operating temperature -30 to 45 C (-22 to 113 F). IP56.
- Integral BMS with patented software; SkyBMS Telematics for battery performance and fleet activity; optional integrated heaters; truck adapters available to meet OEM weight requirements.
- "Over 3500 cycles at 80% DoD with no maintenance" (maker claim, no end-of-life basis stated).
- Not stated: dimensions, weight, warranty, charger model, communication protocols, certifications other than IP56. The sheet says the information is informational and may change without notice.
