# Crown Battery Manufacturing

## Definition

US battery company, listed among forklift battery makers, whose site shows an EVOLUTION charger series with the same text as the Power Designers REVOLUTION series.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Battery Maker, Brand Owner.
- Crown Battery's Power Line page describes an EVOLUTION Series of modular chargers with the same specification table as the REVOLUTION Series and says combined with the PowerTrac it becomes multi-voltage (24/36/48), and refers to a PowerHouse EV. Source: Crown Battery power line page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://crownbattery.com/lp-power-line>
- A supplier list describes Crown Battery as a maker of lithium-ion, deep cycle, starter and lead-acid batteries along with chargers. Source: Foxtron Power Solutions list (secondary, dated) (T4), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.foxtronpowersolutions.com/forklift-battery-manufacturers/>
- **Name caution (C27):** unrelated to Crown Equipment as far as retrieved sources show; no link is established either way.
- A MODEX 2020 exhibitor listing says Crown Battery Manufacturing has made lead-acid batteries since 1926 at its headquarters in Fremont, Ohio, lists the PowerHouse line under batteries, chargers and motors, and gives 14 sales and distribution offices. Source: MODEX 2020 exhibitor listing (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://2020.modexshow.com/listing.aspx?id=46360>
- Crown Battery's site names PowerHouse Battery Chargers and PowerHouse Fast Chargers and calls itself the world leader in energy-efficient chargers for material handling. Source: Crown Battery site (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.crownbattery.com/es/power-that-moves-you>
- In 2017 Crown Battery acquired the distributors Industrial Powersource and Warehouse Energy Power. Source: Batteries International (T2 (dated)), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.batteriesinternational.com/?p=2300>
- **Update (C27 largely resolved):** Crown Battery (Fremont, founded 1926) and Crown Equipment (New Bremen, founded 1945) have different founding dates and headquarters in the sources, so they are separate companies as far as retrieved text shows. Common ownership is not addressed.
