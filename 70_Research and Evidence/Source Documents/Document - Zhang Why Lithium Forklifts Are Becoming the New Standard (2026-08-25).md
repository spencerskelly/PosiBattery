## Notes

**Content recorded (as stated by the source, not independently verified unless marked):**
- Utilization decides the case: single shift or low utilization, lead-acid may stay competitive; two shifts, lithium becomes attractive; three shifts, opportunity charging and no battery swapping matter most; automation raises the value of smart BMS and communication; cold storage needs thermal management from the start.
- Total cost items listed: electricity, maintenance, labor for battery handling, spare batteries, charging infrastructure, downtime, replacement frequency.
- Two packs both rated 48 V 400 Ah LiFePO4 can differ in cell quality, continuous and peak current, BMS, thermal design, connectors, communication protocol, enclosure and charging requirements.
- A smart BMS can monitor cell voltage, current, temperature, SOC, SOH and protection status and talk to the forklift over CAN, RS485 or UART; a pack with the right electrical specification can still fail if the CAN logic does not match the vehicle.
- Battery compartments are rarely universal: dimensions, weight, current, connector, communication, charging, IP protection and mounting must match.
