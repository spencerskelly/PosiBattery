# United States Environmental Protection Agency

## Definition

Federal agency that decides whether California may enforce its own nonroad engine rules under Clean Air Act section 209(e)(2).

## Notes

- Added 2026-10-17 from [[Document - EPA 2012 Notice of Decision on California Large Spark-Ignition Engines (77 FR 20388)]] and the CARB notices in [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]].
- In the CARB notices read, no authorization request for the Zero-Emission Forklift regulation had been made; no EPA decision on it was found.
