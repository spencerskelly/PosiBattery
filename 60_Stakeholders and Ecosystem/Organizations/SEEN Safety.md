# SEEN Safety

## Definition

Maker of the IRIS 860 infrared-laser sensor that detects retroreflective tape on high-visibility apparel and warns forklift drivers and pedestrians.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Accessory Maker.
- SEEN Safety's own page and maker-hosted installation guide describe the IRIS 860 sensor; [[Holt of California]] sells it as a dealer. Source: SEEN Safety (T1), retrieved 2026-10-04. <https://www.seensafety.com/seensystem/iris860>
