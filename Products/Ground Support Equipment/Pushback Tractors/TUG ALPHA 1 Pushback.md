# TUG ALPHA 1 Pushback

## Definition

Textron lithium pushback, CE certified.

## Notes

- A trade listing names TUG ALPHA 1 as a CE-certified lithium pushback. Source: The Flying Engineer listing (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://theflyingengineer.com/?p=25152>
- **Verification 2026-10-03 (round 40, verified; lithium model only):** Textron GSE's introduction release (AviationPros copy) says the TUG ALPHA 1 has three powertrain options (lithium, gas, diesel); the lithium model has a battery management system that protects and monitors battery health and performance including efficiency, temperature and state of charge, with zero-maintenance batteries; a trade article adds a hitch pin sensor that disables pushback mode if the hitch is not secured to the aircraft; a dealer listing adds remote and onboard diagnostics and fleet monitoring. Sources: Textron GSE release (T2) <https://www.aviationpros.com/ground-support-worldwide/gse/pushbacks-tractors-utility-vehicles/press-release/21160222/textron-gse-textron-gse-introduces-the-tug-alpha-1>; Airside International article (T2) <https://www.airsideint.com/issue-article/innovation-in-gse-textrons-tug-alpha-1/>; Fortbrand listing (T3) <https://fortbrand.com/products/tug-alpha-1-electric/>.
