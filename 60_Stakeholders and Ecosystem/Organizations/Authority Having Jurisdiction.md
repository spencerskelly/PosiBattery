# Authority Having Jurisdiction

## Definition

Business role: the local body, such as a fire marshal or building official, that decides whether an installation meets the fire and building codes adopted where the site is, and that interprets those codes for a given site.

## Notes

- Added 2026-10-17 for [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]]. The term appears in the sources read ([[Document - Energy-Storage.news NFPA 855 2026 Edition Updates (2026-04-29)]] advises confirming local adoption with the AHJ), but no AHJ decision, letter or practice for a lithium forklift charging area was found (IB-279).
- Which edition of a code an AHJ enforces, and whether it treats lithium charging under NFPA 855, IFC section 309 or its own interpretation, is unknown and varies by place.
- Not linked to products: no relationship in the vocabulary fits an authority.
