# Document - Connexus Energy Electric Forklift Rebates (2026)

## Definition

Connexus Energy's 2026 rebates of $2,000 per forklift and $75 per shift for high-frequency charging.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- **Rebates:** $2,000 per forklift for fleet addition, conversion or new construction; $500 for fleet retention or refurbished additions; high-frequency charging $75 per shift of member business (set by shifts per day, days per week, weeks per year and number of chargers; not for hybrid-to-high-frequency).
- **Eligibility:** commercial members; forklifts operating at least 40 hours per week; Class 1 36/48/80 V 3,000 to 12,000 lb; Class 2 24/36/48 V 3,000 to 5,500 lb; Class 3 12/24 V 3,500 to 8,000 lb.
- **Limits:** capped at 50 percent of invoiced cost; $30,000 per member per year; pre-approval above $2,500; apply by 2026-12-15.
- **Why it matters (analyst reasoning):** the charger incentive is paid per shift of use, so it pays for shift data a charger or software already records.
