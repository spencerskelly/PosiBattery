# Leoch PzS Traction Battery

## Definition

Leoch PzS traction lead-acid battery cells (2 V, 200 to 400 Ah at 5 h) with tubular positive plates and reduced water loss.

## Notes

- Leoch's page lists PzS cells of 2 V from 2PzS200 to 5PzS400 (200 to 400 Ah at 5 h), with tubular positive plates, lower water loss and fewer maintenance visits ('half maintenance'), for forklifts, tractors, carriers and similar equipment. Source: Leoch motive power page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://leoch.com/product/power/127.html>
