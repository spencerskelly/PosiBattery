# Exide MARATHON Classic Battery

## Definition

Exide (GNB) tubular-plate MARATHON Classic range in FT and FF versions for warehousing, cleaning machines, tractors, lifts and road vehicles.

## Notes

- FT range 900 cycles (IEC 60254-1), e.g. 6 V 180/200 Ah, 12 V 110 Ah; FF range 300 cycles for high-rate discharge, 6 V 187-297 Ah, 12 V 40-158 Ah (C5). Part of the family listed in [[Exide MARATHON Battery]]. Source: GNB motive power overview 2021-08 (T1, second-hand; parts garbled), <https://www.exidegroup.com/eu/sites/default/files/2021-08/GNB_MP_Overview_EN_web.pdf>
