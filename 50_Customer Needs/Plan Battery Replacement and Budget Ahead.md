# Plan Battery Replacement and Budget Ahead

## Definition

Customer need: Plan Battery Replacement and Budget Ahead. The problem behind it: A manager with many batteries needs to know each battery's age, condition and remaining life so replacement can be planned and budgeted instead of handled after failures.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: need hypothesis.** The statements below are what makers, dealers and trade press say fleet managers do or want. At most one named customer source supports any need; none ranks it. Per the ruleset, source research becomes a validated need only after customer-side evidence. Full evidence is in [[Fleet Manager and Admin Digital Access Evidence]].
- **Problem solved (analyst wording):** A manager with many batteries needs to know each battery's age, condition and remaining life so replacement can be planned and budgeted instead of handled after failures.
- **Who has the problem:** [[Fleet Operations Manager]].
- **Realized by (specific functions; products link to these):** [[Predict Battery Replacement Timing]], [[Estimate State of Health]], [[Upload Battery Data to Cloud Portal]], [[Log Battery Events and Usage]].
- **Evidence (by source):**

(T2, 2017) [[Document - Supply Chain 24-7 Real-Time Battery Monitoring Fills in the Data Gaps (2017-09-05)]]
  - F12: a Crown column advises recording each battery's condition and capacity yearly, logging battery hours and number of charges, and using monitoring to see remaining cycles. Vendor-authored. (T2) [[Document - MMH Crown Other Voices Five Steps to a Healthier Forklift Fleet (2020-02-27)]]
  - F6: Flux SkyEMS 3.0 adds pack-level state of health diagnostics, lifecycle tracking and end-of-life forecasting. (T2, 2026) [[Document - Charged EVs Flux Power SkyEMS 3.0 (2026-07-09)]]
  - F13: an unnamed German dealer customer of Exide Motion+ Fleet said it could 'monitor and plan our new battery orders much better' (reported by the vendor's trade-press item). (T2) [[Document - Logistics Matters Cloud Management for Forklift Charging (Exide Motion+ Fleet, 2022-02-18)]]
  - F9: a vendor case study says an unnamed grocer using Philadelphia Scientific iBOS cut battery-change costs by 'nearly $195K', freed about $600K of excess battery inventory and extended battery life 28%. The page does not say which reports were used. See C196. (T2, vendor-produced) [[Document - MMH 237K in Annual Battery Savings in a Grocery DC (2026-03-06)]]
- **Gaps:** no customer-side source with a name; the savings figures are vendor-produced and unreconciled (C196). The vault has no source stating how fleets decide a battery is at end of life; see [[SOC and SOH Decision Criteria]] for the SOH threshold question.
