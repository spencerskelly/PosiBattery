# Stryten M-Series T330 Battery

## Definition

Stryten round-tubular flooded battery for opportunity and fast charging, claiming up to 18 percent more capacity than conventional flooded batteries in the same container.

## Notes

- **Identity:** flooded lead-acid traction battery made by [[Stryten Energy]] (formerly GNB Industrial Power); family [[Flooded Lead-Acid Traction Battery]]; subtype electrical (anything electricity passes through).
- **Specifications (as stated):**
| Parameter | Value as stated |
|---|---|
| Chemistry and plates | flooded lead-acid, round tubular plates |
| Capacity claim | up to 18% more capacity than conventional flooded batteries in the same container |
| Charging | designed for opportunity and fast charging |
| Run time claim | longer run times than competitive square tube designs |
| Voltage, Ah, size, weight | not stated in the lineup sheet (to-do) |
| Watering interval | not stated (T310, a sibling, states up to 90 days) |
| Warranty, cycle life | not stated (to-do) |
