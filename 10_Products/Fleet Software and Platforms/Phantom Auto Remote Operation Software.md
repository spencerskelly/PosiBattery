# Phantom Auto Remote Operation Software

## Definition

Phantom Auto remote operation software integrated into specific UniCarriers and Mitsubishi electric trucks for unmanned operation.

## Notes

- Partnership item dated 2021-05-13. Lets operators drive unmanned tuggers, pallet jacks and counterbalance trucks remotely and take over from AGV/AMR autonomy. Which truck models are integrated is not stated (the MX2/MXL link is provisional: they are the UniCarriers electric trucks in the vault). Source: <https://www.mmh.com/article/phantom_auto_partners_with_mitsubishi_logisnext_americas_group> (T2, second-hand).
