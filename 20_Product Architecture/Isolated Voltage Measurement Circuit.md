# Isolated Voltage Measurement Circuit

## Definition

Voltage-measurement circuit that transfers the measured battery voltage across a galvanic isolation boundary.

## Notes

- Candidate devices include isolation amplifiers, isolated ADCs, isolated sigma-delta modulators, or complete isolated measurement modules.
- No specific product is assigned without evidence that isolation is actually used.
