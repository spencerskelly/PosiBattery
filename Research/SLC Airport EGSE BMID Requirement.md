# SLC Airport EGSE BMID Requirement

## Definition

Airport rule that requires lithium-ion batteries and Battery Monitor and Identifier Modules on electric ground service equipment.

## Notes

- Salt Lake City airport inspection procedures state that, effective 2020-09-15, ground support equipment entering the bag room or covered terminal areas except tug tunnels must be electric, and all electric ground service equipment must have lithium-ion batteries and Battery Monitor and Identifier Modules (BMIDs) installed, and are inspected for battery type and battery monitor. Source: Salt Lake City Department of Airports EGSE inspection procedures (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.Slcairport.Com/assets/pdfDocuments/EGSEInspectionProcedures.pdf>
- **Reading caution:** the document uses 'BMID' generically and names no vendor. Whether it means the PosiCharge BMID or any monitor and identifier module is not stated (conflicts C17).
- **Why it matters:** it is the only retrieved evidence that a BMID-class device is a site requirement for GSE, and it applies to lithium batteries.
