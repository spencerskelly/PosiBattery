# Document - Khan et al. Flooded Lead Acid SOC Estimation Using Adaptive Unscented Kalman Filter (Energies 2024)

## Definition

Open-access journal article (Energies 17(6), 1275, 2024) on state of charge for a sealed flooded lead-acid battery with an open-circuit-voltage hysteresis model and an adaptive unscented Kalman filter.

## Provenance

- **Source address:** https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1073/17/6/1275
- **Repo file:** Downloads/energies-17-01275.pdf (15 pages), added by the owner on 2026-10-18. Text was extracted from the file and the passages below were read; this is a first-hand read of the file, but not every section was read in full.
- **Evidence tier:** not covered by T1 to T5 in [[Landscape Evidence and Modeling Conventions]] (paper, standard or catalogue record).
- **Source key in the SOC and SOH notes:** S25.

## Notes

- Test article: one 90 Ah, 12 V sealed flooded lead-acid (SFLA) battery from Sebang (Seoul) in a 25 C chamber. No pack, no traction battery and no voltage above 12 V was tested.
- The paper states hysteresis between charge and discharge OCV is very prominent in lead-acid, especially sealed flooded, and small in most lithium-ion; OCV curves were recorded after 3 min and after 3 h of rest.
- Result as stated in the abstract and conclusion: SOC error about 2 percent with the adaptive filter against about 5 percent with the conventional filter, on random charge/discharge profiles. Error definition and profile current levels were not extracted.
- The paper also says plain current integration suffers cumulative error from current sensor drift.
- Naming: the title says flooded; the body calls the battery sealed flooded lead-acid and says the hysteresis of maintenance-free sealed types had not been investigated before. Whether results carry to open vented traction cells is not stated.
