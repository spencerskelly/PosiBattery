# Wiferion etaTRAY Battery Tray

## Definition

Wiferion etaTRAY: a forklift retrofit lithium-ion battery tray with an integrated etaLINK 3000 receiving unit, 24-48 V, 60-180 A.

## Notes

- Modular capacity sizes; the receiver is in the tray, so the charge path is the wireless pad rather than a plug. Source: <https://www.wiferion.com/us/products> (T1, second-hand).
