# Ampure

## Definition

Parent operating group (Ampure Charging Systems) for PosiCharge, Power Designers Sibex, and the Automotive and Aftermarket EVSE business. In this vault Ampure is the internal group context, not a competitor.

## Notes

- **Group role in this vault:** internal. Its businesses are analyzed as one portfolio; overlap between them is recorded as internal overlap, not competition. See [[Ampure Group Portfolio Context]].
- **Business units (vault owner statement, 2026-10-03):**
  - [[PosiCharge]] — industrial MHE and eGSE charging, battery intelligence and connected fleet-energy operations. Primary improvement focus.
  - [[Power Designers]] (Power Designers Sibex) — industrial conventional, opportunity and fast charging, PowerTrac monitoring, PowerCharge.NET energy management, and electronics manufacturing. Internal industrial portfolio and shared-team capability.
  - [[Ampure Automotive and Aftermarket EVSE]] — automotive OEM, dealer and aftermarket EVSE. Contextual capability source only.
- **Shared teams:** some teams cover more than one business (vault owner statement, 2026-10-03). Which teams, and how, is not yet recorded.
- Ampure Charging Systems, backed by Transom Capital, acquired Power Designers Sibex of Crystal River, Florida, to add power configurations, ISO-certified manufacturing and final assembly, OEM relationships, and predictive-analytics and energy-demand-management software to PosiCharge. Source: Ampure press release (T1), 2025-09-02. <https://www.ampure.com/press-releases/transom-capital-backed-ampure-acquires-power-designers-sibex>
- Industrial products are offered under the PosiCharge brand, including the PosiLink platform. Source: Ampure press release (T1), 2024-04-01. <https://www.ampure.com/press-releases/ampures-posilink-redefines-efficiency-in-material-handling-fleet-operations>
- Ampure was formed when Webasto sold a majority stake in Webasto Charging Solutions to a California private-equity firm, which renamed it Ampure. Source: Automotive News (T2). <https://www.autonews.com/suppliers/webasto-sells-majority-stake-its-ev-charger-business>
- **Lineage caution:** older PosiCharge sheets say 'a product line of AeroVironment, Inc.' (C69). Dates of each ownership change are not recorded here.
