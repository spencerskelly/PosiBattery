# BMID - Retain Battery-Specific Usage History

## Definition

The PosiCharge BMID family shall retain battery-specific charging or usage history sufficient to support later review of the battery's operation and care.

## Notes

- Current family evidence supports storing battery identity, charging profile, and charge-event history.
- Retention duration, event schema, timestamp accuracy, and memory capacity are not yet defined requirements.
- Cloud synchronization is not required at the family level.
