# Document - Canada Clean Fuel Regulations Schedules 3 and 11 Registration and Annual Report Contents (Justice Laws)

## Definition

The registration and annual report schedules of Canada's Clean Fuel Regulations, which key reporting to charging station serial numbers and kWh.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. Schedule 3 (registration report contents, Justice Laws page 20) and Schedule 11 (annual credit-creation report contents, page 28).

- **Registration (Schedule 3):** items 9, 10 and 11 cover charging-network operators for private and public stations and charging-site hosts for other stations, each identified by province.
- **Annual report (Schedule 11):** item 5 (charging-site host) reports, per province, the carbon intensity of electricity and the serial number of each charging station, with GPS coordinates to five decimals; item 6 (network operator) reports kWh supplied.
- **Forklifts, off-road equipment and metering:** not mentioned in these schedules.
- **Implication (analyst reasoning):** the reporting unit is the charging station with its serial number and location, which resembles California's site-level FSE but is more granular.
