# Charge Without Anyone Plugging In

## Definition

Customer need: Charge Without Anyone Plugging In. The problem behind it: someone has to plug in, unplug or swap a battery every time a truck is charged, which costs labor, wears connectors and cannot happen at all for automated or driverless vehicles.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: need hypothesis.** The statements below are what makers say their products do for customers. No customer, user or buyer source in the vault confirms that customers hold this need or rank it. Per the ruleset, source research becomes a validated need only after customer-side evidence.
- **Problem solved (analyst wording):** hands-free charging removes the plug-in step and the worn connector; for automated vehicles it is the only way to charge without a person.
- **Who has the problem:** [[Fleet Operations Manager]], [[Forklift Operator]].
