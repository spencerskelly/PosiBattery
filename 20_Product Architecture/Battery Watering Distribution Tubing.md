# Battery Watering Distribution Tubing

## Definition

Tubing and manifold path that distributes water from a common battery watering connection to individual cell watering valves.

## Notes

- Philadelphia Scientific Stealth explicitly identifies tubing and barbed fittings.
- Flow-Rite explicitly identifies tubing joining its automatic shut-off valves to a common quick connection.
- Exact material, diameter, routing, connectors, and maximum pressure remain product-specific.
