# Toyota Carriage-Mounted Camera

## Definition

Toyota camera on the carriage that helps the operator position forks at height.

## Notes

- Toyota says the Carriage-Mounted Camera gives additional visual aid for positioning forks at high heights. Source: Toyota Assist page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.toyotaforklift.com/toyota-assist>
