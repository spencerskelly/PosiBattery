# Midac

## Definition

Italian battery maker of traction batteries (PzS) and lithium forklift batteries; accessories include watering, electrolyte circulation and a monitoring module.

## Notes

- **Role (working label, not a governed property):** battery maker.
- Midac's trade-fair profile says it was founded in 1989, makes starter, traction and stationary batteries, counts well-known vehicle and device manufacturers among customers, offers lithium forklift batteries, and lists battery charger among its keywords. Source: Automechanika exhibitor profile (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://automechanika.messefrankfurt.com/frankfurt/en/exhibitor-search.detail.html/midac-spa.html>
- A dealer listing says Aquamatic watering, an electrolyte circulation system (EUW), a battery monitoring module and end leads are available as optional accessories for Midac PzS traction batteries. Source: Batterie Siems dealer page (T3), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.batterie-siems.de/en-gb/2-pzs-280-2mdl140>
- **Unknown, do not infer:** who the 'well-known vehicle and device manufacturers' are, and whether any sell Midac batteries under their own brand. No relationship with Triathlon or Sunlight is stated in these sources.
- **Offered or promoted with its batteries (each item with its web page):**
  - Watering: Aquamatic <https://www.batterie-siems.de/en-gb/2-pzs-280-2mdl140>
  - Electrolyte circulation: EUW system <https://www.batterie-siems.de/en-gb/2-pzs-280-2mdl140>
  - Monitoring: battery monitoring module (optional accessory; maker of the module not stated) <https://www.batterie-siems.de/en-gb/2-pzs-280-2mdl140>
  - Chargers: listed as a keyword in the trade-fair profile; models not retrieved <https://automechanika.messefrankfurt.com/frankfurt/en/exhibitor-search.detail.html/midac-spa.html>
