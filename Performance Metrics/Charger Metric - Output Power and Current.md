# Charger Metric - Output Power and Current

## Definition

Output Power and Current: Rated output power and maximum current.

## Notes

- **Code and class:** CM02; charger metric. Unit or format: kW; A per voltage.
- **Comparability rule:** Current depends on voltage and module count; compare at the same battery voltage.
- **Direction:** higher is better.
- **Values on file (as stated in each product note; n/s means not stated):**
  - [[ACT Quantum 2]]: 2-24 kW; max 25-600 A; full-load 2-76 A
  - [[ACT Quantum 3]]: 3-24 kW; Q4 75-300 A; Q8 375-600 A; dual port 150-300 A x2
  - [[Crown V-HFM3 Charger]]: max current: FS3 300 A (24/36 V) to 127.5 A (96 V); FS4 400 to 255 A; FS6 600 to 255 A; kW n/s
  - [[Delta-Q IC650]]: 650 W; 24 V 27.1 A, 36 V 18 A, 48 V 13.5 A
  - [[EnerSys IMPAQ Charger]]: ratings not in the AMER guide (n/s)
  - [[EnerSys NexSys+ Charger]]: ratings not in the AMER guide (n/s)
  - [[Fronius Selectiva 4.0]]: UL brochure: classes 2, 3, 8, 16, 30 kW; 30 kW at 80 V: 250, 300, 375 A (3 x 400 V 49-54 A); 16 kW at 80 V: 120-210 A; earlier note: 16 kW: 96 V 140/160 A, 120 V 110/140 A; 30 kW: 96 V 250/300 A, 120 V 210/250 A
  - [[Lester Summit Series II]]: 1200 W and 1425 W; 40/40/30 A max (1425 W sheet); earlier note: 650 W (24 V 25 A, 36 V 18 A, 48 V 13.5 A), 1050 W, 1425 W
  - [[PosiCharge DVS100]]: 320 A, 20 kW (200 A and 10 kW per port)

  - [[Power Designers REVOLUTION X]]: 3.9-31.2 kW from 1.3 kW modules
  - [[Stryten EHF Charger]]: 24 V: 40-150 A; 36 V: 40-240 A (by model)
  - [[Stryten X-3 Charger]]: 3-bay 3-9 kW; 6-bay 3-18 kW; 10-bay 21-30 kW
  - [[Stryten X-7 Charger]]: 2-bay 5-15 kW and 4-bay 5-30 kW (earlier page); up to 30 kW (2026); earlier note: up to 30 kW
