# California Site Claiming LCFS Forklift Electricity Credits

## Definition

Operating context: a charging location in California whose owner reports forklift charging electricity to CARB's Low Carbon Fuel Standard to earn credits. Participation is optional.

## Notes

- **Evidence:** the facility or location owner has been the first reporting entity for electric forklifts since 2025-07-01, and each charging location is registered as fueling supply equipment ([[Document - CARB LCFS Guidance 25-01 Forklift Fueling Reporting (2025-10)]]).
- **Rental locations** are registered separately, and the guidance does not address fleet-owned forklifts charging at a customer site (confirm with CARB, C283).
- **Participants:** [[Fleet Operations Manager]] and [[Fleet Software Administrator]] as stand-ins; the reporting role needs Signatory Authority in CARB's reporting tool and is not mapped to a vault actor.
- Related: [[Electric Utility or Grid Operator]] (utility pathway status is open, C288). Register: [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]].
