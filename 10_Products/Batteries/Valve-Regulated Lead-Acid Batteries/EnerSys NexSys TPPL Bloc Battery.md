# EnerSys NexSys TPPL Bloc Battery

## Definition

EnerSys 12 V monobloc thin plate pure lead battery line (12NXS26 to 12NXS186) sold beside the NexSys TPPL 2 V cell line.

## Notes

- 14 models, 12 V, 26 to 186 Ah (C6) and 30 to 210 Ah (C20); weights 21.1 to 131.1 lb; terminals M6/M8/3/8-16 female with adapter options A (SAE post) and B (M6 male front); flexible connectors required. Source: EnerSys TPPL 12V data sheet (T1, second-hand), <https://www.enersys.com/4a88c3/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/nexsys/pure-12v/amer/amer-en-td-nex-tppl-1023.pdf>
- Sheet wording: TPPL chemistry "optimizes opportunity charging, reduces sulfation and offers a higher energy throughput" and never requires watering, washing or long equalize charges. [[Charge Battery by Opportunity]] (V): <https://www.enersys.com/4a88c3/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/nexsys/pure-12v/amer/amer-en-td-nex-tppl-1023.pdf>
- Cycle life, charge parameters, warranty and temperature are not stated in the sheet.
- The name "NexSys TPPL Bloc" comes from EnerSys's forklift page and flyer titles; the data sheet title is "NexSys TPPL 12V". Whether the two are the same line is assumed (IB-187).
