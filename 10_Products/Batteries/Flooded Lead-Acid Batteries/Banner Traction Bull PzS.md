# Banner Traction Bull PzS

## Definition

Banner Traction Bull PzS/PzB flooded lead-acid traction battery, certified explosion-safe, with an option to replace water automatically.

## Notes

- Banner's brochure describes the Traction Bull PzS/PzB as a lead-acid battery with liquid electrolyte, 'certified, explosion-safe' and the most cost-efficient, best-proven solution for industrial trucks and other e-vehicles; the text on automatic water replacement is cut off in the retrieved copy. Source: Banner Energy Solutions brochure (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://bannerbatterien.com/energysolutions/upload/files/FolderAGS_GB.pdf>
- **Not stated in the retrieved text:** voltages, capacities, cycle life and warranty.
