# Anderson Double Stack Manual Assist Brackets

## Definition

Anderson double stack manual assist brackets (SKU 994G6 for A320/E320; SBE320/SBX350 versions) that hold two connector sets in parallel for higher charging current and ease mating.

## Notes

- **Update 2026-10-14 (priority 5, unlinked-product review):** linked to [[Connect Battery to Charger or Vehicle]] because the brackets hold two connector sets in parallel and ease mating, like the single side bracket kit already linked. Source: as cited on this note.
- Bracket and connectors are sold separately; no current rating stated. Related single bracket: [[Anderson SBE320-SBX350 Charger-Truck Side Bracket Kit]]. Sources: <https://www.andersonpower.com/product/double-stack-din-truck-charger-brackets-for-a320-and-e320-series/>; <https://www.mouser.in/en/new/anderson-power-products/anderson-pp-double-stack-brackets/> (T1/T2).
