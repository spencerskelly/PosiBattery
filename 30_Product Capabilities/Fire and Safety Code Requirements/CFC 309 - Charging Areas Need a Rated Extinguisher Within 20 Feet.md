# CFC 309 - Charging Areas Need a Rated Extinguisher Within 20 Feet

## Definition

Under section 309.5 of the 2025 California Fire Code, battery-charging areas shall have a section 906 portable extinguisher rated at least 4-A:20-B:C within 20 ft of the charger.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It binds the site.
- The rating is for ordinary-combustible, flammable-liquid and electrical fires; the page says nothing about lithium battery fires, which the insurer sheet describes as hard to extinguish ([[Document - If P&C Hazard Info Sheet Lithium-Ion Stacker Trucks (2024-06)]]).
- Second-hand reading; see [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]].
