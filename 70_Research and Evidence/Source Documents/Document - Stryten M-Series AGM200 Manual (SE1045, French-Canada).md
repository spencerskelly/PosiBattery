# Document - Stryten M-Series AGM200 Manual (SE1045, French-Canada)

## Definition

Stryten M-Series AGM200 installation and operation manual SE1045, French-Canada edition.

## Notes

**How it was read.** Through a fetch tool that returns a model-written summary, not the raw text, on 2026-10-13; values were not checked against the raw file (IB-173 to IB-178).

- Charger must be Stryten-approved and match voltage and capacity, with a constant current, constant voltage, constant current profile and automatic charge control; finishing rate 2 A per 100 Ah; constant-voltage setpoint 2.37 V per cell; charge normally eight hours or less from 80 percent depth of discharge; no removal of vent caps.
- Cooling of at least as long as the charge lasted (eight hours after a full charge); equalizing charge before first use; equalizing charge after every 50 to 100 operating hours, or every two weeks if hours are unknown.
- Normal operating range 15 to 38 C (60 to 100 F): above 38 C shortens life, below 15 C reduces capacity and needs special charging.
- Do not discharge below 1.86 V per cell under load or 2.02 V per cell open circuit (80 percent depth of discharge); truck cutoff set to the same values times the cell count; do not discharge more than 80 percent of rated capacity within six hours; state of charge cannot be read from specific gravity.
- Opportunity charging is not allowed except in approved VGA applications, where Stryten recommends a 7-hour charge and 17-hour discharge control for 24-hour periods.
- The battery has a discharge alarm: an intermittent 80 dB tone when voltage falls to or below the alarm reference for a set period; a continuous tone means 80 percent depth of discharge.
- Warranty is voided by removing vent caps, by adding monitoring devices without written authorization, or by damaging or tampering with monitoring devices.
