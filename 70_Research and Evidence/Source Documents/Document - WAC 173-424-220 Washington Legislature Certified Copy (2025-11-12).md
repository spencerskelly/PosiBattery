# Document - WAC 173-424-220 Washington Legislature Certified Copy (2025-11-12)

## Definition

The official certified text of WAC 173-424-220, with the forklift reporting-entity, operator-notification and designation-contract rules.

## Notes

**How it was read:** first-hand, from the raw file the owner added to the repo (`Downloads/WDOE_WAC 173-424-220.pdf`), with text extracted by pdftotext. Not a summary.

Added from the owner's downloads. Official certified copy (certified 2025-11-12) of WAC 173-424-220, designation of the fuel reporting entity for electricity. The web address uses the Legislature's standard pattern and has not been checked.

- **Electric forklifts (subsection 6):** the fleet owner is the fuel reporting entity and credit generator. The owner must annually notify the forklift operator in writing that the owner is generating credit for the operator's electricity use, with the estimated annual credits and credit revenue based on the previous year's credit price (for 2023 the average of the annual average prices in CARB and Oregon DEQ).
- **Passing the rights to the operator:** if the owner does not generate credits, the operator may, by written contract that the owner will not generate and the operator accepts all CFP responsibilities. The operator then gives the owner the same annual written notice.
- **Designation (subsection 3):** any eligible credit generator may designate another entity by written contract. From 2026-01-01 new contracts must use the quoted sentence waiving credit generation and allocating credits to the designated entity, with the first entity agreeing to provide electricity data. The generator must notify the first entity of total credit revenue for the previous year.
- **No metering mandate here:** no general metering requirement for forklift charging appears in this section; metering and calculation sit in other sections (not read).
- **Conflicts:** matches the Cornell copy (C295) and settles part of C293. The same-named WAC section differs from the Washington slides, which list a special calculation for electric forklifts with model year before 2023 (C303).
