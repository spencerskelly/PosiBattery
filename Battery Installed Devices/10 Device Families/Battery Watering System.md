# Battery Watering System

## Definition

Battery-installed fluid-distribution hardware that enables multiple flooded lead-acid cells to be filled from a common connection while controlling cell fill level.

## Notes

Water supply equipment that is not installed on the battery is adjacent infrastructure; the valves, tubing, manifolds, couplings, and battery-resident portions are within this family.

## Aliases

- Single-Point Battery Watering System
- Battery Watering Manifold
