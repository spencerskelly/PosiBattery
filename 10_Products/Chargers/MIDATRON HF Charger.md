# MIDATRON HF Charger

## Definition

Midac conventional high-frequency charger with IUIa profile, fan cooling, 7 to 12 h, wall mounted, IP20.

## Notes

- A combined HF/HE version is listed for VLA and VRLA in the MDL-MBS and MLM tables; whether it is a separate product is not stated (IB-190). Source: <https://midacbatteries.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/1_CAT-INTEGRATED-SOLUTIONS_MOTIVE_EN_IT_DE_FR-01_2022-5011820-0967_21.pdf>
