# Document - Charged EVs Xendee 2024 Commercial EV Charging Survey (2024-06-21)

## Definition

Charged EVs newswire item on the second annual Xendee Commercial EV Charging Market Survey.

## Notes

Facts below were read through the fetch tool's summary of the page, not from the raw page.

- Xendee sells design and operation software for distributed energy and EV charging; it asked 'leaders in the EV charging industry'. No sample size or respondent split is given on the page, and fleet operators and warehouses are not mentioned.
- About 75 percent called utility grid limits a significant roadblock to commercial EV charging; 63 percent named total infrastructure cost. Demand charges are not mentioned. Respondents named distributed energy resources and microgrids as the most important technology.
- Supply-side respondents, vendor sponsor, commercial road EVs: it supports the problem 'grid limits and cost hinder charging' in general, not for forklift or GSE fleets.
