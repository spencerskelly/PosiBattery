# ENEROC

## Definition

Hangzhou, China maker of lithium batteries, battery management systems and chargers for forklifts and other industrial vehicles; its US arm launched in North America in March 2026.

## Notes

- Founded 2018; regional offices in Germany, Japan, Italy, Korea and Singapore; ENEROC USA launched 'in partnership with' [[CATL]]. Reports over 180,000 packs sold and presence in 30+ countries (company-reported). Source: Supply Chain 24/7 (T2), 2026-03-10.
- Products named for forklift Classes I, II and III, very narrow aisle trucks, golf carts, aerial work platforms, sweepers and scrubbers; chemistry and model data not stated, so no product notes yet.
- ENEROC USA's marketing director published a propane-to-electric sizing article; see [[Document - ENEROC Propane to Electric Battery Sizing (2026-09-23)]] and conflicts C177 and C183.
- Not found: model-level specifications, chargers' ratings, or the nature of the CATL partnership (cells, packs or distribution).
