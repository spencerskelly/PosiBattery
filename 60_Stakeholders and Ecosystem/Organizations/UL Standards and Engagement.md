# UL Standards and Engagement

## Definition

Standards developer behind UL 583 (electric battery-powered industrial trucks), UL 1564 (industrial battery chargers) and UL 3100 (automated guided vehicles); the Standards Council of Canada notice names it as "UL Standards & Engagement".

## Notes

- Added 2026-10-17 for [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]]. Only catalog listings were read; the standards themselves are unread.
- UL's separate testing and certification business is not covered here; no source read separates the two roles for these standards.
- UL 2580 (lithium battery listing) is widely cited in existing vault notes (for example [[Metric - Certifications and Standards]]) but its scope was not read in this pass.
- Not linked to products: no relationship in the vocabulary fits a standards body.
