# Document - Southwest Iowa REC Commercial Electric Forklifts Rebate C15 (2021)

## Definition

A 2021 cooperative rebate of $1,000 per Class 1 or 2 electric forklift, expired.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- **Rebate (expired 2021-12-31):** $1,000 per Class 1 or 2 lift and $350 per Class 3; leased without buyout $100 per lease year up to $1,000; used Class 1 or 2 from $900 (model year 2020) down to $100 (2012); used Class 3 not eligible.
- **Use:** a 2021 baseline showing how incentives rose to $2,000 to $6,000 per truck by 2026.
