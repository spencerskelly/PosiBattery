# Diagnostic-Loop Adaptive Charging

## Definition

Adaptive charge-control strategy that evaluates battery status or capacity through repeated charging-current loops and changes the charging behavior accordingly.

## Notes

- EnerSys describes the IMPAQ heavy-duty wet-cell pulse profile as diagnosing battery status or capacity through continuous current loops.
- This Design records the published implementation principle without inferring the proprietary diagnostic algorithm or loop parameters.
