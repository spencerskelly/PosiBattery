# EnerSys LifeNetwork iQ

## Definition

EnerSys charging-room management software for iQ modular high-frequency chargers, with FIFO queue display and battery rotation.

## Notes

- Up to 512 chargers over Ethernet; database and predefined reports; multiple battery families; off-peak charging; email/SMS reports; Windows 10; remote access with optional hardware. Not available in the United States. Source: EnerSys product page (T1, second-hand), <https://www.enersys.com/en/products/monitoring-and-fleet-management/network-and-softwares/enersys/lifenetwork-iq>
- The EnerSys organization note also names "LifeNetwork iQ" with Xinx; relation to Xinx not established (IB-183).
