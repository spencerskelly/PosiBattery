# Custom RF Interface

## Definition

Wireless interface design using a product-specific or proprietary RF physical/link implementation rather than a named standard such as Bluetooth, LoRa, or Wi-Fi.

## Notes

- Use only when product evidence establishes a custom RF implementation.
- Frequency, modulation, protocol, range, and regulatory details remain product-specific.
