# TLD Group

## Definition

Maker of belt loaders, tractors and aircraft safety docking systems for airports, with a lithium-ion battery system.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Truck OEM.
- A trade magazine says TLD's electric belt loader NBL-E can use lead-acid or lithium-ion batteries and TLD's own patented iBS lithium-ion system (page extraction partly garbled). Source: Ground Handling International (April 2020) (T2 (extraction partly garbled)), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://powerstow.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/GHI_Apr2020_Power-Stow.pdf>
- TLD's Group Chief Procurement Officer says the TLD aircraft safety docking (ASD) system is now an industry standard, automatically reducing speed close to aircraft and reporting collisions. Source: Ground Handling International (April 2023) (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://ghi.mydigitalpublication.co.uk/april-2023/page-44>
