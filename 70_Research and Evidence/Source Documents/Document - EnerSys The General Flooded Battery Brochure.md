# Document - EnerSys The General Flooded Battery Brochure

## Definition

Manufacturer brochure for EnerSys The General flooded battery line and PowerPak battery-with-charger units.

## Provenance

- **Local artifact:** `Forklift-and-Equipment-Batteries-General-Brochure-V2.pdf` in `Reference Files`; image-only PDF read by OCR on 2026-10-13.
- **Author or publisher:** EnerSys (named on the cover); no web address printed on the pages read.
- **Original web address:** not captured in the saved copy.
- **Evidence tier:** T1 (see [[Landscape Evidence and Modeling Conventions]]).

## Notes

- Manufacturer brochure for The General flooded lead-acid batteries for lift trucks and pallet jacks (EnerSys logo on the cover and the battery label). Maker named: [[EnerSys]].
- Claims: ISO 9001:2004 certified management system, 'first in our industry'; lead insert bushings (prevent electrolyte creepage); injection-moulded polypropylene jars with heat-sealed jar-to-cover seals, guaranteed against leakage for life; 3-D computer-designed grid; Retent-a-tape positive plate (glass mat bonded to the plate to stop shedding); sleeved positive separator.
- The General PowerPak: a 12 V or 24 V battery with a built-in plug-in charger (SCR technology and dv/dt termination), for pallet jacks, stand-up riders, tuggers and order pickers.
- Data tables list heights over the lifting ear of 14.87 to 30.56 in and plate counts of 5 to 33 for the G series, and 5 to 11 plates for the PowerPak; the Ah-per-positive-plate column was not legible in the OCR and is not recorded.
