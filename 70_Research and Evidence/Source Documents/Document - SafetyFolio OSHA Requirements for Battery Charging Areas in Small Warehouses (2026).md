# Document - SafetyFolio OSHA Requirements for Battery Charging Areas in Small Warehouses (2026)

## Definition

A compliance-software vendor's guide to OSHA battery charging area requirements, with a lead-acid versus lithium-ion comparison. A secondary source used for the statement that no lithium-specific OSHA standard exists.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand). The article is a secondary source; the summary tool itself advised checking osha.gov.

- **Framework:** OSHA has no single battery-charging standard; requirements come from 29 CFR 1910.178(g) with 1910.303, 1910.305, 1910.1200, 1910.94 and 1910.151(c) also relevant.
- **Eyewash:** the "within 10 seconds" figure is attributed to ANSI Z358.1, used by OSHA as an enforcement benchmark under 1910.151(c), not to OSHA text.
- **Ventilation:** no numeric OSHA limit; an industry target of hydrogen below 25 percent of the lower explosive limit is cited.
- **Lithium-ion (per the article):** the same general framework applies including 1910.178(g); hydrogen concern is "minimal" but thermal runaway is a fire risk; for fire protection it points to NFPA 855 and the local fire authority; as of mid-2025 no lithium-ion-specific OSHA standard exists. It does not distinguish no-smoking rules by battery type.
- **Challenge:** the pointer to NFPA 855 is not backed by any scope statement in the article; it is the origin of the backlog's NFPA 855 premise at best, and is unverified (C314).
