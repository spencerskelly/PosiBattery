# Philadelphia Scientific Water Injector System

## Definition

Philadelphia Scientific water injector watering system for industrial batteries.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Philadelphia Scientific single-point water injector system that fills an industrial battery in about 15 seconds without floats.

**Marketed features:**
- Fills an 18-cell truck battery in 15 seconds or less (FAQ: 15-30 s), claimed eight times faster than float systems
- Precision level-sensing valve shuts off at the Injector tip; no floats or exposed parts
- Classic (series tubing) and Spider (parallel manifold) versions
- Unaffected by oil and tar; works in extreme heat and cold; cold-store drain-back tubing
- Instant-Install (about 3 min, one part number) or Semi-Assembled options
- Marketed as paying back within the first year
