## Notes

**Content recorded (as stated by the source, not independently verified unless marked):**
- Flux Power engineers its batteries to work with a defined set of compatible chargers 'including a range of certified partners like Stanbury, Delta-Q, and PosiCharge'.
- Verify voltage, amperage and communication protocol compatibility with the battery's BMS before adding a charger; some advanced chargers handle multiple charging profiles during a phased rollout.
- Opportunity charging should follow duty cycle and shift pattern; excessive fast charging or charging in poor temperature conditions can accelerate degradation.
- Overcharging risks unstable internal conditions and, in extreme cases, thermal runaway; the charger and BMS are the safeguard. Routine deep discharge shortens life.
- Intelligent charging that adjusts charge rate to temperature and battery condition helps in facilities without climate control.
- Telematics: SkyEMS 3.0 dashboard shows how charging affects battery health; the page footer lists 'SkyBMS' as a trademark and the body names 'SkyEMS' (the Flux Power note already records both).

**Checks made:**
