## Notes

**Content recorded (as stated by the source, not independently verified unless marked):**
- Rule of thumb: any break of 15 minutes or longer is worth plugging in for; no minimum discharge depth before charging.
- A 48 V-class BlitzNXT pack is rated for 54 V charge voltage and 200 A standard charge current. Which model is not stated (C181).
- Charge only between about 0 and 45 C (32 to 113 F). Store at roughly 50 percent SOC in a cool, dry place if idle for more than a few weeks.
- BMS functions: over-charge, over-discharge, over-current, short-circuit, cell balancing, temperature monitoring. Log BMS fault codes, long charge times and temperature warnings as early signals.
- Charging area: marked location away from aisles, chargers protected from truck impact, ventilation, bay labels by voltage class, extinguisher access, trained personnel. References OSHA 29 CFR 1910.178(g) and NFPA 505.
- Claims 3,000+ cycles and 10+ years for EVE Grade A LiFePO4 packs; says a battery on a multi-shift fleet is a five- to six-figure investment.
