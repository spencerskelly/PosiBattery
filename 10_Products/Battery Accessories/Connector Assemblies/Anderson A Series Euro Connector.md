# Anderson A Series Euro Connector

## Definition

Anderson A Series (A80, A160, A320) Euro-type battery connectors with configuration codes A to F for auxiliary contacts, male or female, and grey, green or yellow keys.

## Notes

- Relation to the EBC and to the DIN A and DIN E families in Anderson's catalog is not stated (C253). Source: <https://www.farnell.com/datasheets/3799502.pdf> (T1, second-hand).
