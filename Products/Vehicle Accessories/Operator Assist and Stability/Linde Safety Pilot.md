# Linde Safety Pilot

## Definition

Linde driver assistance system that shows load weight, centre of gravity, lift height and tilt against limits and intervenes in lift functions to prevent overturning.

## Notes

- A Linde-side report says the Safety Pilot indicates when the truck approaches its limits with the load centre of gravity, current load weight, lift height, tilt angle and maximum lift height; KION says it automatically intervenes in operating and lifting functions to prevent overturning; a dealer brochure names Safety Pilot 'active' and 'select' variants and Dynamic Mast Control. Source: Logistics Matters, KION story and Linde dealer brochure (T2/T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://logisticsmatters.co.uk/page_513926.asp>
- KION says Linde Load Assist and Linde Safety Pilot are installed as standard in many forklifts, and Active Stability Control compensates floor irregularities at lift heights up to 18 m. Source: KION Group stories (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.kiongroup.com/en/Newsroom/Story-Categories/Innovation/Article/7-solutions-that-make-the-warehouse-safer.html>

  - [[Sense Load Weight and Lift Height]] (V): <https://www.kiongroup.com/en/Newsroom/Story-Categories/Innovation/Article/7-solutions-that-make-the-warehouse-safer.html>

Source: Linde Rhein-Ruhr safety brochure (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.lmh-rr.de/media/Linde-Rhein-Ruhr/02_PDF/Safety/broschuere_safety.pdf>
