# Document - Cattwyk Omnibus Package IV Amendments to the Battery Ordinance (2025)

## Definition

Law-firm news commentary on Omnibus IV proposals that postpone the battery due diligence obligations and exempt smaller companies.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Due diligence start moves from 2025-08-18 to 2027-08-18 (the intro gives a typo "August 27" that conflicts with the body); the Commission's guidelines deadline moves to 2026-07-26.
- Proposal to exempt companies under EUR 150 million revenue from Chapter VII (the Commission FAQ and other sources read give different thresholds; see C334).
- Says nothing about the battery passport, QR code, labelling or carbon footprint.
- Status described is mid-2025 (negotiations under way); later events appear only in [[Document - BatteryMBA EU Battery Regulation Passport Dates Page (2026-07-26)]].
