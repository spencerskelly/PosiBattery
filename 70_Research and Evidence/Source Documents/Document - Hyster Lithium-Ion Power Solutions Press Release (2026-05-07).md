# Document - Hyster Lithium-Ion Power Solutions Press Release (2026-05-07)

## Definition

Hyster press release announcing lithium-ion batteries and chargers sold as one factory-validated package for Hyster forklifts.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw page. A trade-press copy ([The Machine Maker](https://themachinemaker.com/news/hyster-launches-integrated-lithium-ion-power-solutions-for-forklift-fleets/)) adds nothing beyond the release.

- **What it says:** LFP batteries with modular construction (individual modules serviceable); "compact high-speed" chargers; each element "validated by the factory" as an integrated ecosystem; the battery talks to other truck systems and feeds Hyster Tracker telemetry with alerts (low charge, high temperature); the charge cable goes to a port reachable without opening the hood and interlock sensors block truck movement while charging; chargers can sit in high-traffic areas for opportunity charging; dealers give a single point of contact for the truck, battery and charger, including warranty administration.
- **Third-party power:** only a contrast: the integrated system reduces mismatch risk "commonly associated with third-party power systems". It does not say third-party batteries or chargers are unsupported.
- **Not stated:** who builds the battery or charger, model names, voltages, capacities, charge times, warranty terms, price, launch date or regions.
