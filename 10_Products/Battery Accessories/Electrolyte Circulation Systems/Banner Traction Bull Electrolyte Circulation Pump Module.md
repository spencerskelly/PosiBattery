# Banner Traction Bull Electrolyte Circulation Pump Module

## Definition

Optional pump module for Banner Traction Bull wet batteries that mixes the electrolyte during charging.

## Notes

- **Update 2026-10-14 (priority 5, unlinked-product review):** linked to [[Circulate Electrolyte]] because Banner's page says the pump module mixes the electrolyte during charging. Source: as cited on this note.
- **Working name given by the analyst; Banner gives none.** Pump module for homogeneous mixing during charging; prevents electrolyte and temperature stratification. Banner claims (marketing, unverified): up to 20% lower energy consumption, charging time cut by up to 30%, up to 80% less water consumption. Source: Banner Traction Bull page (T1, second-hand), <https://www.bannerbatterien.com/en/Products/Industrial-Batteries/Traction-Bull/244-Wet>
- Amaron claims up to 60% less water and up to 20% less energy for its own system ([[Amaron Electrolyte Circulation System]]); basis for none of these figures is stated, so they are not comparable (IB-185).
- Air or pump type, voltage and compatible cells not stated.
