# Document - Digi 2G 3G 4G LTE Network Shutdown Updates (2025-12-12)

## Definition

Blog by a cellular-module vendor's CTO listing 2G, 3G and 4G shutdowns by carrier and region, updated December 2025.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- **AT&T:** 2G stopped in 2017; 3G "sunsetted ... in February of 2022"; no AT&T LTE end date given.
- **Verizon:** 2G CDMA phased out end of 2020; CDMA/3G retired 2022-12-31 (decommissioned by 2023-01-03; the page gives both a January 2023 and a December 2022 wording).
- **T-Mobile:** 3G retired 2022-07-01; 2G (GSM) retirement timing "varies", no date.
- **Canada:** Bell 2G shut June 2018; Rogers began retiring 3G 2025-08-07; Telus 3G in Manitoba 2025-12-31 (rural) and 2026-03-31 (Winnipeg and Brandon); no Canada-wide LTE date; Rogers voice after 3G needs VoLTE.
- **Europe:** Vodafone plans 2G off by 2030 (UK "during 2030"; Germany disables 2G in September 2028 but keeps it for certain critical IoT until end of 2030); 3G timing varies widely by country.
- **LTE generally:** "4G LTE will be available for at least a decade to come." LTE-M and NB-IoT: no dates; recommends LTE-M or NB-IoT for low-bandwidth sensors "where available".
