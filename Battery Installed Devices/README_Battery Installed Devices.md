# Battery Installed Devices

## What is here

A market reference for products and reusable device families that are physically installed on a battery, integrated into a battery/pack, or directly form part of the battery assembly.

Initial emphasis is industrial forklift/MHE and GSE batteries. Adjacent applications are intentionally included when they may expose transferable technology, interfaces, packaging, sensors, communications, maintenance methods, or business models.

## What belongs here

- battery monitoring and diagnostic devices;
- battery identification and charger-interface devices;
- electrolyte/water-level monitors;
- single-point watering and electrolyte-management hardware;
- integrated battery-management, protection, telematics, thermal-management, and safety devices as research expands;
- commercial products that implement those reusable concepts.

Vehicle-mounted displays, charger-side gateways, site infrastructure, and cloud software are **adjacent**, not battery-installed. They may be linked later where needed, but should not be mixed into the core installed-device inventory.

## Modeling approach

Commercial products are reusable Object definitions. Their use on a particular battery, forklift, GSE vehicle, or other system is contextual and belongs in a Local Model occurrence when that architecture is modeled.
