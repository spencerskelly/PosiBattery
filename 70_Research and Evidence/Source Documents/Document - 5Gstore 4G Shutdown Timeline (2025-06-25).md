# Document - 5Gstore 4G Shutdown Timeline (2025-06-25)

## Definition

Retailer blog estimating when 4G LTE will shut down, with no carrier-attributed dates.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- States "no U.S. carrier has officially announced a 4G shutdown date" (June 2025).
- Estimates without a cited source: first notices 2032 to 2035; widespread phase-out 2035 to 2045; complete shutdown mid-2040s or later. The page contradicts itself (the conclusion says shutdown "may not be officially" until 2032).
- Says legacy LTE modules will be phased out as IoT moves to NB-IoT and 5G RedCap, while low-band LTE may last longer for IoT.
- The publisher sells cellular routers and antennas and promotes RedCap, a conflict of interest for the migration message.
