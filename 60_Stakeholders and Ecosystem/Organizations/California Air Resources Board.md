# California Air Resources Board

## Definition

California state air agency that adopted the Zero-Emission Forklift regulation in June 2024 and administers the Low Carbon Fuel Standard, including forklift charging reporting.

## Notes

- Added 2026-10-17 from the sources listed in [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]]; no further agency facts were researched.
- Enforcement stance on the forklift rule differs by party (private fleets paused; government fleets bound); see the register.
- Not linked to products: no relationship in the vocabulary fits a regulator.
