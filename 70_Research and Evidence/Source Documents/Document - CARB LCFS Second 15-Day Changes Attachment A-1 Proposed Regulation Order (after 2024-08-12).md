# Document - CARB LCFS Second 15-Day Changes Attachment A-1 Proposed Regulation Order (after 2024-08-12)

## Definition

CARB's proposed LCFS regulation order after the second 15-day changes, with the forklift EER, benchmark, reporting entity and forklift reporting text.

## Notes

**How it was read:** first-hand, from the raw file the owner added to the repo (`Downloads/CARB_2nd_15day_atta-1.pdf`), with text extracted by pdftotext. Not a summary.

Added from the owner's downloads. Strikethrough and underline version of the proposed LCFS regulation order after CARB's second set of 15-day changes (about 14,000 lines of text). The file carries no issue date; its own note says the first 15-day text was released 2024-08-12. It is still a proposed order, not the adopted one.

- **EER values (section 95486.1(a), Table 5):** electric forklifts with lift capacity under 12,000 lb: 2.4; 12,000 lb and over: 3.8. Hydrogen fuel cell forklifts: 1.6 (under 12,000 lb) and 2.1 (12,000 lb and over). Also listed: eTRU 3.4, eCHE 2.7, eOGV 2.6, propane 0.9 (as the comparison fuel), Table 5 note on BEV, PHEV, FCV and ICEV.
- **Benchmarks (section 95484):** diesel and substitutes 87.89 gCO2e/MJ for 2024, 81.70 for 2025, 80.17 for 2026, 78.63 for 2027, 77.10 for 2028 and lower through 2038 (35.95). The 2025 step is -7.0 percent against 2024 in this table.
- **Reporting entity (section 95483(b)(4)):** for electric forklifts, eTRU, eCHE and eOGV the owner of the FSE is the fuel reporting entity and credit generator. The owner may designate another entity by written contract that (1) the owner will not generate credits and will provide the electricity data to the designated entity, and (2) the designated entity accepts all LCFS responsibilities. Generators must meet section 95491(e)(5).
- **FSE meaning (section 95483.2(b)(6) as numbered in this text):** for electric forklifts, eCHE, eOGV or eTRU, FSE refers to the facility or location where electricity is dispensed for fueling. If several pieces of equipment at the site can measure the electricity dispensed, providing each one's OEM serial number and OEM name is optional. So the registered unit is the site, not the charger or the forklift.
- **Reporting (section 95491(d)(3)(E)):** beginning with 2026 reporting the quantity of electricity dispensed (kWh) must be reported per FSE with a certified fuel pathway code and transaction type 'Forklift Electricity Fueling'; before 2026 the quantity may be estimated using a CARB-approved methodology. The reporting entity must report the number of electric forklifts in the fleet.
- **Conflicts:** confirms 2.4 and 3.8 as proposed values (C296); gives the benchmark 80.17, not 85.38 (C298); the 9 percent cut in a trade post does not match this table (C300). Whether the adopted order matches this text is unverified (IB-242).
