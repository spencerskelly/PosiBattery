# Document - CARB ZEF Authorization Update Bulletin (2025-05-27)

## Definition

CARB's e-mail bulletin of 27 May 2025 announcing the enforcement pause for private fleets, manufacturers and dealers.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic. CARB e-mail bulletin dated 2025-05-27 announcing an authorization update on the Zero-Emission Forklift regulation. The advisory PDF it points to carries the date 2025-05-29 ([[Document - CARB ZEF Implementation Update Advisory (2025-05-29)]]); the two-day difference is logged in C281.

- CARB approved adoption in June 2024 and has not requested EPA authorization under Clean Air Act section 209(e)(2)(A); the bulletin gives no timeline for a request.
- Until EPA grants authorization or determines it is unnecessary, CARB staff will not enforce the regulation against privately owned or operated fleets, manufacturers or dealerships, including for earlier acts or omissions.
- The bulletin says the advisory explains how the regulation affects state and local government fleets but does not itself state their enforcement status.
- CARB encourages voluntary reporting and continued emission reductions.
