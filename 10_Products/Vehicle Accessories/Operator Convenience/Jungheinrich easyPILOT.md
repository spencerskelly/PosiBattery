# Jungheinrich easyPILOT

## Definition

Jungheinrich belt-worn remote that moves an order picker truck with the operator, with a Follow mode that makes the truck follow the operator.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Jungheinrich hands-free remote control for ECE horizontal order pickers that lets the truck move with or follow the operator.

**Marketed features:**
- Marketed as up to 30 percent more picks per hour
- easyPILOT Follow: truck follows the operator wearing the Follow Bar, with auto-pairing
- easyPILOT Control: Control Bar, wrist Control Band or keypad moves the truck a configurable distance
- Side-mounted signal lights, DayLEDs and collision protection
- Available on all ECE order pickers; seamless switching with jetPILOT steering
