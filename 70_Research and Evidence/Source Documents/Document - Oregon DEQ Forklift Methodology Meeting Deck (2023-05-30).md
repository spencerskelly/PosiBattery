# Document - Oregon DEQ Forklift Methodology Meeting Deck (2023-05-30)

## Definition

Oregon DEQ's 2023 forklift meeting deck, with the count of registered electric forklifts and their share of electricity credits.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the business case. DEQ slides from the 2023-05-30 meeting on the proposed forklift estimation method.

- **Population:** "more than 3,500 electric forklifts are registered in CFP" in May 2023; credits from electric forklifts "represent over a third of electricity credits in recent quarters", which the deck says largely reflects fuller reporting of the current population.
- **Method:** electricity per forklift = 80 percent of battery capacity x adjustment factors x shifts per work day x work days; the proposal lowered the assumption to 20 percent per shift and two shifts per day. No battery capacity or adjustment values are given.
- **Rule text quoted:** fuel quantities must be reported "according to accurate metering for all other fuels when they are dispensed"; DEQ "may approve an alternative methodology" if fuel cannot be accurately metered at dispensation.
- **Not stated:** per-forklift kWh or credit value, meter types, charger efficiency. Figures are from 2023 and older than the 2024 metering change.
