# ITW GSE

## Definition

Airport ground-support equipment maker (ITW GSE ApS, Odense, Denmark; ITW GSE Inc., Palmetto, Florida) offering ground power units, pre-conditioned air units, DC chargers and gate power management.

## Notes

- Offerings named: 2400 GPU (ground power unit), 3500 PCA (pre-conditioned air unit), DC Charger, Power Share, Intelligent Power Management (IPM) and EcoGate. Source: ITW GSE EcoGate page (T1).
- EcoGate is 'a design approach ... not a single product'; IPM is hosted in the 3500 PCA; Power Share distributes surplus grid power to eGPUs and other electric GSE. None of the three has published specifications, so they are recorded here and not as product notes.
- Only the DC Charger has published figures (60 kW per outlet, CCS2); see [[ITW GSE DC Charger]].
- Claims 'no major airport infrastructure upgrade required' for the approach; unsubstantiated manufacturer claim.
