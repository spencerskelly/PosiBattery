# Internal Thermistor Temperature Sensor

## Definition

Temperature sensing with a thermistor integrated inside the monitoring device rather than connected as an external probe.

## Notes

- [[Power Designers PowerTrac SP+]] explicitly lists an internal thermistor as an available option.
- This is more specific than [[Internal Temperature Sensor]] because the sensor technology is identified as a thermistor.
- It remains separate from [[External Thermistor Temperature Sensor]] because installation and thermal coupling differ even though both use thermistor technology.
- Exact thermistor type, package, resistance curve, conditioning network, and physical location inside the product are not stated.
