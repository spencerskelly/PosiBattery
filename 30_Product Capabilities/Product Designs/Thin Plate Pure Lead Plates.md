# Thin Plate Pure Lead Plates

## Definition

Thin plate pure lead plate construction in a sealed battery.

## Notes

- **Charge-rate role (added 2026-10-14, IB-233):** EnerSys lists TPPL charge rates of 0.26 to 0.4 C6 for fast charge against 0.2 to 0.25 C6 standard. An outside article (vendor-affiliated, T3) reports the high rate lasts only briefly and slows near 70 percent charge (C272). Source: [[Document - OneCharge Lithium-Ion vs TPPL Article (Food Logistics, 2020)]]; EnerSys cell sheet cited on [[EnerSys NexSys TPPL Battery]].
- Design characteristic found in products, not a decision by us. Links to products are made only where a source states it.
- No Requirement is linked (intentional gap).
