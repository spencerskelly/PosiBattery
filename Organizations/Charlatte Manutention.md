# Charlatte Manutention

## Definition

French maker (Fayat Group) of battery-powered airport ramp vehicles, with a US branch Charlatte America.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Truck OEM.
- A listing describes Charlatte Manutention (FAYAT Group) as a global maker of battery-powered electric vehicles for airport GSE, industrial and military uses, with 40+ years in electric handling. Source: The Flying Engineer listing (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://theflyingengineer.com/listing/charlatte-manutention/>
- A trade profile says Charlatte America launched in the US in 1993 as a branch of its French parent and is a leading maker of battery-powered airport vehicles, with electric baggage and tow tractors, belt loaders, pushbacks, maintenance carriers and lavatory service vehicles. Source: AirlineGeeks and AviationPros (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://airlinegeeks.com/2019/07/18/additional-assets-a-look-at-the-competitive-ground-service-equipment-market/>
