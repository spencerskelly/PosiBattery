# Plug Power

## Definition

Maker of GenDrive hydrogen fuel cell power units for electric material handling trucks.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Battery Maker.
- Plug Power says GenDrive units are drop-in replacements for batteries in electric material handling equipment, with more than 100 products to match every forklift in a fleet. Source: Plug Power material handling page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.plugpower.com/applications/material-handling/>
