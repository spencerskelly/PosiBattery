# Document - ANSI Webstore UL 3100 Ed 1-2018 Listing (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

Catalog entry for the first edition of UL 3100, an outline of investigation for automated guided vehicles. Read for scope; it is a historical edition and the current edition was not read.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), from a standards-seller catalog page.

- **Title and edition:** UL 3100 Ed. 1-2018, "Outline of Investigation for Automated Guides Vehicles (AGVs)" (the page's spelling), published 2018-03-20, listed as historical and revised by Ed. 2-2020. The Ed. 2 page was not read.
- **Scope:** battery-operated AGVs for indoor commercial or industrial use (industrial truck AGVs, load-bearing and non-load-bearing service AGVs). Excludes people movers, industrial robots (UL 1740) and floor cleaning equipment (ANSI C22.2 No. 336).
- **Charging and batteries:** covers the specific charging device intended for the AGV; batteries are charged through a conductive system; off-board chargers are installed under the NEC (NFPA 70); AGVs are powered by lead-acid or lithium-ion batteries.
- **AMRs:** the page does not mention AMRs or automated mobile platforms. Whether the later edition covers them is unknown (IB-283).
- **Use here:** shows an AGV standard that explicitly covers lithium batteries and the charger, in contrast with UL 583 and UL 1564 listings that name no chemistry.
