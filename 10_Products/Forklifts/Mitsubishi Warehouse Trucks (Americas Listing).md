# Mitsubishi Warehouse Trucks (Americas Listing)

## Definition

Mitsubishi walkie, rider, stacker, pallet-jack, tow-tractor and order-picker lines in the Americas listing, mostly unread.

## Notes

- Counts only: 18 pallet jacks, 2 pallet stackers, 1 tow tractor, 1 order picker. Read cards are manual 5,500 lb pallet jacks A500002276, A500002279, A5Q0001662. The European catalog lists AXiA stackers, PREMiA pallet trucks, VANTiA tow trucks and VELiA order pickers (24 V stackers and pallet trucks), but which are sold in the Americas is unconfirmed (C244). Classes II and III are mixed here. Sources: <https://www.allmachines.com/forklifts/view-all?brand=mitsubishi&category=stackers&page=1&sortBy=default>; <https://www.directindustry.com/prod/mitsubishi-forklift-trucks-17797.html> (T3, second-hand).
