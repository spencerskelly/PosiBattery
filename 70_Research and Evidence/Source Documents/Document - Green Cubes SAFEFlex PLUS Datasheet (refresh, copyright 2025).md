# Document - Green Cubes SAFEFlex PLUS Datasheet (refresh, copyright 2025)

## Definition

Earlier Green Cubes SAFEFlex PLUS datasheet (copyright 2025, uploaded 2025-11), covering only the 36 and 48 V classes, with a model matrix.

## Notes

**How it was read.** Through a fetch tool that returns a model-written summary, not the raw text, on 2026-10-13. The tool itself said the table layout was garbled and that some column assignments were inferred, so per-class values below are unverified until the raw file is read (IB-168).

- 36 V class: 38.4 V nominal, 37.2 to 42.6 V operating, 630 to 840 Ah, 24 to 32 kWh. 48 V class: 51.2 V nominal, 49.6 to 56.8 V operating, 525 to 840 Ah, 26 to 43 kWh. The later datasheet gives 36 V as 525 to 945 Ah, 20 to 36 kWh and 36 to 43.2 V (C215).
- 48 V peak discharge 636 A for 30 s and 1,200 A for 7 s; continuous discharge and charge 320 A (column mapping unclear). Operating temperature -4 to 122 F (-20 to 50 C).
- Communication listed as CAN bus, USB, Ethernet and Wi-Fi (Ethernet is not on the later sources). Certifications listed as UL 2580 **pending**, ISO 13849 and UN 38.3.
- Custom BMS monitoring voltage, current and temperature and controlling internal safety contactors and a disconnect fuse; no BMS specifications.
- Design model matrix: 26 FBP2-036 and 10 FBP2-048 model codes; 48 V weights about 1,061 to 1,811 lb (481 to 821 kg); 36 V about 736 to 1,158 lb (334 to 525 kg). The summary says models, capacities, weights and dimensions do not line up one to one, so no per-model pairing is recorded (IB-170).
- No warranty or revision date.
