# Document - SRP E-Tech Program Factsheet FY27 (2026-05)

## Definition

SRP's FY27 rebates of $2,000 per truck replacing an internal combustion forklift and $180 per high-frequency charger.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- **Rebates (program year 2026-05-01 to 2027-04-30):** Class 1-2 forklift replacing an internal combustion truck $2,000; fleet expansion or first purchase $800; Class 3 $300; high-frequency forklift battery charger $180 (replacing a non-high-frequency electric charger is allowable). Prescriptive rebates need no pre-approval; leased or used equipment may be prorated.
- **Caps:** $450,000 per customer across SRP programs; Electric Technology area cap $100,000.
- **Price plans:** E-31, E-32, E-33, E-34, E-36, E-47, E-48, E-61, E-63, E-65, E-66, E-67.
- **Not stated:** efficiency, network or demand-response requirements, which may be in the program manual at srp.net/etech (C312).
