# California Private Forklift Fleet Site

## Definition

Operating context: a privately owned or operated forklift fleet in California, including dealers, manufacturers and rental agencies, where the Zero-Emission Forklift rule exists but CARB staff will not enforce it.

## Notes

- **Evidence:** CARB says it will not enforce until EPA authorizes the rule or finds authorization unnecessary, and keeps voluntary reporting open ([[Document - CARB ZEF Implementation Update Advisory (2025-05-29)]], [[Document - CARB ZEF Government Fleets Overview Fact Sheet (2026-01)]]). The 2024 adoption text said sales of new LSI forklifts would end from 2026 ([[Document - CARB News Release Forklifts to Become Cleaner and Less Polluting (2024)]]); see C279 to C282 in [[Battery Product Landscape Conflicts and Open Questions]].
- **Why it is modeled:** if enforcement resumes, demand here is far larger than in government fleets (CARB counts more than 89,000 spark-ignited forklifts in California across all fleets, government share unknown, IB-251). Until then electrification here is an economic choice (analyst reasoning).
- **Participants:** [[Fleet Operations Manager]] as stand-in (see [[California State or Local Government Forklift Fleet Site]]).
- Register: [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]].
