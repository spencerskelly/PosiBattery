# Document - New Mexico CTFP 101 Fact Sheet

## Definition

New Mexico's program overview sheet with targets, credit mechanics and equity rule; no forklift-specific content.

## Notes

**How it was read:** first-hand, from the raw file the owner added to the repo (`Downloads/NMED_CTFP-101-Factsheet.pdf`), with text extracted by pdftotext. Not a summary.

Added from the owner's downloads. One-sheet overview of the Clean Transportation Fuel Program. The address given is the program page; the direct file address was not found and is not guessed.

- **Targets:** legally binding carbon intensity reductions of 20 percent by 2030 and 30 percent by 2040 against a 2018 baseline, through annual declining standards; applies to transportation fuels produced, imported or dispensed for use in New Mexico.
- **Credit market:** credits are earned by fuels below the standard (the sheet lists renewable diesel, biodiesel and electricity), deficits by fuels above it; credits are tradeable and bankable. For the first ten years credits are expected to come mostly from renewable diesel and biodiesel; after 2035 the vehicle emission standards and the Renewable Portfolio Standard are expected to supply credits from electrification.
- **Equity:** at least 50 percent of credit revenue from electric utilities must be invested in low-income and underserved communities.
- **Model:** carbon intensity from NM-GREET v1.0, customized from R&D GREET.
- **Not stated:** forklifts, special equipment, metering, credit price. Forklifts appear only in the Roads to Revenue brief (C294).
- **Implication (analyst reasoning):** the heavy early dependence on biofuel credits suggests credit supply will be ample early, which may keep prices low, but no price is given (IB-255).
