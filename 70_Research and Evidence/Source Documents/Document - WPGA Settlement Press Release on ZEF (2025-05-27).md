# Document - WPGA Settlement Press Release on ZEF (2025-05-27)

## Definition

Western Propane Gas Association's announcement of its settlement with CARB over the Zero-Emission Forklift regulation.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic. Press release by the Western Propane Gas Association (the plaintiff) announcing a settlement with CARB. It is the plaintiff's own account; CARB's wording is in [[Document - CARB ZEF Implementation Update Advisory (2025-05-29)]].

- **Case:** WPGA v. CARB, Fresno County Superior Court; WPGA filed in August 2024; the settlement was announced 2025-05-27.
- **Terms as summarized:** CARB will not enforce the ZEF regulation, including its reporting requirements, against privately owned or operated fleets (and those working on public sites) until it has federal authorization, and not for acts or omissions before authorization; reporting is not required until the first deadline after EPA publishes an authorization decision; CARB will not bar manufacturers, dealers or rental agencies from producing, selling or renting LSI forklifts that meet other rules; if CARB seeks authorization it will consider adjusting deadlines so model year 2025 and later LSI forklifts keep their useful life.
- **Other terms:** WPGA dismisses its suit but may refile if CARB does not meet the terms; the terms apply for the duration of the current federal administration; CARB names a Deputy Executive Officer as liaison to forklift stakeholders; any future forklift rulemaking gets at least three public workshops on alternatives, mitigation and economic impacts.
- **Status of the rule:** the headline says the rule is unenforceable; the body as summarized does not say it is repealed. CARB's own notices do not mention the duration clause (C281).
