# Anderson SBE SBX SBO Connector Series

## Definition

Anderson SBO/SBE/SBX connector line (SBO60, SBE80, SBE160, SBX175, SBE320, SBX350): two primary power contacts plus up to 8 last-mate/first-break auxiliaries, touch-safe, voltage-keyed.

## Notes

- SBE is the chemical-resistant PBT/PC version tested to EN 1175:2020; SBX is the impact-resistant PC version tested to UL/CSA and, per Anderson's SBX350 page, does not meet EN 1175-1 for industrial trucks. SBE320 and SBX350 housings with the same colour code cannot be mated. Used on trucks as the black SBE320 for 80 V in a Yale sheet ([[Yale ERC045-070VG Electric Forklifts]]). Per-pole current figures disagree (C250). Sources: <https://www.andersonpower.com/product-lines/sbexo/>; <https://www.andersonpower.com/?p=974>; <https://www.andersonpower.com/zh-hans/product/sbx350-housing-gray-also-sbe-320/> (T1, second-hand).
