# Ampure Group Portfolio Context

## Definition

Governing group-level model for PosiCharge analysis: which Ampure businesses exist, what role each plays in the analysis, and how internal capability is checked before an external gap or partner need is declared.

## Notes

### Group model

```text
Ampure
├── PosiCharge — primary improvement focus
│   industrial MHE and eGSE charging, battery intelligence,
│   connected fleet-energy operations
├── Power Designers Sibex — internal industrial portfolio
│   conventional, opportunity and fast charging, PowerTrac monitoring,
│   PowerCharge.NET energy management, electronics manufacturing
└── Automotive and Aftermarket EVSE — contextual only
    OEM, dealer and aftermarket EVSE; portable and fixed AC charging
```

Sources and evidence for each unit are in [[Ampure]], [[Power Designers]] and [[Ampure Automotive and Aftermarket EVSE]].

### Analysis treatment by unit

| Unit | Treatment | Default question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[PosiCharge]] | Primary subject | Where can PosiCharge win, and what holds it back? |
| [[Power Designers]] | Internal portfolio | Where do offers complement or overlap, and what can be reused or integrated? |
| [[Ampure Automotive and Aftermarket EVSE]] | Contextual capability source | What can be transferred, not what competes? |
| External organizations | Offer-scoped classification | See [[PosiCharge Competitive and Partner Landscape]] |

### Rules

1. **No Ampure business is an external competitor.** Overlap between group offers is recorded in [[Ampure Industrial Portfolio Overlap and Synergy Map]] and kept visible.
2. **EVSE boundary.** Treat an EVSE offer as an internal capability reference, not a PosiCharge competitor or comparison row, unless the same customer job, industrial operating context and buying alternative are documented.
3. **Group check before gap.** A capability missing from PosiCharge is not an Ampure gap until Power Designers Sibex and the EVSE business have been checked.
4. **No rationalization by default.** This note does not decide that any product line merges, retires or migrates. Those are decision questions for the overlap map.
5. **External competitors are benchmarked against the combined industrial portfolio** once the overlap map is complete.

### Response paths for a capability need

| Path | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| Build in PosiCharge | The capability differentiates PosiCharge and needs its own implementation |
| Reuse or integrate from Power Designers Sibex | A group product, technology, process, software or channel can meet or speed up the need |
| Transfer or adapt from Automotive and Aftermarket EVSE | An EVSE engineering, compliance, connectivity or sourcing asset applies to the industrial context |
| Partner externally | No group asset fits, or speed, coverage or interoperability favour a partner |
| Research further | Evidence is not sufficient to choose |

Record the chosen path, and the internal source checked, in [[PosiCharge Capability Gap Assessment]].

### Shared teams

Some teams serve more than one business. When proposing work, state whether it is PosiCharge-only, shared industrial (PosiCharge and Power Designers Sibex), or an EVSE transfer, so capacity and ownership conflicts are visible. Team-level mapping is open in [[PosiCharge Opportunity Backlog]].
