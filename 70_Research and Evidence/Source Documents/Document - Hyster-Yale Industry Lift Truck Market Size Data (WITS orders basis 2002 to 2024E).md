## Notes

**Content recorded:**
- WITS orders basis, global total in units: 2002 562,736; 2005 749,390; 2007 950,954; 2008 871,531; 2009 546,832; 2010 794,452; 2011 974,582; 2012 944,405; 2013 1,009,777; 2014 1,093,961; 2015 1,099,880; 2016 1,182,098; 2017 1,394,995; 2018 1,537,979; 2019 1,506,876; 2020 1,637,614; 2021 2,340,587; 2022 2,181,850; 2023 2,088,397; 2024E 1,902,000.
- Regions are also given (Europe, North America, total Americas, Asia-Pacific/China/Japan); the register note reproduces them. Pre-WITS series for 1992 to 2001 and a different-basis series for 2002 to 2009 come from ITA, JIVA, FEM and WITA; reporting bases vary (factory shipments, bookings, retail bookings) and total Americas before 2009 includes government and Latin America. From 2014 Asia-Pacific includes India local production.
- The page does not label units; they are read as trucks. The 2024 value is an estimate made before the 2024 data were final.
