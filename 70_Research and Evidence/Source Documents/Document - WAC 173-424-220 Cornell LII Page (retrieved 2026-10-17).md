# Document - WAC 173-424-220 Cornell LII Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

Washington Administrative Code section naming the fleet owner as the fuel reporting entity and credit generator for forklift charging electricity.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the western-states clean fuel topic. Cornell Legal Information Institute copy of Washington Administrative Code section 173-424-220, designation of the fuel reporting entity for electricity under the Clean Fuel Standard (CFS).

- **Forklifts:** under (6)(a) "the fleet owner is the fuel reporting entity and the credit generator"; if the owner does not generate credits, the forklift operator may, under a written contract (6)(b).
- **Related equipment:** electric cargo handling equipment owner is the reporting entity and credit generator (8)(a); electric ground support equipment is covered in (10).
- **Metering:** the section has no general metering requirement; (11)(b)(i) addresses metered residential charging only.
- **Currency:** the Notes say adopted by WSR 22-24-004, filed 2022-11-28, effective 2022-12-29. Cornell is a secondary host; check the current text on Washington's own site.
- **Conflict:** whether forklift charging must be metered in Washington is not answered here; Ecology's slides mention direct metering (C293).
