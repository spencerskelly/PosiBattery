# Document - CARB LCFS Initial Statement of Reasons (2023-12-19)

## Definition

CARB's staff report proposing to modify crediting for zero-emission forklifts under 12,000 lb, read only to the start of that section.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. CARB staff report for the 45-day proposal; the fetch tool returned only the first 100,000 characters and cut off at the start of the electric forklifts subsection.

- **Table 2 summary line:** 'Modify crediting potential for zero-emission forklifts with lift capacities less than 12,000 lbs.' This confirms that the lift-capacity split originates in the staff proposal (C296).
- **Purpose:** Section II.G says the forklift changes align with State goals and the 2022 Scoping Plan Update.
