# Mitsubishi FB 3-Wheel Electric Forklifts

## Definition

Mitsubishi FB16PNT and FB20PNT 48 V three-wheel electric forklifts with AC drive, PM-1000 controllers, electric power steering, an Integrated Presence System and optional lithium-ion.

## Notes

- Dealer data repeating Mitsubishi's text says the FB16PNT and FB20PNT have heavy-duty AC drive and hydraulic motors, Mitsubishi PM-1000 controllers, electric power steering, an integrated presence system and optional lithium-ion battery solutions; marketing images show a Red Halo Light (not described in the retrieved text). Source: AllMachines listings of Mitsubishi FB models (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.allmachines.com/forklifts/mitsubishi-fb20pnt>
