# Document - 7Gen CFR Credit Price in 2026 (2026-06-11)

## Definition

A fleet-services vendor's claim that Canadian Clean Fuel Regulations credits passed $400 each in early 2026.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the business case. Blog by 7Gen, which sells EV leasing, charging, fleet software and paid credit management; dated 2026-06-11.

- **Price claim:** Canada Clean Fuel Regulations credits "traded north of $400 each for the first time" in early 2026, after a long modest range. The page gives no dated series, no per-tonne price and no price after June 2026. Its $110 per tonne figure is a different charge, not a credit price.
- **Who earns:** the fleet that charges creates the credit and fuel suppliers buy it; credits cannot be claimed retroactively. The vendor claims "several hundred dollars per vehicle each month" with no method.
- **Not stated:** forklift or off-road eligibility (the page covers on-road trucks and vans), registration process and eligibility criteria.
- **Interest:** the vendor profits from credit management, so the price framing favours participation. The government credit market report was not read (IB-261).
