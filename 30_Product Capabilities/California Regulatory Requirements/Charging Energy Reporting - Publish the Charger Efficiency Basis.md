# Charging Energy Reporting - Publish the Charger Efficiency Basis

## Definition

Each industrial battery charger model shall publish its efficiency in a form a site owner can use for energy reporting, stating whether the figure is peak or full-cycle and the conditions under which it was established.

## Notes

- **Proposed from the regulation text; there is no customer or stakeholder source for it.** It stays Draft until a person decides whether it is a requirement. Hypothesis ids H1 to H4 are in [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]].
- Why: the guidance uses the charger's efficiency from manufacturer specifications, or 85 percent if none is given. Vault values (90 to 97 percent, mostly peak or unstated) would raise reportable kWh by about 5.9 to 14.1 percent versus the default when the meter is upstream.
- Open: CARB has not said whether peak or full-cycle efficiency is meant (C287, IB-243). The efficiency value is a product decision and is not set here.
- Vault data: [[Metric - Peak Efficiency]], [[Metric - Full-Cycle Efficiency]].
