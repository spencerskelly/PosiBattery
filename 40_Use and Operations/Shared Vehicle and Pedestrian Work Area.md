# Shared Vehicle and Pedestrian Work Area

## Definition

Operating context where powered industrial vehicles and pedestrians share travel paths, crossings, aisle ends or adjacent work zones.

## Notes

- Use this context for proximity detection, warnings, automatic slowing and stopping, and hazardous-zone speed management scenarios.
- Derived from the pedestrian-warning, operator-warning and hazardous-zone customer needs.
- This context describes the shared operating environment; it is not itself a safety Function.
