# Logisnext Europe

## Definition

European holding of Mitsubishi Logisnext (Almere, Netherlands), formerly Mitsubishi Logisnext Europe, selling Mitsubishi Forklift Trucks, Cat Lift Trucks, TCM and Rocla in Europe, CIS, the Middle East and Africa.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Truck OEM, Brand Owner.
- The European history page says the company began as Mitsubishi Caterpillar Forklift Europe (MCFE) and UniCarriers Europe (UCE), became a holding entity on 1 April 2018, rationalized its brand portfolio and distribution network in Europe, and took the name Logisnext Europe effective 30 April 2026. Source: Logisnext Europe history page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://logisnext.eu/corporate/history>
- A directory profile gives the head office as Almere, the Netherlands, subsidiaries in 10 countries plus dealers across Europe, CIS, Middle East and Africa, production and development in Finland, Spain and Sweden, and four brand propositions (Mitsubishi Forklift Trucks, Cat Lift Trucks, TCM and Rocla); an earlier profile also names UniCarriers. Source: Dujat and Prospeo directory profiles (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.dujat.nl/?p=3734>
- **Name:** the vault note uses the new name; 'Mitsubishi Logisnext Europe' and MLE appear in older sources (aliases).
