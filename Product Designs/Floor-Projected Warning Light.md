# Floor-Projected Warning Light

## Definition

Lights that project a spot or zone on the floor in the truck's direction of travel.

## Notes

- Design characteristic found in truck-side products, not a decision by us. No Requirement is linked.
- **Sources** (product, evidence level, web page):
