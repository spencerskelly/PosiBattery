# Linde connect charger

## Definition

Linde connect:charger charge management module that limits power, sets time slots and prioritises up to 50 chargers.

## Notes

- Source: [[Document - Logistics Business Linde connect charger Article (2023-04-26)]] (T2, second-hand). Central hardware unit with up to 50 wired chargers; power limits, time slots, priority by charge level; dashboard of readiness and energy use. Wireless, cloud and energy-management integration described as planned (not checked against Linde's own page). Retrofit possible on chargers with an integrated interface, 2018 model year onward.
- Written as a module of Linde's fleet management solution; whether it is part of [[Linde connect]] in software terms is not stated.
