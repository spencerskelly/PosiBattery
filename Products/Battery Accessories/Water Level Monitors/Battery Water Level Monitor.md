# Battery Water Level Monitor

## Definition

Battery monitoring device that senses electrolyte level in a flooded lead-acid battery and provides an indication that supports watering decisions.

## Notes

The indication may be local visual/audible feedback or part of a broader monitoring system. Do not assume the device actively adds water.

## Aliases

- Electrolyte Level Monitor
- Battery Watering Monitor
