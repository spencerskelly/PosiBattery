# Xnergy Wireless Charging Series

## Definition

Xnergy Beyond BE (3-9 kW) and Phoenix PH (1.5 kW) wireless chargers for AMRs and AGVs.

## Notes

- PH certified to FCC, UL and CE (2026-06-09); BE ratings are from a supplier-copied list (T4) and are names-and-range only. Sources: <https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20260609ae79134/> (T2); <https://www.agvnetwork.com/wireless-charging-for-agv-and-autonomous-mobile-robots> (T4).

## Aliases

- Xnergy Beyond BE
- Xnergy Phoenix PH
