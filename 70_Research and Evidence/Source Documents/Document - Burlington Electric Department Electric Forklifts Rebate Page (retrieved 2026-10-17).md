# Document - Burlington Electric Department Electric Forklifts Rebate Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

Burlington Electric's $6,000 rebate for a new electric forklift, with an inconsistent pre-owned line.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- **Rebate:** $6,000 per new battery-powered forklift; the page also lists $3,000 for pre-owned but its terms say the offer is only valid on new electric forklifts (C311).
- **Conditions:** first electric forklift or replacing a retiring fossil-fuel forklift; at least 3,000 lb capacity; purchased in Vermont; one rebate per account; submit within 90 days. A dealer may add up to $2,000 at purchase.
- **Valid through:** 2026-12-31. **Not stated:** lithium versus lead-acid, charger rebate.
