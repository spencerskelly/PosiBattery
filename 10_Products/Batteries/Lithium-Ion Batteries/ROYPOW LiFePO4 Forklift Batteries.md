# ROYPOW LiFePO4 Forklift Batteries

## Definition

ROYPOW LiFePO4 forklift batteries from 24 V to 96 V, 100-1,120 Ah, sold as drop-in replacements for lead-acid, with explosion-proof, cold-storage and liquid-cooled variants.

## Notes

- Working name analyst-given (`unnamed-in-source`): the brochure names a line and example models F24100, F36840, F48560DZ, F80690AK, F80840K, F961120. Source: <https://www.roypow.com/uploads/ROYPOW-LiFePO4-Batteries-for-Forklifts-Brochure-Ver.-September-02-2026.pdf> (T1, second-hand).
- Charge access: pack-side REMA 320 A (male and female) and, on some models, Anderson SB350 connectors, so the charger plugs into the battery ([[Battery-Direct Charge Connection]]); lift power is disconnected during charging. Vendor claims: >3,500 cycles, 5-year warranty, IP65 standard.
- Installation: described as a "drop-in replacement" for lead-acid; compatible trucks are not listed.
