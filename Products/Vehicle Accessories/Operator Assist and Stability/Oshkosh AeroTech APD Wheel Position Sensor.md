# Oshkosh AeroTech APD Wheel Position Sensor

## Definition

APD sensor that warns the operator when the wheels are not centered on selecting reverse, and can disable reverse if an aircraft is in short radar range.

## Notes

- The APD brochure says a wheel position sensor can be interlocked to give a visual and audible warning if the wheels are not centered when reverse is selected, or to disable reverse drive completely if an aircraft is in short range of the radar and the wheels are not centered, because off-center wheels make the loader move sideways unexpectedly. Source: Oshkosh AeroTech APD brochure (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://oshkoshaerotech.com/aircraft-proximity-detection-apd-06-18-24>
- **Functions performed, with citations:**

- **GSE parts (round 33):** stated: acts on [[GSE Drive and Brakes]] (can disable reverse drive) | typical: mounts on [[GSE Drive and Brakes]] (at the steered wheels; not named). See [[GSE Part Connection Register]].
