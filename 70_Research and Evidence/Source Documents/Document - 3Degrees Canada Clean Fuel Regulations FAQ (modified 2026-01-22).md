# Document - 3Degrees Canada Clean Fuel Regulations FAQ (modified 2026-01-22)

## Definition

A credit-services firm's FAQ on Canada's federal Clean Fuel Regulations, listing electric forklifts among eligible opportunities.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the North American programs survey. FAQ by 3Degrees (a firm that sells credit services); no publication date, modified 2026-01-22 per page metadata.

- **Forklifts:** the FAQ lists "battery electric and hydrogen fuel cell electric forklifts" and "electric yard trucks/tractors and other off road cargo handling equipment" as credit opportunities, and says there is "no explicit distinction between on- and off-road vehicle opportunities".
- **Process:** registered creators show the fuel meets eligibility criteria, meet reporting requirements and undergo third-party verification; credits are registered and traded in the Credit and Tracking System (CATS). The program aims to cut emissions by 26 million tons by 2030.
- **Not stated:** the eligible party for forklifts, metering rules, credit price, registration steps and dates.
- **Interest:** the author sells credit-generation services.
