# Automated Battery Swap

## Definition

The depleted battery is removed and a charged one is fitted by a machine at a station, so charging happens off the vehicle and no person handles the battery.

## Notes

- Examples: [[Concentric PowerHIVE]] (forklift), [[HOPPECKE AGV Battery Exchange System]] (AGV). Differs from [[Quick-Change Battery Compartment]], where a person or truck changes the battery. Not a charger-to-vehicle connection: the connection happens at the station.
