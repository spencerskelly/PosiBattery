# Anderson EBC Euro Battery Connector

## Definition

Anderson Euro Battery Connector in DIN sizes 80, 160 and 320 with up to four auxiliary contacts, being phased out for the IBC.

## Notes

- Per the Anderson page, up to 450 A, EN 1175:2020, DIN 43589-1, VDE 0623-589, colour-coded hexagonal keys. Successor: [[Anderson IBC Industrial Battery Connector]]. Source: <https://www.andersonpower.com/ebc/> (T1, second-hand).

- **Update 2026-10-14 (data sheet):** ratings by wire size, 150 V AC/DC per EN1175 and 600 V per CSA/UL, IP23, hot-plug test at 96 V (5 cycles at 200, 400, 800 A), coding pins grey wet, green dry, yellow universal (male housing only); the data sheet cites EN1175-1:1998 while the web page cites EN 1175:2020 (C249). Source: <https://docs.rs-online.com/a386/A700000013352650.pdf> (T1, second-hand).
