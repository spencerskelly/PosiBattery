# TLD RBL Electric Regional Belt Loader

## Definition

TLD electric belt loader for regional aircraft and congested areas, with rotating beacon, horn, work lights, dead man switch and an optional 3D-camera Aircraft Safe Docking system.

## Notes

- A dealer listing says the TLD RBL has a rotating beacon, belt speed adjustment, a hydraulically powered heavy-duty rear drive and steering wheel, solenoid-valve electric boom and belt controls, a dead man switch on the tiller head, a 24 VDC accessory system, an electric horn, work lights and an amber flashing beacon, and an optional Aircraft Safe Docking system whose 3D camera detects obstacles in front of the vehicle up to 7 m; the operator enters the safety area, slows and presses the ASD button. Source: Aero Specialties dealer listing (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.aerospecialties.com/product/tld-rbl/>
