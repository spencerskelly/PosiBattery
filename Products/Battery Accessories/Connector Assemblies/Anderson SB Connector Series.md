# Anderson SB Connector Series

## Definition

Anderson SB series power connectors (SB 50, 175, 350 and larger) used on forklift and pallet jack batteries, rated 600 V.

## Notes

- An industrial knowledge-base article says SB connectors have no male or female half, are color-coded by voltage, are rated to 600 V AC/DC and are the standard battery connection on electric forklifts and pallet jacks; SB 175 and SB 350 housings are named. Source: Industrial Monitor Direct knowledge base (T4), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://industrialmonitordirect.com/zh-hans/blogs/knowledgebase/anderson-sb-175-to-sb-350-battery-connector-adapter-guide>
- Crown's parts shop lists SB contacts and accessories in SB 50, 175, 350, 320 SBE and 350 SBX sizes. Source: Crown parts shop (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://shop.crown.com/crown/en/Batteries-and-Chargers/Battery-and-Charger-Parts-and-Accessories/Battery-Connectors/c/battery_connectors>
