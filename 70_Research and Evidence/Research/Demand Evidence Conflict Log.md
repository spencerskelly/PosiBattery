### Corrections made during review

- An earlier reply said Hyster was involved in the Supply Chain 24/7 article. The article quotes Green Cubes Technology, Concentric Power Services, UgoWork and Stryten Energy; Hyster is not among them.
- The "connected batteries are not actionable" quote is from UgoWork's CEO, a battery-management vendor, in an article dated 2025-08-01, not from an unnamed executive in 2026.
- An earlier summary set RuiDeJin's "not a drop-in replacement" against blitznxt. That statement concerns truck starting batteries and alternators, so it is not a forklift conflict and is removed from C2.
- An earlier reply called AGVs and AMRs outside the vault's scope. The vault has no AGV or AMR product category but mentions them in a few notes, so they are an uncovered segment.
