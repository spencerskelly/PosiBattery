# Powered Industrial Truck

## Definition

Reusable family for forklifts and other powered industrial trucks, organized by the seven Industrial Truck Association classes.

## Notes

- OSHA's forklift eTool lists seven classes of commonly used powered industrial trucks and notes the list does not include all trucks covered by the standard. Source: OSHA powered industrial trucks eTool (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://osha.prod.pace.dol.gov/etools/powered-industrial-trucks/types-fundamentals/types>
- **Scope (owner decision 2026-10-03, Q13):** stay with electric trucks for now, but record any features that internal combustion and fuel-cell trucks have that electric trucks may lack (see [[ICE and Fuel Cell Feature Gap Review]]). Earlier wording:  the survey follows batteries, so electric trucks (Classes I, II, III and electric Class VI) are the focus; internal combustion and fuel-cell trucks are listed for context only until decided.
- **Subtype rule (owner decision 2026-10-03, Q14):** every powered industrial truck, including internal combustion and fuel-cell trucks, uses subtype electrical, because all of them have electrical systems even if the power source is fuel.
