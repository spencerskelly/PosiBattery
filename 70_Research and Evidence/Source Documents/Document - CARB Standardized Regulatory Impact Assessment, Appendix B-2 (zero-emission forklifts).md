## Notes

**Content recorded:**
- The proposal would affect about 95,000 forklifts owned by roughly 11,000 fleets; low-use forklifts are those operating under 200 hours a year. Table titles show prices by weight class, infrastructure, electricity, energy and maintenance costs in 2021 dollars, but the table values were not in the text read.

**Update 2026-10-18: file now in the repo and read first-hand** (Downloads/ZE-Forklift-SRIA-to-DOF.pdf, text extracted and searched; the table values the earlier read lacked are now recorded):

- **Fleet size:** about 95,000 LSI (large spark-ignition) forklifts from about 11,000 fleets subject by 2026; roughly 70,000 lead-acid forklifts are already in operation in California; a staff search found almost 250 zero-emission models from 11 manufacturers between 2,500 and 12,000 lb lift capacity.
- **Hours and energy:** affected forklifts average 1,848 operating hours a year (LSI Inventory Model, from a 2020 survey of DOORS-reported fleets and a 2017 CSU Fullerton survey); electricity consumption 7.5 kWh per hour of operation (Table 13). Derived: 7.5 x 1,848 = 13,860 kWh a year per truck. The same 7.5 kWh per hour appears in the Argonne study; whether the model took it from there is not stated. A small-business case assumes 1,044 hours a year and a typical business 1,914.
- **Technology mix assumption:** about 7 to 10 percent of new industrial batteries sold use lithium (c. 2022 estimate); staff assume 10 percent of new battery-electric forklifts lithium in 2022, 35 percent in 2026, 48 percent in 2028, 61 percent in 2030, 100 percent in 2037 (linear interpolation), plus 10 percent of added zero-emission forklifts as fuel cell. This is an assumption in the cost model, not a market measurement (C354).
- **Prices (2021 dollars, Table 11):** Class IV/V propane 3,000 lb and less $32,625 against lead-acid electric $45,911 and lithium electric $59,854; 5,001 to 6,000 lb $47,292, $64,844, $78,326; 10,001 to 12,500 lb $77,520, $97,347, $112,214. Lead-acid electric price includes one extra battery pack (two in total); charger unit costs are included in the forklift price.
- **Electricity (Table 12):** statewide weighted average $0.18 per kWh (small fleet $0.22, large $0.16); LADWP $0.12, PG&E $0.17, SMUD $0.14, SDG&E $0.26, SCE $0.19. Maintenance for battery-electric $1.77 per hour against $2.63 for propane (Table 14). Infrastructure $3,375 per electric forklift (Table 15), modeled as a Level 2 car charger install; the staff footnote cites a 16 kW (80 V x 200 A) charger for a 10,700 lb lithium truck.
- **LCFS revenue (Table 17):** $0.67 per operating hour for a battery-electric forklift in 2026 (about $1,200 a year at 1,848 hours) against $0.09 for propane; credit price basis is the September 2022 monthly transfer report; value not captured here. Compare the vault's current-factor figures (C352).
