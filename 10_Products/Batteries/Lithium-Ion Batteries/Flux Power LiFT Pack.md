# Flux Power LiFT Pack

## Definition

Flux Power lithium packs for material handling, starting with a 24 V 250 Ah unit.

## Notes

- Flux introduced the LiFT Pack line with a 250 Ahe, 24 V first product, and its accessories include stand-alone battery management, stackable chargers, programming software and display systems. Source: Flux Power release (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://ir.fluxpower.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/78/flux-power-introduces-new-line-of-advanced-lithium-battery>
- Flux Power's brochures and FAQ say the LiFT Pack uses lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) cells with integrated heaters for cold temperatures, a charge temperature range of 0 to 45 C (32 to 113 F) and a discharge range of -20 to 55 C, a maximum continuous external charge current of 350 A (M36 and X80), fast charge in as little as one hour (M36), opportunity charging at any state of charge with no negative effect, a patented BMS that monitors charging so the pack cannot be overcharged, CAN integration with forklift trucks, and an onboard charger because the pack needs a higher charge voltage than lead-acid (a lead-acid charger can partly charge it in some cases); the FAQ says it charges at constant current until the pack voltage reaches a set value (the rest is cut off). Source: Flux Power M36 and X80 brochures and LiFT Pack FAQ (v1.5) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.fluxpower.com/hubfs/M36%20Battery%20Pack%20-Brochure-1.pdf>
- **Design characteristics, with citations (round 40, gap review 2026-10-03):**
  - [[Integrated Battery Heater]] (V): <https://www.fluxpower.com/hubfs/M36%20Battery%20Pack%20-Brochure-1.pdf>

- **Update 2026-10-13:** a 2014 trade item names a 24 V LiFT Pack for Class III Toyota and Raymond walkie trucks, sold through those OEMs' US dealer networks, for new trucks and as a replacement for existing trucks. This makes the pack both a dealer-channel option and a retrofit; the vault's other LiFT Pack sources do not name those trucks. Source: <https://www.mhlnews.com/powered-vehicles/lithium-battery-packs-forklifts-new-product> (T2, second-hand).
