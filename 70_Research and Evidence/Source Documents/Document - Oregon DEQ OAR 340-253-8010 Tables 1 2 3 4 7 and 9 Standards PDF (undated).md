# Document - Oregon DEQ OAR 340-253-8010 Tables 1 2 3 4 7 and 9 Standards PDF (undated)

## Definition

Oregon DEQ's fuel standard tables for gasoline and diesel, the electric forklift EER of 3.8 and the average electricity carbon intensity of 150.00.

## Notes

**How it was read:** first-hand, from the raw file the owner added to the repo (`Downloads/ODEQ_340-253-8010.pdf`), with text extracted by pdftotext. Not a summary.

Added from the owner's downloads. Oregon DEQ's attachment of tables to OAR 340-253-8010. The file carries no issue date; it quotes standards for 2015 to 2035 and beyond, and its 2025 and 2026 values match the web copy already on file (Document - Oregon DEQ Rule Attachments OAR 340-253-8010 Tables 4 7 and 9 (effective 2025-01-09)), which did not include the diesel standard table.

- **Table 2 diesel and diesel substitutes (gCO2e/MJ):** baseline 98.74 for 2019 to 2025 and 102.73 for 2026 and beyond. Standard: 2024 90.84, 2025 88.87, 2026 90.40, 2027 88.34, 2028 86.29, 2030 82.18, 2035 and beyond 64.72 (37 percent reduction). The 2026 value is higher than the 2025 value because the baseline was raised; the percent reduction rises from 10 to 12 percent.
- **Table 1 gasoline (gCO2e/MJ):** baseline 98.06 for 2019 to 2025 and 96.50 for 2026 and beyond. Standard: 2025 88.25, 2026 84.92, 2030 77.20, 2035 and beyond 60.80.
- **Table 7 EER:** Electricity/Electric Forklift 3.8, listed in the heavy-duty and off-road (diesel replacement) column; propane forklift 0.9; hydrogen fuel cell forklift 2.1; electricity/cargo handling equipment 2.7; electricity/ground support equipment 3.2; eTRU 3.4; battery electric vehicle on the light-duty side 3.4. The extracted column layout is jumbled, so the row-to-column pairing was read from the table order and should be checked against the PDF image.
- **Table 9 temporary pathway code ORELEC600T:** Oregon average electricity mix 150.00 gCO2e/MJ. Table 4 lists solar and wind at 0 gCO2e/MJ (ORELEC100, ORELEC101).
- **Analyst calculation (not in the document):** if Oregon applies the diesel-side standard to electric forklifts (Table 7 places them in the diesel-replacement column; the credit formula text was not read), credits per MWh = (standard x EER - CI) x 3,600 MJ / 1,000,000. At EER 3.8: 2026 standard 90.40 and CI 150.00 gives 0.697 tonne per MWh, about $56 at DEQ's 2025 price of $80.51 per tonne; CI 0 (renewable pathway) gives 1.237 tonnes, about $100. With the 2025 standard of 88.87 and CI 150.00 the value is 0.676 tonne. Compare California 2.4 EER: 0.458 tonne per MWh at the 2026 benchmark and grid CI 65.07.
- **Conflicts:** supports C307 and resolves the diesel-standard part of C308 and IB-264; the credit formula, the forklift pathway code and the 2026 price remain open.
