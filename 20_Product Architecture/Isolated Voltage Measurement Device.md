# Isolated Voltage Measurement Device

## Definition

Component or module that measures/transfers an analog voltage across a galvanic isolation boundary.

## Notes

Examples include an isolation amplifier, isolated ADC, isolated sigma-delta modulator, or complete isolated measurement module. No manufacturer or part number is selected at this reusable level.
