# Linde 1293 Series (E20BHP and E25BHP)

## Definition

Linde 4,000 to 5,000 lb electric counterbalance forklifts powered by Linde lithium-ion batteries for indoor and outdoor use.

## Notes

- KION North America says the E20BHP and E25BHP are electric counterbalance forklifts of 4,000 to 5,000 lb powered by Linde lithium-ion batteries, for indoor and outdoor applications. Source: KION North America release via MHEDA (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.mheda.org/news/kion-north-america-launches-new-electric-forklift-the-linde-series-1293/>

- **Battery integration (2026-10-14):** the dealer-hosted catalog lists vertical battery change as standard, 80 V 360 Ah lead-acid; the lithium-ion option is stated by a trade article (E20-E25 B 1293 "available with a lithium-ion power source", <https://www.constructionnews.co.nz/2024/10/29/lithium-ion-powered/>, T3) with no installation or charging detail. Source: <https://plic.co.th/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Linde-series-1293-E20-E25B-01-Electric-catalog.pdf> (T2)
