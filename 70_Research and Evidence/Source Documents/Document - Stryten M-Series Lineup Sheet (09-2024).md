# Document - Stryten M-Series Lineup Sheet (09-2024)

## Definition

Stryten M-Series lineup sheet dated 09/2024 (hosted by MHI).

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw PDF.

- Gives one-line descriptions only; no voltages, capacities or warranties except T330 (up to 18% more capacity than conventional flooded in the same container) and AGM220 (24 V walkie, up to 4,500 lb, four 6 V AGM210, smart HF charger with auto stop/start).
- Charging classes: F100 conventional; F110 opportunity; T300 conventional and opportunity-capable; T310 opportunity, up to 90-day watering; T330 opportunity and fast; AGM200 never needs watering.
- Chargers: X-3 and X-7 modular multi-voltage opportunity chargers (silicon carbide MOSFET, can charge almost any motive battery including Li600, CEC versions); EHI programmable profiles, temperature over the power line, wireless download to PC; EHY heavy-duty conventional; EHF light to heavy duty, multi-battery trucks.
- Li610 is not on this sheet.
