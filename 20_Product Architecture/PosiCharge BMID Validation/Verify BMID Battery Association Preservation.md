# Verify BMID Battery Association Preservation

## Definition

Verification intent to demonstrate that a supported PosiCharge BMID configuration preserves the association between stored battery identity or history and the battery to which the BMID is installed during normal intended use.

## Notes

- The Requirement does not yet prescribe mounting, serialization, pairing, replacement, commissioning, or service behavior.
- A concrete Procedure would require those lifecycle details to be defined first.
- No approved Procedure, Setup, Plan, or executed Result is currently available in the governed model.
- This verification intentionally preserves the open implementation decision rather than assuming a specific association mechanism.
