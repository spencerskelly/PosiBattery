# Fronius PV Connect

## Definition

Fronius PV Connect function of the Charge & Connect portal that plans battery charging around on-site solar output.

## Notes

- Source: [[Document - Warehouse News Fronius PV Connect Article (2023-06-13)]] (T2, second-hand). PV-maximum and PV-optimum modes with a load balancing charging algorithm and weather forecasts. Requires Fronius chargers, Charge & Connect, a PV system and a Fronius inverter.
- Part of [[Fronius Charge & Connect]] per the article.
