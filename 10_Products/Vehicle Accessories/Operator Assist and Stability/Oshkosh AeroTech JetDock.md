# Oshkosh AeroTech JetDock

## Definition

JetDock operator-assist automated docking option for Commander loaders, offered as a retrofit-ready autonomous docking system on the Ranger.

## Notes

- Oshkosh AeroTech lists JetDock operator assist as an automated docking option on the Commander 30i and 15i loaders, and its Ranger page says the loader is retrofit-ready for features such as telematics and JetDock autonomous docking systems.
