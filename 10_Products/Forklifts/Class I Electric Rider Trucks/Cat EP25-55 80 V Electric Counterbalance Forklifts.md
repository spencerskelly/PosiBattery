# Cat EP25-55 80 V Electric Counterbalance Forklifts

## Definition

Cat 80 V electric counterbalance forklifts (2.5 to 3.5 t and 4.0 to 5.5 t) with lithium-ion or lead-acid batteries and a long option list.

## Notes

- Cat says the EP40-55(C)N(H) 4.0 to 5.5 t trucks offer lithium-ion and lead-acid batteries (an easy charging hatch and socket are standard with lithium-ion), mirrors, mounts for document holders, computers and scanners, road lights, amber strobes, red or blue warning spots and red lines marking exclusion boundaries, and a raised operator compartment. Source: Cat Lift Trucks blog (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.catlifttruck.com/blog/importance-options-forklift-specification>
- **Design characteristics, with citations:**
  - [[Truck Charging Port]] (V): <https://www.catlifttruck.com/blog/importance-options-forklift-specification>

- **Options (2026-10-14, brochures CEBC2177 and CEBC2120):** Li-ion battery option and fast battery compartment access (change method and charge port not stated, see the Forklift Battery Integration and Charging Interface Register), [[Cat Panel Cabin Options]], [[Cat Intelligent Speed and Movement Reduction]], 360 degree steering, OmniTurn, Palm Steering, SilentRun+ pumps, automatic hydraulic tuning. Sources: <https://www.catlifttruck.com/sites/default/files/documents/cebc2177.pdf>; <https://www.catlifttruck.com/sites/default/files/downloads/cebc2120.pdf> (T1)
