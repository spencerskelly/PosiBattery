# Document - ZVEI Leaflet 14 Ventilation of Battery Charging Rooms for Lead Traction Batteries (2020-05)

## Definition

Association leaflet (ZVEI Batteries Division, May 2020) that applies DIN EN 62485-3 to ventilation of charging rooms for lead traction batteries, with a worked example.

## Provenance

- **Repo file:** Downloads/14e-Leaflet-Ventilation_of_battery_charging_rooms_for_lead_traction_batteries_01.pdf (3 pages), added by the owner. Text was extracted from the file and read directly on 2026-10-18 (first-hand).
- **Source address:** https://www.zvei.org/ (the wishlist row gave the publisher address; the exact download address was not recorded where this shows only a site root).
- **Evidence tier:** T2, industry association leaflet, not a manufacturer sheet or a standard.

## Notes

- Hazard stated: during charging an explosive hydrogen and oxygen mixture escapes through the plug openings; near the end of charge almost the whole charging current produces gas; 0.450 L hydrogen and 0.225 L oxygen per hour per ampere of gassing current at 25 C. Ventilation must keep hydrogen below the lower explosion limit of 4 percent by volume.
- Formula (per DIN EN 62485-3 as quoted): air flow Q = 0.055 m3/Ah x n x Igas, with n the number of cells (the leaflet gives 40 for an 80 V battery and 12 for 24 V) and Igas the hydrogen-generating current from the charger maker. If Igas is unknown, at least 40 percent of the maximum charging current on the charger name plate is to be used. For several batteries in one room the flows add, on the assumption that all gas at the maximum rate.
- Worked example in the leaflet: ten PzS 80 V 420 Ah batteries on W0Wa chargers with Igas 21 A need 46.2 m3/h each (462.0 m3/h); six PzV 48 V 300 Ah on IUIa chargers with Igas 3.6 A need 4.8 m3/h each (28.8 m3/h); total 490.8 m3/h.
- Natural ventilation: free room volume at least 2.5 times the hourly flow and air speed of at least 0.1 m/s at inlets and outlets; opening area A = 28 cm2 h/m3 x Q (example 13,742 cm2); inlets near the floor, outlets high; openings on the same wall at least 2 m apart.
- Forced ventilation if natural ventilation cannot be guaranteed: batteries shall only be charged if the forced ventilation is effective, and function and effectiveness must be demonstrated at commissioning and at regular intervals. Exhaust must go to open air; air supply must be free of chlorine and ammonia; ducts in the exhaust flow must be acid resistant.
- Safety distance: 0.5 m air gap from a cell opening, within which no open flames, sparks, arcs or glowing objects (maximum surface temperature 300 C).
- Scope: lead batteries only. The leaflet does not address lithium. Whether a statutory text in a given country makes this binding is not stated in the leaflet.
