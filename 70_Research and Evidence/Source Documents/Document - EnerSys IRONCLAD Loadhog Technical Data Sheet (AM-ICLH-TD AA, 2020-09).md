# Document - EnerSys IRONCLAD Loadhog Technical Data Sheet (AM-ICLH-TD AA, 2020-09)

## Definition

EnerSys IRONCLAD Loadhog technical data sheet AM-ICLH-TD AA (September 2020): square tubular cell series, capacities, dimensions, weights, warranty.

## Notes

**How it was read.** Through a fetch tool that returns a model-written summary, not the raw text, on 2026-10-13; values were not checked against the raw file (IB-179 to IB-182).

- Series E55L, E75L, E100 and E140, square tubular, named for amp-hours per positive plate (55, 75, 100, 140). Capacity at the 6-hour rate (77 F) or 5-hour rate (30 C) to 1.70 V per cell: E55L 110 to 880 Ah (5 to 33 plates), E75L 150 to 1,200 Ah, E100 200 to 1,600 Ah, E140 280 to 1,960 Ah (5 to 29 plates); 0.21 to 3.78 kWh per cell.
- Claims: first to fit 100 Ah per positive plate in a standard 85 Ah compartment, up to 18 percent more capacity than similarly sized conventional batteries, up to 84 percent more positive plate area.
- Cell weights with tray 23 lb (10.4 kg) for a 5-plate E55L to 255 lb (115.7 kg) for a 29-plate E140; standard coverless heights 14.87 in (E55L) to 30.56 in (E140).
- "5+1 YEAR LIMITED WARRANTY", "up to 5-year +1 warranty depending on charging applications". Not stated: grid alloy, separators, charging, watering, monitoring, nominal voltage.
