# Fire and Insurer Review - Charger Shall Take Charge Permission and Limits From the Lithium Battery BMS

## Definition

The charger shall take charge permission and charge limits from the lithium battery's BMS and not charge outside them.

## Notes

- **Analyst-proposed.** Derived from one insurer's ask (REQ-00074); the OEM-charger clause in that ask is not addressed here and would limit third-party chargers regardless.
- Candidate function: [[Charge Under BMS Control]].
- See [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]].
