# HVPS Communications Kit

## Definition

Communication kit included with the DC variant of the PosiCharge High Voltage Power Station; it balances energy transfer with the other stations.

## Notes

- **Source:** owner statement 2026-10-13 (U), which also gave the name "HVPS Communications Kit" ("HVPS" read as High Voltage Power Station). No web page names or describes the kit; the product card and both listing pages do not mention it (IB-162).
- The AC variant does not need the kit because it does not share power (owner statement, U): [[PosiCharge High Voltage Power Station (AC)]] is not linked.
- **Unknown, not assumed:** maker and part number (madeBy left unset), contents and interface (the card lists CAN, Wi-Fi and 4G LTE for the station but does not tie any to the kit), whether it is orderable separately or built into the DC unit (offeredWith is used as the neutral link), and whether the shared card's ratings apply with the kit.
- **Functions performed, with citations:**
