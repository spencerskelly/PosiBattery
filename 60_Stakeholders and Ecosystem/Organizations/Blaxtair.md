# Blaxtair

## Definition

Maker of the Blaxtair embedded pedestrian detection system for industrial vehicles.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Accessory Maker.
- Blaxtair describes an embedded pedestrian detection system for industrial vehicles with a stereoscopic camera, AI processing unit and a 7 inch display or LED and buzzer. Source: SafetyMag product item (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://thesafetymag.com/ca/products/miscellaneous/blaxtair-inc-embedded-pedestrian-detection-system/416736>
- A trade page calls Blaxtair a brand from Arcure, and a 2016 report says Jungheinrich built its reversing camera with person detection on the Blaxtair camera in partnership with Arcure. Source: SOE resource page and Logistics Business (T4/T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.soe.org.uk/resources/safe-operations.html>
- **Parent:** see [[Arcure]] (brand or legal entity not stated).
