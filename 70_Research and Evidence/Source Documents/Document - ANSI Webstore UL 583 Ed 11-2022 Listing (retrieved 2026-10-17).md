# Document - ANSI Webstore UL 583 Ed 11-2022 Listing (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

Catalog entry for UL 583, the safety standard for electric battery-powered industrial trucks. Read for scope and edition; the standard text was not read.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), from a standards-seller catalog page. A related Standards Council of Canada notice of intent was also read: <https://scc-ccn.ca/standards/notices-of-intent/underwriters-laboratories-inc-ul/standard-safety-electric-battery>.

- **Title and edition:** UL 583, Electric-Battery-Powered Industrial Trucks, 11th edition (2022), revising the 10th edition (2012).
- **Published date on the page:** 2025-08-07. This sits oddly beside an edition year of 2022 (C317); the page does not explain it.
- **Scope:** battery-powered industrial trucks (tractors, platform-lift trucks, fork-lift trucks) for fire, electric shock and explosion risk, and the power sources intended for them; truck types E, CGH, E or CGH, EE, ES, EX. It does not address other risks from using the trucks.
- **Lithium:** the page names no battery chemistry, so whether the 11th edition covers lithium-ion is unknown. A 2024 panelist says UL 583 predates lithium ([[Document - MHI Blog Navigating Third-Party Safety Standards for Lithium Batteries in Lift Trucks (2024-10-08)]]).
- **Canadian notice:** the SCC notice (posted 2021-02-01, scope updated 2022-02-01, status "proceeding to development") describes a new edition as a joint Canada and US standard, ANSI/CAN/UL 583, and does not mention lithium.
