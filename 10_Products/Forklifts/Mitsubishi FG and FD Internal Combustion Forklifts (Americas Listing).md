# Mitsubishi FG and FD Internal Combustion Forklifts (Americas Listing)

## Definition

Mitsubishi FG (LPG/gasoline) and FD (diesel) internal combustion forklifts in the Americas listing, 79 models claimed.

## Notes

- Models named: FD70HP (diesel, 15,500 lb, Kubota V3800, 118 in), FG25N, FG45N, FG50N, FG50CN. Cushion (C) and pneumatic variants are mixed in the listing, so the Class IV/V placement is provisional. IC trucks are not battery-powered; kept for completeness of the MLA product list. Source: <https://www.allmachines.com/forklifts/view-all?brand=mitsubishi&category=stackers&page=1&sortBy=default> (T3, second-hand).
