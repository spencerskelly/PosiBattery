# Linde Rotating Operator Workstation

## Definition

Linde rotating driver's workstation that turns toward the work; on 10 to 32 t trucks the whole cabin turns 180 degrees left and 65 degrees right.

## Notes

- The brochure says the rotating workstation is available factory-fitted or as a retrofit on E25 to E50 electric (series 387-388) and H20 to H80 diesel and LPG (series 391-396) trucks, and cites a BGHW test per DIN EN 1005-4 for lower musculoskeletal strain. Source: Linde Rhein-Ruhr safety brochure (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.lmh-rr.de/media/Linde-Rhein-Ruhr/02_PDF/Safety/broschuere_safety.pdf>
