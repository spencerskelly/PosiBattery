## Notes

**Content recorded:**
- Preliminary PEM fuel cell against battery comparison: refuelling or battery change 4 to 8 minutes a day against 45 to 60 minutes plus 8 hours of recharging and cooling; labor USD 1,100 a year against USD 8,750; net present value of capital USD 12,600 (USD 18,000 without incentives) against USD 14,000; operating cost including fuel USD 52,000 against USD 128,000; ratios 1.5 times lower maintenance, 8 times lower refuelling labor, 2 times lower system cost. The deck does not give the battery price, life or hours behind these values.
