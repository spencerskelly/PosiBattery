# Obtain Fire Authority and Insurer Approval for a Charging Area

## Definition

A site presents its charging area, chargers and documentation to the fire authority and its insurer, resolves their questions and records the outcome, so charging can start or continue.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: analyst hypothesis, drafted 2026-10-17.** Steps (analyst wording): identify the adopted code and edition; collect charger listing and manual; show separation, ventilation, detection and shut-off arrangements; answer reviewer comments; keep the approval record.
- Completion: written acceptance from both parties. Not evidenced: that this is how sites actually proceed, who leads it, or its duration (IB-279).
