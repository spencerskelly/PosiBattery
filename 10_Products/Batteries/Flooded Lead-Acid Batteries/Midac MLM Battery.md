# Midac MLM Battery

## Definition

Midac low-maintenance flooded traction battery, 100 to 1,550 Ah, topping up up to 12 weeks, up to 1,500 cycles.

## Notes

- Source: Midac integrated solutions catalogue (T1, second-hand), <https://midacbatteries.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/1_CAT-INTEGRATED-SOLUTIONS_MOTIVE_EN_IT_DE_FR-01_2022-5011820-0967_21.pdf>. Single-point watering and electrolyte level sensor standard; air mixing optional (not on 2MLM-E). Requires MIDATRON HE charger (8 h charge) and the BMU. Warranty not stated.
