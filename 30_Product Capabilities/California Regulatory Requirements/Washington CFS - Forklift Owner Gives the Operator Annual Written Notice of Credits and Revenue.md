# Washington CFS - Forklift Owner Gives the Operator Annual Written Notice of Credits and Revenue

## Definition

The forklift fleet owner that generates credits shall each year notify the forklift operator in writing that it is generating credit for the operator's electricity use, with the estimated annual credits and credit revenue based on the previous year's credit price; where the operator holds the credit rights, the operator gives the owner the same notice.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It binds the owner or operator named in the definition, not a charger.
- **Read first-hand:** [[Document - WAC 173-424-220 Washington Legislature Certified Copy (2025-11-12)]], subsection 6(a) and 6(c). For the 2023 calendar year the price to use was the average of CARB's and Oregon DEQ's annual averages.
- Slides in [[Document - Washington Ecology Clean Fuel Standard Rule Presentation (2022-08-23)]] list the same duty ('forklift and eCHE owner must notify operator').
- Product use: UC-00072 ([[Notify Forklift Operator of Annual Credits and Estimated Revenue]]) describes the activity; no derived product requirement is proposed yet because the data inputs are the same as in REQ-00058.
