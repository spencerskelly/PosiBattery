# Komatsu FB Series Electric Forklifts

## Definition

Komatsu FB series battery-electric forklifts (FB25/30-12, AM50 3-wheel and BBX50 models) with operator presence sensing and KOMTRAX.

## Notes

- Komatsu says the FB25/30-12 (2,500 or 3,000 kg) has a programmable controller for travel, lift and tilt speeds, operator presence sensing, a combination lever and KOMTRAX remote equipment and fleet monitoring, with DT connectors for waterproofing. Source: Komatsu Australia FB page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.komatsu.com/en-au/products/equipment/forklifts/standard-electric-powered/komatsu-fb>
- A dealer-data site lists FB15U-12 to FB20AU-12 (AM50 series, 3-wheel, for wet and corrosive environments, options rearview mirrors, strobe lights and a digital load scale) and FB20SHU-6 to FB32SHU-6 (BBX50 series, 36 V 13.5 hp or 48 V 18 hp drive), all with an operator presence system and auto-speed control. Source: AllMachines listings (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.allmachines.com/forklifts/komatsu-fb20au-12>
