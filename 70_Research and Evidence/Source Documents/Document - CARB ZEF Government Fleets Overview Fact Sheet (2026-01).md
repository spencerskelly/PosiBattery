# Document - CARB ZEF Government Fleets Overview Fact Sheet (2026-01)

## Definition

CARB's January 2026 overview of the Zero-Emission Forklift regulation for state and local government fleets, with phase-out dates by class and fleet size.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic. CARB fact sheet dated January 2026, "Zero-Emission Forklift Regulation Overview for State and Local Government Fleets". Two copies of the same title sit under different upload folders (2026-02 and 2026-03); only the 2026-02 copy could be read, and whether the two differ was not checked.

- **Who it applies to:** any state or local government entity that owns, operates, leases or rents one or more LSI forklifts in California. Covered: cushion-tired (Class IV) forklifts of all lift capacities and pneumatic-tired (Class V) forklifts rated 12,000 pounds or less. Excluded: rough-terrain, vehicle-mounted and telescoping boom forklifts, pallet jacks, forklifts used solely for personal purposes, combat or tactical support, and forklifts at ports and railyards.
- **Effective date:** from 2026-01-01 a fleet operator may not acquire and operate an LSI forklift unless an exception applies (for example a used 2025-or-earlier model year not yet phased out, or an approved exemption or extension). Operators must contact their electric utility by 2026-03-31 for each separately metered location with affected forklifts.
- **Phase-out dates (large fleets = 26 or more forklifts; small fleets = 25 or fewer, alternative schedule):**
  - Class IV, large: 2028-01-01 model year 2018 and older; 2031-01-01 model years 2019 to 2021; 2033-01-01 model years 2022 to 2023; 2035-01-01 model years 2024 to 2025.
  - Class IV, small: 2029-01-01 model year 2016 and older; 2032-01-01 model years 2017 to 2019; 2034-01-01 model years 2020 to 2021; 2036-01-01 model years 2022 to 2023; 2038-01-01 model years 2024 to 2025.
  - Class V (not for forklifts over 12,000 pounds reported under the heavy Class V LSI reporting): 2030-01-01 model year 2017 and older; 2033-01-01 model years 2018 to 2020; 2035-01-01 model years 2021 to 2022; 2038-01-01 model years 2023 to 2028.
  - The Class IV rows carry a clause about 2025 model year and older forklifts under 12,000 pounds whose meaning is unclear in the summary; read the raw sheet before relying on it. At the first compliance date required turnover is capped at 50 percent of forklifts for large fleets and 25 percent for small fleets.
- **Relation to the private-fleet pause:** until EPA grants authorization or determines it is unnecessary, CARB staff will not enforce the regulation against privately owned or operated fleets, including dealers, manufacturers and rental agencies. The sheet says this pause does not apply to state and local government fleets, which must comply now.
- **EPA status:** the sheet says CARB has not yet sought EPA authorization under section 209(e)(2)(A) and does not explain how the rule is enforceable against state and local fleets without it.
