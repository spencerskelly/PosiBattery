# Document - Food Logistics Ndustrial LCFS Updates and Fleet Electrification (2025-01-24)

## Definition

A January 2025 vendor-sponsored article that lists metered forklift charging from 2026 among the LCFS changes.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic. Article by Manuel Aguirre published 2025-01-24 in Food Logistics (IRONMARKETS), tagged as Ndustrial content, so vendor-sponsored. It predates adoption of the amendments, so it reflects the proposed text.

- Companies adding electric forklifts or other electric yard equipment must "install accurate metering at the charging station" to generate LCFS credits.
- Forklift charging must be metered rather than estimated from 2026-01-01, in the article's list of notable changes for end users.
- It gives no credit values, prices, kWh figures or per-forklift estimates, and does not say who benefits from forklift credits.
