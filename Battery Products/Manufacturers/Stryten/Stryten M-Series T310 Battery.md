# Stryten M-Series T310 Battery

## Definition

Stryten tubular flooded battery family with watering cycles of up to 90 days and more than 70 percent more electrolyte headspace.

## Notes

- Stryten's lineup sheet says the T310 tubular family gives up to 90-day watering cycles through a plate design with more than 70 percent more electrolyte headspace, and is ideal for opportunity charge applications. Source: Stryten lineup sheet (09/2024) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/14502/133723547947804003.pdf>
