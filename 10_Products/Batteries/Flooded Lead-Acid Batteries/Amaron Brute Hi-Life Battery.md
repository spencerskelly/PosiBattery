# Amaron Brute Hi-Life Battery

## Definition

Amara Raja Amaron Brute Hi-Life motive power batteries for forklifts; only a marketing description was retrieved.

## Notes

- Amara Raja says Amaron Brute Hi-Life Batteries are made for motive power in manufacturing, processing and warehousing, offer the highest cycle life among existing products and were tested in European facilities. Source: Maritime Gateway (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.maritimegateway.com/amara-raja-launches-amaron-brute-hi-life-batteries-revolutionize-logistics-industry/>
- **Type note:** the item does not state the chemistry; it is filed as flooded lead-acid by default and should be confirmed.
- **Not stated:** voltages, capacities, cycle life numbers and warranty.

- **Official brochure found 2026-10-13 (read through a summarizing tool, IB-181):** [[Document - Amaron Brute Motive Power Brochure (ARBL-IBD-AB-0422)]] <https://www.amararajaeandm.com/amaron-brute/downloads/brochure.pdf>. It names the product "Amaron Brute"; "Hi-Life" appears there only as a brand name, so how Brute and Brute Hi-Life relate is unconfirmed.
- **Type note resolved in part (C95):** the brochure lists DIN (PzS) and BS (PzB) cell ranges, flip-top plugs and an electrolyte circulation option, which fits flooded cells; it never writes "flooded" and calls Amara Raja a VRLA pioneer. The filing as flooded is supported, not confirmed.
- **Construction:** tubular spine positive plates with dry red lead filling; patented paste mix; polyester separators. Cycle life "1600 - 1700 cycles @ 80% DoD @ 25" (unit cut off). DIN 120 to 1,550 Ah and BS 110 to 1,000 Ah at C5. Source: Amaron Brute brochure (T1). <https://www.amararajaeandm.com/amaron-brute/downloads/brochure.pdf>
- **Design characteristics, with citations:**

- **Offered with:** [[Amaron Electrolyte Circulation System]] and [[Amaron Aqua Filling Plug]] (V, options named in the brochure). 
- **Not stated anywhere read:** voltage, warranty, grid alloy.
