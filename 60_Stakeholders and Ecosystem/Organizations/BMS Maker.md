# BMS Maker

## Definition

Business role: company that makes a battery management system, sold separately or as the BMS of its own packs.

## Notes

- Flux Power also plays this role through the SkyBMS name; its packs with integrated BMS are covered under [[Integrated Battery Management System]].
- Battery makers that build the BMS into their own pack (EnerSys, Green Cubes, Hyster and others) are not listed here because the sources read do not say they make the BMS or buy it.
