# Vahle Charging Contacts

## Definition

Vahle charging contacts: a vehicle current collector (BLS) and a stationary base module (BLK) for AGV and forklift charging.

## Notes

- Working name analyst-given (`unnamed-in-source`): the catalog names the families BLS and BLK. 2-pole 50-200 A (with or without pilot), 1-pole 200-600 A, standard up to 60 VDC / 25 VAC, custom to 230/400 V; drive-on, drive-over and pressure contact. Source: <https://vahle.com/fileadmin/cavok_upload/Energie%C3%BCbertragung/Ladekontakte/Ladekontakte/Englisch/Cat_10a_en_Charging_Contacts.pdf> (T1, second-hand).
