# HDI Risk Consulting

## Definition

Risk-engineering arm of an insurer (HDI Risk Consulting GmbH) that publishes property risk guidelines, including one on charging industrial-truck batteries.

## Notes

- Added 2026-10-17 for [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]]. Nothing was researched about the company itself.
- The guideline read is lead-acid-oriented and dated (2016 imprint); it gives no lithium guidance ([[Document - HDI Risk Engineering Guideline Battery Charging (2016)]]).
- Not linked to products: no relationship in the vocabulary fits an insurer.
