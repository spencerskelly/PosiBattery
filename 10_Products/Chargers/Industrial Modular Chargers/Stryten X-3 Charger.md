# Stryten X-3 Charger

## Definition

Stryten M-Series modular multi-voltage opportunity charger using silicon-carbide MOSFETs, sibling of the X-7.

## Notes

- Stryten says the M-Series X-3 and X-7 are modular multi-voltage opportunity chargers ready to charge almost any motive battery including the Li600, using silicon carbide MOSFET power technology for multi-shift opportunity and fast charging, all available CEC-certified. Source: Stryten lineup sheet (09/2024) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/14502/133723547947804003.pdf>
- **Related-product note:** the sheet describes the X-3 and X-7 together and gives no separate ratings; differences between them are not stated (to-do).
