# Metric - Charging Gas Emissions

## Definition

Charging Gas Emissions: whether a battery gasses during charging, which decides whether a ventilated charging room is needed.

## Notes

- **Code and class:** BM13; battery metric. Unit or format: statement (none or gassing).
- **Comparability rule:** Flooded lead-acid batteries gas while charging; lithium-ion and sealed lead-acid statements come from product text and are not measurements.
- **Direction:** none is better.
- **Values on file (as stated in each product note; n/s means not stated):**
  - [[Crown V-Force Lithium-Ion ESS]]: zero emissions during charging, no gassing
  - [[Linde Ei Series]]: no gases released during charging; no ventilated charging room needed
  - [[Triathlon Lithium-Ion Battery for UniCarriers]]: no gassing, zero emissions
  - [[Yale ERC050-060VGL]]: no gassing during charging (dealer text, T3)
- **Source rule:** the product note holds the source URL for each value; this note copies the value for comparison. If they differ, the product note wins and this note is fixed.
- **Gaps and to-do:** created in round 27 when the function 'Charge Without Gas Emissions' was retired as a property, not a behavior.
