# Cold Storage Operating Environment

## Definition

Low-temperature operating and charging context such as freezer or cold-storage facilities where battery behavior, charging rules and equipment durability differ from ambient operation.

## Notes

- Use this as a scenario condition for charging, shift-readiness, maintenance and vehicle operation in low-temperature environments.
- Supported by [[Keep Equipment Working in Cold, Wet and Dusty Conditions]] and [[Cold Storage Charging Rules for Traction Batteries (ZVEI)]].
- This context constrains operational Use Cases; it does not replace Functions such as [[Operate in Cold Storage]] or [[Charge in Cold Storage]].
