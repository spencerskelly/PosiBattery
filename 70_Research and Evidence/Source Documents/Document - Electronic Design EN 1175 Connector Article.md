# Document - Electronic Design EN 1175 Connector Article

## Definition

Electronic Design article on EN 1175 connector requirements.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- Annex A of EN 1175 covers energy-source connections with wear, damage and environment tests, acid and oil insulation tests, temperature rise at rated current; auxiliary signalling must meet a performance level with no prescribed layout; latching is optional; no charging-specific connector standard is named.
