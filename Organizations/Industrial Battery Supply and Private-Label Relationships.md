# Industrial Battery Supply and Private-Label Relationships

## Definition

Register of documented supply, private-label, rebrand, integration and channel relationships among industrial battery makers, truck OEMs and monitor makers, with evidence status and explicit unknowns.

## Notes

- **Term use:** 'private label' and 'powered by' are used only as the source words them. Integration approvals, acquisitions and distributor listings are separate kinds and are not white labeling.
- **Main finding:** the only explicit private-label relationships found are Flux Power's two with unnamed forklift OEMs. No source retrieved names a truck OEM as a private-label customer of EnerSys, Exide, East Penn or HOPPECKE. That is absence of public evidence, not evidence that none exist.
- **Why public evidence is thin:** private-label supply is usually under contract and unlabeled on the product. Ways to learn more outside web search: battery nameplates and UL file numbers, dealer catalogs that say 'made by', and trade-show disclosure.
