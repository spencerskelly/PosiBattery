# Document - AT&T Business 3G Shutdown FAQ (undated, 2022)

## Definition

AT&T Business FAQ on its 3G network turn-down, the only carrier-published text read on AT&T's LTE plans.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- 3G: "network turn down is not until 2022"; announced 2019; IoT customers without a stop date in their agreement were to stop 3G activations by 2020-02-21.
- LTE: "We have not currently announced a turn down date for our 4G LTE network"; points IoT customers to LTE-M.
- Customers are responsible for planning hardware upgrades "in a reasonable timeframe"; AT&T does not pay for disposal.
- The document is undated beyond a 2022 copyright; it is older than the MCA page, so it cannot rule out a later 2027 commitment, but no later AT&T text confirming one was found (C328).
