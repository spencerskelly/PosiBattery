# BMID Charger Battery Information

## Definition

Reusable information-flow definition for supported battery identity, condition, temperature, or related charging information supplied by a BMID-family device to a compatible charger.

## Notes

- The exact data set and transport are variant-specific.
- This flow carries information only; it does not assign charger power-control authority to the BMID.
- Local Model occurrences provide the contextual source and destination roles.
