# Exide 2100 Li High Output Charger

## Definition

Exide high-frequency lithium charger in the 2100 Li range, up to 300 A, with CAN bus communication.

## Notes

- Up to 300 A fast recharge; CAN bus; LCD and LED status; based on the 2100 HP range with matching power range. Source: Exide product page (T1, second-hand), <https://www.exidegroup.com/en/product/2100-li-high-output>
- **Functions with citations:** [[Charge Lithium-Ion Battery]] (V, "Li" range, 300 A fast recharge); [[Communicate with Charger]] (V, CAN bus; what is communicated is not stated). <https://www.exidegroup.com/en/product/2100-li-high-output>
- **Naming unresolved:** the Sonnenschein truck sheet shows a "2100HP" charger and a "GNB Lithium Charger"; this page uses neither name and carries no GNB branding. Whether these are one product or several is not known (C224, IB-184). Linked to the Sonnenschein battery on the basis of the vault's earlier sheet reading, which does not name this model.
- Voltage, kW and compatible chemistries not stated.
