# PosiCharge PosiNet

## Definition

PosiCharge data service that works with the cellular Battery Rx to collect battery data.

## Notes

- PosiCharge's GSE sheets say PosiNet works with the cellular Battery Rx to collect battery data, and that Battery Rx has optional cellular connectivity and data services. Source: PosiCharge GSE charger sheets (MVS400) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/16696/131261342583679925.pdf>
