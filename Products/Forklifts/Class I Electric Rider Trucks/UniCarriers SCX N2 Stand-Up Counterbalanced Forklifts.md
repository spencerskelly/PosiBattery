# UniCarriers SCX N2 Stand-Up Counterbalanced Forklifts

## Definition

UniCarriers SCX N2 electric stand-up counterbalanced trucks: 36 V AC, 16 hours on one charge, lithium-ion, lighting packages, freezer options and in-cab accessories.

## Notes

- Mitsubishi Logisnext Americas says the SCX N2 has an improved turning radius, 16 hours on a single charge, an adjustable anti-fatigue floorboard, a 36 V AC system with 6th generation AC induction motors, lithium-ion technology, lighting packages, freezer options and in-cab accessories (USB ports, operator fan and mirror). Source: UniCarriers SCX N2 launch release (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.ajot.com/news/mitsubishi-logisnext-americas-group-launches-new-unicarriers-forklift-scx-n2-series-of-electric-stand-up-counterbalanced-lift-trucks>
