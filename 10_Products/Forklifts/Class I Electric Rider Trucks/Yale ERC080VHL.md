# Yale ERC080VHL

## Definition

Yale 8,000 lb four-wheel electric cushion-tire forklift built around a fully integrated lithium-ion battery.

## Notes

- Yale says the ERC080VHL is designed around an integrated lithium-ion pack with greater operator space, zero emissions, no battery maintenance, full power until depletion and fast charging, for heavy-duty indoor use or converting internal combustion fleets, with Yale Vision telemetry. Source: Yale ERC080VHL page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.yale.com/en-us/north-america/lithium-ion-forklifts/erc080vhl/>
