# Document - FuSE CARB's 2025 LCFS Regulation Amendments Electricity Credit Generation (2024-11-14)

## Definition

A blog post stating that California cut the forklift energy economy ratio from 3.8 to 2.4 in the 2025 LCFS amendments.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the business case and the open EER question (C286). Blog post by Todd Trauman dated 2024-11-14 on the 2025 LCFS amendments; the author states opinions.

- **Forklift EER:** the energy economy ratio for forklifts under 12,000 lbs lift capacity was "reduced from 3.8 to 2.4"; the author writes that the cut was unjust. No 1.9 value appears.
- **Metering and entity:** from 2026, forklift electricity reporting must use measured, not estimated, volumes; forklifts fall under a broader fuel supply equipment definition so owners of charging assets can be credit generators.
- **Other:** one-time 9 percent benchmark cut in 2025; 2030 target raised from 20 to 30 percent; prices fell 75 to 80 percent between early 2020 and mid-2024; third-party verification from 2026-01-01 for transactions above 10,000 credits a year.
- **Not stated:** an effective date for the EER change, benchmark values, grid carbon intensity and an absolute credit price.
- **Conflict:** 2.4 differs from 1.9 in [[Document - Smart Charging Technologies Comment Letter on Electric Forklift EER (2024-05-09)]] and 3.8 in [[Document - 17 CCR 95486.1 Cornell LII Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)]] (C296).
