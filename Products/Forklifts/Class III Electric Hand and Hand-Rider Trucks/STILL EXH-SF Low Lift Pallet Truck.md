# STILL EXH-SF Low Lift Pallet Truck

## Definition

STILL EXH-SF 16C/20C stand-on low lift pallet truck with Curve Speed Control as standard and optional lithium-ion technology.

## Notes

- STILL says the EXH-SF 16C/20C features Curve Speed Control as standard, travels at 8.5 km/h, offers lithium-ion that reaches 50 percent charge in 30 minutes and full charge in 90 minutes (capacities up to 500 Ah), PIN access without the Smart Portal, and the Smart Portal for operator management, impact detection and access control. Source: STILL EXH-SF page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.still.co.uk/forklift-trucks/new-forklifts/low-lift-pallet-trucks/exh-sf-16c-20c.html>
