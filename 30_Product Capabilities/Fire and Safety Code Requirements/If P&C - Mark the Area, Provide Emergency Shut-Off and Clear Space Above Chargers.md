# If P&C - Mark the Area, Provide Emergency Shut-Off and Clear Space Above Chargers

## Definition

One insurer's hazard sheet says lithium charging areas should be marked for first responders, have an emergency power shut-off near the entrance or further away, and keep combustibles, cables and cable trays out of the space above chargers.

## Notes

- **Insurer guidance, not a code.** Mostly site obligations; the emergency shut-off is also a possible external-control use of the charger (see REQ-00076).
- Second-hand reading; see [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]].
