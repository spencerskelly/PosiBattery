# Battery-Installed Device

## Definition

Reusable family for a device or hardware system that is physically installed on, attached to, or integrated into a battery or battery pack and remains with that battery during normal use.

## Notes

This is a classification root for the market reference, not a deployable product. Application deployment belongs in Local Model occurrences rather than duplicating this definition.

## Aliases

- Battery-Mounted Device
- Battery-Integrated Device
