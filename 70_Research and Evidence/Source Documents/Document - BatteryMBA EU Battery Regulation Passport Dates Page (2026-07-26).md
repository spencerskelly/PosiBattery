# Document - BatteryMBA EU Battery Regulation Passport Dates Page (2026-07-26)

## Definition

Training-provider summary of Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 dates and the implementation status of delegated and implementing acts, as of July 2026.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand). The page cites EUR-Lex and Commission material but gave no article numbers for the passport, QR code or removability.

- Passport from 2027-02-18 for EV, light means of transport and industrial batteries above 2 kWh; accessible "through a QR code linked to a unique identifier".
- Due diligence (Art. 48) postponed from 2025-08-18 to 2027-08-18 by Regulation (EU) 2025/1561. Recycled content: lead 85 percent from 2031-08-18; lead recovery 90 percent by 2027-12-31.
- Carbon footprint: staged; EV delegated act not adopted and its original 2025-02-18 date has lapsed; industrial carbon footprint methodology published by the JRC in April 2025, neither delegated act in force.
- Passport access-rights implementing act: expected late 2026, not adopted.
- Omnibus IV (COM(2025) 501): provisional agreement 2026-06-09, awaiting formal adoption.
- Does not state whether a lead-acid battery is in scope of the passport or the carbon footprint rules (see [[Document - Arianee Battery Passport for Material Handling Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)]], which says it is).
