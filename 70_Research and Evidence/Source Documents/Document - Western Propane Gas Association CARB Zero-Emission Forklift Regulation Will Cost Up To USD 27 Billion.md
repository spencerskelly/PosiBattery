**Content recorded:**
- WPGA says 220,000 forklifts (more than half of those in the state) are affected against CARB's 95,000. Claimed costs up to USD 27bn: USD 10bn ICE replacement, USD 4.6bn utilization loss by 2038, battery costs above USD 2.8bn by 2038, charging stations above USD 6.3bn; excludes power supply upgrades. Components listed sum to about USD 23.7bn; the release does not explain the rest. Author of the economic report not named. Position: propane industry, opposing the rule.
