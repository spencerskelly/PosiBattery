# Battery Monitoring Device

## Definition

Battery-installed device whose primary reusable purpose is to measure, record, indicate, or communicate battery operating condition or usage.

## Notes

Specific monitored quantities vary by product and must come from product evidence. Monitoring may coexist with identification, charger communication, alerts, or telemetry.

## Aliases

- Battery Performance Monitor
- Battery Condition Monitor
