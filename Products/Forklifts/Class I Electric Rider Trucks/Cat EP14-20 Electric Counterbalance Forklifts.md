# Cat EP14-20 Electric Counterbalance Forklifts

## Definition

Cat 48 V electric counterbalance forklifts (1.4 to 2.0 t, 3-wheel and 4-wheel) with a Presence Detection System and lead-acid or lithium-ion batteries.

## Notes

- Cat says the 48 V EP14-20 trucks offer lead-acid or lithium-ion batteries, a Presence Detection System+ (PDS+) and a variety of safety lights as options; a 2024 Cat brochure covers the EP14-20A(C)N(T), EP25-35A(C)N and EP40-55(C)N(H) range at 1.4 to 5.5 t. Source: Cat Lift Trucks blog and brochure (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.catlifttruck.com/blog/switch-cat-electric-lift-trucks>
