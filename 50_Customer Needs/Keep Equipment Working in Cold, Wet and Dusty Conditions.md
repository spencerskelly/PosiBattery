# Keep Equipment Working in Cold, Wet and Dusty Conditions

## Definition

Customer need: Keep Equipment Working in Cold, Wet and Dusty Conditions. The problem behind it: Freezers, wash-down and outdoor work attack electronics, connectors and batteries and change charging rules.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: need hypothesis.** The statements below are what makers and dealers say their products do for customers. No customer, user or buyer source in the vault confirms that customers hold this need or rank it. Per the ruleset, source research becomes a validated need only after customer-side evidence.
- **Problem solved (analyst wording):** Freezers, wash-down and outdoor work attack electronics, connectors and batteries and change charging rules.
- **Who has the problem:** [[Fleet Operations Manager]], [[Forklift Operator]].
- **Operating segments (analyst crosswalk to [[PosiCharge Market Segments and Jobs-to-Be-Done]], hypothesis):** Material-handling fleets, Airport eGSE fleets.
- **Realized by (specific functions; products link to these):** [[Operate in Cold Storage]], [[Operate in Wet or Dusty Conditions]], [[Charge in Cold Storage]], [[Shelter Operator from Weather]].
