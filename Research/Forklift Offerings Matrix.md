# Forklift Offerings Matrix

## Definition

Matrix of forklift families by maker with the batteries, chargers, telematics and programs sold with them, with explicit unknowns.

## Notes

- **Method (2026-10-03):** model families are those named on the cited maker or trade pages; a family is one note even where the maker sells many models. A dash or 'not retrieved' means not researched, not absent. Battery makers are named only where a source names them; otherwise the maker is unknown.
- **First named truck-maker to battery-maker supply found:** Mitsubishi Logisnext Americas and Triathlon (UniCarriers lithium-ion battery and charger, factory option). Raymond's Energy Essentials is 'Distributed by Raymond', maker not stated.
- **Class coverage:** Class I and II electric trucks and Class III pallet trucks are modeled; Class IV, V, VI and VII are context only until Q13 is decided.
