# Document - 17 CCR 95484 Cornell LII Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

Cornell's copy of the LCFS benchmark table, probably before the 2025 amendments.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the business case. Cornell copy of the LCFS annual carbon intensity benchmarks section.

- **Values:** diesel fuel and its substitutes 86.64 gCO2e/MJ for 2025 and 85.38 gCO2e/MJ for 2026, with a base-year ULSD value of 100.45.
- **Currency:** the Notes list amendments only through a section filed 2019-01-04, so this copy probably predates the 2025 amendments, which include a one-time 9 percent benchmark reduction in 2025 per [[Document - FuSE CARB's 2025 LCFS Regulation Amendments Electricity Credit Generation (2024-11-14)]]. The 2026 benchmark in force is therefore uncertain (C298).
