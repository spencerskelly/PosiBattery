# Keep Trucks Working Without Battery Maintenance Labor

## Definition

Customer need: Keep Trucks Working Without Battery Maintenance Labor. The problem behind it: Lead-acid batteries need regular watering, cleaning and equalizing, which costs labor hours and maintenance visits.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: need hypothesis.** The statements below are what makers and dealers say their products do for customers. No customer, user or buyer source in the vault confirms that customers hold this need or rank it. Per the ruleset, source research becomes a validated need only after customer-side evidence.
- **Problem solved (analyst wording):** Lead-acid batteries need regular watering, cleaning and equalizing, which costs labor hours and maintenance visits.
- **Who has the problem:** [[Maintenance Technician]], [[Fleet Operations Manager]].
- **Operating segments (analyst crosswalk to [[PosiCharge Market Segments and Jobs-to-Be-Done]], hypothesis):** Material-handling fleets, Battery-room and centralized charging, Mixed-chemistry fleets.
- **Realized by (specific functions; products link to these):** [[Water Battery Cells]], [[Alert on Low Electrolyte Level]], [[Sense Electrolyte Level]], [[Charge Lithium-Ion Battery]].
- **Products reaching this need:** 71 by function, design or option (owner decision, round 40: design and option routes count as reached); 52 of those perform a realizing function. Largest families by function: Battery Accessories/Monitoring Devices (17), Chargers/Industrial Modular Chargers (14), Battery Accessories/Watering Systems (6), Battery Accessories/Water Level Monitors (4), Battery Accessories/Identification and Charge Interface Devices (4), Chargers/Light-Duty Chargers (3). The metric route (hypothesis) adds 5 more. Per-product routes in [[Product to Customer Need Map]].
- **Vendor-stated evidence (by product):**
  - [[Deka Dominator Battery]]: Correction: East Penn's forklift products page describes Dominator as a maintenance-free gel design to save maintenance hours, designed for conventional and opportunity charging, spillproof and leakproof. Source: East Penn forklift products page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.eastpennmanufacturing.com/forklift-products/>
  - [[Leoch PzS Traction Battery]]: Leoch's page lists PzS cells of 2 V from 2PzS200 to 5PzS400 (200 to 400 Ah at 5 h), with tubular positive plates, lower water loss and fewer maintenance visits ('half maintenance'), for forklifts, tractors, carriers and similar equipment. Source: Leoch motive power page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://leoch.com/product/power/127.html>
  - [[Yale ERC080VHL]]: Yale says the ERC080VHL is designed around an integrated lithium-ion pack with greater operator space, zero emissions, no battery maintenance, full power until depletion and fast charging, for heavy-duty indoor use or converting internal combustion fleets, with Yale Vision telemetry. Source: Yale ERC080VHL page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.yale.com/en-us/north-america/lithium-ion-forklifts/erc080vhl/>
  - [[Exide Automatic Watering System and Level Sensor]]: Exide's overview says an electrolyte level sensor and an automatic watering system are available for its motive power batteries. Source: GNB motive power overview (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.exidegroup.com/eu/sites/default/files/2021-08/GNB_MP_Overview_EN_web.pdf>
