# Document - Oregon DEQ 2025 Annual Costs and Benefits of the Clean Fuels Program

## Definition

Oregon DEQ's 2025 cost-and-benefit calculation giving a $80.51 per ton credit price and $0.0858 per kWh average electricity cost-saving.

## Notes

**How it was read:** first-hand, from the raw file the owner added to the repo (`Downloads/ODEQ_CFPCost2025.pdf`), with text extracted by pdftotext. Not a summary.

Added from the owner's downloads. DEQ document under HB 2017, which requires the average cost or cost-saving per gallon of gasoline and diesel each April. The web address is inferred from the file name the owner used and has not been checked.

- **Credit price used:** $80.51 per ton in all 2025 formulas; the document says credit price comes from the Monthly Credit Transaction Report and does not label it as an annual average, so treat it as DEQ's 2025 figure (C301).
- **Electricity:** average cost-saving of $0.0858 per kWh (8.58 cents) for 2025, from (88.25 gCO2e/MJ standard less 3.91 gCO2e/MJ electricity CI divided by EER 3.4) x 3.6 MJ per kWh x EER 3.4 x $80.51 per ton / 1,000,000. The CI is the volume-weighted electricity value from DEQ's quarterly data and the EER is the general Table 7 value. It is a generic electricity figure, not a forklift one; Oregon's forklift EER and CI were not found (IB-264).
- **Other results:** E10 gasoline +9.35 cents per gallon cost; B5 diesel and biodiesel figures; renewable diesel and natural gas lines. The program displaced about 324 million gallons of fossil fuel (as stated).
- **Caveat stated by DEQ:** the approach is conservative (likely overstates cost per gallon).
