# EnerSys Aquamatic Water Refilling System

## Definition

Optional automatic water refilling system for HAWKER Perfect Plus batteries.

## Notes

- Needs 0.2 to 0.6 bar, at least 2 m height difference, water at or below 30 uS/cm, at most 20 cells in series, weekly refill. Name "Aquamatic" is also used by a Midac option ([[Midac Aquamatic Watering System]]); no link between them is shown. Source: HAWKER Perfect Plus instructions (T1, second-hand), <https://enersys.com/4a6cd5/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/hawker/perfect-plus/emea/hawker-perfect-plus-instruction-for-use-english.pdf>
