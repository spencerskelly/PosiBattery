# Litum PathAware

## Definition

Litum PathAware retrofit forklift safety system in Core, IQ and IQ+ tiers with SlowDown and ZoneLite add-ons.

## Notes

- Per-vehicle subscription; in-cab Onboard Hub; UWB and AI sensing; tagless pedestrian detection; no cameras; works across brands, leased or owned. Source: <https://www.newswire.com/news/litum-expands-pathaware-from-collision-warning-to-forklift-safety-intelligence> (T1, second-hand).
  - **Core:** detection, configurable audio/visual warnings, UWB vehicle-to-vehicle awareness.
  - **IQ:** adds event dashboards and operator identification/access control (NFC card or compatible Litum tag).
  - **IQ+:** adds digital pre-operation inspections and utilization tracking.
  - **SlowDown (add-on):** automatic speed reduction. **ZoneLite (add-on):** geofencing.
- **Naming conflict (C236):** earlier research listed "ZoneLight" and "People Tags"; this release spells ZoneLite and names no People Tags product.
