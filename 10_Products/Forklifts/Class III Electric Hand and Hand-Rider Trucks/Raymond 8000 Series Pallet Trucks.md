# Raymond 8000 Series Pallet Trucks

## Definition

Raymond walkie and rider pallet trucks including a lithium-ion pallet jack.

## Notes

- Raymond's literature page lists the 8210 walkie pallet truck, 8250 lithium-ion pallet jack, 8310 walkie pallet jack and 8510 center rider pallet truck; ProMat 2023 coverage adds the 8810 side-entry pallet truck. Source: Raymond literature page and ProMat report (T1/T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://test-raymondcorp.raymondcorp.com/information/lift-truck-literature> and <https://www.robotics247.com/article/raymond_shows_off_integrated_intralogistics_systems_promat_2023/Raymond_Corp>
- Raymond says the 8250 walkie pallet jack has a 24 V (26 V nominal) lithium-ion battery of 78 Ah (156 Ah option), made in the US exclusively for Raymond, UL E/EE, UN 38.3 and UL 2271 approved, maintenance-free with no watering, equalizing or cleaning; the 8210 has optional hot-dip galvanization (Extreme Environment Corrosion Protection) for wet, caustic and briny applications; the 8310 has a pin code pad option. Source: Raymond 8250 page and Johnston Equipment listing (T1/T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://raymondcorp.com/forklifts/pallet-trucks/8250-lithium-ion-pallet-jack>
