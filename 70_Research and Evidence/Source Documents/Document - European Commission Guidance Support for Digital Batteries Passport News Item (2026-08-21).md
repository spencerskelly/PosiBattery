# Document - European Commission Guidance Support for Digital Batteries Passport News Item (2026-08-21)

## Definition

European Commission news item announcing a guidance document that lists the 71 battery passport data points by category and whether each is mandatory from February 2027.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- From 18 February 2027 "each EV battery, each LMT battery and each industrial battery with a capacity greater than 2 kWh" needs a passport when placed on the market or put into service; no threshold is set for EV or LMT batteries.
- Responsible parties: economic operators across the value chain, including manufacturers and importers, plus organizations making passport information available.
- The guidance "Digital Batteries Passport - data points by category" lists 71 data points per category with status mandatory, optional, conditional or not required as of February 2027, with legal source. It adds no legal requirement and is not the Commission's authoritative interpretation.
- Not mentioned: QR code, standards, passport service providers, delays, transitional rules.
