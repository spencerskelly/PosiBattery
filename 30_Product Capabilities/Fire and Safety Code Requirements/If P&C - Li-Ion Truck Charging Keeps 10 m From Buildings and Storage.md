# If P&C - Li-Ion Truck Charging Keeps 10 m From Buildings and Storage

## Definition

One insurer's hazard sheet says lithium-ion truck charging that is not inside the building should be in a non-combustible building at least 10 m from surrounding buildings and storage, and that a 10 m safety distance applies even to open or canopy charging areas.

## Notes

- **Insurer guidance, not a code.** Compare the 3 ft combustible-storage clearance in REQ-00065: different purpose and source, not one number in conflict (C321).
- US relevance unknown (IB-278). Second-hand reading; see [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]].
