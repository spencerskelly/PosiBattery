# Document - Amaron Brute Motive Power Brochure (ARBL-IBD-AB-0422)

## Definition

Amara Raja Amaron Brute motive power brochure (reference code ARBL/IBD/AB/0422): tubular spine plates, DIN and BS cell ranges, cycle life, options.

## Notes

**How it was read.** Through a fetch tool that returns a model-written summary, not the raw text, on 2026-10-13; values were not checked against the raw file (IB-179 to IB-182).

- Positive plates use a tubular spine filled dry with 100 percent red lead; positive mass 99.99 percent primary lead; patented paste mix on both plates that reduces water consumption; reinforced non-woven polyester separator ("Gauntlet" design). Grid alloy not specified. The page calls Amara Raja a pioneer of VRLA batteries and does not say the Brute is VRLA; its DIN (PzS) and BS (PzB) cell types, flip-top plugs and electrolyte circulation option are consistent with flooded cells.
- Cycle life "1600 - 1700 cycles @ 80% DoD @ 25" (the temperature unit is cut off in the source).
- Cell ranges at C5 per IEC 60254-1: DIN (PzS) 120 to 1,550 Ah, 8.5 to 82.4 kg filled and charged; BS (PzB) 110 to 1,000 Ah, 7.9 to 48.9 kg. Cell designations AB 2 to AB 10 are not explained. Length, width and height tables could not be mapped to models.
- Options and features: Electrolyte Circulation System ("reduces water consumption by upto 60%" and "energy consumption by upto 20%", maker claims), AFS system (optional), Aqua Filling Plug (optional), flip-top plugs supplied, a "DIY Rx" diagnosis feature (not explained). "HI LIFE BATTERIES" appears only as a brand name.
- Plants ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018; every cell air-pressure tested for acid leakage. Not stated: voltage, warranty.
