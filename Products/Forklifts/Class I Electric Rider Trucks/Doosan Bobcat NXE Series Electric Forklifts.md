# Doosan Bobcat NXE Series Electric Forklifts

## Definition

Bobcat NXE series 4-wheel electric forklifts with lead-acid or lithium-ion power, IP54 motors for sites down to -20 C, Guardian Stability System and an electric parking brake.

## Notes
