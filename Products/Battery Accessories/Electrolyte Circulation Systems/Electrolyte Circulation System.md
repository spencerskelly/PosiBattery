# Electrolyte Circulation System

## Definition

Hardware that moves air or electrolyte inside lead-acid cells during charging to reduce acid stratification.

## Notes

- **Locus:** split. Cell-level tubing and distribution sit on the battery; the pneumatic pump may sit on the battery or the charger depending on vendor. Treat locus as unresolved.
- **Chemistry:** flooded lead-acid only.
- HOPPECKE trak|air is described as a battery-equipped circulation system that injects air to prevent acid stratification, shorten charge time and reduce water refilling and energy cost. Source: HOPPECKE (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.hoppecke.com/en/product/trak-uplift-air>
- Fronius offers an optional electrolyte circulation system on its chargers; its Air-Puls option drives circulation with pneumatic pumps and is claimed to save up to two hours of charge time. Source: Fronius (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.fronius.com/en-gb/uk/battery-charging-technology/our-expertise/intralogistics/process-optimisation>
- **Link to connectors:** the REMA air plug accessory (see [[Battery Connector Assembly]]) suggests air can pass through the charging connector. Source is a reseller listing only.

## Aliases

- ECS
- Air mixing system
