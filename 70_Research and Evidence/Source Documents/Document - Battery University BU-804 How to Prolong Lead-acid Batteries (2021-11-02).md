# Document - Battery University BU-804 How to Prolong Lead-acid Batteries (2021-11-02)

## Definition

Battery University overview of lead-acid wear mechanisms and handling practices.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- Full saturation charge of 14 to 16 hours; if the cycle does not allow it, one every few weeks. The same figure appears weekly in a dealer blog ([[Document - Raymond West Tips to Extend the Life of Your Forklift Batteries (2023-01-30)]]; C344).
- Operate at moderate temperature, avoid deep discharge, charge as often as possible. No temperature-life figure on the page.
- Retire at 70 to 80 percent capacity; deep-cycle batteries deliver about 100 to 200 cycles before gradual decline begins (C346: far from the traction cycle counts in the ZVEI leaflet).
- Wear mechanisms listed: corrosion, shedding, internal shorts, sulfation, water loss, acid stratification, surface charge. Cites the 2010 BCI Failure Modes Study for failure-mode shares (not read).
- Does not cover depth-of-discharge tables, charge voltages, storage, watering or equalizing detail (links to a separate equalizing page, not read).
