# Document - CARB LCFS Final Regulation Order OAL-Approved Unofficial Version (effective 2025-07-01)

## Definition

CARB's adopted LCFS regulation order with the forklift EER of 2.4 and 3.8, the 2025 and 2026 benchmarks, and the forklift reporting rules.

## Notes

**How it was read:** first-hand, from the raw file the owner added to the repo (`Downloads/CARB_2025_lcfs_fro_oal-approved_unofficial_07012025.pdf`), with text extracted by pdftotext. Not a summary.

Added from the owner's downloads. CARB staff's unofficial electronic copy (underline and strikeout removed) of the final LCFS regulation order, sections 95480 to 95503, approved by the Office of Administrative Law on 2025-06-27 and effective 2025-07-01. CARB's own disclaimer says the official legal edition on the OAL site takes precedence.

- **Table 5 forklift EER (section 95486.1(a), printed page 87):** electricity forklifts with lift capacity under 12,000 lb: 2.4; 12,000 lb and over: 3.8. Hydrogen fuel cell forklifts: 1.6 and 2.1. Propane 0.9 (comparison fuel); eTRU 3.4; eCHE 2.7; eOGV 2.6. These match the second 15-day proposed text, so the proposal was adopted unchanged for these rows.
- **Diesel benchmark (section 95484):** 81.70 gCO2e/MJ for 2025 and 80.17 for 2026 (the table also marks 2025 with footnotes a, b and **). 85.38 for 2026 is not in this order.
- **Reporting entity (section 95483(b)(4)):** the owner of the FSE is the fuel reporting entity and credit generator for electric forklifts, eTRU, eCHE and eOGV; the owner may designate another entity if both sign a written contract that the owner will not generate credits and will provide the electricity data, and that the designated entity accepts all LCFS responsibilities. A generator must meet section 95491(e)(5).
- **FSE meaning (section 95483.2(b)(8)(6)):** for electric forklifts, eCHE, eOGV or eTRU the FSE is the facility or location where electricity is dispensed for fueling. Where several devices can measure the electricity dispensed at the facility, giving each device's serial number is optional.
- **Forklift reporting (section 95491(d)(3)(E)):** from 2026 reporting the kWh dispensed is reported per FSE with a certified fuel pathway code and transaction type 'Forklift Electricity Fueling'; before 2026 the quantity may be estimated with a CARB-approved methodology; the reporting entity reports the number of electric forklifts in the fleet. A transaction-type list later in the order also includes 'Forklift Electricity Fueling' (section not identified).
- **Conflicts:** C296, C298, C283, C300 and C305 are settled for California by this text (see the updates in the conflict register). Items not read: the Table 5 note on how the EER is applied per lift class beyond what is above, and the credit formula text in section 95486.1(b) onward.
- **Limit:** the file is CARB's unofficial copy, not the OAL legal edition; page numbers are the order's printed page numbers.
