# Fronius Charge & Connect

## Definition

Fronius software connection for Selectiva chargers showing state of charge, energy use and charger status.

## Notes

- Fronius's Selectiva 4.0 flyer says chargers connect to Charge & Connect for state of charge, energy use and charger status. Source: Fronius Selectiva 4.0 96 V / 120 V flyer (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://fronius.com/~/downloads/Perfect%20Charging/Flyer/PC_FLY_Selectiva_4.0_96V-120V_EN_fin-MRM_.pdf>
