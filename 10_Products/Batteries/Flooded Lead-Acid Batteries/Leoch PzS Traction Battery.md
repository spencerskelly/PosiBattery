# Leoch PzS Traction Battery

## Definition

Leoch PzS traction lead-acid battery cells (2 V, 200 to 400 Ah at 5 h) with tubular positive plates and reduced water loss.

## Notes

- Leoch's page lists PzS cells of 2 V from 2PzS200 to 5PzS400 (200 to 400 Ah at 5 h), with tubular positive plates, lower water loss and fewer maintenance visits ('half maintenance'), for forklifts, tractors, carriers and similar equipment. Source: Leoch motive power page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://leoch.com/product/power/127.html>
- **Not stated:** cycle life and warranty.
- **Design characteristics, with citations:**
  - [[Tubular Plate Construction]] (V): <https://leoch.com/product/power/127.html>

- **Update 2026-10-13:** the Leoch page lists about 60 models (2PzS to 10PzS...), weight with acid 12.6 to 79.1 kg (per-model table garbled), grid alloy for lower water loss ("half maintenance"), optional [[Leoch PzS Automatic Water Filling System]]. Cycle life, warranty and standards not on the page. Source: <https://leoch.com/product/power/127.html>
