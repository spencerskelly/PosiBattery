# Washington Site Claiming Clean Fuel Standard Forklift Credits

## Definition

Operating context: a charging location in Washington whose forklift fleet owner (or its operator under written contract) reports forklift charging electricity under the Clean Fuel Standard to earn credits.

## Notes

- **Evidence:** the fleet owner is the fuel reporting entity and credit generator ([[Document - WAC 173-424-220 Cornell LII Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)]]; slides in [[Document - Washington Ecology Clean Fuel Standard Rule Presentation (2022-08-23)]]). Whether metering is required for forklift charging is open (C293).
- Participants are stand-ins (IB-252).
- Register: [[Western State Clean Fuel Programs for Forklift Electricity Register]].
