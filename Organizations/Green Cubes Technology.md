# Green Cubes Technology

## Definition

US lithium-ion battery maker for motive, industrial, medical and ground support equipment that also makes chargers.

## Notes

- **Role (working label, not a governed property):** battery maker (lithium).
- Green Cubes was awarded a patent for a dual-CAN-transceiver BMS for industrial trucks and states it is the only maker of both Li-ion batteries and chargers for material handling (unverified superlative). Source: Ground Handling International (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.groundhandlinginternational.com/content/news/green-cubes-announces-patent-award-for-bms>
- A supplier list describes Green Cubes as founded in 1986 and headquartered in Kokomo, Indiana, designing and manufacturing lithium-ion battery systems for motive, industrial, medical and ground support equipment. Source: Foxtron Power Solutions list (secondary, dated) (T4), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.foxtronpowersolutions.com/forklift-battery-manufacturers/>
- **Not found:** private-label supply to a named truck OEM.
- **Offered or promoted with its batteries (each item with its web page):**
  - Battery lines: Lithium SAFEFlex (material handling, 48 V FBP-1000), SAFEFlex PLUS (UL EE), GSE packs with heaters, swappable workstation platform <https://www.energy-xprt.com/companies/green-cubes-technology-131836/products>; <https://www.aviationpros.com/ground-support-worldwide/gse/press-release/55139765/green-cubes-technology-green-cubes-technology-unveils-new-li-ion-battery-for-ground-support-equipment>
  - Chargers: Lithium SAFEFlex FBC series, custom OEM chargers <https://www.globalspec.com/FeaturedProducts/Detail/GreenCubesTechnology/Chargers/354867/1>
  - Battery-side electronics: BMS on top of the pack with CANbus interface <https://www.aviationpros.com/gse/video/55251746/green-cubes-technology-highlights-lithium-safeflex-batteries-for-gse>
  - Retrofit: battery retrofit kit for OneCharge batteries <https://www.globalspec.com/supplier/ProductAnnouncements/GreenCubesTechnology>
