# Flux Power GSE2

## Definition

GSE2 model of the [[Flux Power GSE Pack]] family, a lithium battery for airport ground support equipment.

## Notes

- **2021 brochure (Document - Flux Power GSE Battery Pack V.2 Brochure (2021)):** 44.2 x 14.0 x 22.5 in (112.3 x 35.6 x 57.2 cm); 535 lb (243 kg); maximum continuous external charge current 200 A; 80 V (76.8 V) nominal; charge 0 to 55 C, discharge -20 to 55 C (family-wide); BMS 2.0; isolated external CANbus. Source: Flux Power GSE pack brochure (T1, read through a summarizing tool), retrieved 2026-10-13. <https://www.fluxpower.com/hubfs/GSE%20Battery%20Pack%20V.2%20-%20Brochure.pdf>
- Capacity and energy: not mapped to this model by the 2021 brochure as read (the sheet lists 210, 315, 420 and 630 Ah for the family). The size order suggests the same order of capacities, which is an analyst inference and not a source statement (IB-166).
- Functions, designs and the SkyBMS link are held on the family note [[Flux Power GSE Pack]]; values here were read through a summarizing tool and not checked against the raw PDFs (IB-166).
