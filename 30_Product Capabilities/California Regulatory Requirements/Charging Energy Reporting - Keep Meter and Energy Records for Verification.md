# Charging Energy Reporting - Keep Meter and Energy Records for Verification

## Definition

Energy records and the identity of the measuring equipment (such as serial numbers and any calibration history) shall be retained and exportable for third-party verification.

## Notes

- **Proposed from the regulation text; there is no customer or stakeholder source for it.** It stays Draft until a person decides whether it is a requirement. Hypothesis ids H1 to H4 are in [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]].
- Why: verification starts with the 2026 data year and the guidance names meter serial numbers and rental records as records to keep.
- Retention period and record format are not stated in the sources read (IB-247).
