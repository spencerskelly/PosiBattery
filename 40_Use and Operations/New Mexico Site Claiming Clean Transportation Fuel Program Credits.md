# New Mexico Site Claiming Clean Transportation Fuel Program Credits

## Definition

Operating context: a charging location in New Mexico where a fleet that owns chargers reports metered kWh to the Clean Transportation Fuel Program, reporting from 2026-04-01.

## Notes

- **Evidence:** [[Document - New Mexico CTFP Roads to Revenue with Fleet Electrification (2026-07)]] lists electric forklifts as special equipment and requires quarterly metered kWh; [[Document - Trinity Consultants New Mexico Becomes Fourth State to Adopt a Clean Fuel Program (2026-02-27)]] does not mention forklifts (C294). The rule text (20.2.92 NMAC) was not read.
- Vendors that own chargers and aggregators can also participate, so the reporting party may not be the fleet (C295).
- Participants are stand-ins (IB-252).
- Register: [[Western State Clean Fuel Programs for Forklift Electricity Register]].
