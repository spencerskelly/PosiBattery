# ACT Quantum 2

## Definition

ACT cloud-connected modular charger paired with the BATTview monitor.

## Notes

- The spec sheet lists 24 to 96 V, peak efficiency over 94 percent and full-cycle efficiency over 93 percent, support for lithium-ion, sealed lead-acid and flooded batteries, a touchscreen, LED status bar, Wi-Fi to ACTview and ACTintelligent, and remote firmware updates. Source: ACT Quantum 2 sheet (MHI member site) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/41607/133717591610794845.pdf>
