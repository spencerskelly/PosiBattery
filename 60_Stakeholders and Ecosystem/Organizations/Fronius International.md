# Fronius International

## Definition

Austrian maker of Selectiva and SelectION industrial battery chargers and the TagID battery sensor, selling to users and to forklift and AGV manufacturers.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Charger Maker, Monitor Maker.
- Fronius says it offers charging solutions for both users and manufacturers of industrial forklift trucks and AGVs, with chargers for lead-acid (Selectiva 4.0) and lithium-ion (SelectION) batteries and software. Source: Fronius charging solutions page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.fronius.com/en/battery-charging-technology/product-list>
- **Direction (see backlog):** 'manufacturers of forklift trucks' hints at OEM supply; no OEM is named in retrieved sources.
