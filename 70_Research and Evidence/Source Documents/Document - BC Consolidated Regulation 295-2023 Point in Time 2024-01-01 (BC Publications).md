# Document - BC Consolidated Regulation 295-2023 Point in Time 2024-01-01 (BC Publications)

## Definition

A consolidated BC regulation defining a heavy forklift (over 6,800 kg) and giving electricity an EER of 3.9 for heavy forklifts and a 12.14 gCO2e/MJ default carbon intensity.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. A consolidated BC regulation as of 2024-01-01 hosted by a publisher of BC regulations. The regulation's title was not captured; it carries schedules of EER and carbon intensity and the final supply equipment reporting rules, and may be a different instrument from the Order in Council 699 General Regulation already on file (C306).

- **Definition (s. 1):** a 'heavy forklift' is a rough-terrain forklift or a forklift with a lifting capacity of more than 6,800 kg.
- **EER (Schedule 2, Table 1, diesel category):** electricity used in a heavy forklift 3.9. Gasoline category: 3.5 for light-duty motor vehicles, 1.0 for other or unknown use. Jet fuel category: 2.5. Energy density of electricity 3.6 MJ per kWh.
- **Default carbon intensity (Schedule 3, Table 2):** electricity 12.14 gCO2e/MJ.
- **Reporting (s. 17):** per piece of final supply equipment the compliance report includes the registration number and days of responsibility; without a registration number it needs the address and GPS coordinates, manufacturer, serial number, level and intended use.
- **Not found in the text read:** credit generator, utility-bill rule, metering.
