# Document - 3Degrees Revenue Streams from Electric Forklifts Under Clean Fuel Standards (2025-09-25)

## Definition

A credit-services firm's overview of forklift eligibility under California, Oregon and Washington clean fuel programs.

## Notes

- **Update 2026-10-17 (customer traceability review):** re-read for airport GSE: the page lists electric airport GSE as eligible in Oregon and airport eGSE in British Columbia; California LCFS and Washington CFS eligible lists do not include airport GSE; Canada CFR does not mention GSE; it says the owner is usually the credit generator; no metering rule, revenue figure or GSE EER given. Used in [[Installed Base Monetization Opportunity Register]] (C348).
**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the western-states clean fuel topic. Article by 3Degrees (a firm that sells environmental-credit services), published 2025-09-25 and last modified 2025-11-18 per page metadata.

- **Eligibility table:** California LCFS, Oregon CFP and Washington CFS each list forklifts as eligible equipment. California and Washington also offer capacity-based credits for zero-emission-vehicle infrastructure; this is not stated for forklifts.
- **Reporting entity:** the article says generally that in most programs "it is the owner that is eligible for credit generation"; it gives no program-specific definition.
- **Not stated:** metering rules, EER, program-specific reporting entities, reporting frequency, carbon intensity or credit calculation, and rule citations. The article does not match Oregon's metered-only rule or California's metering dates and cannot be used for them.
- **Interest:** the author sells credit-generation services, so the framing favours participation.
