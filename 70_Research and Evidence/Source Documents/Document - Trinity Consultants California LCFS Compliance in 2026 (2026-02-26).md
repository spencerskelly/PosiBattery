# Document - Trinity Consultants California LCFS Compliance in 2026 (2026-02-26)

## Definition

A February 2026 consultancy summary of LCFS requirements for 2026, including metered forklift electricity and 2027 verification.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic. Consultancy article published 2026-02-26 (page metadata shows an update on 2026-03-11); no individual author is named.

- Starting with 2026 reporting, forklift electricity use must be metered rather than estimated, which the article says standardizes reporting accuracy.
- Third-party verification of 2026 EV charging data is required in 2027, and CARB is expected to issue more verifier guidance for electricity pathways during 2026.
- The amendments include EV forklift crediting changes, with no details; the article does not mention energy economy ratio values or section numbers. Its heading calls the items "now in effect" but it places verification in 2027.
