## Definition

Eleven near-templated market-report posts about forklift batteries and chargers. Retained for the figures and company lists they carry, with low evidential weight.

## Notes

**Content recorded (as stated by the source, not independently verified unless marked):**
- Figures as stated, one row per post, are in the table of the register note. Headline figures: lead-acid forklift battery market USD 894 million (2025) to USD 1.48 billion (2035), CAGR 5.2 percent; lithium-ion forklift battery market USD 1.3 billion to USD 2.94 billion, CAGR 8.5 percent; commercial electric forklift battery market USD 2.76 billion to USD 7.5 billion, CAGR 10.5 percent; forklift battery charger market USD 1.26 billion to USD 2.05 billion, about 5.0 percent; forklift battery rental market USD 160.8 billion to USD 322.28 billion, 7.2 percent.
- Three lead-acid posts carry the same figures and the same report address id (998527) on two different publisher domains; counted as one source.
- Company lists: lead-acid posts name Johnson Controls, Exide Technologies, East Penn Manufacturing, EnerSys, GS Yuasa, Crown Battery, Clarios, Hoppecke Batterien, Leoch International, Narada Power; the lithium post adds BYD Company, Samsung SDI, LG Chem, Panasonic, Toshiba; the sustainability post names EnerSys, Hoppecke, East Penn Manufacturing, Exide Technologies, Hitachi Chemical, GS Yuasa, Systems Sunlight, Saft, Electrovaya, FAAM; the charger post names Proterra, EnerSys, Crown Equipment Corporation, Hyster-Yale, Toyota Material Handling, JLG, Plug Power, East Penn, Ametek, Delta-Q.
- Segmentation printed in the lead-acid posts: flooded and sealed lead-acid; 12 V and 24 V; up to 200 Ah and 201 to 400 Ah; warehouse operations and manufacturing plants. Lithium posts: LiFePO4 and NMC; counterbalance and reach trucks; opportunity and fast charging.
- Four of the eleven posts carry no market figures: the lead-acid 'Practical Market Outlook' (generic text), the lithium 'Competitive Strategies' post, 'Technology Trends Across the Energy Value Chain', and 'AGV Forklift Lithium Battery Market'. The 'Battery Stands' post text describes renewable energy and utilities, not battery stands.
