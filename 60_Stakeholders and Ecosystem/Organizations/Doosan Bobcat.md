# Doosan Bobcat

## Definition

Korean maker of Doosan industrial vehicles, ninth in the 2024 ranking.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Truck OEM.
- The 2024 ranking lists Doosan Bobcat ninth with $1.52B in lift truck revenue, down 24 percent. Source: Modern Materials Handling 2025 ranking (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.supplychain247.com/article/top-20-lift-truck-suppliers-2025>
- **Not researched:** models, battery and charger options.
- A trade report says Bobcat, through the acquisition of its sister company Doosan Industrial Vehicle, offers diesel (2 to 16 t), LPG (1.5 to 7 t) and electric (1.5 to 10 t) forklifts and has shown a B18NT 3-wheel lithium electric prototype, a B30X-7 Plus hydrogen forklift, the NXE electric series and an AI pedestrian detection camera. Source: IVT International (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.ivtinternational.com/?p=21644>
- Green Cubes named Doosan Industrial Vehicle America Corporation's preferred power systems vendor, supplying Lithium SAFEFlex batteries and chargers for Doosan's electric trucks (the agreement date is not in the retrieved text). Source: Supply Chain 24/7 (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.supplychain247.com/article/green_cubes_announces_preferred_supplier_agreement_with_doosan>
