# Stryten M-Series AGM210 Battery

## Definition

Stryten 6 V AGM battery.

## Notes

- Stryten's lineup sheet lists 6 V AGM210 batteries. Source: Stryten lineup sheet (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/14502/133723547947804003.pdf>
- Stryten's sheet says four 6 V AGM210 batteries are housed in the AGM220 integrated battery and charger system. Source: Stryten lineup sheet (09/2024) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/14502/133723547947804003.pdf>
- **Related-product note:** AGM210 is a component of [[Stryten M-Series AGM220 Battery]]; sold separately as a 6 V battery.
