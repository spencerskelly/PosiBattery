# Deka Ready Power Lithium Battery

## Definition

Deka lithium-ion forklift battery with a multi-module BMS design communicating over CAN.

## Notes

- East Penn's 2019 launch says Deka Ready Power uses a BMS design that monitors each module and the overall system, with battery data communicated to each BMS over a controller area network. Source: East Penn news (ProMat 2019) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.eastpennmanufacturing.com/east-penn-launches-new-li-ion-product-at-promat-2019/>
