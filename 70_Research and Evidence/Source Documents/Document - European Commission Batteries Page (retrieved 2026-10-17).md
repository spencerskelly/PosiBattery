# Document - European Commission Batteries Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

European Commission page on the Batteries Regulation, with the battery passport date, an indicative implementation timeline and links to guidance.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand).

- "18 February 2027: the battery passport becomes mandatory for relevant battery categories placed on the EU market."
- Lists in-scope categories (EV, e-bike/e-moped/e-scooter, home storage and industrial batteries); no kWh threshold is stated on this page (the 2 kWh threshold for industrial batteries is on the news item).
- Indicative timeline, "subject to publication requirements": implementing act for the passport registry adopted July 2026; registry "operational" 20 July 2026; implementing decision adopting six of eight harmonised standards from CEN-CENELEC JTC 24 in July 2026.
- The page mentions no omnibus, simplification or postponement affecting the passport.
- Links a batteries FAQ and the data-points guidance PDF (updated 2026-08-15).
