# Operate Truck Remotely

## Definition

Let a remote operator drive or take over a truck that has no driver on board.

## Notes

- Stated: Phantom Auto software lets operators drive unmanned tuggers, pallet jacks and counterbalance trucks remotely and take over from AGV or AMR autonomy (2021 partnership item, T2 second-hand, on the product note).
