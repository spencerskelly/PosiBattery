# GSE Vehicle Anatomy

## Definition

Abstract assembly of the 12 generic subsystems shared by electric GSE vehicles.

## Notes

- Assembly note: hasPart lists the 12 shared GSE parts; vehicle types and models are not linked to it individually yet.
- The parts follow the truck anatomy approach ([[Industrial Truck Anatomy]]) but are adapted to GSE: the mast and forks become the load-handling structure, and a towing interface, a front body and bumper, and a canopy are added; wheels sit under drive and brakes.
