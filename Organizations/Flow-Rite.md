# Flow-Rite

## Definition

Michigan battery watering company that also makes the Eagle Eye electrolyte level sensors and the Maverick watering system.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Monitor Maker.
- Flow-Rite is described as a US-based battery watering company whose Eagle Eye electrolyte sensor range received ETL approval. Source: Intertek ETL notice via BEST magazine (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.bestmag.co.uk/flow-rite-receives-etl-standard-electrolyte-sensor-range/>
- Flow-Rite (Byron Center, Michigan) introduced Eagle Eye as an electrolyte level indicator for flooded lead-acid batteries in lift trucks. Source: Manufacturing.net (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.manufacturing.net/industry40/press-release/13218751/impo-eagle-eye-electrolyte-indicator>
