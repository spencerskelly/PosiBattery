# Powerfleet Pedestrian Proximity Detection

## Definition

Powerfleet pedestrian proximity detection that identifies risks without special apparel or wearable tags.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Powerfleet AI camera system that detects pedestrians and vehicles in a forklift's path without wearable tags and alerts the operator.

**Marketed features:**
- No wearable tags or special apparel needed
- Caution (yellow) and danger (red) zones with visual/audible alerts
- With Speed Manager: reduces speed or acceleration on zone breach
- Up to four cameras, five alert zones each; auto-switch to direction of travel
- Wi-Fi, LTE or Bluetooth; near-miss capture of location, time and driver ID via Forklift Gateway/Unity
- In-cab monitor and Pedestrian Alert lighting options
