# Stryten EHF Charger

## Definition

Stryten industrial charger family for light to heavy duty where batteries are exchanged at the end of each shift.

## Notes

- Stryten says the EHF industrial chargers cover light-duty to heavy-duty uses with a wide range of options, for applications that typically have more than one battery per truck with exchange at the end of each work shift. Source: Stryten lineup sheet (09/2024) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/14502/133723547947804003.pdf>
- **Functions performed, with citations** (V = verified this pass):
  - [[Charge Battery Conventionally]] (V): <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/14502/133723547947804003.pdf>
- Stryten's EHF brochure (SE1060, 2/23) says the series provides a multi-curve range for standard flooded, low-maintenance flooded and low-maintenance valve-regulated batteries, with algorithms for the M-Series T300, T310, T330, F100 and F110, a data archive from the front panel, and multi-voltage models in 24 V (40 to 150 A, 260 to 1500 Ah) and 36 V (40 to 240 A, 260 to 1600 Ah) with 55 lb (G1) and 142 lb (G2) cabinets. Source: Stryten EHF brochure (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.stryten.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Stryten_M-Series_ProductBrochure_EHF_SE1060_DIGITAL.pdf>
