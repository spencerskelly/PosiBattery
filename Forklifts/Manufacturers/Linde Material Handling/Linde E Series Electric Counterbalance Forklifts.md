# Linde E Series Electric Counterbalance Forklifts

## Definition

Linde electric counterbalance forklifts available with lead-acid or lithium-ion batteries across all capacities.

## Notes

- Linde says all capacities of its electric counterbalance range are available with lithium-ion batteries, giving a choice of lead-acid or lithium-ion for most of its warehouse equipment as well. Source: Warehouse News (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://warehousenews.co.uk/?p=62842>
- A dealer listing describes the 2023 Linde E20S as a 4,000 lb three-wheel electric forklift with a 36 V lithium-ion battery and charger. Source: Construction Equipment Guide listing (T3), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.constructionequipmentguide.com/used-equipment/linde/forklifts/e20s/id/55018778>
