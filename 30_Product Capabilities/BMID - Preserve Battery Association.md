# BMID - Preserve Battery Association

## Definition

The PosiCharge BMID family shall maintain the association between its stored identity or history and the battery to which the BMID is installed during normal intended use.

## Notes

- This requirement preserves the meaning of battery-specific data.
- It does not prescribe mounting, serialization, pairing, replacement, commissioning, or service implementation.
