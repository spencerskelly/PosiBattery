# Injector Level-Sensing Watering

## Definition

Battery watering using an injector with a level-sensing shutoff mechanism that stops water flow when the target cell fill level is reached.

## Notes

- [[Philadelphia Scientific Water Injector System]] explicitly states that a precision level-sensing valve shuts off at the injector tip and that the system does not use floats.
- The exact sensing principle inside the injector tip is not published in the modeled source.
