# California ZEF - Government Fleets Replace LSI Forklifts on the Phase-Out Schedule

## Definition

A state or local government fleet operator shall not acquire and operate an LSI forklift from 2026-01-01 unless an exception applies, and shall phase out covered LSI forklifts by model-year groups on the dates in the schedule (2028 to 2038).

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It binds the party named in the definition, not a charger. Product consequences are the derived requirements REQ-00051 to REQ-00053 (analyst-proposed).
- Second-hand reading; see C-items in [[Battery Product Landscape Conflicts and Open Questions]] and [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]].
- **Status:** in force for government fleets (enforced; the January 2026 fact sheet says the pause does not apply to them).
- Dates are tabulated in [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]] (Table 2); the Class IV clause about forklifts under 12,000 pounds is unclear in the summary (read the raw sheet).
- Open: how the rule is enforceable against government fleets before EPA authorization is not explained by CARB (C279).
