# Green Cubes SAFEFlex Battery

## Definition

Green Cubes drop-in lithium battery for material handling, 48 V FBP-1000 series.

## Notes

- The listing says SAFEFlex FBP-1000 48 V material handling batteries are drop-in replacements sized for standard lead-acid battery compartments with fully customizable features. Source: Energy-Xprt listing (T3), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.energy-xprt.com/companies/green-cubes-technology-131836/products>
- A forklift site says the batteries have a built-in BMS, continuous monitoring of voltage, current and temperature, controlled contactors, a single-point connection, and use lithium iron phosphate. Source: Forklift Action shopfront (T3), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.forkliftaction.com/cards/1518/green-cubes-technology/default.aspx>
