# Products Offered or Promoted with Industrial Batteries

## Definition

Matrix of the trucks, chargers, monitors, watering, circulation, handling and software products that battery makers and truck OEMs name alongside their batteries.

## Notes

- **Method:** product names are those the maker or a dealer lists next to its batteries on the cited page. A dash means none found in retrieved sources, not that none exist. Full lists with a link per item are on each organization note under 'Offered or promoted with its batteries'.
- **Pattern 1 (charger plus monitor bundles):** EnerSys, HOPPECKE, Crown and Exide all name a battery-side monitor that works with their charger. EnerSys states charger temperature compensation and battery identity via Wi-iQ <https://www.enersys.com/490f6e/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/nexsys/modular-charger/amer/impaq-nexsys-plus-modular-charger-product-guide-1020.pdf>; HOPPECKE states temperature-controlled charging with trak | collect <https://hoppecke.com/en/stories/show/switch-from-gas-to-electrically-powered-forklift-trucks>; Crown lists a BMID as a charger option <https://crown.com/en-br/batteries-and-chargers/vhfm3-charger.html>.
- **Pattern 2 (software layer):** every maker with a researched monitor also names a fleet portal (E Connect and Xinx, Motion+ Fleet and GNB Cloud, inCOMMAND, SkyEMS, InfoLink, iWarehouse, trak | monitor 4.0). Source: the matrix links below.
- **Pattern 3 (lithium moves the monitor inside the battery):** Stryten Li610, Exide Solition, Flux Power SkyBMS and Green Cubes put a BMS and display or CANbus in the pack, so an add-on BMID-class device is not needed for those batteries <https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260413514429/en/Stryten-Energy-Launches-New-MSeries-Li610-LithiumIon-Battery-at-MODEX>; <https://exidegroup.com/us/en/document/solition-light-traction-battery-leaflet>; <https://www.aviationpros.com/gse/video/55251746/green-cubes-technology-highlights-lithium-safeflex-batteries-for-gse>. PosiGuard's lithium support is the counterexample <https://posicharge.com/products/posiguard/>.
- **Gap:** East Penn names 'battery to charger communication devices' without a product name <https://www.eastpennmanufacturing.com/wp-content/uploads/Fast-Charge-Flyer-1611.pdf>; Midac and Triathlon name monitoring modules without detail. These are open items for the BMID competitor list.
