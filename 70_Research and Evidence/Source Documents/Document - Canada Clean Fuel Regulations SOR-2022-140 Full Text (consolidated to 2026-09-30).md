# Document - Canada Clean Fuel Regulations SOR-2022-140 Full Text (consolidated to 2026-09-30)

## Definition

Full consolidated text of Canada's Clean Fuel Regulations (SOR-2022-140), current to 2026-09-30 and last amended 2024-09-30 per its cover.

## Provenance

- **Repo file:** Downloads/Clean Fuel Regulations.pdf (273 pages), added by the owner. Text was extracted from the file and read directly on 2026-10-18 (first-hand).
- **Source address:** https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-140/FullText.html (the wishlist row gave the publisher address; the exact download address was not recorded where this shows only a site root).
- **Evidence tier:** primary legal text, not covered by T1 to T5 in [[Landscape Evidence and Modeling Conventions]].

## Notes

- Definition of electric vehicle: a vehicle propelled by an electric motor whose source of electricity is a rechargeable battery charged from a source not on board; it includes plug-in hybrids. Forklifts are not named anywhere in the sections searched (text search for forklift and lift truck found nothing).
- Section 101: a charging-site host may create provisional compliance credits by displacing liquid fuel with electricity supplied to an electric vehicle of a class listed in the Specifications for Fuel LCA Model CI Calculations. Reference energy efficiency ratio is the Specifications value for the class or, by election, 2.5. This agrees with [[Document - Canada Clean Fuel Regulations Section 101 Charging Site Host Credits (Justice Laws)]].
- Consequence: whether a forklift is an eligible vehicle class depends on the Specifications document, which is not in the repo. The vendor FAQ that lists electric forklifts as an opportunity ([[Document - 3Degrees Canada Clean Fuel Regulations FAQ (modified 2026-01-22)]]) therefore remains unconfirmed by primary text (IB-266).
- Not read in full: the 273 pages were searched, not read; registration, verification and schedule provisions were not extracted.
