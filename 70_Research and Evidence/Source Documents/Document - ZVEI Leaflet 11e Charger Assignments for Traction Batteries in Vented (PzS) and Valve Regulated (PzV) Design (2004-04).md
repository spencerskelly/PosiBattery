# Document - ZVEI Leaflet 11e Charger Assignments for Traction Batteries in Vented (PzS) and Valve Regulated (PzV) Design (2004-04)

## Definition

Association leaflet (ZVEI, April 2004) on matching chargers to vented and valve-regulated traction batteries, with the German charging-characteristic notation.

- **Evidence tier:** T2, industry association leaflet, not a manufacturer sheet or a standard.

## Notes

- Charging-characteristic notation: W (taper), U (constant voltage), I (constant current), with suffixes a (automatic shut off) and 0 (automatic switch over), for example Wa, W0Wa, IUIa. DIN 41772 describes the characteristics; tolerances are plus or minus 2 percent on current and plus or minus 1 percent on voltage for I characteristics (DIN 41773) and plus or minus 0.05 volt per cell for W characteristics (DIN 41774).
- Reference values: battery nominal temperature 30 C and 80 percent depth of discharge; DIN reference end-of-charge voltage 2.65 V per cell; gassing voltage 2.4 V per cell at 30 C. Charging factor (charged Ah over discharged Ah) standard 1.2, for example 480 Ah into a 500 Ah battery discharged 80 percent; batteries with air mixing need about 1.07.
- Final charge current ranges shown in the diagrams include 12 to 14 A per 100 Ah and 1.0 to 1.4 A per 100 Ah (exact use per diagram not extracted).
- Assignment criteria: nominal voltage and capacity, battery technology, battery periphery (water topping, air mix, cooling), breaks or rest times, charging time, opportunity charge. Opportunity charges are partial charges and cannot replace regular full charges. Taper chargers do not control current, so current varies with mains voltage (a 5 percent mains change shown against the plus or minus 0.05 V per cell band).
