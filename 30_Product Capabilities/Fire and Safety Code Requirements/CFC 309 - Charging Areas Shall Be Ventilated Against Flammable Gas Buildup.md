# CFC 309 - Charging Areas Shall Be Ventilated Against Flammable Gas Buildup

## Definition

Under section 309.4 of the 2025 California Fire Code, battery-charging areas shall have approved ventilation to prevent a dangerous accumulation of flammable gases.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** The text names no chemistry. Whether a lithium-only charging area still needs it is the fire authority's reading; nothing read says lithium is exempt.
- **Conflict with the vault's existing need:** [[Charge Without a Ventilated Battery Room]] assumes lithium removes the ventilated-room need. This rule and the insurer ventilation ask (REQ-00073) still apply to lithium charging areas as read (C318). The original need text is not edited.
- Second-hand reading; see [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]].
