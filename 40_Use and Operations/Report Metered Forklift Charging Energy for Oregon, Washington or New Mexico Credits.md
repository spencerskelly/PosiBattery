# Report Metered Forklift Charging Energy for Oregon, Washington or New Mexico Credits

## Definition

A site owner or fleet manager collects metered energy for each charger or forklift, registers the metering point with the state program, and files quarterly reports so credits can be earned.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: analyst hypothesis, drafted 2026-10-17.** Steps are reasoned from [[Document - Oregon DEQ Forklift Transition Guidance (2023)]], [[Document - WAC 173-424-220 Cornell LII Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)]] and [[Document - New Mexico CTFP Roads to Revenue with Fleet Electrification (2026-07)]]; no customer procedure was read.
- Steps (analyst wording): choose the meter point (panel, charger or cable, forklift, or telematics in Oregon); document how the data is collected; register the equipment with the state; collect quarterly kWh without adjusting it (Oregon); file by the state's due dates; for rental forklifts, avoid double counting with the renter.
- Compare with the California activity [[Report Metered Forklift Charging Energy for LCFS Credits]], which applies a charger-efficiency adjustment for upstream meters (C291).
- **Not evidenced:** whether customers claim credits in these states, credit prices, and who performs this work.
