# Raymond Travel Speed Control

## Definition

Raymond configurable option that limits truck travel speed to a preset value.

## Notes

- Raymond's options sheet describes a configurable Travel Speed Control that limits truck travel speed to a preset value (the range is cut off in the retrieved text). Source: Raymond options sell sheet (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.johnstonequipment.com/-/media/raymond/literature/truck-literature/counterbalanced-trucks/raymond-stand-up-counterbalanced-options-sell-sheet.pdf>
- **Functions performed, with citations:**
  - [[Limit Truck Speed Automatically]] (V): <https://www.johnstonequipment.com/-/media/raymond/literature/truck-literature/counterbalanced-trucks/raymond-stand-up-counterbalanced-options-sell-sheet.pdf>
- The options sheet says the configurable option limits travel speed to a preset value between 1 and 6 mph when the forks exit free lift.
