# Single-Point Watering Manifold Assembly

## Definition

Battery-installed watering assembly that distributes water from one external connection to multiple cell-level shutoff valves.

## Notes

- The reusable architecture covers a common inlet, distribution tubing/manifold, and automatic cell-level shutoff devices.
- External carts, tanks, deionizers, pumps, and facility water infrastructure are outside this assembly unless explicitly modeled for a product.
