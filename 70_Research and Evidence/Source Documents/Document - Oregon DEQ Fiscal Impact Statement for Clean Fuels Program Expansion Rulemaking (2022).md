# Document - Oregon DEQ Fiscal Impact Statement for Clean Fuels Program Expansion Rulemaking (2022)

## Definition

Oregon DEQ's 2022 fiscal statement giving a $125 market credit price and about 220 registered entities.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. DEQ fiscal impact statement for the 2022 rulemaking (rules effective 2023-01-01).

- **Credit price:** the current market price was $125 per credit and the 2022 Credit Clearance Market maximum $230.43.
- **Participants:** about 220 entities registered in the program, about 60 of them regulated parties.
- **Electricity:** no value stated; no forklift content. Notes a proposed EER for airport electric ground service equipment.
- **Use:** a historical price point showing Oregon's fall from $125 (2022) to $47.98 (April 2025) and $80.51 (2025 cost report, C301).
