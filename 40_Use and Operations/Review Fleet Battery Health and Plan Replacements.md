# Review Fleet Battery Health and Plan Replacements

## Definition

A fleet manager reviews the age, state of health, usage and exceptions of every battery in the fleet and sets which batteries to service, rotate or replace and when.

## Notes

- Completion: each battery has a planned action or a stated reason to keep it in service.
- The state of health values depend on the method chosen; see [[SOC and SOH Methods Reference]].
- Primary context: [[Material-Handling Fleet Site]].
- Evidence for the steps is in [[Fleet Manager and Admin Digital Access Evidence]]; the steps below are analyst wording of what vendors and trade press describe, not an observed customer procedure.
