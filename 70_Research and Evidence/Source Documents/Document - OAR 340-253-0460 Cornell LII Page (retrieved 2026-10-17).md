# Document - OAR 340-253-0460 Cornell LII Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

Oregon's rule for applying for a custom EER, requiring operating data per vehicle.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. Cornell copy of the Oregon rule on EER-adjusted carbon intensity applications.

- **What it allows:** applications submitted under this rule are modified Tier 2 pathway applications for electric vehicles capable of full normal operation from onboard batteries or fuel cells. Oregon-based vehicle owners or operators and manufacturers may apply alone or jointly.
- **Data:** at least three months of typical operating data and at least 300 hours per vehicle; manufacturers may instead use duty-cycle testing with DEQ's written approval.
- **Review and use:** DEQ reviews completeness and accuracy; approvals go through a 30-day public comment; an approved value can be used only by its applicant.
- **Duties:** annual usage and energy data per vehicle; DEQ may require third-party verification and may modify values.
- **Forklifts:** not mentioned. Whether any forklift EER has been approved this way was not found (IB-268).
- **Currency:** notes list amendments through DEQ 2-2025 (effective 2025-01-09).
