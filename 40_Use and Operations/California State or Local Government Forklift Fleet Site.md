# California State or Local Government Forklift Fleet Site

## Definition

Operating context: a site in California where a state or local government entity owns, operates, leases or rents one or more large spark-ignition (LSI) forklifts. This is the one place where CARB's Zero-Emission Forklift rule is enforced today.

## Notes

- **Evidence:** compliance applies from 2026-01-01 ([[Document - CARB ZEF Government Fleets Overview Fact Sheet (2026-01)]], [[Document - CARB ZEF Brochure for State and Local Government Fleets (2026-04)]]). Excluded equipment (rough-terrain, vehicle-mounted and telescoping boom forklifts, pallet jacks, port and railyard forklifts) is listed in the fact sheet.
- **Customer types (analyst reasoning from the rule text):** state agencies, cities, counties, school districts and transit or utility departments that run warehouses or yards. Which of these customers exist for PosiCharge products is not evidenced.
- **Related contexts:** [[Material-Handling Fleet Site]] (general) and [[California Private Forklift Fleet Site]] (paused for private fleets).
- **Participants:** the compliance owner at a government fleet is not identified in any source; [[Fleet Operations Manager]] is used as a stand-in until the owner says who it is.
- Register: [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]].
