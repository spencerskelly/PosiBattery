# Stryten EHF Charger

## Definition

Stryten industrial charger family for light to heavy duty where batteries are exchanged at the end of each shift.

## Notes

- Stryten says the EHF industrial chargers cover light-duty to heavy-duty uses with a wide range of options, for applications that typically have more than one battery per truck with exchange at the end of each work shift. Source: Stryten lineup sheet (09/2024) (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://og.mhi.org/media/members/14502/133723547947804003.pdf>
