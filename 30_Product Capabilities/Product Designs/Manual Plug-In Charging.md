# Manual Plug-In Charging

## Definition

A person plugs a charger connector into a battery connector or truck inlet by hand.

## Notes

- Contrast case for the hands-free designs; where the plug goes is a separate axis ([[Truck Charging Port]], [[Battery-Direct Charge Connection]], [[Battery Onboard Charger]]). Connector families are in [[Charger-Vehicle Interface Register]].
