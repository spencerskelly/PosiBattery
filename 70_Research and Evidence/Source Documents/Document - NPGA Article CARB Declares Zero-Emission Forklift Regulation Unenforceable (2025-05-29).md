# Document - NPGA Article CARB Declares Zero-Emission Forklift Regulation Unenforceable (2025-05-29)

## Definition

NPGA's news item reporting the WPGA and CARB settlement over the Zero-Emission Forklift regulation.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic. National Propane Gas Association news item dated 2025-05-29; it reports the WPGA settlement "earlier this week" without a day.

- The headline says CARB declared the rule unenforceable; the article body describes a negotiated non-enforcement settlement, not a court ruling or a federal finding.
- CARB will not enforce the ZEF regulation for now; any future action would consider adjusting deadlines so fleets can keep operating propane forklifts of model year 2025 or later.
- Manufacturers may keep producing affected propane forklifts for sale in California. The article says the original rule would have banned new propane forklift sales from 2026.
- It does not mention EPA authorization or a waiver, and does not address government fleets, private businesses or dealers separately. WPGA's president is quoted as calling the rule costly at best and infeasible at worst.
- Use: shows how the trade press framed the settlement; the headline overstates what CARB's own notices say (C279).
