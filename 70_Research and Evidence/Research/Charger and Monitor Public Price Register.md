# Charger and Monitor Public Price Register

## Definition

Public price points for forklift battery chargers, battery monitors and charger installation, collected 2026-10-18 from public pages only. Purpose: give the product and business notes a price floor and an installed-cost basis, and show where public prices do not exist. No PosiCharge price or cost is in this note. Every price is a listing seen once, undated, through a summarizing fetch tool [S].

## Notes

### Bottom line

1. **Public charger prices exist only for small units.** Seven chargers on the Crown parts store run $307 to $992 (12 to 36 V, on-board or portable); the highest-power listings found are 80 V / 100 A lithium chargers (about 8 kW at nominal voltage) at GBP 2,300 and 9,850 PLN, both ex tax, and a 48 V / 100 A (about 4.8 kW) unit at $2,869. Chargers for Class 1 trucks at higher power, and the multi-charger systems PosiCharge-type products serve, show no public price.
2. **The only per-truck installed-cost figure is a regulator's, and it is disputed.** CARB staff used $3,375 (impact assessment, modeled as a Level 2 car charger install) and $3,650 per forklift (as reported by trade press); industry says real facility upgrades run to "hundreds of thousands to millions" for some sites (C356).
3. **No public price exists for a battery monitor, BMID or fleet-monitoring subscription.** The vault's $60 per battery per year is a placeholder in [[PosiCharge Roadmap and Business Plan 2027-2030]], not a market price.
4. **Rebates are the best public price signal for chargers.** They are capped well below the price points above, so they are partial offsets, not costs (Table C).

### What is not public

- Prices for chargers above about 8 kW, for charger banks, and for chargers with load management or metering.
- Monitor, BMID and gateway hardware prices, and any per-battery or per-truck software fee.
- Installed cost by site type (new building, retrofit, cold storage) and utility make-ready charges.
- Sold prices: the one auction lot found (Hawker EnerSys TE 80 V / 100 A, Belgium) showed no bid; the Canadian tender for a forklift and charger (NRC, closing 2025-12-22) states no price.

### Sources read that held no usable price

Hyster Clean Power forklift electrification FAQ (no dollar figures, TCO stated qualitatively); Racklify charger guide (none). Not repeated in future rounds.
