# Power Designers REVOLUTION X

## Definition

Power Designers modular high-frequency charger series for conventional, opportunity and fast charging, multi-voltage when paired with PowerTrac.

## Notes

- The overview lists 1.3 kW multi-voltage power modules combined to make 3.9 to 31.2 kW chargers, 480 V AC three-phase input, programming for conventional (17 percent start rate), opportunity (25 percent) or fast (40 percent) charging, and operation continuing if a module fails. Source: REVOLUTION series overview (2017, via Carolina Handling) (T3 (dealer-hosted, dated)), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.carolinahandling.com/-/media/dealers/carolina-handling/literature/battery-chargers/ch-rev-series_overview_01-2017.pdf>
- The series is described with peak efficiency greater than 93 percent, and with the PowerTrac data logger the charger can be multi-voltage (24/36/48) and adapt to battery voltage and amp-hour capacity. Source: Carolina Handling product page and REVOLUTION fast charger sheet (T3), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.carolinahandling.com/-/media/dealers/carolina-handling/literature/battery-chargers/ch-rev-f_revolution-fast-charger.pdf>
- The vendor's current overview says REVOLUTION covers 75 to 700 Ah batteries, three-phase 208, 240 and 480 V input, efficiency above 92 percent throughout the charge on the REVOLUTION X page, compatibility with lithium-ion, lead-acid and other batteries, a built-in desulfation cycle, and use with PowerTrac 3 or SP+. Source: Power Designers REVOLUTION overview and REVOLUTION X page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.powerdesignerssibex.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/PDS-REV_Revolution_Series_Overview_Modular_Battery_Charger.pdf>
- **Conflict-visible note (C35):** the 2017 dealer-hosted overview above says peak efficiency above 93 percent; the REVOLUTION X page says above 92 percent. Possibly different generations; not resolved.
