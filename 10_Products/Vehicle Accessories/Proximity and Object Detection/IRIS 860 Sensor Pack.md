# IRIS 860 Sensor Pack

## Definition

Sensor pack that recognizes reflective tape on safety gear and warns the forklift operator, with a detection zone of up to 8 m.

## Notes

**Summary:**
SEEN Safety IRIS 860 infrared-laser sensor that detects retroreflective tape on high-visibility PPE and audibly warns driver and pedestrian.

**Marketed features:**
- Detects Class II high-visibility apparel from 0.8 to 8 m (26 ft); 60 x 45 degree field of view
- 94 dB alert warns driver and pedestrian simultaneously; pre-alert option
- Works in darkness, sun glare, rain and dust; IP67; -20 to +60 C
- Configurable via free setup app; reverse-only and direction-dependent modes
- Class 1 laser; self-check diagnostics; accessory output for machine slowdown
- Pack SI-120: sensor, 4 m cable, stainless bracket, screws; 2-year warranty
