# Access Control Group SMARTIE

## Definition

Access SMARTIE Bluetooth electrolyte level and temperature sensor for flooded batteries with on-demand watering lists.

## Notes

- Logs low-water and over-temperature alarms; Android app; rugged tablet list of batteries needing water; AssetPro 360 cloud; two-year warranty; 2016 item, current availability unchecked. Whether it replaces or relates to [[Access Control Group CellVue]] / [[Access Control Group CellTrac]] is not stated.
