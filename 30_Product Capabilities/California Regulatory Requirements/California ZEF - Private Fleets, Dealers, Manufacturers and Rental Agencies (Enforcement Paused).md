# California ZEF - Private Fleets, Dealers, Manufacturers and Rental Agencies (Enforcement Paused)

## Definition

CARB adopted the ZEF rule for fleet operators, rental agencies, dealers and manufacturers, and has said staff will not enforce it against privately owned or operated fleets until EPA authorizes it or finds authorization unnecessary.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It binds the party named in the definition, not a charger. Product consequences are the derived requirements REQ-00051 to REQ-00053 (analyst-proposed).
- Second-hand reading; see C-items in [[Battery Product Landscape Conflicts and Open Questions]] and [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]].
- **Status:** paused, not repealed. Voluntary reporting stays open. CARB had not requested EPA authorization in the notices read.
- The private-fleet phase-out terms and the 2026 sales prohibition are in the 2024 adoption text ([[Document - CARB News Release Forklifts to Become Cleaner and Less Polluting (2024)]]) and were not read in full; they are not restated here (IB-245).
- Conflicts: C279 to C282, C285.
