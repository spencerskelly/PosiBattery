# Notify Forklift Operator of Annual Credits and Estimated Revenue

## Definition

A forklift fleet owner that generates Washington Clean Fuel Standard credits for electricity the operator uses sends the operator a yearly written notice stating that the owner is generating the credit, the estimated annual credits and the estimated credit revenue.

## Notes

- **Evidence status: analyst hypothesis, drafted 2026-10-17.** The obligation is in the official text ([[Document - WAC 173-424-220 Washington Legislature Certified Copy (2025-11-12)]]); how customers do it was not found.
- The estimate uses the previous year's credit price. The text also requires an operator that holds the credit rights to send the same notice to the owner, and a designated credit generator to report total credit revenue for the previous year to the first fuel reporting entity.
- Inputs needed (analyst reasoning): kWh per forklift or per site for the year, the credit factors and the last year's price. Whether any PosiCharge data feeds this was not evidenced (IB-258).
- **Not evidenced:** who writes the notice, how often it is skipped, and whether operators are separate companies from owners (leased trucks and third-party logistics sites).
