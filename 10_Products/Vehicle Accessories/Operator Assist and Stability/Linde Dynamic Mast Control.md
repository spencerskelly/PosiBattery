# Linde Dynamic Mast Control

## Definition

Linde system for reach trucks whose electric thrust drive counters mast oscillation and deflection at height.

## Notes

**Summary:**
Linde reach-truck assistance system whose electric thrust drive counteracts mast oscillation and deflection at height.

**Marketed features:**
- Reduces mast oscillation and deflection for greater safety
- Fast, precise counter-movements by the electric push drive
- Faster load handling with less waiting time
- Reduces damage to racking, loads and vehicles
