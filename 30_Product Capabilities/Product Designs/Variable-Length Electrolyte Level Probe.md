# Variable-Length Electrolyte Level Probe

## Definition

Electrolyte-level sensing using a probe whose effective length is adjustable to suit the target cell and required level threshold.

## Notes

- [[Power Designers PowerTrac 3]] explicitly specifies a standard electrolyte sensor with a variable-length probe.
- The published specification establishes the adjustable probe form but does not state whether sensing is conductive, capacitive, or another principle.
- This Design therefore captures the verified physical implementation without asserting an electrical sensing method.
