# California LCFS - Forklift Volumes Are Reported by Lift Capacity Class

## Definition

From Q3 2025 reporting, forklift charging is reported under one of two application types, lift capacity under 12,000 lb or 12,000 lb and over; if the reporting entity cannot distinguish lift capacities for charging events, all volumes must be reported under the lowest-credit type, under 12,000 lb.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** The class sets the energy economy ratio and therefore the credit amount: 2.4 for under 12,000 lb and 3.8 for 12,000 lb and over in the proposed text ([[Document - CARB LCFS Second 15-Day Changes Attachment A-1 Proposed Regulation Order (after 2024-08-12)]]); the adopted values are unread (C296).
- **Read first-hand:** [[Document - CARB LCFS Guidance 25-01 Forklift Fueling Reporting (2025-10)]], fuel application section. The reporting entity must also report the number of electric forklifts in the fleet ([[Document - CARB LCFS Second 15-Day Changes Attachment A-1 Proposed Regulation Order (after 2024-08-12)]], proposed 95491(d)(3)(E)).
- Money effect (analyst calculation, see [[Forklift Clean Fuel Credit Business Case]]): reporting a 12,000 lb or heavier truck's charging energy under the lighter class would lose about 47 percent of that energy's credit value (0.458 versus 0.863 credits per MWh, using benchmark 80.17 and grid CI 65.07 from the proposed text, assumptions in the business case note).
- Hydrogen fuel cell forklifts follow the same two-class rule in the guidance.
