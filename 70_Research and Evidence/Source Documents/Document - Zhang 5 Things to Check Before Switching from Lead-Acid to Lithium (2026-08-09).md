## Notes

**Content recorded (as stated by the source, not independently verified unless marked):**
- Replacement is not drop-in: check peak discharge current, voltage stability under load, internal resistance and low-temperature starting.
- Charging systems differ: alternators designed for lead-acid can exceed a lithium battery's safe charge current, so charge-current limitation, BMS protection strategy and vehicle communication matter.
- Charging below 0 C needs special protection to avoid lithium plating; ask about heating, low-temperature charge management, pre-heat time and available capacity when cold.
- A BMS should monitor cell voltage, temperature, charge and discharge current, balancing, over-charge, over-discharge and short-circuit protection; fleets also need CAN or J1939, remote monitoring, fault diagnosis and health tracking.
- Evaluate testing standards, production quality control, warranty and after-sales support, not only price.
