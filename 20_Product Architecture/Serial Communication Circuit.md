# Serial Communication Circuit

## Definition

Wired serial communication circuit using a controller serial peripheral and an external physical-layer transceiver where required.

## Notes

- RS-232 and RS-485 are representative physical-layer realizations. The generic circuit does not assert that both are present.
- UART/TTL-level proprietary serial may also be implemented without an RS-232/RS-485 transceiver.
