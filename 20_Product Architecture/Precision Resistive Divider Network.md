# Precision Resistive Divider Network

## Definition

High-value precision resistor network that scales traction-battery voltage into the safe input range of measurement electronics.

## Notes

- May be implemented with discrete series resistors, matched networks, or multiple voltage-rated elements.
- Values, tolerance, temperature coefficient, power rating, and voltage rating remain product-specific.
