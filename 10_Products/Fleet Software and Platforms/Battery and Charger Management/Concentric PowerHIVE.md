# Concentric PowerHIVE

## Definition

Concentric PowerHIVE: autonomous forklift battery management that swaps a standardized lithium-ion battery in about two minutes with centralized charging.

## Notes

- Over 250,000 swaps reported; the battery weighs about 3,000 lb; the mechanism, voltage and trucks supported are not stated. Source: <https://chargedevs.com/newswire/concentrics-autonomous-forklift-battery-management-system-has-performed-250000-two-minute-battery-swaps/> (T2).
