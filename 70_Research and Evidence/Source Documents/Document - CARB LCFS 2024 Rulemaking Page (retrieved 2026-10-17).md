# Document - CARB LCFS 2024 Rulemaking Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

CARB's rulemaking page, which in the tool's copy shows final approval as not reached, contradicting other sources.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. CARB's rulemaking page for the 2024 LCFS amendments.

- **As the fetch tool read it:** 'Final Approval / OAL Action: this stage has not yet been reached', no adopted regulations listed, FSOR 'upon its completion'. This conflicts with four other sources that report OAL approval on 2025-06-27 and effect on 2025-07-01 (C305). The tool's copy may be stale.
- **Related CARB page:** the LCFS Regulation page lists an 'unofficial electronic version of complete regulation effective July 1, 2025' and 'Attachment A-1 unofficial version of the Final Regulation Order'. The PDF is at <https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2025-07/2025_lcfs_fro_oal-approved_unofficial_07012025.pdf>; the fetch tool returned only its first 93,000 characters (to section 95482), and the shell is blocked from CARB, so the EER and benchmark tables could not be read (IB-242).
