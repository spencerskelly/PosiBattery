# Thermistor Temperature Sensor

## Definition

Thermistor sensing element whose resistance varies with temperature and is read by a temperature-measurement circuit.

## Notes

- PosiCharge publicly identifies an electrolyte-immersed thermistor in the BMID, so a thermistor sensing element is verified at the technology level.
- Exact thermistor type, resistance curve, package, supplier, and part number are not established by the public source.
