# California LCFS - Facility Owner Registers Charging Locations and Reports as First Reporting Entity

## Definition

The owner of a facility or location where electric forklifts charge is the first fuel reporting entity (since 2025-07-01) and shall register each charging location as fueling supply equipment and report its forklift charging electricity.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** It binds the party named in the definition, not a charger. Product consequences are the derived requirements REQ-00051 to REQ-00053 (analyst-proposed).
- Second-hand reading; see C-items in [[Battery Product Landscape Conflicts and Open Questions]] and [[California Forklift Electrification Regulations Register]].
- **Status:** applies to parties who claim credits. Registration needs an Admin role with Signatory Authority in CARB's reporting tool (details in Guidance 19-04, not read).
- Open: fleet-owned forklifts charging at a customer site are not addressed (confirm with CARB); the Cornell copy of the regulation lets the fleet owner or designee report (C283).
