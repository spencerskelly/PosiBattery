# Document - Xpansiv April 2025 CARB Regulation Update on LCFS Amendments (2025-04-09)

## Definition

An April 2025 market commentary that dates the forklift metering requirement to 2026-01-01 in the proposed LCFS amendments.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic. Xpansiv blog post dated 2025-04-09, no named author. The old srectrade.com address redirects here. It describes the proposed amendments before adoption.

- It states California will require metering for all material handling equipment, including forklifts, from 2026-01-01, and says there was no change on this point in the latest update.
- Metering accuracy is said to be verified by third parties; verification requirements for electricity start in 2026.
- Process status at the time: CARB published the revised text on 4 April (2025), comments were due 21 April, and the Office of Administrative Law review was still pending. No forklift energy economy ratio is given.
- Use: shows the metering date was already in the proposed text in April 2025, before the amendments took effect on 2025-07-01.
