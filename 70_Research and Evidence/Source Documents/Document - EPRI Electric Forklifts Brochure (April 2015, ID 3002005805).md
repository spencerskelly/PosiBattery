**Update 2026-10-18: file now in the repo and read first-hand** (Downloads/epri_2015.pdf, a short brochure):

- **Voltage by truck class (EPRI wording):** most Class 1 electric trucks operate at 36 V, 48 V or 80 V (typical lift 3,000 to 12,000 lb); Class 2 narrow-aisle at 24, 36 or 48 V; Class 3 at 12 or 24 V. This is consistent with the owner's statement that 80 V is a reasonable maximum for most packs ([[SOC and SOH Decision Criteria]]), but it gives no installed-base shares and does not mention 96 V.
- **Energy:** typical input demand 7.6 to 30 kW; typical annual energy 12,960 to 25,932 kWh; a truck is typically in use about 50 percent of the time, so most modern trucks can run two 8-hour shifts on one battery and charger.
