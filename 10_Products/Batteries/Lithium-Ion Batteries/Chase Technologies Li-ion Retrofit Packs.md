# Chase Technologies Li-ion Retrofit Packs

## Definition

Chase Technologies modular lithium-ion packs sized to fit existing forklift battery tanks, with 90-minute full charge.

## Notes

- Working name analyst-given (`unnamed-in-source`): the article names no model. Packs are built to fit the tank of any brand if its size is known; standard 100-200 A charger; full charge about 90 min, 50% in about 30 min; five-year warranty; cold-store variant to -20 C with insulation and heater; built-in control system; SIM remote monitoring on newer units. Voltage and capacity not stated. Source: <https://www.crown.co.za/images/Latest_news_2020/mechchem-africa/Li-ion_batteries_the_cost-effective_retrofit.pdf> (T2, second-hand).
