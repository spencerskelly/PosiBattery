# Document - Industrial Monitor Direct SB 175 to SB 350 Article

## Definition

Industrial Monitor Direct article on adapting SB 175 to SB 350 connectors.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

- SB 175 and SB 350 are rated 600 V AC/DC; ratings are contact ratings at 40 C ambient, derating 15% per 10 C above; any SB mates with another of the same voltage colour code regardless of current rating; colour code: yellow 12 V, orange 18 V, red 24 V, grey 36 V, blue 48 V, green 72 V; polarity reversal is the most common failure.
