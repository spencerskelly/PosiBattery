# Document - CARB ZEF Implementation Update Advisory (2025-05-29)

## Definition

CARB's May 2025 notice that it will not enforce the Zero-Emission Forklift regulation against private fleets until EPA authorizes it or finds authorization unnecessary.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic (Knowledge Base Backlog, parking-lot item on CARB rules). CARB notice headed as an implementation update and dated 2025-05-29 in the text read.

- **Rule identity:** CARB adopted the Zero-Emission Forklift (ZEF) regulation in June 2024; the Office of Administrative Law approved it on 2024-09-16. The notice cites California Code of Regulations title 13, sections 3000 to 3011. It targets large spark-ignition (LSI) forklifts and covers fleet operators, rental agencies, dealers and manufacturers.
- **Enforcement pause (private fleets):** until U.S. EPA authorizes the regulation or determines authorization is unnecessary, CARB staff will not enforce it against privately owned or operated fleets, including for acts or omissions before authorization.
- **Sales and rentals:** CARB will not stop manufacturers, dealers or rental agencies from producing, selling or renting LSI forklifts in California, provided the forklifts meet all other applicable federal and state rules.
- **Reporting:** CARB keeps the online reporting system open for voluntary reporting and encourages fleets to report and comply voluntarily until authorization is granted.
- **Authorization status:** CARB states it has not yet requested authorization from EPA under Clean Air Act section 209(e)(2)(A) and will publish a notice if authorization is granted.
- **What the notice does not say:** as summarized, it addresses privately owned or operated fleets only and does not say how the pause applies to state and local government fleets (the January 2026 fact sheet says the pause does not apply to them: [[Document - CARB ZEF Government Fleets Overview Fact Sheet (2026-01)]]). It gives no end date and does not mention the WPGA lawsuit settlement.
- **Conflict:** the 2024 adoption news release says manufacturers could not produce or sell Class IV and V LSI forklifts for California use from 2026; this notice says CARB will not stop that. Logged as C280 (the later notice does not repeal the rule; it pauses enforcement).
