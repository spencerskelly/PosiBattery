# Godrej Lithium-Ion Forklift Battery

## Definition

Godrej lithium-ion forklift battery with a Godrej-designed battery management system and cells developed with Log9 Materials.

## Notes

- Godrej says the lithium-ion packs provide 5,000 charge cycles against 1,200 for lead-acid, charge from 20 to 80 percent in 2.5 hours against 6, use 30 percent less energy, give up to 15 percent more run time on 2 to 3 t forklifts, are modular, and use an Indian-designed battery management system with cells 30 percent localized, developed with Log9 Materials. Source: Godrej release and Equipment India (T1/T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.godrejenterprises.com/newsroom/press-releases/godrej-and-boyce-launches-india-s-first-lithium-ion-powered-forklift-truck-with-fully-indigenous-battery-management-system>
