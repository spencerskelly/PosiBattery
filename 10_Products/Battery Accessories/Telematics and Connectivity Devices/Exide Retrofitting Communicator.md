# Exide Retrofitting Communicator

## Definition

Optional communicator installed inside an Exide Sonnenschein Lithium battery that shows battery information on the truck's existing displays.

## Notes

- The sheet says the optional Retrofitting Communicator installs inside the battery, shows battery information on the truck's existing displays and state-of-charge indicator so no external display is needed, and enables built-in low-voltage safety features such as disabling the fork lift function. Source: Exide GNB Sonnenschein Lithium and GNB Fleet sheet (T1, read through a summarizing tool), retrieved 2026-10-13. <https://exidegroup.com/es/sites/default/files/2020-11/GNB_MP_Sonnenschein_Lithium_MHB_GNB_Fleet_EN.pdf>
- **Not stated:** interface to the truck (CAN or other), part number, compatible trucks, whether the low-voltage action is truck-side or battery-side (IB-176).
- **Functions performed, with citations:**
  - [[Display Battery Status to Operator]] (V, partial): shows battery information on the truck's displays. <https://exidegroup.com/es/sites/default/files/2020-11/GNB_MP_Sonnenschein_Lithium_MHB_GNB_Fleet_EN.pdf>
- **Design characteristics, with citations:**
  - [[Truck Battery Discharge Interlock]] (V, partial): low-voltage safety such as disabling the fork lift function; the mechanism is not described. <https://exidegroup.com/es/sites/default/files/2020-11/GNB_MP_Sonnenschein_Lithium_MHB_GNB_Fleet_EN.pdf>
