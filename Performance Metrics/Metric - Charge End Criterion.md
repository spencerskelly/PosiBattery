# Metric - Charge End Criterion

## Definition

Charge End Criterion: rule for when a charge is finished

## Notes

- **Code and class:** BM14; battery metric. Unit or format: rule for when a charge is finished.
- **Comparability rule:** Rules differ by maker (time at constant readings, a cell voltage, a current drop); compare the rule, not a single number. See the comparison note for makers without a product note.
- **Direction:** not ranked.
- **Values on file (as stated in each product note; n/s means not stated):**
  - [[HAWKER Perfect Plus Battery]]: finished when the electrolyte specific gravity and the battery voltage have stayed constant for 2 hours
  - [[Stryten M-Series T310 Battery]]: freshening charge until specific gravity shows no increase for three readings (T310-FP manual)
- **Source rule:** the product note holds the source URL for each value; this note copies the value for comparison. If they differ, the product note wins and this note is fixed.
- **Gaps and to-do:** Add East Penn finish rates by cell size (tables not extractable), Stryten and HOPPECKE rules; the Exide Industries manual gives a cell voltage of 2.65 V per cell or a dV/dt cut-off but its page is gone; see [[Flooded Lead-Acid Charge Profile Comparison]].
