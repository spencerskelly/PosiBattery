## Notes

**How it was read.** Through a fetch tool that returns a model-written summary, not the raw text, on 2026-10-13; values were not checked against the raw file (IB-173 to IB-178).

- Claims: 24 percent more power per cubic meter, up to 33 percent more pallet movements per charge, up to 10 percent better total cost of ownership than other lithium-ion technologies, up to 52 extra days of uptime a year with point-of-use charging. Savings figures for a 25-vehicle fleet are given as EUR 500,000, EUR 1,125,000 and "over" EUR 1,000,000 in different places (not reconciled).
- Deep discharge protection and state-of-charge reporting; DIN tray dimensions and weights for 1:1 lead-acid replacement; integrated monitoring with automatic reports and a traffic-light system.
- Charger: "2100HP", "designed for GNB batteries and GNB Fleet ready", full recharge in up to one hour, weatherproof and dustproof, point-of-use installation; the page does not explain the name.
- Retrofitting Communicator (optional): installed inside the battery, shows battery information on the truck's existing displays and state-of-charge indicator, enables built-in low-voltage safety features such as disabling the fork lift function.
- GNB Fleet tiers from battery rotation to multi-site management: monitors battery and charger vital parameters, sends automated alarm emails, lets batteries and chargers request service, secure cloud access, data analysis for energy management.
- Not stated: voltages, capacities, cycle life, warranty, CAN, product certifications.
