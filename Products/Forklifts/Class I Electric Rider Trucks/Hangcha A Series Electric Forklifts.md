# Hangcha A Series Electric Forklifts

## Definition

Hangcha A series electric forklifts (1.0 to 3.5 t) with LiFePO4 batteries and an optional sideways battery change.

## Notes

- Hangcha's A series brochure (1.0 to 3.5 t) lists an AC drive motor, multi-function dashboard, soft landing system, reversing beeper, an optional sideways battery change with a pallet truck or trolley, and Li-ion (LiFePO4) batteries.
