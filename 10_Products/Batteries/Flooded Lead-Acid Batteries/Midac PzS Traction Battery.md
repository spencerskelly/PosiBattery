# Midac PzS Traction Battery

## Definition

Midac tubular plate traction battery with optional watering, electrolyte circulation and monitoring module.

## Notes

- A dealer says Midac PzS cells use tubular plate technology for trucks, tow tractors and cleaning machines, with Aquamatic, EUW electrolyte circulation, a battery monitoring module and end leads as optional accessories. Source: Batterie Siems dealer page (T3), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://www.batterie-siems.de/en-gb/2-pzs-280-2mdl140>
- **Design characteristics, with citations:**
  - [[Tubular Plate Construction]] (V): <https://www.batterie-siems.de/en-gb/2-pzs-280-2mdl140>

- **Update 2026-10-13 (Midac's own catalogues, read through a summary):** Midac's catalogue names the DIN range MDL (2 V cells, C5 100 to 1,550 Ah, design life 1,500+ cycles VLA / 1,200+ VRLA) and the BS range MBS ([[Midac MBS Traction Battery]]); this PzS note likely corresponds to MDL, which is the analyst's mapping and not stated by Midac. Options in the catalogue: single-point watering, air mixing ([[Midac Air-Tech Air-Mixing System]]), [[Midac Electrolyte Level Sensor]], welded inter-cell connections, optional [[MIDATRON BMU]]; compatible chargers [[MIDATRON MTB Charger]], [[MIDATRON MTT Charger]], [[MIDATRON HF Charger]]. Midac's other lines: [[Midac MLM Battery]], [[Midac MLMx Battery]], [[Midac MHE Battery]], [[Midac MDLblock Battery]], [[Midac FORCEblock Battery]], [[Midac LFPblock Battery]], [[Midac Li-Ion Battery]]. Sources: <https://midacbatteries.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/CAT-MDL-MBS_MOTIVE_EN_IT-02_2023-5011742-0312-00.pdf>; <https://midacbatteries.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/1_CAT-INTEGRATED-SOLUTIONS_MOTIVE_EN_IT_DE_FR-01_2022-5011820-0967_21.pdf>
- **Conflict-visible (C228):** the dealer page names Aquamatic and EUW options and end leads; Midac's catalogues name single-point watering, Air-Tech air mixing and an electrolyte level sensor, and do not mention Aquamatic, EUW or end leads.
- **Relationship flagged (C229):** this note carries `partOf` the EUW circulation system, which says a battery is part of an accessory; no source says so.
