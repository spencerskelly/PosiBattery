## Notes

**Content recorded (as stated by the source, not independently verified unless marked):**
- Parked batteries still draw standby power for BMS, display, communication or GPS; at low SOC this can reach BMS low-voltage protection, so storage needs periodic checks. Storage SOC should follow the manufacturer's range rather than 100 percent.
- High temperature with high SOC accelerates ageing; charging graphite-anode cells at low temperature risks lithium plating and irreversible capacity loss. Cold-storage packs may need multiple temperature sensors, current limits by temperature and heating.
- Opportunity charging still needs matched charger, validated parameters and no BMS bypass; do not charge outside the permitted temperature range or with active alarms.
- BMS low-voltage protection is a last line of protection, not a normal operating point; at low SOC cell differences and voltage sag under heavy load grow.
- Inspect connections, cables, terminals, discoloration, BMS alarms, cell-voltage consistency, temperature-sensor readings and enclosure; with CAN, RS485 or Bluetooth, operating data can show early abnormal behavior.
- Says 'maintenance-free' is misleading: lithium removes much routine maintenance but still needs battery management.
