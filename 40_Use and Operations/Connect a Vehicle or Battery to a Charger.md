# Connect a Vehicle or Battery to a Charger

## Definition

The operator connects a vehicle or battery to an appropriate charger so charging can begin safely.

## Notes

- Primary contexts: [[Centralized Battery Room and Charging Area]] and [[Distributed and Opportunity Charging Area]].
- Trigger: the vehicle or battery requires charging.
- Completion: a valid charging connection is established and charging can begin without a blocking condition.
- Product-specific connection, inspection, and disconnect instructions remain in the applicable product documentation and are not generalized here.
