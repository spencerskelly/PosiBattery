# Document - 17 CCR 95491 Cornell LII Page (retrieved 2026-10-17)

## Definition

A secondary-host copy of the LCFS reporting section with the forklift electricity provision and its three reporting routes.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added for the regulatory-driver topic. Legal Information Institute copy of California Code of Regulations title 17, section 95491 (LCFS fuel transactions and compliance reporting). The currency of the copy is not shown; it may predate the amendments effective 2025-07-01 (C283).

- **Forklift provision, subsection (d)(3)(E):** the quantity of electricity used by electric forklifts is reported in kWh per fueling supply equipment with a certified fuel pathway code; the summary names the transaction type once as "EV Forklifts Fueling" and once as "Forklift Electricity Fueling".
- **Three routes as summarized:** (1) a fleet owner or its designee that generates credits reports metered electricity per FSE, measured as set out in section 95483.2(b); (2) the reporting entity estimates with a CARB-approved methodology and also reports the number of electric forklifts used to generate credits; (3) when a utility claims forklift credits, CARB staff calculate the electricity supplied to forklifts in its territory, exempt from the quarterly deadlines.
- **General rules:** reports go through the LRT-CBTS with an accuracy attestation and digital signature (subsection (a)); quarterly data is uploaded within 45 days after quarter end and quarterly reports are due 30 June, 30 September, 31 December and 31 March, with annual compliance reports due 30 April (subsection (b)); corrections need Executive Officer approval and credits cannot be claimed retroactively for a closed quarter (subsection (h)).
- **Differences from CARB's October 2025 guidance:** the estimation route has no end date here and the reporting entity is the fleet owner or designee; the guidance ends estimation with Q1 2026 reports and names the facility or location owner as first reporting entity from 2025-07-01 ([[Document - CARB LCFS Guidance 25-01 Forklift Fueling Reporting (2025-10)]]).
