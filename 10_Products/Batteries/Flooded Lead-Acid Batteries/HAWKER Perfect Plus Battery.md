# HAWKER Perfect Plus Battery

## Definition

EnerSys HAWKER Perfect Plus flooded lead-acid traction battery with positive tubular plates (PzS and PzB), with optional Aquamatic water refilling, electrolyte circulation and Wi-iQ monitoring.

## Notes

- EnerSys' owner's manual (EMEA-EN-OM-PP-1124, 2024) gives for the Perfect Plus PzS/PzB: nominal voltage 2.0 V times the number of cells, capacity C5 (5 h discharge current) on the type plate, nominal electrolyte specific gravity 1.29 kg/l reached within the first 10 cycles, rated temperature 30 C, positive tubular plates; options are the Aquamatic water refilling system (water pressure 0.2 to 0.6 bar, water conductance up to 30 uS/cm, at most 20 cells in series), an electrolyte circulation system with an air pump and a stated charge factor of 1.07, and the Wi-iQ monitoring device. Source: EnerSys Perfect Plus owner's manual (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://enersys.com/4a6cd5/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/hawker/perfect-plus/emea/hawker-perfect-plus-instruction-for-use-english.pdf>
- **Charge and discharge rules in the manual:** see [[Flooded Lead-Acid Charge Profile Comparison]]; the manual says the battery must be charged after every discharge and never left discharged.

- The manual says: charge with direct current only, start only if the electrolyte is below 45 C and at least 10 C (it rises about 10 C during charge), a charge is finished when the specific gravity and the battery voltage stay constant for 2 hours, equalizing charges follow deep discharges, repeated incomplete recharges and IU-characteristic charges at no more than 5 A per 100 Ah, 55 C is the upper temperature limit, avoid operating discharges beyond 80 percent of rated capacity (1.14 kg/l at 30 C), the specific gravity correction is -0.0007 kg/l per C, and for storage either a monthly equalizing charge or float charging at 2.27 V per cell. Source: EnerSys Perfect Plus owner's manual (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://enersys.com/4a6cd5/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/hawker/perfect-plus/emea/hawker-perfect-plus-instruction-for-use-english.pdf>
