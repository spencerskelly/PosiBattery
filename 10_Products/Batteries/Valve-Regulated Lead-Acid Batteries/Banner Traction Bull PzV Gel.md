# Banner Traction Bull PzV Gel

## Definition

Banner Traction Bull PzV/PzVB maintenance-free gel traction battery.

## Notes

- Banner's brochure describes the Traction Bull PzV/PzVB as a maintenance-free gel technology drive battery; the Bloc DB is a sealed valve-regulated gel battery and the Bloc range also offers AGM Deep Cycle. Source: Banner Energy Solutions brochure (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://bannerbatterien.com/energysolutions/upload/files/FolderAGS_GB.pdf>
- **Not stated in the retrieved text:** voltages, capacities, cycle life and warranty.
- **Design characteristics, with citations:**
  - [[Gel Electrolyte]] (V): <https://bannerbatterien.com/energysolutions/upload/files/FolderAGS_GB.pdf>

- **Update 2026-10-13 (folder read through a summary):** 134 to 567 Ah (C5 per positive plate at 30 C), types 2 PzVB 134 to 7 PzVB 567, bolted BS cells 158 mm wide, positive tubular plates, solid gel electrolyte, no topping up for life, EN 60254-2 / IEC 254-2. Options: battery controller, cold store version, Ex-protection (same three as the PzS cells; [[Banner Traction Bull Battery Controller]]). Voltage, cycle life, warranty and charging data are not in the retrieved text (IB-187). Source: <https://www.bannerbatterien.com/energysolutions/upload/files/FolderTractionBullGelPzVB_gb.pdf>
