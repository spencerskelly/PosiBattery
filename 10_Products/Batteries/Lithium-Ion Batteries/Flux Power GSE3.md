# Flux Power GSE3

## Definition

GSE3 model of the [[Flux Power GSE Pack]] family, a lithium battery for airport ground support equipment.

## Notes

- **2021 brochure (Document - Flux Power GSE Battery Pack V.2 Brochure (2021)):** 35.3 x 19.9 x 26.2 in (89.7 x 50.5 x 66.5 cm); 875 lb (397 kg); maximum continuous external charge current 300 A; 80 V (76.8 V) nominal; charge 0 to 55 C, discharge -20 to 55 C (family-wide); BMS 2.0; isolated external CANbus. Source: Flux Power GSE pack brochure (T1, read through a summarizing tool), retrieved 2026-10-13. <https://www.fluxpower.com/hubfs/GSE%20Battery%20Pack%20V.2%20-%20Brochure.pdf>
- **2023 sheet (Document - Flux Power GSE3 Product Brochure (SA-808, 2023-12-18)):** 80 V (76.8 V); 315 Ah; 24.2 kWh; LiFePO4; 150 A maximum continuous external charge current; -30 to 45 C operating; IP56; integral BMS; SkyBMS Telematics; optional integrated heaters; over 3,500 cycles at 80 percent depth of discharge (maker claim); truck adapters available. Source: Flux Power GSE3 brochure (T1, read through a summarizing tool), retrieved 2026-10-13. <https://www.fluxpower.com/hubfs/GSE3%20Product%20Brochure.pdf>
- Capacity and energy: 315 Ah and 24.2 kWh at 76.8 V (2023 sheet). The 2021 brochure lists 25.2 kWh among its energies; matching it to the GSE3 is an analyst inference from 80 V x 315 Ah (C213, IB-166).
- Functions, designs and the SkyBMS link are held on the family note [[Flux Power GSE Pack]]; values here were read through a summarizing tool and not checked against the raw PDFs (IB-166).
