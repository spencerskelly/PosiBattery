# Document - Fasken Hosting a Charging Station Under the Federal Clean Fuel Regulations (2022-08)

## Definition

A law firm's 2022 summary of who can create credits for charging under Canada's Clean Fuel Regulations.

## Notes

**How it was read:** through a summarizing fetch tool (second-hand), not the raw file.

Added in the 2026-10-17 web research round. Law firm article on hosting a charging station under the CFR (2022).

- **Who creates credits:** a person recognized as a registered creator; a charging-site host (owns or leases a station and has the legal right to have it installed) may create credits and sell them to a primary supplier, or work through a registered creator.
- **Method:** gap between baseline carbon intensity and the electricity's carbon intensity, times kWh supplied to electric vehicles; each credit equals one tonne CO2e.
- **Reporting:** the station must communicate with a server (internet, cellular or connected-vehicle link) to report the quantity of electricity and when it was supplied; no metering standard stated.
- **Not stated:** whether only road vehicles qualify; forklifts.
