## Notes

**Content recorded:**
- Company says it is one of the leaders in the lift truck industry by market share in the Americas and worldwide; no numeric share and no named competitors. Lists suppliers of alternative power sources including lithium-ion and hydrogen as competitive pressure and calls lithium-ion and fuel-cell lift trucks a developing market.
- Revenue mix 2025 / 2024 / 2023: lift trucks 71 / 75 / 74%; Bolzoni 5 / 4 / 5%; parts 16 / 14 / 15%; service, rental and other 8 / 7 / 6%. Internal combustion lift trucks 40 / 45 / 44% and electric lift trucks 31 / 30 / 30% of revenue. Direct customer sales program 28 / 22 / 19% of new lift truck revenue. Dealers in 111 countries; at 2025-12-31 Hyster 103, Yale 57, dual-branded 57, Maximal 39. Sumitomo NACCO (50% JV) sold about 9,000 lift trucks in 2025. Nuvera merged into the HYMH subsidiary in Q2 2025 and is reported within Americas. The fetch stopped before the results section, so revenue, unit shipments and Nuvera figures were not read here.
