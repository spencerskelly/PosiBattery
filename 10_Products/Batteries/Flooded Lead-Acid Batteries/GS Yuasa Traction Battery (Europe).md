# GS Yuasa Traction Battery (Europe)

## Definition

GS Yuasa 24, 48 and 80 V traction batteries built from 2 V cells in European DIN and BS sizes, with an optional Air-Mix electrolyte mixing system.

## Notes

- GS Yuasa says its new European range (October 2021) is available in 24, 48 and 80 V from 2 V traction cells, built to DIN/EN 60254-2 in DIN and BS sizes, supplied in a dip-coated tray, plug-and-play, low maintenance with minimised water consumption and quick charging, and fully recyclable; an optional Air-Mix system improves electrolyte mixing to reduce stratification and premature failure. Source: Logistics Business, Warehouse News and Autobiz (T2), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.logisticsbusiness.com/?p=40376>
- **Type note:** the sources say 'minimised water consumption' and give no chemistry label, so it is filed as flooded lead-acid on that wording.
