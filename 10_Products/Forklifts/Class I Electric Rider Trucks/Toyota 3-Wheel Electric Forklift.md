# Toyota 3-Wheel Electric Forklift

## Definition

Toyota multi-use three-wheel electric counterbalance forklift with a 36/48 V system, AC lift and drive, MyInsights telematics pre-installed and optional lithium-ion battery.

## Notes

- Toyota says the 3-Wheel Electric Forklift has a 36/48 V electrical system with an AC lift and drive system, maintenance-free AC motors, a programmable maintenance timer, MyInsights telematics pre-installed, and lithium-ion batteries as an option. Source: Toyota forklift site (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.toyotaforklift.com/forklifts/3-wheel-electric-forklift>
- Toyota's electric line covers lift capacities from 2,000 to 17,500 lb and includes forklifts, walkie pallet jacks, stackers, order pickers and reach trucks, each with an optional lithium-ion battery. Source: Toyota blog (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.toyotaforklift.com/resource-library/blog/toyota-products/electric-products-for-any-warehousing-need>
