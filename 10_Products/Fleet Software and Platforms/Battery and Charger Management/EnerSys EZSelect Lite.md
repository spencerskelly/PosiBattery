# EnerSys EZSelect Lite

## Definition

EnerSys entry-level battery-room management system: a Charger Monitoring Device on each charger guides operators to the next battery.

## Notes

- CMD per charger with blue LED for next battery; tracks charging and cooling activity; diagnostics and early alerts; no certified installer or configuration. Announced 2026-06-10. Source: EnerSys news release (T1, second-hand), <https://www.enersys.com/en/about-us/news/enersys-has-introduced-ezselect-lite-system.-real-time-battery-monitoring-performance-optimisation-features-prevention-over-correction/>
- **Relation to "EZ Select" unresolved:** the IRONCLAD guide lists "EZ Select"; this release names "EZSelect Lite" (entry level) and a product-page slug says "EZSelect battery selection system". Whether Lite is a tier of one product or a separate product is not known (C223, IB-183). No separate EZ Select note is created yet.
- Vendor claims (time saved, payback, battery life) are marketing figures and are not recorded as metrics.
