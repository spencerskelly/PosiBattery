# Cat Lithium-Ion Battery Option

## Definition

Cat lithium-ion battery option on its electric counterbalance trucks, with an easy charging hatch and socket as standard.

## Notes

- Cat says both lithium-ion and lead-acid batteries are available on its electric trucks, and an easy charging hatch and socket come as standard with lithium-ion; a Cat page says the Li-ion option enhances truck performance and enables fast charging. Source: Cat Lift Trucks blog and 48 V release (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.catlifttruck.com/blog/importance-options-forklift-specification>
- **Not stated:** the battery maker (Triathlon USA supplies the UniCarriers lithium option in North America; nothing says it also supplies Cat).
- **Design characteristics, with citations:**
  - [[Truck Charging Port]] (V): <https://www.catlifttruck.com/blog/importance-options-forklift-specification>

- **Update 2026-10-13:** a search for supplier, voltage and capacity returned only Cat truck listings; nothing new was found. Left thin (IB-191).

- **Update 2026-10-13:** Cat EAME page: Li-ion option on almost all Cat electric counterbalance and warehouse truck ranges; full charge in as little as 1 hour; lifespan 3 to 4 times lead-acid; optional 5-year warranty subject to annual check-ups; intelligent BMS; losses up to 30% lower. The 2020-06-30 release names a "LIBAT" option with a retrofit conversion kit for the 80 V EP25-35(C)N range. No battery maker is named on either page (footer: Logisnext Europe B.V.). Sources: <https://www.catlifttruck.com/solutions/power-sources/li-ion-batteries>; <https://www.catlifttruck.com/news/li-ion-technology-80v>
