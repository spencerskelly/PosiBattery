# Powerfleet

## Definition

Telematics vendor with a forklift gateway, pedestrian proximity detection and forklift safety lights.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Accessory Maker, Software Vendor.
- Powerfleet's material handling telematics page describes vehicle access control by unique driver identification, a machine-learning impact sensor on the forklift frame, a Forklift Gateway (VAC) that connects optional sensors and cameras, pedestrian proximity detection without wearable tags, and a Forklift Safety Light family. Source: Powerfleet material handling telematics page (T1), retrieved 2026-10-03. <https://www.powerfleet.com/?p=30065>
