# OSHA 1910.178(g)(1) - Charging Installations Shall Be in Designated Areas

## Definition

Per a training-slide paraphrase of 29 CFR 1910.178(g)(1), battery charging installations shall be located in designated areas.

## Notes

- **External obligation, not a product requirement.** The standard text itself was not read; the paragraph number and wording come from undated slides of uncertain origin (IB-280).
- The same slides' battery wording (electrolyte, gassing, vent caps) is lead-acid language; how paragraph (g) reads for lithium is unknown (C319).
- See [[Lithium Forklift Charging Area Fire and Safety Code Register]] and [[Occupational Safety and Health Administration]].
