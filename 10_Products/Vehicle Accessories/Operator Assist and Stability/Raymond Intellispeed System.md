# Raymond Intellispeed System

## Definition

Raymond option that adjusts orderpicker travel speed to load height.

## Notes

- Listed as "advanced intellispeed" on the 5500 page. Distinct from [[Raymond Travel Speed Control]] (configurable 1 to 6 mph limit on the 4000 Series); whether they share hardware is not stated. Source: <https://www.raymondcorp.com/forklifts/orderpickers/5500-orderpicker> (T1, second-hand).
