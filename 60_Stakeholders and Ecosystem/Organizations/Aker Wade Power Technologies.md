# Aker Wade Power Technologies

## Definition

Charger and monitoring company acquired by Exide; its chargers and battery monitoring were to be sold under both the Aker Wade and GNB Industrial Power brands.

## Notes

- **Role (working label):** Charger Maker, Monitor Maker.
- Exide announced the acquisition of Aker Wade Power Technologies, which designs and manufactures advanced charging systems for industrial forklifts, and said chargers and battery monitoring solutions would be marketed under both brands. Source: EE Power news report (T2), retrieved 2026-10-02. <https://eepower.com/news/exide-expands-motive-power-offering-with-aker-wade-acquisition/>
