# Wiferion CW1000 Wireless Charging System

## Definition

Wiferion CW1000, a 1 kW inductive charger (15-60 V, up to 42 A, 5-30 mm gap, +/-30 mm tolerance) for small AGVs and AMRs.

## Notes

- The page states both 1 kW and 1,250 W continuous (C256). Source: <https://www.wiferion.com/us/products/cw-1000-wireless-charging-1000w/> (T1, second-hand).
