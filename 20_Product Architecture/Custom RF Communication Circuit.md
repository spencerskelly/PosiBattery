# Custom RF Communication Circuit

## Definition

Product-specific or proprietary RF communication circuit that does not map cleanly to Bluetooth, LoRa, or Wi-Fi.

## Notes

- Frequency, modulation, protocol, and peer architecture must be established by product evidence before this solution is assigned to a product.
