# UniCarriers Lighting Packages

## Definition

UniCarriers lighting packages listed as options on the SCX N2 (contents not described); MX2 trucks list extra lights as a customization.

## Notes

**Summary:**
UniCarriers optional lighting packages for SCX N2, MX2 and other trucks that improve visibility and operator comfort.

**Marketed features:**
- SCX: fan and compartment light package on the overhead guard canopy
- Overhead-guard LED headlights and rear LED work light
- Optional front work lights and rear strobe
- GO6 Deluxe Light Package: headlights, front turn signals, rear brake/turn/backup combination lights
- Rotating beacon available
